      The Compendium⚓︎

      Changelog⚓︎

      Version 1.7.4⚓︎

      August 12, 2026

      General⚓︎
      • Updated Critical Hit Table: now uses more restrained durations and conditions, but expanded effectiveness vs. specific armor types and creature types. Aberrations and Dragons don't have any effective damage types. Medium Armor, Heavy Armor, and Beasts are the only types that have multiple effective damage types, due to how common they are.
      Damage Type Effective Against... Reasoning
      Bludgeoning Heavy Armor Sundering plated defenses
      Piercing Medium Armor Piercing gaps in armor
      Slashing Unarmored, Light Armor Slashing non-metal material
      Acid Constructs Melting inorganic material
      Cold Oozes Low temperatures restrict creatures that are mostly fluid
      Fire Beasts, Plants Natural creatures have few protections from flames
      Lightning Medium Armor, Heavy Armor Metallic armor conducts electricity
      Poison Giants, Humanoids Standard biology is susceptible to poison
      Thunder Elementals Concussive force against indeterminate forms
      Force — Most neutral damage type
      Necrotic Celestials Evil is effective against Good
      Psychic Beasts, Monstrosities Simple minds are easily manipulated/disturbed
      Radiant Fiends, Undead Good is effective against Evil
      Conditions⚓︎
      • Added Withering: a necrotic DoT that reduces max HP. Currently only inflicted by Critical Hits.
      Bastions⚓︎
      • Added Studio for Bardic Bastions.
      Magic Items⚓︎
      • Added section sorted by item type, in addition to rarity.
      • Added the Rare and Very Rare tiers.
      • Added Champion's Weapon, Weapon of Elements.
      • Updated Medusan Signet to be non-attunement, but only 1 use.
      • Added Cursed as a rarity: Cursed items can't be crafted or infused, always require Attunement, and share one rule for ending that Attunement.
      Updates⚓︎
      • A number of items from Rare and Very Rare have been downgraded to Uncommon or Rare, respectively. Many of these are items whose bonuses to Attack, Damage, Spell Save DC, or AC are now covered by Masterwork and had that bonus stripped.
      • Split the Feather Token into the Anchor, Fan, and Tree Tokens, joined by the Bird, Swan Boat, and Whip Tokens.
      Items⚓︎
      • Rare and Very Rare Potions and Scrolls have been ported over.
      • Added the Potion of Giant Strength, replacing the Potion of Hill Giant Strength with a single card covering every giant.
      Races⚓︎
      • Added Elven Name Generator and Dark Elven Name Generator.

      Version 1.7.3⚓︎

      August 5, 2026

      General⚓︎
      Navigation⚓︎
      • Flattened and reordered the Rules Rolodex; the Adventuring and Positioning groupings are dissolved, and each rule now sits directly in its own rules page.
      • Removed the in-page tables of contents. The left navigation and mega-menu handle page-to-page navigation; the alphanumeric jump rails (the keyword index and catalog pages) remain, and now scroll rather than wrap.
      • Added a floating back-to-top button.
      Rules Organization⚓︎
      • Merged Visibility (Obscurement, Lighting, and Senses) into Targeting.
      • Merged Time into Resting, adding the Week, Tenday, and Month.
      • Folded Abilities and Proficiency into the Dice Rolls overview.
      Feats⚓︎
      • Overhaul of Fighting Style & Weapon Mastery systems.
      • Weapon Masteries are now Weapon Techniques, and are made available through Fighting Style feats.
      • Some weapon & armor related General Feats have been turned into Fighting Styles (Lightly Armored, Charger, etc.).
      • Features and Classes that used to grant Weapon Masteries now grant Fighting Style feats, and general distribution of Fighting Styles has increased.
      LV Fighter / Barbarian / Rogue Ranger / Paladin Artificer / Monk Bladesinger
      1 3 3 - -
      2 3 3 2 -
      3 3 3 2 1
      4 4 4 3 1
      5 4 4 3 1
      6 4 4 3 2
      7 5 4 3 2
      8 5 4 3 2
      9 5 4 3 2
      10 6 5 4 2
      11 6 5 4 2
      • This doesn't count other features that may grant specific weapon techniques (e.g. Fighter's Tactical Master or Monk's Martial Arts).
      • Notes
        • Fighter and Barbarian are the baseline martial progression. Fighter actually has a higher budget, as the archetypal "flexible" martial class, but it's partially spent on the new Technique Initiate feature that replaced the Weapon Mastery feature.
        • Rogue is defaulting to Barbarian's progression for now. When Rogue is fully ported, it may be brought to a different level.
        • Fighter starts higher as the archetypal flexible martial class.
        • Ranger and Paladin start at the same baseline but progress slower, since they get Spellcasting at level 2.
        • Artificer starts even lower and a level later, since Fighting Styles are an accent to their kit, not foundational, and their Spellcasting includes Cantrips at level 1. Ranger and Paladin can functionally choose to drop down to this tier by taking Blessed/Druidic Warrior.
        • Monk uses the Artificer's scaling for similar reasons: most Monk features and subclasses include unique "Weapon Techniques" for Unarmed Strikes/Monk Weapons, so general Fighting Styles are accents. A weapons master subclass, like Kensei, would likely receive additional Fighting Styles to fit their theme.
        • Bladesinger includes "Full Spellcasters with Martial Subclasses" like Bladesinger, Swords Bard, etc. They mirror Third-Caster subclasses for Martials that get a taste of simple magic, but not true caster progression.
      Updated⚓︎
      • Renamed Fey Touched and Shadow Touched to Touch of the Fey and Touch of the Shadows (opening up room for other, easily searchable, "Touch of the X" feats later).
      • Fighting Styles have been overhauled into three tiers: Initiate, Expert, and Master.
        • Initiate Fighting Styles are generically useful and/or provide the Proficiencies to take other Fighting Styles, and usually have no prerequisites (except Class-specific Fighting Styles).
        • Expert Fighting Styles enhance a wide array of builds that span weapon types, and often have an Ability prerequisite.
        • Master Fighting Styles become available at Level 4 and require a specific Expert Fighting Style and/or Proficiency that they enhance.
        • Fighting Initiate makes it easier for a non-martial character to gain a Weapon proficiency and a corresponding Technique/Fighting Style.

      Removed⚓︎
      • Poisoner. Redundant with better poisons, no Poisoner's Kit trade, and the Elemental Adept feat.
      • Weapon Master. Redundant with Weapon Masteries becoming Fighting Styles.
      Conditions⚓︎
      • Prone no longer triggers an Opportunity Attack on standing up.
      Races⚓︎
      • The Races table now lists each race's Abilities, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Reach, Hit Points, and Senses.
      Classes⚓︎
      • Renamed Feature Ability back to Class Ability.
      Barbarian⚓︎
      • Fighting Style is moved to Level 1, replaced at Level 2 with Danger Sense; formerly part of Unarmored Stance, now stands alone albeit one level later.
      Fighter⚓︎
      • Level 1's Weapon Mastery is now Technique Initiate; gives a taste of the level 7 Technique Master.
      Monk⚓︎
      • Slow Fall wrapped into Unarmored Stance to make room for Fighting Styles.
      • Martial Arts now builds on the Striker fighting style, rather than duplicating a slightly parallel effect.
      Ranger⚓︎
      • Fighting Style is moved to Level 1, replaced at Level 2 with Favored Enemy, restoring some of the old hunter flavor.
      Bestiary⚓︎
      • Moved Non-Player Characters (Disposition, Companions, Familiars, and Mounts) into the Creature Codex.
      Spells⚓︎
      • Booming Blade now triggers when the target expends movement, not when the target moves; this means that standing up from Prone will trigger the rider, but being propelled or teleporting won't.

      Version 1.7.2⚓︎

      July 25, 2026

      General⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Codified Nonlethal Attacks. Any Melee attack can now be declared Nonlethal, not just weapon attacks.
      • Codified Bloodied state; not a condition, unlike its siblings Downed and Stable, since Bloodied doesn't have any inherent rules of its own.
      • Created a new Positioning section in the Rules Rolodex covering Movement, Cover, Visibility, Range & Targeting, and Areas of Effect.
      • Added Wall shape to Areas of Effect: a rectangular length × width × height. On a 5-foot grid, walls that are meant to occupy tiles (Wall of Water, Wall of Fire) now fill entire spaces on a grid. Walls that are meant to be barriers (Wall of Force, Wall of Stone) are drawn along the boundaries of the grid.
      Updates⚓︎
      • Moved the Keyword Index into the main Rules Rolodex page. Fixed misaligned links, repeated keywords, etc.
      • Moved Character Creation into the main Hero Handbook Page.
      • Removed the Stabilize Action; its functionality is scoped to the Downed condition.
      • Removed Carry Capacity; Powerful Build (Ranger Imprint, Goliath Feature) now doubles Jump distance instead of carry capacity.
      Items⚓︎
      • Recategorized Potions, Poisons, Scrolls, and Materials as their own Adventuring Gear categories.
      • Consolidated rules for Crafting and Masterwork to the general Item Index page.
      • Consolidated rules for Weapon Types to the general Weapons page. Weapon Properties and Weapon Masteries are still their own pages.
      • Consolidated rules for Armor Types and Shields to a single page.
      • Consolidated rules for Infusing Items to the general Magic Items page.
      • Added definitions for Natural Weapons and Natural Armor.
      Spells⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Added 5th-Level Spells.
      Updates⚓︎
      • Animate Objects, Banishing Smite, Conjure Volley, Destructive Wave, Fabricate, Swift Quiver, Tree Stride are moved to 3rd Level for Artificer, Paladin, and Ranger access.
      • Animate Objects, Fabricate, and Summon Construct are now Artificer-exclusive, to keep the Wizard from completely subsuming the magical crafting archetype.
      • Augury, Divination, Commune, Contact Other Plane, and Legend Lore are removed; their function is encompassed by INT checks or the corresponding Cleric/Warlock features.
      • Increased Insect Plague's damage to 6d10.
      • Revivify and Raise Dead now give Exhaustion levels.
      Classes⚓︎
      Updates⚓︎
      • Renamed Class Ability to Starting Ability and Secondary Ability to Feature Ability; the old names were confusing alongside Primary and Secondary Class.

      Version 1.7.1⚓︎

      July 17, 2026

      General⚓︎
      Updates⚓︎
      • Dispositions are now canonically Ally, Independent, and Enemy. Effects that used to use "friendly", "indifferent", "hostile", "willing", etc. now use one of these three; Allies are always willing, Combat begins when an Enemy is in an Encounter, and Independent creatures aren't your friend or foe.
      • Fixed a formatting issue with nested stat blocks (e.g. Naval Ram inside the Hammerhead stat block).
      Bestiary⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Added the Creature Codex: rules for NPCs, Character Traits, and Creature Types have migrated out of the Rules Rolodex.
      • Summon Stat Blocks have been migrated into the Bestiary. Each stat block links back to the spell, feature, or item that summons it.
      Spells⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Added 4th-Level Spells.
      Updates⚓︎
      • Some 4th-Level spells demoted to 3rd to maintain half-caster access: Aura of Life, Aura of Purity, Fabricate, Secret Chest, Stone Shape, Summon Construct

      Version 1.7.0⚓︎

      July 13, 2026

      General⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Campaign Playlists added.
      • Enabled heading ⚓︎ permalinks for quick copy-pasting URLs.
      Feats⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Added Strike of the Giants feats as Goliath Ancestry Feats.
      Magic Items⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • Common and Uncommon Infusions added.
      • All homebrew, custom, and higher-rarity Infusions encountered during the campaign added.
      • Boots of the Savannah, paralleling the Boots of the Winterlands
      Updates⚓︎
      • Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location -> Amulet of Nondetection
      • Spell Scroll scaling changed; caps at 6th-level, and rarities for 3rd-level and higher scrolls increased. Crafting costs and times adjusted accordingly (generally, scrolls are more expensive).
      • Enspelled Items (Armor, Staff, Weapon) are likewise scaled, capping at 5th-level.
      • Rod of the Pact Keeper and Wraps of Unarmed Power become Common items and lose their bonus to attack/save DCs (in favor of the Masterwork system).
      • Unified some wording and mechanics on summoning items (Bag of Tricks, Dancing Broom, Elemental Gem, Silver Raven Figurine, Staff of the Python) to use companion rules.
      • Items that destroy themselves randomly now don't. Items that were gated behind a random chance per use now have a set number of uses each day (Wind Fan)
      Spells⚓︎
      New⚓︎
      • 4th-Level Dragonmark Spells added.
