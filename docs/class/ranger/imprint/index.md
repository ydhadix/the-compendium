# Primal Imprints

Primal Imprints are traits a ranger copies from the beasts they study, learning to survive by watching how the wild around them adapts. Each Imprint mirrors a creature's natural gift — keen senses, a burrower's claws, a glider's wings, or the lungs to breathe underwater.


### Level 1

| Imprints | Description |
|---|---|
| [Bioluminescence](bioluminescence.md) | Shed light from your own body, glowing bright or dim at will. |
| [Camouflage](camouflage.md) | Blend into your surroundings so others struggle to spot you. |
| [Hold Breath](hold-breath.md) | Hold your breath for up to an hour. |
| [Keen Senses](keen-senses.md) | Sharp eyes and ears grant Advantage on sight- and hearing-based Perception. |
| [Powerful Build](powerful-build.md) | Beastly might grants Advantage on Athletics and doubles what you can carry. |
| [Sure-Footed](sure-footed.md) | Keep your footing against knockdowns and climb as easily as you walk. |

### Level 4
| Imprints | Description |
|---|---|
| [Amphibious](amphibious.md) | Breathe underwater and swim at your full Speed. |
| [Cold Tolerance](cold-tolerance.md) | Shrug off ice, snow, and the bite of extreme cold. |
| [Heat Tolerance](heat-tolerance.md) | Cross scorching sand and endure extreme heat and flame. |
| [Jumper](jumper.md) | Leap with your Dexterity and climb at your full Speed. |
| [Mimicry](mimicry.md) | Imitate simple sounds you've heard to deceive the ear. |
| [Nocturnal](nocturnal.md) | See in the dark out to 120 feet. |
| [Roving](roving.md) | Move without provoking Opportunity Attacks, and range farther each turn. |
| [Skittish](skittish.md) | Dart away or slip out of sight as a Bonus Action. |

### Level 7
| Imprints | Description |
|---|---|
| [Amorphous](amorphous.md) | Slip free of grapples and squeeze through the narrowest gaps. |
| [Echolocation](echolocation.md) | Map your surroundings by sound, gaining Blindsight while you can hear. |
| [Glider](glider.md) | Ride the air on your turn and fall without harm. |
| [Web Walker](web-walker.md) | Ignore clinging webs and climb sheer surfaces with ease. |

### Level 10
| Imprints | Description |
|---|---|
| [Burrower](burrower.md) | Dig through earth, leaving a tunnel in your wake. |
