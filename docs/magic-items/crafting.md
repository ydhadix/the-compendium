# Crafting Magic Items

Nonmagical items can be infused with magical effects, turning them into magic items. An item can have up to two magical effects at a time, and only one of those effects can require attunement.

## Requirements

To infuse an item with magic, you must have proficiency with Arcana and the Artisan's Tool used to craft the item.

If the magical effect has the Spellcasting property or uses the effect of a spell in its description, you must know or prepare those spells every day you spend infusing the item.

## Time and Cost

The cost of infusing an item is paid in raw materials and time based on the magical effect's rarity. Every day of infusing requires 8 hours of work, but the total hours and days don't need to be consecutive.

Characters can work together to shorten the infusing time, provided each character has the appropriate proficiencies and spells. Divide the time required by the number of characters working on the item.

| Rarity | Time* | Cost* |
|---|---|---|
| common | 5 days | 50 gold |
| uncommon | 10 days | 200 gold |
| rare | 50 days | 2,000 gold |
| very rare | 125 days | 20,000 gold |
| legendary | 250 days | 100,000 gold |
| *consumables take half as long and cost half as much | | |

### Spell Scrolls

The time and cost to craft a Spell Scroll is based on the level of the spell it contains, instead of the rarity of the scroll.

| Spell Level | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| cantrip | 1 day | 15 gold |
| 1st | 1 day | 25 gold |
| 2nd | 3 days | 100 gold |
| 3rd | 5 days | 150 gold |
| 4th | 10 days | 1,000 gold |
| 5th | 25 days | 1,500 gold |
| 6th | 40 days | 10,000 gold |
| 7th | 50 days | 12,500 gold |
| 8th | 60 days | 15,000 gold |
| 9th | 120 days | 50,000 gold |

## Removing Magic Effects

Before you infuse a magical effect into an item that already has two effects, you must choose one existing effect to end. If the new effect requires attunement, you must choose an existing attunement effect to end, if possible. The chosen effect is destroyed as part of the crafting process.

If you have Dispel Magic known or prepared, you can also destroy an item's magical effect with 8 hours of work.

The magical effect of an Artifact or cursed item cannot be removed without special methods unique to the item.

## Transferring Magic Effects

You can transfer an existing magical effect to an appropriate item. You only need Arcana proficiency to transfer a magical effect, and the process costs no gold and takes only half the time it would take to infuse the effect normally.
