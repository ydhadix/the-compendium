    #### Gloves of the Stormrider

    | Rare Clothing (Gloves) | Attunement |
    |---|---|

    ###### Storm Sense
    You know the general direction and distance to any active thunderstorms within **5** miles.

    ###### Lightning Resistance
    You gain Resistance to Lightning Damage.

    ###### Bolt Rider
    _Reaction_

    After any target within **30** feet takes Lightning Damage, you can teleport to a space within **5** feet of that target.

    ###### Ride the Lightning
    _Reaction_

    After the path of a Ranged Attack or effect that deals Lightning Damage passes within your Reach, you can teleport up to **100** feet up or down the path.
