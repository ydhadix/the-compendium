    #### Ring of Spell Storing

    | Rare Clothing (Jewelry) | attunement (spellcaster) |
    |---|---|
    | charges | 5 (1d4 + 1 at dawn) |

    Before you cast a spell using a spell slot whose level is no more than this item’s remaining charges, you can roll a d6.
    Spell’s Level or higher:  You can cast the spell using a charge for each level of the spell instead of a spell slot.
    Less than Spell’s Level:  You cast the spell using a spell slot as normal.  If you don’t have a spell slot to use, the casting fails (You still use the casting time needed for the spell).
