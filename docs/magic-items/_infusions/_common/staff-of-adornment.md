    #### Staff of Adornment

    | Common Weapon (Stave) | — |
    |---|---|

    If you place a Tiny object weighing no more than **1** pound above the tip of this staff, the object floats **1** inch from the staff's tip and remains there until it is removed.
    The staff can have up to **3** objects floating at any given time.
