    #### Hat of Vermin

    | Common Clothing (Hat) | — |
    |---|---|
    | Charges | 3 per Day |

    _Magic Action_

    _1 Charge_

    While holding the hat, you can summon your choice of a Bat, a Frog, or a Rat in the hat.  The summoned creature is Indifferent toward you, isn't under your control, and tries to get away from you as quickly as possible.  It otherwise behaves normally and disappears after **1** hour.
