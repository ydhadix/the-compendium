    #### Ersatz Eye

    | Common Wondrous Item | — |
    |---|---|

    While this prosthetic eye is inserted, you can see through it as though it were your natural eye, and it can't be removed against your will.

    ###### Insert or Remove Eye
    _Magic Action_

    You can insert this prosthetic to replace a lost eye, or remove the eye.
