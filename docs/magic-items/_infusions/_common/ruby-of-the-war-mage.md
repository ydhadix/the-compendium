??? infusion "Ruby of the War Mage | Focus (Any)"

    #### Ruby of the War Mage

    | Common Focus (Any) | Attunement (Spellcaster) |
    |---|---|

    While this ruby is attached to a weapon, you can use the weapon as a Spellcasting Focus, and the ruby can't be removed against your will.

    ###### Attach or Detach Ruby
    _Magic Action_

    You can attach this ruby to a weapon or detach it.
