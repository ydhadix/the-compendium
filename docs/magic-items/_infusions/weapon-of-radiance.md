    #### Weapon of Radiance

    | Uncommon Weapon (Any) | attunement (spellcaster) |
    |---|---|
    | charges | 4 (1d4 at dawn) |

    This weapon emits 30 feet of Bright Light and Dim Light 30 feet further.
    Bonus Action: You can extinguish or reignite the light.
    Reaction, 1 Charge: Before you are attacked, you can force the attacker to make a DC 15 Fortitude save.  On a failed save, the attacker is Blinded until the end of its next turn.
