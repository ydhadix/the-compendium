    #### Orrery of the Wanderer

    | Artifact | — |
    |---|---|

    _Hyperion: Astral Adventure_

    This delicate and exquisitely crafted clockwork orrery features multiple geared components whose sweeping hands and dials represent the complex interplay of planar and magical realms.  The main body is about **6** inches in diameter, and contains up to **6** additional components, individually powerful relics containing a portion of the orrery's power.

    ###### Part of a Whole
    While a component is not installed in the completed chassis, Attuning to the component may have unpredictable side effects, as determined by the DM.

    ##### Chassis
    _Attunement (Special)_

    This hollow, golden shell contains the framework necessary to connect the other components of the orrery.

    ###### Some Assembly Required
    You can spend **1** hour to install or remove a component from the chassis, provided you have Proficiency in Tinker's Tools.  While a component is installed, you can't Attune to it directly.

    One creature can Attune to the chassis for each component installed in it _(minimum **0**)_.  Attuning to the chassis doesn't count against your Attunement limit, and being away from the chassis doesn't end your Attunement to it.

    After you Attune to the chassis, choose one of the installed components.  You can use the features of the chosen component.  Each component can be chosen by only one creature at a time.

    After you finish a Short Rest within **1** mile of the chassis, you can change your choice to a different component.

    ###### Sentience
    The chassis is a sentient item with an Intelligence of **+4**, a Wisdom of **+4**, and a Charisma of **+0**.  It has hearing and Darkvision out to **120** feet.  Its alignment is Lawful Neutral.

    The chassis knows all Standard languages, Celestial, Deep Speech, and Gith, but communicates exclusively in Elvish using its Dimensional Radio.

    ###### Dimensional Radio
    The chassis and all creatures Attuned to it can communicate telepathically, broadcasting to the chassis and each other creature Attuned to it.

    ##### Chronometer
    _Attunement — 1 Charge (1 per Day)_

    This precision timepiece uses the position of celestial bodies to pinpoint your location and heading.  This component adds **+1** to your Armor Class, and you always know which way is north.

    ###### Dimensional Anchor
    You have Advantage on Saving Throws to resist being sent to another plane of existence against your will.

    ###### Dimensional Smite
    _Free Action_

    _1 Charge_

    After you damage a creature, you can have it make a Will save _(DC = **15**)_.

    | | |
    |---|---|
    | **Fail** | The creature is banished to a harmless demiplane until the end of its next Turn. |
    | **Success** | No effect. |

    ##### Dimensional Loop
    _Attunement — 1 Charge (1 per Day)_

    This ovular, golden loop detects and manipulates interdimensional portals.  This component adds **+1** to your Will saves, and you can't be surprised.

    ###### Portal Sense
    _Magic Action_

    You can cast _Detect Portal_ without a Spell Slot.

    ###### Portal Cracker
    _Magic Action_

    _1 Charge_

    Choose a portal within your Reach and make a DC **20** Arcana check.

    | | |
    |---|---|
    | **Success** | You force the portal open or closed for **1** hour. |
    | **Fail** | You take **3d8** Psychic Damage. |

    ##### Far Gear
    _Attunement — 1 Charge (1 per Day)_

    This set of intricate, platinum gears calculate the shortest path to a destination.  This component adds **+1** to your Attack Rolls, and Difficult Terrain doesn't cost you extra movement.

    ###### Fast Traveler
    _Magic Action_

    You can cast _Longstrider_, targeting yourself, without a Spell Slot.

    ###### Far Traveler
    _Free Action_

    _1 Charge_

    Choose a space you can see within **60** feet.  Until the end of your Turn, you treat that space as if it were within **5** feet of you.

    ##### Rotor of Return
    _Attunement — 1 Charge (1 per Day)_

    Shifting runes on this circular disk perpetually record recent dates, times, and coordinates.  This component adds **+1** to your Reflex saves, and you can accurately recall anything you have perceived within the past **30** days.

    ###### Rebound Code
    After you become Stabilized with **0** Hit Points, you heal by **[CON]** and gain **1** stack of Exhaustion.

    ###### Recall Code
    _Magic Action_

    You record your current location in the rotor.

    ###### Recall Signal

    _Bonus Action_

    _1 Charge_

    You can teleport up to **500** feet to the last location recorded in the rotor.

    ##### Timepiece of Travel
    _Attunement — 1 Charge (1 per Day)_

    This set of shimmering brass dials and gears track the passage of time across different planes.  This component adds **+1** to your Spell Save DC, and you always know what hour of the day it is.

    ###### Time Share
    _Free Action_

    After you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with one willing ally in the same combat.  You can't make this swap if you or the ally are Incapacitated.

    ###### Time Snare
    _Reaction_

    _1 Charge_

    Before a creature rolls a d20 , you can record the result and reroll the die.  A recorded result does not carry between Days.

    ###### Time Slip
    _Reaction_

    _1 recorded result_

    Before a creature rolls a d20, you can replace the d20 with the recorded result.

    ##### Wheel of Stars
    _Attunement — 1 Charge (1 per Day)_

    This astrolabe is etched with spinning constellations and a single, shifting line.  This component adds **+1** to your Fortitude saves, and you don't need to eat or drink.

    ###### Certain Fate
    Your Ranged Attack Rolls ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover.

    ###### Assert Fate
    _Reaction_

    _1 Charge_

    After a creature within **30** feet takes damage, you can grant it one additional Action.
