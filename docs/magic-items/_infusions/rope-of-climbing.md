    #### Rope of Climbing

    | Uncommon Wondrous Item | — |
    |---|---|

    This rope is 60 feet long and can hold up to 3,000 pounds.  It has AC 20, HP 20, and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage.  It regains 1 Hit Point every 5 minutes, but if it drops to 0 Hit Points it is destroyed.
    Magic Action: While holding one end of the rope, you can speak one of the following command words, causing the rope to animate, or its opposite command word to end the effect.
    **Command Word**: Effect
    **move / stop**: choose a location within 60 feet.  the other end of the rope moves 10 feet at the end of each of your turns until it reaches the location, or you tell it to stop.
    **fasten / unfasten**: the other end of the rope securely fastens itself to an object.
    **knot / unknot**: large knots appear at 1-foot intervals along the rope, shortening the rope to 50 feet and giving advantage to checks made to climb the rope.
    **coil / uncoil**: the rope ends all other effects and coils itself for carrying.
