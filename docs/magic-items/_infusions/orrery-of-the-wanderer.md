### *Orrery of the Wanderer*

| Artifact (Hyperion: Astral Adventure) | — |
|---|---|

This delicate and exquisitely crafted clockwork orrery features multiple geared components whose sweeping hands and dials represent the complex interplay of planar and magical realms.  The main body is about 6 inches in diameter, and contains up to 6 additional components, individually powerful relics containing a portion of the orrery’s power.

#### Part of a Whole
While a component is not installed in the completed chassis, attuning to the component may have unpredictable side effects, as determined by the DM.

### Chassis
*Attunement (Special)*
This hollow, golden shell contains the framework necessary to connect the other components of the orrery.

#### Some Assembly Required
A proficient creature with Tinker’s Tools in hand can spend 1 hour to install or remove a component of the Orrery of the Wanderer to the chassis.  While a component is installed in the chassis, creatures cannot be attuned to it.
One creature can attune to the chassis for each component installed in it (minimum 0).  Attuning to the chassis doesn’t count against your attunement limit, and being away from the chassis doesn’t end your attunement to it.
When you attune to the chassis, choose one of the installed components.  You can use the features of the chosen component.  Each component can be chosen by only one creature at a time.
When you finish a Short Rest within 1 mile of the chassis, you can change your choice to a different component.

#### Sentience
The chassis is a sentient item with an Intelligence of 4, a Wisdom of 4, and a Charisma of 0.  It has hearing and Darkvision out to 120 feet.  Its alignment is Lawful Neutral.
The chassis knows all standard languages, Celestial, Deep Speech, and Gith, but communicates exclusively in Elvish using its Dimensional Radio.

### Chronometer
*Attunement
1 Charge (1 at Dawn)*
This precision timepiece uses the position of celestial bodies to pinpoint your location and heading.
You have a +1 bonus to Armor Class, and you always know which way is north.

#### Dimensional Anchor
You have advantage on saves to resist being sent to another plane of existence against your will.

#### Dimensional Smite
1 Charge – After you damage a creature, you can force it to make a DC 15 Will save.  On a failed save, the creature is banished to a harmless demiplane until the end of its next turn.

### Dimensional Loop
*Attunement
1 Charge (1 at Dawn)*
This ovular, golden loop detects and manipulates interdimensional portals.
You have a +1 bonus to Will saves, and you can’t be surprised.

#### Portal Sense
Spellcasting – You cast the Detect Portal spell without a spell slot.

#### Portal Cracker
Magic Action – 1 Charge – Choose a portal within reach and make a DC 20 Arcana check.  On a success, you force the portal open or closed for 1 hour.  On a failure, you take 3d8 Psychic damage.

### Far Gear
*Attunement
1 Charge (1 at Dawn)*
This set of intricate, platinum gears calculate the shortest path to a destination.
You have a +1 bonus to Attack Rolls, and Difficult Terrain doesn’t cost you extra movement.

#### Fast Traveler
Spellcasting – You cast the Longstrider spell, targeting yourself, without a spell slot.

#### Far Traveler
Free Action – 1 Charge – Choose a space you can see within 60 feet.  Until the end of the turn, you treat that space as if it were within 5 feet of you.  (You can perceive through, attack through, move through, and otherwise interact with that space as though you were adjacent to that space.)

### Rotor of Return
*Attunement
1 Charge (1 at Dawn)*
Shifting runes on this circular disk perpetually record recent dates, times, and coordinates.
You have a +1 bonus to Reflex saves, and you can accurately recall anything you have perceived within the past 30 days.

#### Rebound Code
When you become Stabilized, if you have 0 hit points, you heal by your Constitution and gain 1 stack of Exhaustion.

#### Recall Code
Magic Action – You record your current location in the rotor.
Bonus Action – 1 Charge – You teleport up to 500 feet to the last location recorded in the rotor.  (If the location is more than 500 feet away, you teleport 500 feet in a straight line towards that location instead.)

### Timepiece of Travel
*Attunement
1 Charge (1 at Dawn)*
This set of shimmering brass dials and gears track the passage of time across different planes.
You have a +1 bonus to Saving Throw DCs, and you always know what hour of the day it is.

#### Time Share
Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with one willing ally in the same combat.  You can’t make this swap if you or the ally are Incapacitated.

#### Time Snare
Reaction – 1 Charge – After a creature makes a d20 Roll, you can record the result and reroll the d20.  A recorded roll is lost at Dawn.
Reaction – 1 recorded result – Before a creature makes a d20 Roll, you can replace the d20 with the recorded result.

### Wheel of Stars
*Attunement
1 Charge (1 at Dawn)*
This astrolabe is etched with spinning constellations and a single, shifting line.
You have a +1 bonus to Fortitude saves, and you don’t need to eat or drink.

#### Certain Fate
Your Ranged attack rolls ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters cover.

#### Assert Fate
Reaction – 1 Charge – After a creature within 30 feet takes damage, that creature takes an additional Action.
