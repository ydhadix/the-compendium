    #### Ring of Shooting Stars

    | Very Rare Clothing (Jewelry) | attunement (spellcaster) |
    |---|---|
    | charges | 6 (1d6 at dawn) |

    #### Guiding Lights
    Spellcasting: You can cast Dancing Lights, Light, or Starry Wisp.
    Spellcasting, 1 to 3 charges: You can cast Cure Wounds, Faerie Fire, or Guiding Bolt.  For each additional charge, increase the spell’s level by 1.

    #### Shooting Stars
    Magic Action, 1 to 3 charges: For each charge, choose a 10-foot radius Sphere within 60 feet.  Everything in each area makes a Reflex save.
    Fail: You deal 5d4 Radiant damage.
    Success: You deal half as much damage.

    #### Celestial Body
    Each day at dawn, choose one of the following effects:
    Sun: Before you use magic to deal Fire damage, you can change the damage type to Radiant, or vice-versa.
    Moon: After you use magic to heal another creature, you can gain your spellcasting ability as Temporary Hit Points.  While you have these THP, you are immune to being Frightened.
    Meteor: After you use Shooting Stars, you can teleport to a space in any of the areas.
    Bonus Action, 1 charge: You can change your Celestial Body.
