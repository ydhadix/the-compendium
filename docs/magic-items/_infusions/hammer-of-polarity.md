### *Hammer of Polarity*

| Very Rare Weapon (Hammer) | attunement (spellcaster) |
|---|---|
| charges | 7 (1d4+3 at dawn) |

#### Magnetic Compass
While you aren’t holding this weapon, it hovers in your space, remaining upright and within reach while leaving your hands free.  The weapon moves around you to indicate magnetic north without impeding your movement.
Magic Action: You turn this effect on or off.

#### Electromagic Conductor
This weapon can be used as a Spellcasting Focus.
Whenever you cast a spell that deals Lightning or Thunder damage, you can add your spellcasting ability to damage rolls of those types.

#### Attraction Point
This weapon has Thrown (20/60) and deals an additional 1d6 Lightning damage.  After you make a ranged attack with this weapon, it returns to your hand immediately after the attack hits or misses.
Once per Turn: After you hit a target up to your size with this weapon, you can pull the target 10 feet closer to you or knock the target Prone.

#### Repulsion Field
Reaction, 1 Charge: You can cast Shield.
Reaction, 4 Charges: Before you or a creature within 5 feet is attacked or makes a save, you can summon an immobile barrier of invisible force in a 5-foot radius aura for 1 minute (Concentration).
Nothing can pass into or out of the barrier, and anything inside the barrier can’t be affected by attacks or effects originating outside the barrier, and vice-versa (Including the triggering attack or effect).
