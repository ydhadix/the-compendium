### *Alchemy Jug*

| Uncommon Wondrous Item | — |
|---|---|
| charges | 1 at dawn |

This ceramic jug appears to hold a gallon of liquid and sloshes when shaken, even if the jug is empty.
Magic Action, 1 Charge: Choose one of the following liquids.  Afterwards, you can uncork the jug as a Utilize Action and pour that liquid out, up to 2 gallons per minute.  The maximum amount of liquid depends on the named liquid.
Once a liquid is named, the jug can’t produce a different liquid or more than the maximum amount of the named liquid until the next Dawn.
**Liquid**: Maximum Amount
**acid**: 8 ounces
**basic poison**: 4 ounces
**beer**: 4 gallons
**honey**: 1 gallon
**mayonnaise**: 2 gallons
**oil**: 1 quart
**vinegar**: 2 gallons
**water, fresh**: 8 gallons
**water, salt**: 12 gallons
**wine**: 1 gallon
