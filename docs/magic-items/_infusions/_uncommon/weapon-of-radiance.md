??? infusion "Weapon of Radiance | Weapon (Any)"

    #### Weapon of Radiance

    | Uncommon Weapon (Any) | Attunement (Spellcaster) |
    |---|---|
    | charges | 4 (1d4 at dawn) |

    This weapon emits **30** feet of Bright Light and Dim Light **30** feet further.

    ###### Toggle Light
    _Bonus Action_

    You can extinguish or reignite the light.

    ###### Blinding Flash
    _Reaction_

    _1 Charge_

    Before a creature attacks you, the attacker makes a Fortitude save _(DC = **15**)_.

    | | |
    |---|---|
    | **Fail** | The attacker is Blinded until the end of its next Turn. |
    | **Success** | No effect. |
