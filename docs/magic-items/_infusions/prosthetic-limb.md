    #### Prosthetic Limb

    | Common Wondrous Item | — |
    |---|---|

    While attached, you can use this limb as though it was your natural limb, and it can’t be removed against your will.
    Magic Action: You can attach this prosthetic to replace a lost limb or detach it.
