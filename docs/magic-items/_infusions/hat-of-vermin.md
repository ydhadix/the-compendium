### *Hat of Vermin*

| Common Clothing (Hat) | — |
|---|---|
| charges | 3 at dawn |

Magic Action, 1 Charge: While holding the hat, you can summon your choice of a Bat, a Frog, or a Rat in the hat.  The summoned creature is indifferent to you, isn’t under your control, and tries to get away from you as quickly as possible.  It otherwise behaves normally and disappears after 1 hour.
