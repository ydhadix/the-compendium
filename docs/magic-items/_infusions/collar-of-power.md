### *Collar of Power*

| Very Rare Clothing (Jewelry) | — |
|---|---|
| charges | 1 (1 at dawn) |

#### Bonding
Magic Action: You place this collar on a willing Companion within reach or remove it.  While this collar is attached, it can only be removed by the companion’s bonded creature and resizes to fit the companion comfortably.

#### Enlarge
Bonus Action, 1 charge: Your companion can gain the enlarge effect of Enlarge/Reduce, without concentration.

#### Enrage
1 charge:  After you drop to 0 hit points, your companion gains the enlarge effect of Enlarge/Reduce, without concentration, and gains 2d10 + your Character Level as Temporary Hit Points.  While enlarged, whenever your companion takes the Attack action on its turn, it can make an additional attack.
