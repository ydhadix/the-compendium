### *Bag of Holding*

| Uncommon Wondrous Item | — |
|---|---|

This bag’s interior is larger than its outside dimensions.  The bag can hold up to 500 pounds, not exceeding a volume of 64 cubic feet in a space roughly 2 feet square and 4 feet deep.  The bag holds enough air for 10 minutes of breathing, divided by the number of breathing creatures inside.
If the bag is overloaded, pierced, or torn, it is destroyed, and its contents scattered a random place in the Astral Plane.
If the bag is turned inside out, its contents spill forth unharmed.
Utilize Action: You can retrieve an item from the bag.
