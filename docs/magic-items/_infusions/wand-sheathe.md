    #### Wand Sheathe

    | Common Wondrous Item | attunement (construct) |
    |---|---|

    This item attaches to your arm and can’t be removed from you while you’re attuned to it.
    Magic Action:  You can insert a wand into the sheathe or remove a wand in the sheathe.  The sheathe can only hold one wand at a time.  While you are attuned to a wand in the sheathe, the wand and this item count as a single item for attunement.
    Bonus Action:  You can extend or retract a wand in the sheathe.  While extended, you can use the wand as if you were holding it, but your hand remains free.
