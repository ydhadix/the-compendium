# Bastions

A Bastion is a location or structure that belongs to you, such as a stronghold or sailing ship. Bastions offer refuge from the dangers of adventuring, and provide a location to conduct research, craft items, and more.

## Gaining a Bastion

Bastions are awarded and expanded on a per-character basis. You typically receive your Bastion around Character Level 5.

Multiple Bastions can be combined into shared structures, or they can be kept independent from each other.

You, the other members of your party, and the DM decide how your Bastions come into your possession. The shape, style, and function of your Bastion are yours to decide.

## Facilities

Every Bastion has facilities that serve basic needs, and specialized facilities for unique tasks.

### Facility Space

The size of a Facility determines its maximum area in 5-foot squares. The area of a facility can be spread across multiple levels, provided they are all connected.

| Size | minimum squares | maximum squares |
|---|---|---|
| cramped | 2 squares | 4 squares |
| roomy | 4 squares | 16 squares |
| vast | 16 squares | 36 squares |

## Basic Facilities

A basic Facility doesn't have any game effects, but makes a Bastion more comfortable to inhabit. Basic facilities can aesthetically resemble special facilities, but don't grant any of the mechanical benefits a special facility does.

**Example Basic Facilities**

- barracks
- bedroom
- courtyard
- dining room
- dormitory
- office
- pub
- storage

### Adding Basic Facilities

Each Bastion starts with two basic Facilities; one Cramped, and one Roomy. You can add new basic Facilities or enlarge existing ones by spending money and time, though you don't need to be present while Facilities are being constructed.

The amount of money and time it takes to enlarge a basic Facility is equal to the difference in cost between its current size and new size.

| Size | Cost |
|---|---|
| cramped | 500 gold |
| roomy | 1,000 gold |
| vast | 3,000 gold |

## Special Facilities

Special facilities are Bastion locations where certain activities can yield mechanical benefits.

### Adding Special Facilities

Each Bastion starts with two special Facilities of your choice. Unlike basic Facilities, special Facilities can't be bought. Additional special Facilities are acquired at certain character levels.

When you gain a level, you can change one of your special Facilities.

| Character Level | Special Facilities |
|---|---|
| 5 | 2 |
| 9 | 3 |
| 13 | 4 |
| 17 | 5 |

### Hirelings

Special Facilities comes with at least one hireling who works in the facility and maintains it. Hirelings are proficient in any tools or skills associated with that Facility, and can help with checks or crafting.

### Arcanum

| prerequisite | spellcasting with an arcane focus |
|---|---|
| size | cramped |
| hirelings | 1 |
| tools | alchemist's supplies |
| skills | arcana |

#### Glyph of Revelation

When a creature who meets this Facility's prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Charm of Identify. This charm functions as a Spell Scroll of Identify, and disappears after 10 days, after you use it, or when you gain another charm from this feature.

#### Upgrading the Facility

2,000 Gold – The Arcanum becomes a Laboratory. Its size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Arcane Charm can bestow a Charm of Magic Aura instead.

7,000 Gold – The Laboratory becomes a Thaumatorium. Its size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Arcane Charm can bestow a Charm of Dispel Magic instead.

### Infirmary

| prerequisite | — |
|---|---|
| size | cramped |
| hirelings | 1 |
| tools | — |
| skills | medicine |

#### Preventative Care

When a creature finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, it recovers from a single Poison affecting it, and its Hit Die maximum increases by 1 for 24 hours.

#### Upgrading the Facility

2,000 Gold – The Infirmary becomes a Clinic. Its size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Preventative Care also gives one additional Hit Die and maximizes all healing from Hit Dice for 24 hours.

7,000 Gold – The Clinic becomes a Hospital. Its size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Preventative Care also gives one additional Hit Die and grants Resistance to Death and Poison for 24 hours.

---

### Kitchen

| prerequisite | — |
|---|---|
| size | cramped |
| hirelings | 1 |
| tools | cook's utensils |

#### Hearty Meals

When a creature finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, its Exhaustion level is reduced by 1. (In addition to the Long Rest's normal Exhaustion reduction.)

#### Upgrading the Facility

2,000 Gold – The Kitchen becomes a Cafeteria. Its size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Hearty Meals also increases a creature's Hit Point Maximum by your Character Level for 24 hours. (The creature doesn't have to reduce Exhaustion to gain this benefit).

7,000 Gold – The Cafeteria becomes a Commissary. Its size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Hearty Meals also grants Resistance to Exhaustion and Frightened for 24 hours.

### Training Yard

| prerequisite | fighting style, unarmored form, or weapon mastery |
|---|---|
| size | cramped |
| hirelings | 1 |
| tools | — |
| skills | acrobatics, athletics, insight, intimidation, perception, performance |

#### Practiced Form

When a creature who meets this Facility's prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Boon of Training. This boon gives you Proficiency in one of this facility's skills, a martial weapon category, or a weapon mastery, and disappears after 10 days or when you gain another boon from this feature.

#### Upgrading the Facility

2,000 Gold – The Training Yard becomes an Arena. Its size becomes Roomy, and its Practiced Form can bestow a Boon of Fighting Styles. This boon gives you a Fighting Style feat.

7,000 Gold – The Arena becomes a Gymnasium. Its size becomes Vast, and its Practiced Form can bestow a Boon of Improved Critical. This boon expands your physical attacks' Critical Hit range by 1.
