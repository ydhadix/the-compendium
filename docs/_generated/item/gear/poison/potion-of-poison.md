##### Potion of Poison
Poison (Ingested), Consumable, 200 gold
{ .subtitle }

This poison looks, smells, and tastes like a beneficial Potion, though _Identify_ reveals its true nature.

After consuming this poison, the poison deals **4d6** Poison Damage to you and you make a Fortitude Save _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You are Sickened for **1** hour. |
| Success | No effect. |
