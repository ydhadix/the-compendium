##### Oil of Slipperiness
Potion, Consumable, 200 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion comes in a Vial.

###### Apply
10 minutes
{ .subtitle }

You can apply this oil to a creature within Reach.  The creature gains the effect of _Freedom of Movement_ for **8** hours.

One vial coats a Medium or smaller creature and its equipment; each Size above Medium needs another vial.

###### Pour
Utilize Action
{ .subtitle }

You can pour the oil across a **10**-foot Square.  The area gains the effect of _Grease_ for **8** hours.
