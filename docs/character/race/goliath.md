## Features

### Powerful Build

Your carry capacity is doubled.

### Large Form

_Bonus Action_

_1 use per Long Rest_

For **10** minutes or until you end this effect _(no action required)_, you are Large, you have Advantage on Strength checks, and your Speed increases by **10** feet.

### Giant Ancestry

Choose an ancestry. Each ancestry determines the type of your Resistance and gives you a different effect.

##### Cloud Goliath

You gain Resistance to Thunder Damage.

###### Cloud Jaunt

_Bonus Action_

_Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest_

You can teleport up to **30** feet.

##### Fire Goliath

You gain Resistance to Fire Damage.

###### Fire Smite

_Free Action_

_Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest_

Before you deal damage, you can add **1d10** Fire Damage.

##### Frost Goliath

You gain Resistance to Cold Damage.

###### Frost Smite

_Free Action_

_Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest_

Before you deal damage, you can add **1d6** Cold Damage. After you deal that damage, the target's Speed is reduced by **10** feet until the start of your next Turn.

##### Hill Goliath

You gain Resistance to Poison Damage.

###### Hill Quake

_Free Action_

_Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest_

After you deal damage, you can knock the target Prone.

##### Stone Goliath

You gain Resistance to Poison Damage.

###### Stone Endurance

_Reaction_

_Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest_

Before you take damage, you can reduce the damage by **[1d12 + CON]**.

##### Storm Goliath

You gain Resistance to Lightning Damage.

###### Storm Rebuke

_Reaction_

_Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest_

After you take damage from a creature within **60** feet of you, you can deal **1d8** Thunder Damage to the target.
