# Races

Your race outlines your physical characteristics and grants a set of Traits and Features. Choose one of the following to shape your character's heritage.


| Races | Description |
|---|---|
| [Aasimar](aasimar.md) | Celestial-touched mortals who channel a divine revelation to sprout wings, blaze with light, or instill fear. |
| [Dragonborn](dragonborn.md) | Dragon-blooded warriors who breathe elemental death and resist the damage of their ancestry. |
| [Dwarf](dwarf.md) | Hardy mountain folk who resist poison, stay on their feet, and sense buried metal and gems. |
| [Elf](elf.md) | Graceful, charm-resistant folk who rest in a waking trance and wield innate ancestral magic. |
| [Gnome](gnome.md) | Small, clever folk with will-resistant minds, a knack for hiding, and innate magic. |
| [Goliath](goliath.md) | Giant-blooded titans of immense strength who can grow larger and wield an elemental giant power. |
| [Halfling](halfling.md) | Small, nimble folk who shrug off fear, slip out of sight, and turn bad luck aside. |
| [Orc](orc.md) | Powerful, hard-to-kill warriors who cling to life and surge with adrenaline in a fight. |
| [Prime](prime.md) | Ordinary humans with no supernatural gifts, whose strength is sheer adaptability. |
| [Synth](synth.md) | Purpose-built constructed folk who don't eat, sleep, or breathe — warforged, reploids, or androids. |
| [Tiefling](tiefling.md) | Fiend-blooded folk with silver tongues, charm resistance, and inborn infernal magic. |
