## Features

### Naturally Stealth

You can Hide even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.

### Nimble

You can move through the space of any creature that is no more than one size larger than you, but you can't stop in the same space.

### Halfling Luck

Before you roll a **1** on a d20, you can reroll the die.
