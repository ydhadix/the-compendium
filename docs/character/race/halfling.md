# Halfling
**Halflings** are a small and nimble people blessed with uncanny luck, slipping through danger where larger folk would be obstructed.
{ .subtitle }

|---|---|

| Reach | **5** ft. |
| Hit Points | **8** |
| Protections | Fear |

## Halfling Features
### Naturally Stealth
You can Hide even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least **1** Size larger than you.

### Nimble
You can move through the space of any creature that is no more than **1** Size larger than you, but you can't stop in the same space.

### Halfling Luck
Before you roll a **1** on a d20, you can reroll the die.
