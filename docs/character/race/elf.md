## Elf Features
### Keen Senses
You gain Insight, Perception, or Survival Proficiency.

### Trance
You don't need to sleep, and you gain Sleep Immunity.

You can finish a Long Rest in **4** hours if you spend those hours in a trancelike meditation. You remain conscious.

### Elven Ancestry
Choose an ancestry.

Each ancestry has different Spells and effects.
You learn this ancestry's Spells at the corresponding Levels. You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

##### Free Casting
**Once each per Long Rest:** You can cast one of this ancestry's Spells without a Spell Slot.

##### Dark Elf
Your Darkvision increases by **60** feet.
###### Dark Elf Spells

##### High Elf
After a Long Rest, you can replace one of this ancestry's Spells with another same-Level Wizard Spell.
