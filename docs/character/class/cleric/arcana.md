# Arcana Domain
Clerics of the Arcana domain bridge divine and arcane magic, wielding wizardly Spells alongside the gifts of their deity.
{ .subtitle }

|---|---|

| 6 | [Spell Breaker](#6-spell-breaker) |
| 9 | [Arcane Mastery](#9-arcane-mastery) |

## Subclass Features
### 3: Arcana Spells
Your specialization grants you additional Spells. You learn these Spells at the corresponding Cleric Levels.

| Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3rd | _Detect Magic_, _Magic Aura_, _Magic Missile_, _Magic Weapon_ |
| 5th | _Counterspell_, _Secret Chest_ |
| 7th | _Arcane Eye_, _Banishment_ |
| 9th | _Planar Binding_, _Teleportation Circle_ |


### 3: Arcane Initiate
You gain Arcana Proficiency and learn **2** Wizard Cantrips as Cleric Cantrips.


### 3: Turn Arcane
Before you use Channel Divinity: Turn Undead, choose **1** Planar Creature Type. This use of Turn Undead affects Creatures of the chosen Creature Type.


### 6: Spell Breaker
**Free Action:** Before you heal an Ally by expending a Spell Slot or use of Channel Divinity, you can also end one Spell affecting that Ally. The ended Spell's Level can be at most the expended Spell Slot's Level, or less than **Class Ability** if you used Channel Divinity.


### 9: Arcane Mastery
You learn **3** Wizard Spells: one 3rd-Level, one 4th-Level, and one 5th-Level Spell.

##### Free Casting
**Once each per Long Rest:** You can cast one of this feature's Spells without a Spell Slot.
