##### Bioluminescence
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can emit Bright Light out to **10** feet and Dim Light **10** feet further.  Using this Action again ends this effect.

##### Camouflage
A creature has Disadvantage on Perception Checks to see you.

##### Hold Breath
You can hold your breath for up to **1** hour.

##### Keen Senses
You have Advantage on Perception Checks that rely on hearing or sight.

##### Powerful Build
You have Advantage on Athletics Checks, and your Carry Capacity doubles.

##### Sure-Footed
You have Prone Protection, and you gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.

##### Amphibious
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

You can breathe underwater, and you gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

##### Cold Tolerance
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

You ignore Difficult Terrain caused by ice and snow, and you gain Protection from Extreme Cold and Cold Damage.

##### Heat Tolerance
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

You ignore Difficult Terrain caused by sand, and you gain Protection from Extreme Heat and Fire Damage.

##### Jumper
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

You can use your Dexterity to determine your jumping distance, and you gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.

##### Mimicry
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

You can mimic simple sounds you have heard. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful Insight Check against your Ranger Spell Save DC.

##### Nocturnal
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

You gain **120** feet of Darkvision.

##### Roving
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks, and your Speed increases by **10** feet.

##### Skittish
Ranger Level 4
{ .subtitle }

Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You can Disengage or Hide.

##### Amorphous
Ranger Level 7
{ .subtitle }

You have Grapple Protection, and you can move through a space as narrow as **1** inch without expending extra movement _(Items you are wearing or carrying do not gain this benefit)_.

##### Echolocation
Ranger Level 7
{ .subtitle }

While you aren't Deafened, you have **30** feet of Blindsight.

##### Glider
Ranger Level 7
{ .subtitle }

You have Resistance to Falling Damage, and during your Turn, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed _(You fall if you end your Turn airborne without another way to stay aloft)_.

##### Web Walker
Ranger Level 7
{ .subtitle }

You ignore movement penalties caused by webs, and you gain a Spider Climb Speed equal to your Speed.
