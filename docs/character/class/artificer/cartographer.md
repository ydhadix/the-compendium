## Subclass Features
### 3: Cartographer Spells
Your specialization grants you additional Spells. You learn these Spells at the corresponding Artificer Levels.

| Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3rd | _Healing Word_, _Magic Missile_ |
| 5th | _Locate Object_, _Pass Without Trace_ |
| 9th | _Call Lightning_, _Locate Creature_ |

### 3: Tools of the Trade
#### Specialized Training
You gain Proficiency and Expertise with Calligrapher's Supplies, Cartographer's Tools, and Survival.
#### Fast Crafting
You craft Spell Scrolls in half the time.

### 3: Surveyor's Assistant
#### Surveyor Upgrade
Your assistant's Hover Speed is equal to your Speed _(minimum **30** feet)_, and your assistant gains **240** feet of Darkvision and Proficiency in Nature, Perception, and Survival.
#### Improved Communication
The range of your Telepathy and sense sharing with your assistant extends to **5** miles while on the same plane. Additionally, you are not Blinded or Deafened while perceiving through your assistant's senses.
#### Improved Spell Delivery
Your assistant can deliver any Spell, not just Reach Spells _(A Spell that targets Self targets the assistant instead of you)_.

### 3: Adventurer's Atlas
After a Long Rest, you can conjure an Adventurer's Atlas. The atlas disappears if you conjure another Adventurer's Atlas.

The atlas contains your name and up to **Class Ability** additional names _(minimum **1**)_. A creature can only write its own name in the atlas, and you can erase any other name in the atlas by touching it. (You can remove your name from the atlas, though that doesn't allow you to add another creature in your place.)
#### Global Positioning
Creatures in the atlas always know the general location and direction to other creatures in the atlas, and don't need to see to target each other with magical effects.
#### Terrain Advantage
The Initiative Checks of creatures in the atlas increase by **1d4**.
##### Secret Shortcuts
**Free Action; 15 feet of movement:** You can teleport up to **10** feet.

### 6: Ingenious Movement
Your Speed increases by **10** feet.

Before or after you expend a use of [Improvise](index.md#2-improvise), one creature in your Adventurer's Atlas can teleport up to **30** feet.

### 9: Superior Surveyor
#### Perfect Pathfinding
You learn _Find the Path_.
##### Free Casting
**Once per Long Rest:** You can cast _Find the Path_ without a Spell Slot or Components.
##### Safe Haven
**Free Action:** Before a creature in your Adventurer's Atlas would drop to **0** Hit Points or be killed, you can remove its name from the atlas to set its Hit Points to **LV × 2** instead, then teleport it to within **5** feet of another creature in the atlas.

A creature whose name you remove this way can't write its name in the atlas again until you finish a Long Rest.
