## Subclass Features
### 3: Armorer Spells
Your specialization grants you additional Spells. You learn these Spells at the corresponding Artificer Levels.

| Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3rd | _Armor of Agathys_, _Magic Missile_ |
| 5th | _Kinetic Jaunt_, _Pass Without Trace_ |
| 9th | _Haste_, _Lightning Bolt_ |

### 3: Tools of the Trade
#### Specialized Training
You gain Proficiency with Heavy Armor.

You gain Proficiency and Expertise with Smith's Tools.

#### Fast Crafting
You craft Armor in half the time.


### 3: Arcane Armor
After a Short Rest, you can turn a suit of Armor within your Reach into Arcane Armor. You can only have **1** Arcane Armor at a time.

While your Arcane Armor is donned, it can't be removed against your will, and you ignore its Strength requirements.

Your Arcane Armor counts as **4** separate items for the purpose of Magic Items and Infusions: a chest piece, boots, helm, and weapon.

##### Quick Equip
**Utilize Action:** You can don or doff your Arcane Armor.


### 3: Armor Model
Choose **1** of the [Armor Models](#armor-models) for your Arcane Armor.

Each Armor Model has a special Weapon.  Attacks with that Weapon can use your Class Ability.

#### Changing Armor Model
After a Short Rest, you can change your Armor Model.

##### Quick Change
**Bonus Action; 1 [Improvise](index.md#2-improvise) use:** You can change your Armor Model.


### 6: Armor Modifications
You can have **2** additional [Infused Items](index.md#1-infuse-items). These Infused Items are part of your Arcane Armor.


### 9: Perfected Armor
Your Arcane Armor Weapons deal **1** additional Damage Die, and your [Armor Model](#armor-models) gains its Perfected effects.

## Armor Models

### Dreadnaught

| Weapon | Damage | Weight | Hands | Properties | Techniques |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wrecking Ball | 1d10 Force | Heavy | One | Reach | Graze, Slow, Topple |

After you damage a creature with your Wrecking Ball, you propel the target up to **10** feet away from you.

##### Giant Stature
**Bonus Action; Class Ability times per Long Rest:** You can become Large for **1** minute, increasing your Reach by **5** feet.

#### Perfected Dreadnaught
Prerequisite: Level 9 Artificer
{ .subtitle }

Giant Stature gains the following effects:

- You can become Huge instead of Large.
- Your Reach increases by an additional **5** feet.
- You gain Advantage on Strength Checks and Fortitude Saves.


### Guardian

| Weapon | Damage | Weight | Hands | Properties | Techniques |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thunder Gauntlets | 1d8 Thunder | Light | One | — | Nick, Slow, Vex |

After you damage a creature with your Thunder Gauntlets, you give the target Disadvantage on Attack Rolls that don't target you until the start of your next Turn.

##### Defensive Field
**Bonus Action:** While Bloodied, you can gain **Character Level** Temporary Hit Points.

#### Perfected Guardian
Prerequisite: Level 9 Artificer
{ .subtitle }

##### Offensive Field
**Reaction; Class Ability times per Long Rest:** After a creature no more than **2** Sizes larger than you ends its Turn within **30** feet, you can force it to make a Fortitude Save against your Artificer Spell Save DC.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You propel the creature up to **30** feet straight toward yourself. Then, if the creature is within your Reach, you can Attack it with your Thunder Gauntlets. |
| Success | No effect. |


### Infiltrator

| Weapon | Damage | Weight | Hands | Properties | Techniques |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lightning Launcher | 1d6 Lightning | — | One | Ammunition (100/400) | Sap, Slow, Vex |

Your Speed increases by **5** feet, and you gain Advantage on Stealth Checks.

##### Lightning Charge
**Free Action; Once per Turn:** Before you deal Lightning Damage with your Lightning Launcher, you can add **1d6** damage.

#### Perfected Infiltrator
Prerequisite: Level 9 Artificer
{ .subtitle }

After you damage a creature with your Lightning Launcher, you make it emit Bright Light out to **5** feet and Dim Light **5** feet further, and give it Disadvantage on Attack Rolls against you until the start of your next Turn.

##### Aerial Dash
**Bonus Action; Class Ability times per Long Rest:** You can gain a Fly Speed equal to twice your Speed until the end of your Turn.
