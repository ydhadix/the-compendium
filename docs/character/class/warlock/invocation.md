##### Armor of Shadows
You learn _Mage Armor_ and can cast it on yourself without a Spell Slot.


##### Eldritch Mind
You have Concentration Protection.


##### Lessons of the First Ones
Choose an Origin Feat. You gain that feat.
###### Repeatable
You can learn this Invocation more than once, but you must choose a different Origin Feat each time.


##### Pact of the Blade
You can summon or bond a Pact Weapon. The bond ends if you bond another weapon, your Pact Weapon leaves your Reach for **1** minute or more, or you die. While bonded, your Pact Weapon gains these benefits:

- You can use it as an Arcane Focus.
- You are proficient with it.
- Your Attacks with it can use your Warlock Ability.
- You can change its damage to Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant Damage.

###### Summon Pact Weapon
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You summon a Simple or Martial Melee weapon to your hand and bond it as your Pact Weapon. It disappears when the bond ends.
###### Bond Magic Weapon
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You bond a Magic Weapon you're carrying as your Pact Weapon. You can't bond a weapon attuned or bonded to another creature.


##### Pact of the Chain
You learn _Find Familiar_ and can cast it as a Magic Action without a Spell Slot. When you cast it, you can choose one of the following special forms for your familiar: Imp, Quasit, Slaad Tadpole, or Sprite.

When you take the Attack Action, you can forgo one of your Attacks to command your familiar to use its Reaction to make one Attack.


##### Pact of the Tome
As part of a Short Rest, you summon a Book of Shadows to your hand. The book disappears if you summon another or you die. You can use the book as an Arcane Focus.

When you summon the book, choose **3** Cantrips and **2** 1st-level Ritual Spells. While the book is on your person, you know the chosen Spells as Warlock Spells.

##### Agonizing Blast
Prerequisite: Warlock 2, a Warlock Cantrip that deals damage
{ .subtitle }

Choose a Warlock Cantrip that deals damage. You add your Warlock Ability to that Spell's Damage Rolls.
###### Repeatable
You can learn this Invocation more than once, but you must choose a different Cantrip each time.


##### Devil's Sight
Prerequisite: Warlock 2
{ .subtitle }

You gain **120** feet of Devil's Sight.


##### Eldritch Smite
Prerequisite: Warlock 2
{ .subtitle }

Free Action, 1 Spell Slot
{ .subtitle }

Before you damage a creature with your Pact Weapon, you can deal an extra **1d8** Force Damage, plus **1d8** for each Level of the Spell Slot expended.  After you deal that damage, if the target is Huge or smaller, you can knock it Prone.


##### Eldritch Spear
Prerequisite: Warlock 2, a Warlock Cantrip with a Range
{ .subtitle }

Choose a Warlock Cantrip that has a Range. That Spell's Range increases by **30** feet for each Warlock Level you have. If the Spell has a Range of Reach, it instead reaches that much farther beyond your Reach.
###### Repeatable
You can learn this Invocation more than once, but you must choose a different Cantrip each time.


##### Fiendish Vigor
Prerequisite: Warlock 2
{ .subtitle }

You learn _False Life_ and can cast it on yourself without a Spell Slot. When you cast it this way, you always gain the maximum Temporary Hit Points.


##### Mask of Many Faces
Prerequisite: Warlock 2
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Disguise Self_ and can cast it without a Spell Slot.


##### Misty Visions
Prerequisite: Warlock 2
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Silent Image_ and can cast it without a Spell Slot.


##### Otherworldly Leap
Prerequisite: Warlock 2
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Jump_ and can cast it on yourself without a Spell Slot.


##### Repelling Blast
Prerequisite: Warlock 2, a Warlock Cantrip with an Attack Roll
{ .subtitle }

Choose a Warlock Cantrip that requires an Attack Roll. When you hit a creature with that Spell, if the target is Large or smaller, you can push it up to **10** feet straight away from you.
###### Repeatable
You can learn this Invocation more than once, but you must choose a different Cantrip each time.

##### Ascendant Step
Prerequisite: Warlock 5
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Levitate_ and can cast it on yourself without a Spell Slot.


##### Gaze of Two Minds
Prerequisite: Warlock 5
{ .subtitle }

Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

Choose a creature within Reach. Until the end of your next Turn, you can perceive through that creature's senses in addition to your own.

While perceiving this way, you can use this Bonus Action again to extend the effect until the end of your next Turn, regardless of the distance between you. You can also cast Spells as though you were in the creature's space, provided it is within **60** feet of you.


##### Gift of the Depths
Prerequisite: Warlock 5
{ .subtitle }

You can breathe underwater and gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed. You also learn _Water Breathing_.
###### Free Casting
1 use per Long Rest
{ .subtitle }

You cast _Water Breathing_ without a Spell Slot.


##### Investment of the Chain Master
Prerequisite: Warlock 5, Pact of the Chain
{ .subtitle }

When you cast _Find Familiar_, your familiar gains the following benefits:

- Your familiar gains a **40**-foot Fly or Swim Speed (your choice).
- Whenever your familiar deals Physical Damage, you can change it to Necrotic or Radiant Damage.
- When your familiar forces a Saving Throw, it uses your Spell Save DC.
###### Resilient Familiar
Reaction
{ .subtitle }

Before your familiar takes damage, you can give it Resistance to that damage.


##### Master of Myriad Forms
Prerequisite: Warlock 5
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Alter Self_ and can cast it without a Spell Slot.


##### One with Shadows
Prerequisite: Warlock 5
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Invisibility_. While in Dim Light or Darkness, you can cast it without a Spell Slot.


##### Thirsting Blade
Prerequisite: Warlock 5, Pact of the Blade
{ .subtitle }

When you take the Attack Action and Attack with your Pact Weapon, you can make one additional Attack as part of that Action.

##### Gift of the Protectors
Prerequisite: Warlock 7, Pact of the Tome
{ .subtitle }

When you summon your Book of Shadows, a new page appears. It can hold a number of names equal to your Warlock Ability (minimum **1**), and a creature can write only its own name on it. You can erase a name by touching it.
###### Guardian's Ward
1 use per Long Rest
{ .subtitle }

Before a creature whose name is on the page would drop to **0** Hit Points or be killed, you can instead make it drop to **1** Hit Point.


##### Life Drinker
Prerequisite: Warlock 7, Pact of the Blade
{ .subtitle }

1 use per Turn
{ .subtitle }

Before you damage a creature with your Pact Weapon, you can expend and roll **1** Hit Die and add your Warlock Ability. You deal that much extra Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant Damage (your choice) to the target and regain that many Hit Points.

If you don't expend a Hit Die, you instead deal **1d6** Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant Damage.


##### Whispers of the Grave
Prerequisite: Warlock 7
{ .subtitle }

You learn _Speak with Dead_ and can cast it without a Spell Slot.
