##### Pact of the Tome
After a Short Rest, you can conjure a Book of Shadows to your hand. The book disappears if you conjure another or you die. You can use the book as an Arcane Focus.

After you conjure the book, choose **3** Cantrips and **2** 1st-Level Ritual Spells. While the book is on your person, you know the chosen Spells as Warlock Spells.
