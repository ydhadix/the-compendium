##### Eldritch Smite
Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock
{ .subtitle }

**Free Action; 1 Spell Slot:** Before you damage a creature with your Pact Weapon, you can add **1d8** Force Damage, plus **1d8** for each Level of the Spell Slot expended.  After you deal that damage, if the target is no more than **2** Sizes larger than you, you can knock it Prone.
