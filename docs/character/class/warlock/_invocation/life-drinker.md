##### Life Drinker
Prerequisite: Level 7 Warlock, Pact of the Blade
{ .subtitle }

**Free Action; Once per Turn:** Before you deal damage with your Pact Weapon, you can add **1d6** Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant Damage.  You can instead expend **1** Hit Die to add **HD + Warlock Ability** damage of that type and heal by the damage dealt.
