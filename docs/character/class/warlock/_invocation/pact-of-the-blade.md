##### Pact of the Blade
You can conjure or bond a Pact Weapon. The bond ends if you bond another Weapon, your Pact Weapon leaves your Reach for **1** minute or more, or you die. While bonded, your Pact Weapon gains the following effects:

- You can use it as an Arcane Focus.
- You gain Proficiency with it.
- Your Attacks with it can use your Warlock Ability.
- It can deal Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant Damage instead of its normal Damage Type.

###### Conjure Pact Weapon
**Bonus Action:** You can conjure a Simple or Martial Melee Weapon to your hand and bond it as your Pact Weapon; it disappears after the bond ends.
###### Bond Magic Weapon
**Bonus Action:** You can bond a Magic Weapon you're carrying as your Pact Weapon. You can't bond a Weapon attuned or bonded to another creature.
