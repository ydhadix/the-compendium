### Pact of the Tome
As part of a Short Rest, you summon a Book of Shadows to your hand. The book disappears if you summon another or you die. You can use the book as an Arcane Focus.

When you summon the book, choose **3** Cantrips and **2** 1st-level Ritual Spells. While the book is on your person, you know the chosen Spells as Warlock Spells.
