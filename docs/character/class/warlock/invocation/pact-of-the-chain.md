### Pact of the Chain
You learn _Find Familiar_ and can cast it as a Magic Action without a Spell Slot. When you cast it, you can choose one of the following special forms for your familiar: Imp, Quasit, Slaad Tadpole, or Sprite.

When you take the Attack Action, you can forgo one of your Attacks to command your familiar to use its Reaction to make one Attack.
