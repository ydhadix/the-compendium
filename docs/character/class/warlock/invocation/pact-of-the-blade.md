### Pact of the Blade
You can summon or bond a Pact Weapon. The bond ends if you bond another weapon, your Pact Weapon leaves your Reach for **1** minute or more, or you die. While bonded, your Pact Weapon gains these benefits:

- You can use it as an Arcane Focus.
- You are proficient with it.
- Your Attacks with it can use your Warlock Ability.
- You can change its damage to Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant Damage.

###### Summon Pact Weapon
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You summon a Simple or Martial Melee weapon to your hand and bond it as your Pact Weapon. It disappears when the bond ends.
###### Bond Magic Weapon
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You bond a Magic Weapon you're carrying as your Pact Weapon. You can't bond a weapon attuned or bonded to another creature.
