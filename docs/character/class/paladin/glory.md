# Oath of Glory Paladin
Glory Paladins believe they and their companions are destined to achieve glory through great deeds of heroism.  They train diligently and encourage their companions, so they're all ready when destiny calls.

## Tenets
The Oath of Glory teaches the following tenets:

- Endeavor to be known by your deeds.
- Face hardships with courage.
- Inspire others to strive for glory.


## Subclass Features
### 3: Glory Spells
You learn the following Spells at the corresponding Paladin Levels.

| Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3rd | _Guiding Bolt_, _Heroism_ |
| 5th | _Enhance Ability_, _Magic Weapon_ |
| 9th | _Haste_, _Protection from Energy_ |

### 3: Channel Conviction: Peerless Athlete
**Bonus Action; 1 Channel Conviction use:** You gain the following effects for **10** minutes:
- You gain Advantage on Athletics and Acrobatics Checks.
- Your maximum Long Jump and High Jump distances increase by **10** feet.

### 3: Channel Conviction: Inspiring Smite
**Bonus Action; 1 Channel Conviction use:** After you cast _Divine Smite_, you can give **2d8 + LV** Temporary Hit Points, divided among any creatures within **30** feet.

Add **1d8** to the Temporary Hit Points at Character Level **5**, **9**, **13**, and **17**.

### 6: Aura of Alacrity
Your Speed increases by **10** feet.

After an Ally enters your Divine Aura for the first time on a turn _(including when they start their turn there)_, you increase their Speed by **10** feet until the end of the Turn.

### 9: Glorious Defense
**Reaction; Class Ability times per Long Rest:** Before you or an Ally within your Divine Aura is hit with an Attack, you can add **Class Ability** to the target's Armor Class.  Then, if the Attack misses, you can attack the attacker.

### 11: Avatar of Legends
When you use your [Avatar of Conviction](index.md#11-avatar-of-conviction), you gain the following effects:

- You gain Advantage on Charisma Checks.
- **Once per Turn:** Before you fail a Saving Throw, you can succeed instead.
- **Once per Turn:** Before you miss an Attack Roll, you can hit instead.
