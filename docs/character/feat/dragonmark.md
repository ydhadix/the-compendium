# Dragonmarks
**Dragonmarks** are magical sigils passed down through bloodlines.
{ .subtitle }

## Gaining Dragonmarks
Possessing a Dragonmark requires a connection to the Mark's Sovereign — either by bloodline, or by acquaintance.  As a result, taking a Dragonmark requires that the setting and story allow it.  Your DM determines whether Dragonmarks are available, and can help weave the Mark's Sovereign into your character's backstory.

#### Types of Dragonmarks
The most powerful Dragonmarks are passed down through a creature's family.  Each Dragonmark has four forms:

| Dragonmark | Description |
|---|---|
| Noble Mark | A naturally manifested Dragonmark.  Noble Marks can develop into Greater Marks of their own accord. |
| Lesser Mark | A Dragonmark granted by a house leader.  Possesses the same might as a Noble Mark, but cannot develop further without the Sovereign's intervention. |
| Greater Mark | The height of a Dragonmark's power, each manifesting unique abilities that set their wielders apart from typical magic users. |
| Sovereign Mark | The singular leader of a Dragonmarked family.  Sovereign Marks are able to grant Lesser and Greater Marks to other creatures. |

#### Greater Dragonmarks
A Noble or Lesser Dragonmark's power can increase to a Greater Mark during a campaign.  A Noble Mark usually develops when its wielder accomplishes a great feat, while a Lesser Mark requires the acknowledgement of the Mark's Sovereign.  In either case, a Greater Mark usually isn't available earlier than Level **5**.

#### Aberrant Dragonmarks
Dragonmarks almost always manifest their magic predictably and constructively.  **Aberrant Marks**, however, manifest spontaneously often have unpredictable and dangerous powers.

You gain **1** of your Dragonmark's Proficiencies.

You learn your Dragonmark's Spells at the corresponding Levels.  You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

#### Changing Spells
Whenever you level up, you can replace one of your Dragonmark's Spells with a Spell from your Dragonmark's Class of the same Level, provided that Spell doesn't deal damage.

##### Free Casting
**Once each per Long Rest:** You can cast one of your Dragonmark's Spells without a Spell Slot.

### Aberrant Dragonmark
Prerequisite: No other Dragonmark
{ .subtitle }

Your Fortitude Saves increase by **1d4**.

You learn **1** Sorcerer Cantrip and **1** 1st-Level Sorcerer Spell.  You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

#### Changing Spells
Whenever you level up, you can replace one of this Dragonmark's Spells with a same-Level Sorcerer Spell.

##### Free Casting
**Once per Short Rest:** You can cast this Dragonmark's Spell without a Spell Slot.

## Greater Marks
A **Greater Mark** builds on its base Dragonmark, deepening its magic and manifesting unique abilities.  Every Greater Mark grants Greater Spellcasting; the abilities that follow it are specific to each Mark.

### Greater Spellcasting
You gain an additional Spell Slot.  The level of this Spell Slot increases at the corresponding Levels.

After you finish a Long Rest, you regain this Spell Slot.

| LV | Slot Level |
|---|---|
| 1st | 1st |
| 3rd | 2nd |
| 5th | 3rd |
| 7th | 4th |
| 9th | 5th |

### Greater Mark of Handling

Your Spells and features can treat Monstrosities as Beasts.

##### Superior Handling
Your mount can take this Reaction.

**Reaction:** After you are hit with an Attack, your mount Moves or Attacks.

##### Subdue
**Magic Action; PB times per Long Rest:** You can force one Beast within **30** feet to make a Will Save _(DC **8 + STR, WIS, or CHA + PB**)_.  The target gains Disadvantage on the Saving Throw if you or an Ally dealt damage to it since the start of your previous Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You frighten the target for **1** minute. |
| Success | No effect. |

Before the target's Turn ends, it repeats this Saving Throw.  On a success, this effect ends on the target.

### Greater Mark of Passage

You gain a Climb Speed and a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.  You ignore Difficult Terrain.

##### Slip Out
**Free Action; 5 feet of movement:** You can escape from a nonmagical restraint or a Grapple.

#### Rising Tide
After an Ally enters your Reach for the first time on a Turn or starts its Turn there, you can give the target this feature's other effects until the end of the target's next Turn.

### Greater Mark of Scribing

The time it takes for you to craft Spell Scrolls is halved.

##### Quick Study
**Bonus Action:** You can Study.

##### Well-Read
**Reaction; PB times per Long Rest:** Before you fail an Ability Check you don't have Expertise in, you can add **PB** to the total.

### Greater Mark of Storms

You gain Lightning and Thunder Resistance and Falling Immunity.

##### Feather Soul
**Free Action:** You can halve your weight until you use this Action again.

##### Tempest Magic
**Free Action:** Before or after you cast a Spell, you can propel yourself up to **10** feet.
