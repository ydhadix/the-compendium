### Magic Initiate

Choose Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard.

You learn **2** Cantrips and **1** 1st-Level Spell from the chosen class's Spell List. You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

#### Changing Spells
Whenever you level up, you can replace one of this feat's Spells with another same-Level Spell from the chosen class's Spell List.

##### Free Casting
**Once per Long Rest:** You can cast this feat's 1st-Level Spell without a Spell Slot.

#### Repeatable
You can take this feat more than once, provided you choose a different class each time.
