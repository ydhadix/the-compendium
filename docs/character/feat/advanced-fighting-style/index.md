# Advanced Fighting Styles

Advanced Fighting Styles are higher-tier combat techniques, each building on a basic Fighting Style you must already have.


| Fighting Styles | Description |
|---|---|
| [Balanced](balanced.md) | Wield versatile weapons two-handed while holding a shield, and bash with either. |
| [Bow Expert](bow-expert.md) | Ignore long-range penalties and loose a second shot at a nearby target. |
| [Crossbow Expert](crossbow-expert.md) | Fire crossbows without loading and punch through physical resistance. |
| [Distance Shooting](distance-shooting.md) | Ignore long-range penalties and shoot past cover with ranged weapons. |
| [Dual Wielding](dual-wielding.md) | Add to Armor Class with two weapons and grant them the Light and Nick properties. |
| [Fencing](fencing.md) | Boost Armor Class with a finesse weapon and riposte against missed attacks. |
| [Firearm Expert](firearm-expert.md) | Fire firearms without loading, reroll low damage, and shoot at initiative. |
| [Grappling](grappling.md) | Advantage against grappled foes and the ability to grapple after a strike. |
| [Great Weapon Expert](great-weapon-expert.md) | Add your Proficiency Bonus to heavy weapon damage and strike again on a kill or crit. |
| [Marking](marking.md) | Mark struck foes to make free, advantaged opportunity attacks against them. |
| [Melee Shooting](melee-shooting.md) | Fire ranged weapons in melee without penalty and for extra damage up close. |
| [Mounted Expert](mounted-expert.md) | Attack with Advantage from your mount and take hits meant for it. |
| [Polearm Expert](polearm-expert.md) | Make an extra polearm attack and strike foes as they approach. |
| [Sentinel](sentinel.md) | Lock foes in place with opportunity attacks and punish those who ignore you. |
| [Shield Throwing](shield-throwing.md) | Hurl a shield as a thrown weapon and gird yourself in a flash. |
| [Shield Warrior](shield-warrior.md) | Wield a heavy shield as a weapon and add to your Armor Class. |
| [Sling Expert](sling-expert.md) | Double a sling's range and widen its critical hit range. |
| [Variable](variable.md) | Boost attack rolls with a lone versatile weapon and utilize items freely. |
| [War Casting](war-casting.md) | Steady your concentration and cast spells in place of opportunity attacks. |
