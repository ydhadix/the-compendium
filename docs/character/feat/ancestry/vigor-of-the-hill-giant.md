### Vigor of the Hill Giant
Prerequisite: Goliath (Hill Ancestry)
{ .subtitle }

After you spend Hit Dice to heal during a Short Rest, you heal by **CON + PB**, provided you eat at least one-half pound of food.

#### Bulwark
Reaction
{ .subtitle }

Before you are propelled or knocked Prone, you can become Anchored until the end of the turn or you end this effect _(no action required)_.
