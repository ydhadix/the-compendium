### Chef

You gain Cook's Utensils Proficiency. If you already have Cook's Utensils Proficiency, you instead gain Expertise.

#### Hearty Meal

After you finish a Short Rest, you can choose up to **4 + PB** creatures that used one or more Hit Dice to heal during this rest. You heal each target by **1d8**.

#### Healthy Treats

After you finish a Short Rest, you can cook **PB** treats. Uneaten treats spoil after **8** hours.
##### Eat Treat
Any creature holding a treat can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature eats it to gain **PB** Temporary Hit Points.
