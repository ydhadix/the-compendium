### Scion of Scorn
Prerequisite: Evil alignment, no Scion Feat
{ .subtitle }

You gain Necrotic Resistance.

You learn _Chill Touch_. You can cast it using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

##### Grasp of Avarice
**Free Action; PB times per Long Rest:** Before you deal damage to a creature within **60** feet, you can add **1d6 + PB** Necrotic Damage and heal by the Necrotic Damage dealt.
