### Scion of Crossroads
Prerequisite: Neutral alignment, no Scion Feat
{ .subtitle }

You gain Psychic Resistance.

You learn _Mage Hand_, _Misty Step_, and _Tongues_. You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

##### Free Casting
**Once each per Long Rest:** You can cast one of this feat's Spells without a Spell Slot.
