### Telekinetic

You learn _Mage Hand_. You can cast it without Components using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability. The Spell's Range increases by **30** feet, and you can make the spectral hand invisible.

##### Telekinetic Shove
**Bonus Action:** You can force a creature within **30** feet to make a Fortitude Save _(DC **8 + INT, WIS, or CHA + PB**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You move the target **5** feet toward or away from you. |
| Success | No effect. |
