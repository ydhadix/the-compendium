### Ritual Caster
Prerequisite: **2** Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma
{ .subtitle }

You learn **PB** 1st-Level Ritual Spells. You can cast them using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability _(Whenever your Proficiency Bonus increases, you learn an additional 1st-Level Ritual Spell)_.

##### Quick Ritual
**Once per Long Rest:** You can cast a Ritual Spell you know without a Spell Slot using its regular Casting Time.
