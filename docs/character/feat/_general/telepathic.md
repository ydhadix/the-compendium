### Telepathic

You gain **60** feet of Telepathy. Your telepathic utterances are in a language you know, and the target understands you only if it knows that language.

You learn _Detect Thoughts_. You can cast it using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

##### Free Casting
**Once per Long Rest:** You can cast _Detect Thoughts_ without a Spell Slot or Components.
