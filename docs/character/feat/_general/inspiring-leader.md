### Inspiring Leader
Prerequisite: **2** Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma
{ .subtitle }

You gain Performance, Persuasion, or Intimidation Proficiency.  If you already have the chosen Proficiency, you instead gain Expertise.

#### Inspiring Speech

After you finish a Short Rest, you can choose up to **6** creatures within **30** feet. Each target gains **LV + INT, WIS, or CHA** Temporary Hit Points.
