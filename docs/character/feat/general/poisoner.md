### Poisoner
#### Potent Poison
Poison Damage you deal ignores Resistance.

#### Poisoner's Kit
You have Poisoner's Kit Proficiency. If you already have Poisoner's Kit Proficiency, you instead have Expertise.

#### Brew Poison
After a Short Rest, you can expend **50** gold in materials to craft **PB** doses of Personal Poison.

After a weapon coated in Personal Poison deals Piercing or Slashing Damage, the target makes a Fortitude Save _(DC = **8 + INT or WIS + PB**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **2d8** Poison Damage. The target is Sickened until the end of your next Turn. |
| Success | No effect. |

#### Apply Poison
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You coat **1** weapon or up to **3** pieces of Ammunition. Once applied, the poison retains potency for **1** minute or until its damage is dealt, whichever comes first.
