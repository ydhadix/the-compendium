### Elemental Adept
Choose an Elemental Damage Type.

#### Energy Mastery
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

Before you roll a **1** on a Damage Die of the chosen Damage Type, you can treat the die as a **2**.

#### Energy Resistance
You have Resistance to the chosen Damage Type.

#### Irresistible Energy
Damage you deal of the chosen Damage Type ignores Resistance.

#### Repeatable
You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different Damage Type each time.
