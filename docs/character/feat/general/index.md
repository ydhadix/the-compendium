# General Feats

General Feats are a broad selection of talents that you can take as your character grows.


| General Feats | Description |
|---|---|
| [Actor](actor.md) | Impersonate others convincingly and mimic voices and creature sounds. |
| [Athlete](athlete.md) | Climb with ease, spring up from prone, and leap without a running start. |
| [Chef](chef.md) | Proficiency with cook's utensils and meals that heal and bolster your companions. |
| [Crusher](crusher.md) | Shove foes with bludgeoning blows and open them up on a crushing critical hit. |
| [Durable](durable.md) | Shrug off death and spend a Hit Die to heal yourself as a Bonus Action. |
| [Elemental Adept](elemental-adept.md) | Master one damage type — resist it, shore up its dice, and ignore resistance to it. |
| [Fey Touched](fey-touched.md) | Learn Misty Step and a divination or enchantment spell, with a free casting each rest. |
| [Heavily Armored](heavily-armored.md) | Gain proficiency with Heavy Armor. |
| [Heavy Armor Master](heavy-armor-master.md) | Add your Proficiency Bonus to your armor's Damage Reduction in Heavy Armor. |
| [Inspiring Leader](inspiring-leader.md) | Rally your allies after a rest, granting them Temporary Hit Points. |
| [Keen Mind](keen-mind.md) | Gain a knowledge proficiency and Study as a Bonus Action. |
| [Lightly Armored](lightly-armored.md) | Gain proficiency with Light Armor. |
| [Mage Slayer](mage-slayer.md) | Disrupt enemy concentration and turn a failed Will Save into a success. |
| [Martial Weapon Training](martial-weapon-training.md) | Gain proficiency with Martial Weapons. |
| [Medium Armor Master](medium-armor-master.md) | Add more of your abilities to Medium Armor and ignore its Stealth penalty. |
| [Mobile](mobile.md) | Move faster, dash through difficult terrain, and dodge opportunity attacks. |
| [Moderately Armored](moderately-armored.md) | Gain proficiency with Medium Armor. |
| [Observant](observant.md) | Gain a perceptive proficiency and Search as a Bonus Action. |
| [Piercer](piercer.md) | Reroll a piercing damage die and pile on damage with piercing critical hits. |
| [Planar Wanderer](planar-wanderer.md) | Resist an element, sense and manipulate portals, and detect planar rifts. |
| [Poisoner](poisoner.md) | Craft potent poisons, coat weapons swiftly, and ignore poison resistance. |
| [Resilient](resilient.md) | Gain a Saving Throw aptitude and turn one failed save into a success each day. |
| [Resourceful](resourceful.md) | Utilize items as a Bonus Action, carry more gear, and never run out of ammunition. |
| [Ritual Caster](ritual-caster.md) | Learn and cast a growing repertoire of ritual spells. |
| [Scion of Chaos](scion-of-chaos.md) | Chaotic characters resist poison and unleash random magical flares. |
| [Scion of Crossroads](scion-of-crossroads.md) | Neutral characters resist psychic harm and learn Misty Step and Tongues. |
| [Scion of Order](scion-of-order.md) | Lawful characters resist force and can restrain foes with a stasis strike. |
| [Scion of Righteousness](scion-of-righteousness.md) | Good characters resist radiance and can soothe an ally's wounds. |
| [Scion of Scorn](scion-of-scorn.md) | Evil characters resist necrotic harm and drain life from their foes. |
| [Shadow Touched](shadow-touched.md) | Learn Invisibility and an illusion or necromancy spell, with a free casting each rest. |
| [Skill Expert](skill-expert.md) | Gain a Skill Proficiency and Expertise in a skill you know. |
| [Skulker](skulker.md) | Gain Blindsight, hide reliably in combat, and stay hidden after a missed shot. |
| [Slasher](slasher.md) | Slow foes with slashing hits and blunt their attacks with slashing critical hits. |
| [Spell Sniper](spell-sniper.md) | Ignore cover, cast in melee freely, and extend the range of your spell attacks. |
| [Spellblade](spellblade.md) | Trade one of your attacks to cast a cantrip in the thick of melee. |
| [Telekinetic](telekinetic.md) | Wield an enhanced Mage Hand and shove creatures with a thought. |
| [Telepathic](telepathic.md) | Speak mind to mind and read the thoughts of others. |
| [War Magus](war-magus.md) | Protect your concentration and turn a failed Reflex Save into a success. |
