# Dragonmarks

Dragonmarks are inherited magical sigils, each tied to a bloodline and its trade. A mark grants a Skill Proficiency and a set of themed spells, and you can bear only one.


| Dragonmarks | Description |
|---|---|
| [Mark of Death](mark-of-death.md) | Necromantic magic of preservation and raising the dead. |
| [Mark of Detection](mark-of-detection.md) | Divination magic for scrying, familiars, and reading thoughts. |
| [Mark of Finding](mark-of-finding.md) | Tracking magic that locates creatures and objects at any distance. |
| [Mark of Handling](mark-of-handling.md) | Beast magic for befriending, summoning, and commanding animals. |
| [Mark of Healing](mark-of-healing.md) | Restorative magic that mends wounds and cures afflictions. |
| [Mark of Hospitality](mark-of-hospitality.md) | Comforting magic of feasts, shelter, and warm welcomes. |
| [Mark of Making](mark-of-making.md) | Crafting magic that mends, enchants, and fabricates objects. |
| [Mark of Passage](mark-of-passage.md) | Travel magic for swift movement and teleportation. |
| [Mark of Scribing](mark-of-scribing.md) | Language magic for writing, communication, and lore. |
| [Mark of Sentinels](mark-of-sentinels.md) | Protective magic that guards allies and wards against attacks. |
| [Mark of Shadows](mark-of-shadows.md) | Illusion magic of disguise, invisibility, and deception. |
| [Mark of Storms](mark-of-storms.md) | Elemental magic of wind, lightning, and flight. |
| [Mark of Warding](mark-of-warding.md) | Defensive magic that locks, conceals, and shields. |
| [Aberrant Mark](aberrant-mark.md) | An unstable, uninherited mark granting sorcerous magic and hardy Fortitude. |

## Greater Dragonmarks

Some Dragonmarks can deepen into Greater Dragonmarks, granting an additional spell slot and potent new powers as you reach higher levels.
