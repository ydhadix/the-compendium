# Dragonmarks
Dragonmarks are magical sigils passed down through bloodlines, granting their bearers innate spells and uncanny talents.

## Gaining Dragonmarks
The most powerful Dragonmarks are passed down through a creature's family.  Each Dragonmark has four forms:

| Dragonmark | Description |
|---|---|
| Sovereign Mark | The singular leader of a Dragonmarked family.  Sovereign Marks are able to grant Lesser and Greater Marks to other creatures. |
| Noble Mark | A naturally manifested Dragonmark.  Noble Marks can develop into Greater Marks of their own accord, and are candidates to become Sovereign Marks if its wielder passes. |
| Lesser Mark | A Dragonmark granted by a house leader.  Possesses the same might as a Noble Mark, but cannot develop further without the Sovereign's intervention. |
| Greater Mark | The height of a Dragonmark's power, each manifesting unique abilities that set their wielders apart from typical magic users. |

#### Choosing a Dragonmark
Possessing a Dragonmark requires a connection to the Mark's Sovereign — either by bloodline, or by acquaintance.  As a result, taking a Dragonmark requires that the setting and story allow it.  Your DM determines whether Dragonmarks are available, and can help weave the Mark's Sovereign into your character's backstory.

#### Greater Dragonmarks
A Noble or Lesser Dragonmark's power can increase to a Greater Mark during a campaign.  A Noble Mark usually develops when its wielder accomplishes a great feat, while a Lesser Mark requires the acknowledgement of the Mark's Sovereign.  In either case, a Greater Mark usually isn't available earlier than Level **5**.
