### Mark of Shadows

_Prerequisite: No other Dragonmark_

#### Mark Proficiency

You have Proficiency in one of Deception or Performance.

#### Mark Spells

You learn this Dragonmark's Spells at the corresponding Levels.  You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

| LV | Spells |
|---|---|
| 1st | _Minor Illusion_, _Disguise Self_ |
| 3rd | _Invisibility_ |
| 5th | _Major Image_ |
| 7th | _Greater Invisibility_ |
| 9th | _Mislead_ |

###### Free Casting

_1 use each per Long Rest_

You can cast one of this Dragonmark's Spells without a Spell Slot.

###### Changing Spells

When you gain a Level, you can replace one of this Dragonmark's Spells with a Bard Spell of the same Level, provided that spell doesn't deal damage.
