### Mark of Hospitality

_Prerequisite: No other Dragonmark_

#### Mark Proficiency

You have Proficiency in one of Insight or Persuasion.

#### Mark Spells

You learn this Dragonmark's Spells at the corresponding Levels.  You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

| LV | Spells |
|---|---|
| 1st | _Friends_, _Goodberry_ |
| 3rd | _Prayer of Healing_ |
| 5th | _Tiny Hut_ |
| 7th | _Aura of Purity_ |
| 9th | _Heroes' Feast_ |

###### Free Casting

_1 use each per Long Rest_

You can cast one of this Dragonmark's Spells without a Spell Slot.

###### Changing Spells

When you gain a Level, you can replace one of this Dragonmark's Spells with a Cleric Spell of the same Level, provided that spell doesn't deal damage.
