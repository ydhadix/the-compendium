### Mark of Hospitality
Prerequisite: No other Dragonmark
{ .subtitle }

#### Mark Proficiency
You have Insight or Persuasion Proficiency.

#### Mark Spells
You learn this Dragonmark's Spells at the corresponding Levels.  You can cast those Spells using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting Ability.

| LV | Spells |
|---|---|
| 1st | _Friends_, _Goodberry_ |
| 3rd | _Prayer of Healing_ |
| 5th | _Tiny Hut_ |
| 7th | _Aura of Purity_ |
| 9th | _Heroes' Feast_ |

###### Free Casting
1 use each per Long Rest
{ .subtitle }

You can cast one of this Dragonmark's Spells without a Spell Slot.

###### Changing Spells
Whenever you level up, you can replace one of this Dragonmark's Spells with a Cleric Spell of the same Level, provided that Spell doesn't deal damage.
