# Origin Feats

Origin Feats are broadly useful talents you gain from your background.


| Origin Feats | Description |
|---|---|
| [Alert](alert.md) | Gain Initiative Proficiency and swap places in the turn order with a willing ally. |
| [Crafter](crafter.md) | Proficiency with artisan's tools, cheaper purchases, and quick crafting of simple items. |
| [Healer](healer.md) | Use a Healer's Kit to restore a creature's Hit Dice, and reroll poor healing results. |
| [Lucky](lucky.md) | Grant yourself Advantage or impose Disadvantage on an attacker a few times per rest. |
| [Magic Initiate](magic-initiate.md) | Learn cantrips and a 1st-level spell from a chosen class, with a free casting each rest. |
| [Musician](musician.md) | Proficiency with musical instruments and a song that grants allies Inspiration. |
| [Skilled](skilled.md) | Proficiency with any three skills, trades, or martial weapon categories. |
| [Tough](tough.md) | A permanent boost to your Hit Point Maximum that grows as you level. |
