### Mobile Master
Prerequisite: Level 4, Skirmisher Fighting Style
{ .subtitle }

Your Speed increases by **10** feet.

After you Dash, you ignore Difficult Terrain until the end of the Turn.

##### Charge
**Free Action; Once per Turn:** After you move at least **10** feet in a line toward a target, you can choose **1** of the following effects for your next Melee Attack against that target:

- Before you deal damage, you add **2d8** to the Attack's damage.
- After you deal damage, if the target is no more than **1** Size larger than you, you propel it up to **10** feet away from you or knock it Prone.
