### Warcasting Master
Prerequisite: Level 4, Mage Slayer Fighting Style
{ .subtitle }

##### War Casting
**1 Attack:** You can cast a Cantrip with a Casting Time under **1** minute.

##### Reflexive Casting
**Free Action:** Before you make an Opportunity Attack, you can replace the attack with casting a Spell with a Casting Time under **1** minute that only targets the attack's target.
