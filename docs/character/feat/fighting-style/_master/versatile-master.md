### Versatile Master
Prerequisite: Level 4, Duelist Fighting Style
{ .subtitle }

While you have a Shield donned, your Versatile weapons can deal their two-handed damage with one hand.

While you don't have a Shield donned, your Attack Rolls with Versatile weapons increase by **2**.

#### Bash
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You can use a Versatile weapon or Shield to Shove a creature.
