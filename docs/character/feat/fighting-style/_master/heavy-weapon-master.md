### Heavy Weapon Master
Prerequisite: Level 4, Juggernaut Fighting Style
{ .subtitle }

Your Damage Rolls with Heavy Weapons increase by **PB**.

##### Follow Through
**Free Action; Once per Turn:** After you attack with a Heavy Weapon and score a Critical Hit or reduce the target to **0** Hit Points, you can make an additional Attack using that weapon.  If that attack was with a Ranged Weapon,  the second target must be in a straight line behind the first.
