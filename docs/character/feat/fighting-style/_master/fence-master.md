### Fence Master
Prerequisite: Level 4, Fence Proficiency, Duelist Fighting Style
{ .subtitle }

While holding a Fence or Sidearm and no other weapons, your Armor Class increases by **1**.

#### Riposte
Reaction
{ .subtitle }

Before you are hit with an Attack, you can use a Fence or Sidearm to add your Dexterity to your Armor Class. If that Attack misses, you can attack the attacker using that weapon.
