### Shield Master
Prerequisite: Level 4, Shieldsman Fighting Style
{ .subtitle }

While you have a Shield donned, your Shield's Armor Class bonus also adds to your Reflex Saves _(Masterwork is included in this bonus)_.

#### Shield Hammer
You can wield a Heavy Shield as a Delta Hammer _(it is still a Shield and only a weapon while attacking)_.

#### Shield Bash
Free Action, 1 use per Turn
{ .subtitle }

After you make a Physical Melee Attack, you can use a Shield to Shove the target.
