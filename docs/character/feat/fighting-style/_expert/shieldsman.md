### Shieldsman
Prerequisite: **2** Strength
{ .subtitle }

You can wield a Medium Shield as a Hammer _(it is still a Shield and only a weapon while attacking)_.

##### Cover
**Reaction:** Before another creature within Reach is hit with an Attack or fails a Reflex Save, you can add your Shield's Armor Class bonus to the target's Armor Class or Reflex Saves until the end of the Turn.
