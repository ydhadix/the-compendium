### Technician

The number of unworn items you have access to in combat increases by **PB**.

#### Steady Stockpile
After you finish a Long Rest, you can turn an Ammunition container within your Reach into a Steady Stockpile. Your Steady Stockpile never runs out of nonmagical Ammunition. Any Ammunition produced this way is destroyed immediately after the Attack.

##### Fast Hands
**Bonus Action:** You can Utilize.
