### Technique Training

You learn **1** Weapon Technique.

##### Use Technique
**Free Action; Once per Attack:** You can use one Weapon Technique you know.

| Technique | |
|---|---|
| Cleave | **Once per Turn:** After you hit with a Cleave weapon, you can make a Melee Attack against a second target within **5** feet of the first. You don't add your ability modifier to the Damage Roll, unless it is negative. |
| Graze | After you miss with a Graze weapon, you can deal **Attack Roll Ability** damage to the target. |
| Nick | After you use a Nick weapon's Light property, you can make the additional Attack as part of the same Attack Action instead of using a Bonus Action. |
| Pierce | **Once per Turn:** After you hit with a Ranged Attack using a Pierce weapon, you can make a Ranged Attack against a second target up to **10** feet in a straight line behind the first. You don't add your ability modifier to the Damage Roll, unless it is negative. |
| Push | After you hit a target up to **1** Size larger than you with a Push weapon, you can propel the target up to **10** feet away from you. |
| Sap | After you hit a creature with a Sap weapon, you can give the target Disadvantage on its next Attack Roll before the start of your next Turn. |
| Slow | After you hit a creature with a Slow weapon, you can decrease the target's Speed by **10** feet until the start of your next Turn. A creature's Speed can't decrease by more than **10** feet this way. |
| Topple | **Once per Turn:** After you hit a target up to **1** Size larger than you with a Topple weapon, you can knock the target Prone. |
| Vex | After you hit a creature with a Vex weapon, you can gain Advantage on the next Attack Roll you make against that target before the end of your next Turn. |

#### Repeatable
You can take this feat more than once, provided you choose a different technique each time.
