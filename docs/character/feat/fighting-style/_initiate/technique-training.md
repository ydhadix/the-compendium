### Technique Training

You learn one of the following Weapon Techniques.

#### Repeatable
You can take this Fighting Style more than once, provided you choose a different technique each time.

##### Cleave
Free Action, 1 use per Turn
{ .subtitle }

After you hit with a Cleave weapon, you can make a Melee Attack against a second target within **5** feet of the first. You don't add your ability modifier to the Damage Roll, unless it is negative.

##### Graze
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After you miss with a Graze weapon, you can deal damage to the target equal to the ability modifier used for the Attack Roll.

##### Nick
After you use a Nick weapon's Light property, the additional Attack is made as part of the same Attack Action instead of requiring a Bonus Action.

##### Pierce
Free Action, 1 use per Turn
{ .subtitle }

After you hit with a Ranged Attack using a Pierce weapon, you can make a Ranged Attack against a second target up to **10** feet in a straight line behind the first. You don't add your ability modifier to the Damage Roll, unless it is negative.

##### Push
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After you hit a target up to one size larger than you with a Push weapon, you can propel the target up to 10 feet.

##### Sap
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After you damage a creature with a Sap weapon, the target's next Attack Roll gains Disadvantage before the start of your next Turn.

##### Slow
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After you damage a creature with a Slow weapon, the target's Speed decreases by **10** feet until the start of your next Turn. A creature's Speed can't decrease by more than 10 feet this way.

##### Topple
Free Action, 1 use per Turn
{ .subtitle }

After you damage a target up to one size larger than you with a Topple weapon, you can knock the target Prone.

##### Vex
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After you hit a creature with a Vex weapon, you gain Advantage on the next Attack Roll you make against that target before the end of your next Turn.
