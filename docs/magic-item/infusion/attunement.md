# Attunement

Some Magic Items require a special bond — called Attunement — before you can use their infused properties.
{ .subtitle }

## Attunement Slots

You can be Attuned to up to **3** Magic Items at a time. Attuning to a fourth item fails; you must end another Attunement first.

## Becoming Attuned

You can Attune to an item by spending a Short Rest focused on only that item while in physical contact with it. This can't be the same Short Rest used to identify the item. After you finish, you become Attuned to the item and can use its full magical capabilities.

## Ending Attunement

You can voluntarily end Attunement by spending another Short Rest focused on the item. Your Attunement also ends if any of these conditions are met:

- The item is more than **100** feet away from you for **24** hours.
- Another creature Attunes to the item.
- You die.
