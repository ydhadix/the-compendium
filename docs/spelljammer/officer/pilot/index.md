# Pilot
The Pilot controls the Ship's movement and position.
{ .subtitle }

Pilots have three Pilot Actions, plus Pilot Maneuvers that enhance those Actions by expending Maneuver Dice. A Pilot begins each combat with **2 × Proficiency Bonus** **d6** Maneuver Dice.

## Pilot Actions
#### Piloting Checks
Some Pilot Actions require a Piloting Check, usually contested by an enemy Ship's Pilot. A Piloting check uses your Precision proficiency with your choice of Strength or Dexterity.

#### Size Class
Every Ship has a Size Class that reduces the total of your Piloting Checks and adds damage to your Ram Action.

| Pilot Action | Description |
|---|---|
| Advance / Retreat | Maneuver closer to or farther from an enemy Ship. |
| Hold Steady | Keep your Ship's path as steady as possible. |
| Ram | Collide with an enemy Ship. |

### Advance / Retreat
You make a Piloting check against an enemy Ship.

| | |
|---|---|
| Success | You move one range closer or farther. |

### Hold Steady
Until your next Turn, your Ship has Advantage on Attacks, but Attacks against your Ship also have Advantage.

### Ram
You make a Piloting check against an enemy Ship within Close range.

| | |
|---|---|
| Success | You roll **2 × your Size Class** **d10s** and deal the total as Bludgeoning Damage to the enemy Ship's hull, and half as much to your own hull. |
| Failure | Until your next Turn, Attacks against your Ship have Advantage. |

## Pilot Maneuvers
A Pilot learns Maneuvers based on their Class Levels.  You can use a Maneuver only when its condition is met, and you must expend one of your Maneuver Dice to do so.

Whenever a Pilot Maneuver refers to a Ship's AC, or to Attacks made against a Ship, this means the AC of the hull as well as all the Ship's Weapons.
