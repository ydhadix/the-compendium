### High Loop

| | |
|---|---|
| Class List | Level 5 Artificer, Monk, Ranger, or Rogue |
| Trigger | Free Action, before you Advance |
| Duration | — |

Add a Maneuver Die to your Piloting check.

| | |
|---|---|
| Success | Add that die's result to your Ship's Damage Rolls until your next Turn. |
