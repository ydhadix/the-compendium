# Spelljammer
The **Spelljammer** sits at the ship's Helm and channels Spell Slots into Protocols, spell-like powers that drive and defend the ship.
{ .subtitle }

While seated in the Ship's spelljamming Helm, the Spelljammer can expend Spell Slots to use Protocols — spell-like effects amplified for Ship Combat.

## Protocols
**Protocols** behave like Spells, except they need no Components. A Jamming Protocol uses your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as its Spellcasting Ability.

Whenever a Protocol refers to a Ship's AC, or to Attacks made against a Ship, this includes the AC of the hull as well as all the Ship's Weapons and Components.

#### Casting Protocols
Each spelljamming Helm comes with a set of inscribed Protocols. You can also use a Protocol that isn't inscribed in the Helm, provided you can cast the Protocol's base Spell.

#### Concentration
While Concentrating on a Protocol, whenever the Ship's hull takes damage, you make a Concentration Save as though you took the damage. Your Concentration ends if you or the Ship are Incapacitated, or if you aren't seated in the Helm.
