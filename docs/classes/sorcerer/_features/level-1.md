### Spellcasting
*Level 1 Sorcerer*

The Sorcerer table shows how many cantrips you know and how many spells you prepare.

##### Spellcasting Ability

Your Sorcerer Ability is your Spellcasting Ability.

##### Spellcasting Focus

Sorcerer spells use an Arcane Focus as a spellcasting focus.

##### Changing Spells

**Level Up**: You can change one of your known cantrips and one of your prepared spells.

### Font of Magic
*Level 1 Sorcerer*

The Sorcerer table shows how many Sorcery Points you have. When you cast a spell, you can expend Sorcery Points to create the required spell slot.

**Long Rest**: You regain all your Sorcery Points.

| Spell Level | Sorcery Points | Minimum Sorcerer Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 2 | 1 |
| 2nd | 3 | 3 |
| 3rd | 5 | 5 |
| 4th | 6 | 7 |
| 5th | 7 | 9 |
| 6th | 9 | 11 |

### Metamagic
*Level 1 Sorcerer*

When you cast a spell, you can expend Sorcery Points to temporarily modify the spell with a Metamagic. You can only use one Metamagic on a spell, unless noted otherwise.

The Sorcerer table shows how many Metamagics you know, and when you learn more.

**Level Up**: You can change one of your known Metamagics.

See [Metamagic](metamagic.md) for the full list of available options.
