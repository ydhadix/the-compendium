# Metamagic

When you cast a spell, you can expend Sorcery Points to modify it with a Metamagic. You can only use one Metamagic on a spell unless noted otherwise. The Sorcerer table shows how many Metamagics you know.

**Level Up**: You can change one of your known Metamagics.
