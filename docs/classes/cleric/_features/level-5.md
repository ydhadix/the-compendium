### Blessed Strikes
*Level 5 Cleric*

Divine power enhances your weapons or spells. Choose one of the following benefits.

#### Divine Strike
Whenever you deal damage with a weapon, you can add 1d8 Necrotic or Radiant damage.

#### Potent Spellcasting
Whenever you deal damage with a Cleric cantrip, you can add your Cleric Ability to the damage.
