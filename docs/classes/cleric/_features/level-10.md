???+ class-feature "Improved Blessed Strikes | Level 10 Cleric"

    #### Improved Blessed Strikes

    Your divine power increases further. The benefit you chose for Blessed Strikes improves.

    ###### Divine Strike
    The extra damage increases to 2d8.

    ###### Potent Spellcasting
    Whenever you deal damage with a Cleric cantrip, you can give any creature within 60 feet of yourself temporary hit points equal to twice your Cleric Ability.
