# Class Codex

Your class reflects your specialized training and defines how you interact with the world as an adventurer.  Each class also has subclasses that further enhance your capabilities.

## Leveling Up
When you gain a level, choose one of your classes to gain that level in, or you can choose a secondary class if you don't have one yet.  You gain the chosen class’s corresponding features.

### Hit Dice and Hit Points
When you gain a level in a class, you gain one of that class’s hit dice.  Add that hit die’s maximum plus your Constitution to your hit point maximum.

### Primary Class
The class you choose at level 1 is your Primary Class.  When you gain your first level in your primary class, you gain the chosen class's starting equipment and primary proficiencies.

### Secondary Class
You can take levels in two classes.  When you gain your first level in your secondary class, you gain the chosen class’s secondary proficiencies.

Once you choose a secondary class, you cannot change it, and you cannot take levels in a third class.

### Character Level
Your character level is equal to the sum of your primary class’s levels and your secondary class’s levels.  Your character level determines your proficiency bonus, your ability maximum, and gives an additional feat and/or bonus to an ability of your choice.

### Multiclass Spellcasting
If you gain the Spellcasting feature from both your primary and secondary classes, your capacity for spell slots depends on your individual levels in those classes.  Spellcasting variants, such as Pact Magic and Sorcery, do not count towards multiclass spellcasting.

| Class | Multiplier |
|---|---| 
| Artificer | × ½ |
| Bard | × 1 |
| Cleric | × 1 |
| Druid | × 1 |
| Fighter | × ⅓ |
| Paladin | × ½ |
| Ranger | × ½ |
| Rogue | × ⅓ |
| Wizard | × 1 |

You prepare your spells for each class individually.  Each spell you prepare is associated with the class you used to prepare it, and you use that class’s spellcasting ability to cast it.

## Class Abilities
Every class has a class ability that is used to calculate the save DCs, spellcasting ability, and how many times you can use some features.  Class abilities are usually referred to by the class their associated with (e.g. Fighter Ability, Wizard Ability).
If a class has a choice of Class Ability, you choose the ability when you take your first level in that class.

### Secondary Abilities
Secondary abilities are just like class abilities, though not every class has a secondary ability.
