# Eldritch Invocations

Eldritch Invocations are arcane techniques you've unlocked through your pact. The Warlock table shows how many you know. You can't pick the same Invocation more than once, unless its description says otherwise.

**Level Up**: You can change one of your known Invocations. You can't change an Invocation if it's a prerequisite for another Invocation you know.

## Starting Invocations

### Armor of Shadows

*No prerequisite*

You learn Mage Armor and can cast it targeting yourself without a spell slot.

### Eldritch Mind

*No prerequisite*

You have Advantage on Concentration saves.

### Lessons of the First Ones

*No prerequisite — Repeatable (different feat each time)*

Choose an Origin Feat. You gain that feat.

### Pact of the Blade

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

You gain the ability to summon or bond a Pact Weapon. Your bond ends if you bond with another weapon, your Pact Weapon is out of your reach for 1 minute or more, or you die.

Your Pact Weapon gains the following benefits:

- You can use the weapon as an Arcane Focus.
- You are proficient with the weapon.
- Attacks with the weapon can use your Warlock Ability.
- You can change damage dealt by the weapon to Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant.

**Bonus Action**: You summon a Simple or Martial Melee weapon to your hand. This weapon becomes bonded as your Pact Weapon, and disappears when the bond ends.

**Bonus Action**: You bond a Magic Weapon you are carrying as your Pact Weapon. You can't bond with a weapon that is attuned or bonded to another creature.

### Pact of the Chain

*No prerequisite*

You learn Find Familiar and can cast it as an Action without a spell slot.

When you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your attacks to command your familiar to use its Reaction to attack.

When you cast Find Familiar, you can choose one of the following special forms: imp, quasit, salad tadpole, sprite.

### Pact of the Tome

*No prerequisite*

As part of a Short Rest, you summon a Book of Shadows to your hand. The book disappears if you summon another Book of Shadows, or you die. You can use the book as an Arcane Focus.

After you summon a Book of Shadows, choose three Cantrips and two 1st-level Ritual Spells. While the Book of Shadows is on your person, you know the chosen spells as Warlock spells.

## Level 2 Invocations

### Agonizing Blast

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock, a damaging Warlock cantrip — Repeatable (different cantrip each time)*

Choose one of your Warlock Cantrips that deals damage. You add your Warlock Ability to that spell's damage rolls.

### Devil's Sight

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

You gain 120 feet of Devil's Sight.

### Eldritch Smite

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

**Free Action, 1 Pact Spell Slot**: When you hit with your Pact Weapon, you deal an additional 1d8 Force damage, plus 1d8 Force damage per level of the spell slot expended. If a target damaged by this feature is Huge or smaller, you can knock it Prone.

### Eldritch Spear

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock, a ranged Warlock cantrip — Repeatable (different cantrip each time)*

Choose one of your Warlock Cantrips that has a Range. That spell's Range increases by 30 feet for each Warlock level you have. (A spell with a Reach Range increases by that distance beyond your Reach.)

### Fiendish Vigor

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

You learn False Life and can cast it targeting yourself without a spell slot. When you do, you always gain the maximum number of Temporary Hit Points.

### Mask of Many Faces

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

You learn Disguise Self and can cast it without a spell slot.

### Misty Visions

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

You learn Silent Image and can cast it without a spell slot.

### Otherworldly Leap

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock*

You learn Jump and can cast it targeting yourself without a spell slot.

### Repelling Blast

*Prerequisite: Level 2 Warlock, an attacking Warlock cantrip — Repeatable (different cantrip each time)*

Choose one of your Warlock Cantrips that has an Attack Roll. After you hit with the chosen spell, if the target is Large or smaller, you can push the target up to 10 feet straight away from you.

## Level 5 Invocations

### Ascendant Step

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock*

You learn Levitate and can cast it targeting yourself without a spell slot.

### Gaze of Two Minds

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock*

**Bonus Action**: Choose a creature within your Reach. You can perceive through the creature's senses until the end of your next turn.

While perceiving through another creature's senses, you can use this Bonus Action regardless of distance to extend the duration. If the creature is within 60 feet of you, you can cast spells as though you were in the creature's space.

### Gift of the Depths

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock*

You can breathe underwater, you gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, and you learn Water Breathing.

**Once per Long Rest**: You can cast Water Breathing without a spell slot.

### Gift of the Protectors

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock, Pact of the Tome*

When you summon your Book of Shadows, a new page appears that can contain your Warlock Ability (min. 1) number of names. A creature can only write their own name on this page. You can erase any name on the page by touching it.

**Once per Long Rest**: Before a creature whose name is written on this page would drop to 0 Hit Points or be killed, you can make them have 1 Hit Point instead.

### Investment of the Chain Master

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock, Pact of the Chain*

When you cast Find Familiar, the summoned familiar gains the following benefits:

- **Aerial or Aquatic**: The familiar gains your choice of a 40-foot Fly or Swim speed.
- **Quick Attack**: **Bonus Action**: You command the familiar to take the Attack Action.
- **Necrotic or Radiant Damage**: When the familiar deals Physical damage, you can make it deal Necrotic or Radiant damage instead.
- **Your Save DC**: When the familiar forces a creature to make a save, it uses your Spell Save DC.
- **Resistance**: **Reaction**: Before the familiar takes damage, it gains Resistance to that damage.

### Master of Myriad Forms

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock*

You learn Alter Self and can cast it without a spell slot.

### One with Shadows

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock*

You learn Invisibility. While in Dim Light or Darkness, you can cast it without a spell slot.

### Thirsting Blade

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock, Pact of the Blade*

When you take the Attack Action on your turn and attack with your Pact Weapon, you can make an additional attack.

## Level 7 Invocations

### Life Drinker

*Prerequisite: Level 7 Warlock, Pact of the Blade*

**Once per Turn**: When you hit with your Pact Weapon, you can expend and roll one Hit Die and add your Warlock Ability. You deal that amount as additional Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage to the target and heal by the same total.

If you don't expend a Hit Die, you instead deal 1d6 Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage to the target.

### Gift of the Protectors

*Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock, Pact of the Tome*

When you summon your Book of Shadows, a new page appears that can contain your Warlock Ability (min. 1) number of names. A creature can only write their own name on this page. You can erase any name on the page by touching it.

**Once per Long Rest**: Before a creature whose name is written on this page would drop to 0 Hit Points or be killed, you can make them have 1 Hit Point instead.

### Whispers of the Grave

*Prerequisite: Level 7 Warlock*

You learn Speak with Dead and can cast it without a spell slot.

## Level 10 Invocations

### Visions of Distant Realms

*Prerequisite: Level 10 Warlock*

You learn Arcane Eye and can cast it without a spell slot.

### Witch Sight

*Prerequisite: Level 10 Warlock*

You gain 30 feet of Truesight.
