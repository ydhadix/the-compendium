??? "Investment of the Chain Master"
    *Prerequisite: Level 5 Warlock, Pact of the Chain*

    When you cast Find Familiar, the summoned familiar gains the following benefits:

    - **Aerial or Aquatic**: The familiar gains your choice of a 40-foot Fly or Swim speed.
    - **Quick Attack**: **Bonus Action**: You command the familiar to take the Attack Action.
    - **Necrotic or Radiant Damage**: When the familiar deals Physical damage, you can make it deal Necrotic or Radiant damage instead.
    - **Your Save DC**: When the familiar forces a creature to make a save, it uses your Spell Save DC.
    - **Resistance**: **Reaction**: Before the familiar takes damage, it gains Resistance to that damage.
