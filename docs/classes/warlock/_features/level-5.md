### Eldritch Disciple
*Level 5 Warlock*

The following Invocations are added to your [Eldritch Invocation](invocations.md#level-5-invocations) options:

- Ascendant Step
- Gaze of Two Minds
- Gift of the Depths
- Gift of the Protectors
- Investment of the Chain Master
- Master of Myriad Forms
- One with Shadows
- Thirsting Blade
