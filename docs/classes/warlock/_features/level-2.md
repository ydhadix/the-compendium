???+ class-feature "Mystic Recovery | Level 2 Warlock"

    #### Mystic Recovery

    **Magic Action, Once per Long Rest**: You regain half of your expended Pact Magic Spell Slots (rounded up).

???+ class-feature "Eldritch Adept | Level 2 Warlock"

    #### Eldritch Adept

    The following Invocations are added to your [Eldritch Invocation](invocations.md#level-2-invocations) options:

    - Agonizing Blast
    - Devil's Sight
    - Eldritch Smite
    - Eldritch Spear
    - Fiendish Vigor
    - Mask of Many Faces
    - Misty Visions
    - Otherworldly Leap
    - Repelling Blast
