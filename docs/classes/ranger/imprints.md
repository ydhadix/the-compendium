# Primal Imprints

Primal Imprints are animalistic techniques you channel to enhance your survival. The Ranger table shows how many Imprints you know. You can't pick the same Imprint more than once.

**Long Rest**: You can change one of your known Imprints.

---

## Starting Imprints

### Bioluminescence

*No prerequisite*

You emit a soft glow that lights your surroundings.

You emit Bright Light out to 10 feet and Dim Light 10 feet further.

**Magic Action**: You turn the light off or on.

### Camouflage

*No prerequisite*

You easily blend into your surroundings, evading detection.

Perception checks to see you are made with Disadvantage.

### Hold Breath

*No prerequisite*

You can sustain yourself without breathing for long periods.

You can hold your breath for up to 1 hour.

### Keen Senses

*No prerequisite*

You sharpen your detection senses.

You have Advantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing or sight.

### Powerful Build

*No prerequisite*

You exert surprising strength for your frame.

You have Advantage on Athletics checks, and your carry capacity is doubled.

### Sure-Footed

*No prerequisite*

You easily maintain your footing on steep or unstable ground.

You have resistance to Prone, and you gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.

## Level 4 Imprints

### Amphibious

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You move and breathe easily in aquatic environments.

You can breathe underwater, and you gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

### Cold Tolerance

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You are adapted to arctic environments.

You ignore Difficult Terrain caused by ice and snow, and you gain resistance to Extreme Cold and Cold damage.

### Heat Tolerance

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You are adapted to desert environments.

You ignore Difficult Terrain caused by sand, and you gain resistance to Extreme Heat and Fire damage.

### Jumper

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You are especially agile at scaling and navigating heights.

You use your Dexterity to determine your jumping distance, and you gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.

### Mimicry

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You can reliably reproduce sounds you've heard.

You can mimic simple sounds you have heard. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful Insight check against your Ranger spell save DC.

### Nocturnal

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

Your vision is adapted for low-light environments.

You gain 120 feet of Darkvision.

### Roving

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You move swiftly and evade retaliation.

You don't provoke Opportunity Attacks, and your Speed increases by 10 feet.

### Skittish

*Prerequisite: Level 4 Ranger*

You evade threats with nimble, unpredictable retreats.

**Bonus Action**: You take the Disengage or Hide action.

---

## Level 7 Imprints

### Amorphous

*Prerequisite: Level 7 Ranger*

You compress your form to slip through tight spaces.

You have Advantage on checks to avoid or escape being Grappled, and you can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement. (Items you are wearing or carrying do not gain this benefit.)

### Echolocation

*Prerequisite: Level 7 Ranger*

You read your surroundings through reflected sound.

While you aren't Deafened, you gain 30 feet of Blindsight.

### Glider

*Prerequisite: Level 7 Ranger*

You can glide and break your falls through controlled movements.

You have resistance to falling damage, and during your turn, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. (You fall if you end your turn airborne without another way to stay aloft.)

### Web Walker

*Prerequisite: Level 7 Ranger*

You can deftly traverse webs and vertical surfaces.

You ignore movement restrictions caused by webs, and you gain a Spider Climb Speed equal to your Speed.

---

## Level 10 Imprints

### Burrower

*Prerequisite: Level 10 Ranger*

You dig through the earth, leaving a secure path behind.

You gain a 15-foot Burrow Speed, and when you burrow, you leave tunnels behind.

### Pack Tactics

*Prerequisite: Level 10 Ranger*

You learn to exploit weaknesses in surrounded prey.

You have Advantage on attack rolls against creatures that are within 5 feet of a non-Incapacitated ally.
