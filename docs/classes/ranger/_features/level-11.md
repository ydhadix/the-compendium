    #### Foe Slayer

    Your hunting prowess lets you exploit even the slightest weakness.

    Whenever you start your turn, you can move your Hunter's Mark to a new creature you can see within range.

    Before you deal damage to a marked creature, you can end your Hunter's Mark to give the target vulnerability to the damage. (If the damage was from an attack, you still add the bonus damage before applying vulnerability.)
