## Class Details

**Class Ability**: Strength or Dexterity

**Minor Ability**: Intelligence or Wisdom

**Hit Die**: d10

**Starting Equipment**: choose 150 gold or:

- leather armor
- scimitar
- shortsword
- longbow
- 20 arrows
- quiver
- druidic focus
- explorer's pack
- 7 gold

### Primary Proficiencies

**Saving Throws**: Reflex, ½ Fortitude

**Armor**: light and medium armor

**Weapons**: simple weapons, foils, and bows

**Tools**: —

**Skills**: Survival and choose 3 from Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Nature, Perception, or Stealth

### Secondary Proficiencies

**Armor**: light and medium armor

**Weapons**: martial weapons

**Tools**: —

**Skills**: Survival and one Ranger skill
