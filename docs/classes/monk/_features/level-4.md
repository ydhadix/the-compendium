### Stunning Strike
*Level 4 Monk*

You focus a strike to overwhelm an opponent's senses.

**Once per Turn, 1 Ki**: When you hit with a Monk Weapon, you can force the target to make a Fortitude save.

**Fail**: The target is Stunned until the end of your next turn.

**Success**: The target's speed is halved until the end of your next turn.
