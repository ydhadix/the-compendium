### Spell-Storing Item
*Level 8 Artificer*

You learn new methods for storing powerful spells.

**Long Rest**: You can choose a weapon or Artificer spellcasting focus and an Artificer spell. The chosen item gains the following effect until you use this feature again:

##### Spell-Storing Item

**Spellcasting, 2 × Artificer Ability per Long Rest**: A creature holding this item can cast the chosen spell using your spellcasting ability.
