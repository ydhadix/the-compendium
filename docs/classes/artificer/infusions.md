# Infusions

Infusions are temporary magic item effects you imbue into mundane items during a Long Rest. Infused items vanish when their infusions end. After infusing an item, you can attune to it immediately.

The number of items you can have infused at one time is shown on the Artificer table. To infuse additional items beyond your limit, you must choose existing infusions to end.
