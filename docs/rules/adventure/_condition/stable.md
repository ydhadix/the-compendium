##### Stable
- You are Dazed.
- Before you take an Action, you make a Fortitude Save _(DC **11**)_; on a failure, the Action has no effect.
- After **1d4** hours pass, you heal **1** Hit Point.
- After you take damage, you are Downed, and you also fail a Death Save if the damage is from a Critical Hit.

###### Ending the Condition
This condition ends after you are Downed or have more than **0** Hit Points.
