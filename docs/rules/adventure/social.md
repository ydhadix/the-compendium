# Socializing

## Disposition

!!! note "In progress"
    These rules haven't been fully written yet.

A creature's Disposition describes its relationship toward you: Friendly, Indifferent, or Hostile. There are no general rules yet for shifting a creature's Disposition.

## Companions

A Companion is a creature with a magical link to another creature. A Companion is always an Ally of the creature it's linked to. Some Spells and features grant Companions that travel with you and aid you in combat and exploration.

### Commanding Companions

Your Companions share your Turn in [Initiative](../encounter/initiative.md). They act or Move only when commanded, and each Companion's Spell or feature sets how you command it.

If you don't command a Companion, it takes the [Dodge Action](../encounter/action/index.md#dodge) and Moves to stay within **10** feet of you, avoiding danger if it can.

While you are Incapacitated, your Companions can act and Move on their own, no longer limited to the Dodge Action.

No matter how many commands you issue, a Companion can take only one of each type of Action on its Turn.

### Companion Statistics

Your Companions use your Character Level and Proficiency Bonus in their stat blocks.

### Communication

Your Companions understand every language you speak, and some can speak those languages as well. A Companion may also know languages of its own.

While within **120** feet of a Companion, you can communicate with it telepathically.

### Summons

A Summon is a Companion conjured by the magic that links it.

### Familiars

A Familiar is a Summon with a special bond, such as the one created by _Find Familiar_. You can usually have only one Familiar at a time.
