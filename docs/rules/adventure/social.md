# Socializing

!!! note "In progress"
    This section is scaffolding. These rules haven't been fully written yet.

## Disposition

A creature's Disposition describes its relationship toward you: Friendly, Indifferent, or Hostile. There are no general rules yet for shifting a creature's Disposition.

## Companions

A Companion is a creature with a magical link to another creature. A Companion is always an Ally of the creature it's linked to.

### Summons

A Summon is a Companion conjured by the magic that links it.

### Familiars

A Familiar is a Summon with a special bond, such as the one created by _Find Familiar_. You can usually have only one Familiar at a time.
