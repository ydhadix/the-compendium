## Suffocation

You can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to **1 + CON** (minimum **30** seconds). After your air runs out, you can survive for a number of Rounds equal to **CON** (minimum **1** Round). After your next Turn begins, you drop to **0** Hit Points and are Downed, and you can't regain Hit Points or become Stable until you can breathe again.
