# Movement
Your Speed is the maximum distance you can Move on your Turn. Your movement can include climbing, crawling, jumping, and swimming.
{ .subtitle }

## Difficult Terrain
Each foot you Move through or out of Difficult Terrain costs **1** extra foot.

## Splitting Movement
You can Move before and after any Action on your Turn. However you break it up, your total movement can't exceed your Speed.

## Dropping Prone
You can drop Prone without spending an Action or any movement, unless your Speed is **0**.

## Moving Through Other Creatures
You can Move freely through the space of Allies and Tiny creatures.

You can also Move through the space of Incapacitated creatures and creatures at least two Size Categories larger or smaller than you, but that space is Difficult Terrain for you.

You can't willingly end your Move in another creature's space. If you end your Turn in a space with another creature, the smaller creature is knocked Prone, unless one is Tiny _(If you are both the same size, both are knocked Prone)_.

### Climbing
Climbing lets you traverse vertical surfaces. You need both hands, and each foot you Move costs **1** extra foot. Climbing a slippery surface or one with few handholds requires a successful Athletics Check.

### Falling
If you are airborne with no way to stay aloft, you immediately fall up to **500** feet. After each of your Turns begins, you fall another **500** feet, until you land or gain a way to stay aloft.

At the end of the fall, you take **1d6** Bludgeoning Damage for every **10** feet you fell and land Prone.

### Jumping
Jumping lets you ignore terrain effects while moving horizontally or vertically. Your Strength sets how far or high you can jump, and each foot you jump costs **1** foot of movement. If you don't Move at least **10** feet in a straight line right before a jump, the distance you can cover is halved.

#### Long Jump
You can cover a horizontal distance up to **10** feet plus five times your Strength. Clearing a low obstacle requires an Athletics Check.

#### High Jump
You can cover a vertical distance up to **3** feet plus your Strength. During the jump, you can extend your arms to reach up to half your height higher.

### Swimming
Swimming lets you Move through liquid. You need both hands, and each foot you Move costs **1** extra foot. Swimming in rough water requires a successful Athletics Check.

## Special Speeds
A [Special Speed](../trait/speed.md) — such as a Climb, Fly, or Swim Speed — removes the added cost or restriction on its kind of movement.
