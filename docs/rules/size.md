# Size

Every creature has a size category, which determines the space they occupy on a map. Your space is the area you control on a map and the area you need to fight effectively. Your race determines your size, though spells and other effects can make you larger or smaller.

| size | space (feet) | space (squares) |
|---|---|---|
| tiny | 2.5 × 2.5 | 4 per square |
| small | 5 × 5 | 1 × 1 |
| medium | 5 × 5 | 1 × 1 |
| large | 10 × 10 | 2 × 2 |
| huge | 15 × 15 | 3 × 3 |
| gargantuan | 20 × 20 or larger | 4 × 4 or larger |
