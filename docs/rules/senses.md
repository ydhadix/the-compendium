# Senses

## Obscurement

Lighting conditions, fog, foliage, and other visual obstacles create levels of obscurement. When seeing through an area, you have different effects based on the area's obscurement:

| obscurement | effect |
|---|---|
| clear | you can see normally. |
| light | you have disadvantage on perception checks that rely on sight. |
| heavy | you are blinded. |

## Lighting

The presence or absence of light in an area creates levels of lighting. When seeing into an area, you have different effects based on the area's lighting.

| lighting | effect |
|---|---|
| bright | you can see normally. |
| dim | the area is lightly obscured. |
| darkness | the area is heavily obscured. |
| magical darkness | the area is heavily obscured and darkvision doesn't help you see into it. |

## Senses

Senses let you perceive your surroundings in unfavorable conditions. Most senses have a range they're effective in — anything outside that range is unaffected by your senses.

### Adding Senses

If you gain the same sense from multiple effects, you add their effective ranges together.
