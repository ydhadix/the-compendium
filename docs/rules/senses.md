# Senses

## Obscurement

Lighting conditions, fog, foliage, and other visual obstacles create levels of obscurement. When seeing through an area, you have different effects based on the area's obscurement:

| obscurement | effect |
|---|---|
| clear | you can see normally. |
| light | you have disadvantage on perception checks that rely on sight. |
| heavy | you are blinded. |

## Lighting

The presence or absence of light in an area creates levels of lighting. When seeing into an area, you have different effects based on the area's lighting.

| lighting | effect |
|---|---|
| bright | you can see normally. |
| dim | the area is lightly obscured. |
| darkness | the area is heavily obscured. |
| magical darkness | the area is heavily obscured and darkvision doesn't help you see into it. |

## Senses

Senses let you perceive your surroundings in unfavorable conditions. Most senses have a range they're effective in — anything outside that range is unaffected by your senses.

### Blindsight

You can see without relying on visual sight. You ignore the effects of being Blinded and ignore Invisibility and half and three-quarters Cover.

### Darkvision

You can see in dim light as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in that darkness, only shades of grey.

### Devilsight

You can see normally in dim light, darkness, and magical darkness.

### Tremorsense

You can pinpoint the location of creatures and moving objects while you are both in contact with the same surface or liquid. This doesn't count as a form of sight.

### Truesight

- You have Blindsight.
- You have Devilsight.
- Visual illusions are translucent to you and you automatically succeed on saving throws against them.
- You can see the true form of creatures or objects transformed by magic.
- You can see into the ethereal plane.

### Adding Senses

If you gain the same sense from multiple effects, you add their effective ranges together.
