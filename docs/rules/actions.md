# Actions

When you do something other than move or speak, you typically use an action to do so. On each of your turns, you can take one of each type of Action.

## Main Actions

Most actions are Main Actions, unless specified otherwise.

The following Main Actions are available regardless of your Class, Race, or other features. Some features can give you alternative ways to use these actions.

You can also describe an action not detailed in the rules, and the DM will tell you whether the action is possible and what d20 roll, if any, you need to make.

### Attack

You make an attack roll using a weapon you're holding or an unarmed strike (see D20 Rolls for more information on making an attack).

### Dash

You gain extra movement equal to your current Speed until the end of the turn.

When you Ready the Dash action, you can use this movement as part of the reaction.

### Disengage

Your movement doesn't provoke Reactions, except the Ready action, until the end of the turn.

### Dodge

You gain the following effects until the start of your next turn, you are Incapacitated, or your Speed becomes 0:

| effect | description |
|---|---|
| evasive | attacks against you have disadvantage. |
| stable | your reflex saves have advantage. |

### Help

Choose an ally or enemy within your reach. Apply the corresponding effect, which lasts until the start of your next turn.

| target | description |
|---|---|
| ally | choose a skill or trade you are proficient with. the chosen ally has advantage on the next check they make using the chosen proficiency. |
| enemy | the next attack an ally makes against the chosen enemy has advantage. |

### Hide

Make a Stealth check. You become Hidden, and the check's total is your Hide DC (see the Hidden Condition for more information).

### Magic

You cast a spell or use a feature or item to produce a magical effect (see the Spellcasting Rules in the Spell Selection for more information about casting spells).

When you ready the Magic action, you must Concentrate until you take the readied action. If your concentration is broken, you cannot take the readied action, but you don't expend a spell slot or any of the action's other costs.

### Ready

Choose another action and a perceivable trigger for it. Until the start of your next turn, you gain the following effect:

**reaction**: After the chosen trigger occurs, you can take the chosen action.

You cannot take the Ready action outside of an Encounter. Attempting to do so starts Initiative before you can take the Ready action.

### Search

You make a Wisdom check to discern something that isn't obvious. What you're trying to detect determines the skill that may apply.

| skill | thing to detect |
|---|---|
| insight | a humanoid's state of mind |
| perception | a hidden creature or object |
| survival | tracks, food, or a beast's state of mind |

### Stabilize

Choose a Downed creature within your reach and make a DC 10 Medicine check. On a success, the creature is Stabilized.

### Study

You make an Intelligence check to discern or recall a piece of information from your memory, a book, or some other source of knowledge. The area of knowledge determines the skill that may apply.

| skill | area of knowledge |
|---|---|
| arcana | spells, magic items, planes, or magical creatures |
| history | ancient civilizations, historic events and people, wars, or long-lived creatures |
| medicine | ailments, anatomy, diseases, or treatments |
| nature | terrain, flora, weather, or natural creatures |
| religion | deities, religious ceremonies, holy symbols, cults, celestial and fiendish creatures |

### Utilize

You interact with one nonmagical object or environmental effect.

## Bonus Actions

You can only use a Bonus Action when a spell or feature lets you. You can also use a Main Action to take a Bonus Action, though you cannot take the same Bonus Action more than once per turn.

## Object Actions

You can interact with one nonmagical object or environmental effect that doesn't require the Utilize Action, such as unlocking a door, picking up an item, or handing an item to another creature.

## Reactions

Reactions are actions taken in response to a triggering event. Each spell or feature that uses a reaction specifies the triggering event that allows you to use it.

Unlike other actions, you can use your reaction on any creature's turn. You can only take one reaction until the start of your next turn.

If your reaction interrupts another creature's turn, their turn resumes immediately after the reaction.
