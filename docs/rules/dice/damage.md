# Damage Rolls
A **Damage Roll** determines how much damage an effect deals.
{ .subtitle }

### Making a Damage Roll
#### 1. Determine modifiers.
Abilities, Resistance, Vulnerability and other effects can add bonuses, penalties, Advantage, or Disadvantage to the Damage Roll.

#### 2. Apply the damage.
Roll the Damage Dice and add the modifiers.  You reduce the target's Hit Points by the total _(minimum **0**)_.

### Damage Types
Every instance of damage has a **Damage Type**.  Damage Types have no rules of their own, but many other rules apply to a Damage Type or to its category. The Damage Types fall into three categories:

#### Physical Damage
| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Bludgeoning | blunt objects, constriction, falling |
| Piercing | fangs, puncturing objects |
| Slashing | claws, cutting objects |

#### Elemental Damage
| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Acid | corrosive liquids, digestive enzymes |
| Cold | freezing water, icy blasts |
| Fire | flames, unbearable heat |
| Lightning | electricity |
| Poison | toxic gas, venom |
| Thunder | concussive sound |

#### Esoteric Damage
| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Force | pure magical energy |
| Necrotic | life-draining energy |
| Psychic | mind-rending energy |
| Radiant | holy energy, searing radiation |

#### Additional Damage
Some effects add damage to another source. If such an effect doesn't state a Damage Type, the additional damage uses the type of the source it adds to. When that source has more than one Damage Type, you choose which of those types the additional damage uses.
