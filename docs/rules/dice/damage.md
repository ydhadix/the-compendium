# Damage Rolls

When an effect deals damage, you make a Damage Roll using the Damage Dice its source describes. Every instance of damage has a Damage Type.

Damage Types have no rules of their own, but many other rules — Resistance, Vulnerability, Immunity, and the like — apply to a Damage Type or to its category. The Damage Types fall into three categories.

## Physical Damage

| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Bludgeoning | blunt objects, constriction, falling |
| Piercing | fangs, puncturing objects |
| Slashing | claws, cutting objects |

## Elemental Damage

| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Acid | corrosive liquids, digestive enzymes |
| Cold | freezing water, icy blasts |
| Fire | flames, unbearable heat |
| Lightning | electricity |
| Poison | toxic gas, venom |
| Thunder | concussive sound |

## Esoteric Damage

| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Force | pure magical energy |
| Necrotic | life-draining energy |
| Psychic | mind-rending energy |
| Radiant | holy energy, searing radiation |

## Special Damage

Falling and Nonlethal aren't true Damage Types, but Defenses still apply to them. Nonlethal is a modifier that can apply to any form of damage.
