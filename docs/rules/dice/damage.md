# Damage Rolls
A Damage Roll measures the harm an attack or effect deals. Its Damage Type decides which defenses can protect against it.
{ .subtitle }

When an effect deals damage, you make a Damage Roll using the Damage Dice its source describes.

## Making a Damage Roll
1. **Determine modifiers.** Abilities, Resistance, Vulnerability and other effects can add bonuses, penalties, Advantage, or Disadvantage to the Damage Roll.
2. **Apply the damage.** Roll the Damage Dice and add modifiers to the result.  The target of the damage reduces their Hit Points by the total _(minimum **0**)_.

## Damage Types
Every instance of damage has a Damage Type.  Damage Types have no rules of their own, but many other rules — Resistance, Vulnerability, Immunity, and the like — apply to a Damage Type or to its category. The Damage Types fall into three categories.

### Physical Damage
| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Bludgeoning | blunt objects, constriction, falling |
| Piercing | fangs, puncturing objects |
| Slashing | claws, cutting objects |

### Elemental Damage
| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Acid | corrosive liquids, digestive enzymes |
| Cold | freezing water, icy blasts |
| Fire | flames, unbearable heat |
| Lightning | electricity |
| Poison | toxic gas, venom |
| Thunder | concussive sound |

### Esoteric Damage
| Damage Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Force | pure magical energy |
| Necrotic | life-draining energy |
| Psychic | mind-rending energy |
| Radiant | holy energy, searing radiation |
