# Attack Rolls
When you make an attack, an Attack Roll determines whether you hit.
{ .subtitle }

To hit, your total must equal or exceed the target's [Armor Class](../defense/armor-class.md).

Every attack is a Weapon or Spell attack, and either Melee or Ranged. These qualities set the attack's Range and the Ability you add to its Attack Roll and Damage Roll.

| Attack | Range | Attack Ability | Damage Ability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Melee Weapon | your Reach | Strength | Strength |
| Ranged Weapon | the weapon's range | Dexterity | Dexterity |
| Melee Spell | your Reach | Spellcasting Ability | — |
| Ranged Spell | the effect's Range | Spellcasting Ability | — |

## Making an Attack
1. **Choose a target** within your attack's Range.
2. **Determine modifiers.** Your Ability, Proficiency, Cover, and other effects can add bonuses, penalties, Advantage, or Disadvantage.
3. **Resolve the attack.** Roll as with any D20 Test. On a hit, you usually make a Damage Roll; the weapon, Spell, or feature you used describes the dice and the Damage Type.

## Ranged Attacks
A ranged attack lists its range in feet. The first number is the Normal Range; a second number, if any, is the Long Range. An attack against a target beyond Normal Range but within Long Range is made with Disadvantage, and you can't hit a target beyond Long Range.

#### Melee Penalty
While you are within a hostile creature's Reach, your Ranged Attacks have Disadvantage.
