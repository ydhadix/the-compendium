### D20 Tests
The three main dice rolls are the Ability Check, the Attack Roll, and the Saving Throw — collectively the D20 Test.  Most D20 Tests add an Ability and a Proficiency to the d20 to determine the total; the situation determines which Ability and Proficiency apply.

Whenever you make a D20 Test, you can choose to fail before you roll.

#### Rolling a 20 or a 1
If you roll a **20** on the d20, the Test succeeds automatically. If you roll a **1**, it fails automatically.

Ability Checks are an exception: some you can never fail, and others you can never succeed, regardless of your bonus.

| D20 Test | Natural 20 | Natural 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Ability Check | compare to DC | compare to DC |
| Attack Roll | Critical Hit | miss |
| Saving Throw | success | fail |

#### Ties Go to the Player
A D20 Test's total is compared to the target's Armor Class or Difficulty Class. On an exact tie, the defender wins — except that a player always wins a tie against the DM. The defender is the creature or effect being attacked or resisting the Test.

| Participants | Winner |
|---|---|
| player vs. DM | player |
| player vs. player | defender |
| DM vs. DM | defender |

### Advantage / Disadvantage
A Dice Roll can have Advantage or Disadvantage, reflecting a favorable or unfavorable circumstance.  Either way, roll the dice twice:

- with Advantage, use the higher total.
- with Disadvantage, use the lower total.

#### Stacking Alone
Multiple sources of Advantage or Disadvantage don't stack — however many you have, you still roll only twice.

#### Stacking Together
A roll can't have both Advantage and Disadvantage. If a roll would have both, count the effects granting each.  The roll keeps whichever side has more, and loses the other.  If the counts are equal, the roll has neither Advantage nor Disadvantage. 

### Rerolls
A Reroll lets you roll a die again when an effect allows it.  When you reroll a die, the new result replaces the old result.

#### Rerolling with Advantage / Disadvantage
When a d20 roll has Advantage or Disadvantage and an effect lets you reroll or replace the roll, you choose one of the d20s to replace, not both.
