# Dice Rolls

## Making a Dice Roll

#### Difficulty Class
Ability Checks and Saving Throws use a **Difficulty Class** to determine success.  The Difficulty Class is set by the effect that required the dice roll, or by the Dungeon Master.

Attack Rolls use the target's Armor Class to determine success instead.

#### Choosing to Fail
You can choose to fail an Ability Check or a Saving Throw, or to be hit by an Attack.

### Advantage / Disadvantage
You gain **Advantage** or **Disadvantage** on a Dice Roll when a circumstance favors or hinders you.  Either way, roll the dice twice:

- with Advantage, use the higher total.
- with Disadvantage, use the lower total.

You can only gain Advantage or Disadvantage before the dice are rolled.  Gaining either after the dice are rolled has no effect.

#### Stacking Advantage
No matter how many times you gain Advantage or Disadvantage on a Dice Roll, you still roll only twice.

#### Stacking Together
You can't have both Advantage and Disadvantage on a Dice Roll.  If you would have both, count how many times you gained each.  You keep whichever you gained more, and lose the other.  If the counts are equal, you have neither on that roll.

### Rerolls
A **Reroll** lets you roll a die again, replacing the old result.

#### Rerolling with Advantage / Disadvantage
Advantage and Disadvantage resolve as soon as the dice are rolled, leaving the roll with a single d20.  A later reroll or replacement acts on that die alone, and can itself have Advantage or Disadvantage if the effect granting it says so.

### Proficiency
**Proficiency** measures how much a creature's training improves its rolls.  Each Proficiency has a tier that sets how much of your Proficiency Bonus applies to it.

| Tier | Proficiency Bonus |
|---|---|
| Aptitude | half, rounded up |
| Proficiency | full |
| Expertise | double |

#### Proficiency Bonus
When you make a roll you're Proficient with, you add your **Proficiency Bonus** to it.  Your Proficiency Bonus is determined by your Level.

#### Applying Proficiency
Most Proficiencies add your Proficiency Bonus to a related roll.  

| Proficiency | Adds your Bonus to |
|---|---|
| Skill | Ability Checks that use the Skill |
| Trade | Ability Checks that use the Trade |
| Saving Throw | Saving Throws of that type |
| Weapon | Attack Rolls with the Weapon |
| Armor | Armor Class |
| Shield | — |

###### Weapons
Proficiency with a Weapon is tied to its specific type, or a broader category: Simple or Martial, and Melee or Ranged.

###### Armor
Proficiency with Armor is tied to its weight: Light, Medium, or Heavy.  A Shield uses the Armor Proficiency of its weight.

Unlike other Proficiencies, Armor Proficiency adds your Proficiency Bonus to your Armor Class rather than to a roll.  Wearing Armor or wielding a Shield you aren't Proficient with grants no benefit, but carries no penalty.

#### Stacking Proficiencies
You can apply only one Proficiency to a single roll; if an Ability Check uses both a Skill and a Trade you're Proficient with, you gain Advantage on it instead.
