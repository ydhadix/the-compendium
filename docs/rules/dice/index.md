# Dice Rolls

## Abilities
Six **Abilities** measure a creature's raw physical and mental capabilities.  Each Ability is a number from **-5** to **+10** you add to the rolls and traits it governs.

| Ability | Description | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Physical might | Melee Attacks, Heavy Armor, Damage Reduction, Jump Distance |
| Dexterity | Accuracy, agility, and balance | Ranged Attacks, Light Armor, Initiative, Reflex Saves |
| Constitution | Health and stamina | Hit Points, Fortitude Saves, Concentration |
| Intelligence | Reasoning, learning, and memory | Spellcasting, Studying, additional Proficiencies |
| Wisdom | Awareness, empathy, and mental resilience | Spellcasting, Will Saves, Searching |
| Charisma | Confidence, poise, and charm | Spellcasting, Socializing |

# Proficiency
**Proficiency** measures how much a creature's training improves its rolls.

## Proficiency Bonus
When you make a roll you're Proficient with, you add your **Proficiency Bonus** to it.  Your Proficiency Bonus is determined by your Level.

### Degrees of Proficiency
Each Proficiency has a tier that sets how much of your Proficiency Bonus applies to it.

| Tier | Proficiency Bonus |
|---|---|
| Aptitude | half, rounded up |
| Proficiency | full |
| Expertise | double |

### Stacking Proficiencies
If you gain a Proficiency you already chose from another feature, you can change that earlier choice to a different Proficiency.

### Applying Proficiency
Most Proficiencies add your Proficiency Bonus to a related roll.  You can apply only one Proficiency to a single roll; if an Ability Check uses both a Skill and a Trade you're Proficient with, you gain Advantage on it instead.

| Proficiency | Adds your Bonus to |
|---|---|
| Skill | Ability Checks that use the Skill |
| Trade | Ability Checks that use the Trade |
| Saving Throw | Saving Throws of that type |
| Weapon | Attack Rolls with the Weapon |
| Armor | Armor Class |
| Shield | — |

#### Weapons
Proficiency with a Weapon is tied to its specific type, or a broader category: Simple or Martial, and Melee or Ranged.

#### Armor
Proficiency with Armor is tied to its weight: Light, Medium, or Heavy.  A Shield uses the Armor Proficiency of its weight.

Unlike other Proficiencies, Armor Proficiency adds your Proficiency Bonus to your Armor Class rather than to a roll.  Wearing Armor or wielding a Shield you aren't Proficient with grants no benefit, but carries no penalty.

## Difficulty Class
Ability Checks and Saving Throws use a **Difficulty Class** to determine success.  The Difficulty Class is set by the effect that required the dice roll, or by the Dungeon Master.

Attack Rolls use the target's Armor Class to determine success instead.

## Advantage / Disadvantage
A Dice Roll can have **Advantage** or **Disadvantage**, reflecting a favorable or unfavorable circumstance.  Either way, roll the dice twice:

- with Advantage, use the higher total.
- with Disadvantage, use the lower total.

Advantage and Disadvantage can only be gained before the dice are rolled.  Gaining either after the dice are rolled has no effect.

#### Stacking Advantage
No matter how many times a dice roll gains Advantage or Disadvantage, you still roll only twice.

#### Stacking Together
A dice roll can't have both Advantage and Disadvantage.  If a dice roll would have both, count how many times it gained each.  The dice roll keeps whichever it gained more, and loses the other.  If the counts are equal, the roll has neither Advantage nor Disadvantage. 

## Rerolls
A **Reroll** lets you roll a die again, replacing the old result.

#### Rerolling with Advantage / Disadvantage
When a d20 roll has Advantage or Disadvantage and an effect lets you reroll or replace the roll, you choose one of the d20s to replace, not both.
