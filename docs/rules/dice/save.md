# Saving Throws

A Saving Throw represents your attempt to resist a Spell, trap, poison, or other harmful effect. You rarely choose to make a Save — an effect calls for one because you are at risk of harm. To succeed, your total must equal or exceed the DC set by that effect.

## Core Saves

Most Saves are Fortitude, Reflex, or Will Saves. Your Primary Class determines which of these you have Proficiency in.

| Save | Ability |
|---|---|
| Fortitude | Constitution |
| Reflex | Dexterity |
| Will | Wisdom |

## Concentration Saves

A Concentration Save determines whether you keep Concentration when you take damage. See [Concentration](../../spell/part/concentration.md).

## Death Saves

A Death Save determines your fate while you are dying. See the [Downed](../condition/index.md) condition.
