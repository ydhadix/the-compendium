# Saving Throws
A Saving Throw represents your attempt to resist a Spell, trap, poison, or other harmful effect.
{ .subtitle }

You rarely choose to make a Save — an effect calls for one because you are at risk of harm. To succeed, your total must equal or exceed the DC set by that effect.

## Core Saves
Most Saves are Fortitude, Reflex, or Will Saves. Your Primary Class determines which of these you have Proficiency in.

| Save | Ability |
|---|---|
| Fortitude | Constitution |
| Reflex | Dexterity |
| Will | Wisdom |

## Concentration Saves
A Concentration Save determines whether you keep [Concentration](../../spell/part/concentration.md) when you take damage.

## Death Saves
A Death Save determines your fate while you are [Downed](../condition/index.md).
