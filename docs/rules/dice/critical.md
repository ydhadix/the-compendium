# Critical Hits
You score a Critical Hit when you roll a **20** on the d20 for an Attack Roll.
{ .subtitle }

## NPC Critical Hits
For an unnamed NPC, a Critical Hit maximizes the attack's Damage Roll: each Damage Die is treated as rolling its highest value.

## Player Critical Hits
For a named NPC or a player character, a Critical Hit can be more devastating. You confirm the Critical Hit by rolling an additional d20, then apply an extra effect based on the Damage Type the attack would deal — choosing one if it could deal several — and the result of that d20.

Some Damage Types are more effective against certain creature types and armors; if you choose a Damage Type that is strong against the target, you have Advantage on the confirmation.
