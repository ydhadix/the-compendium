# Ability Checks

When you attempt something with a chance of failure other than an attack, you make an Ability Check against a Difficulty Class to determine the outcome. To succeed, your total must equal or exceed the DC.

The DM sets the Difficulty Class, or it comes from a contested Ability Check.

## Skills
Most Ability Checks involve a [Skills](../trait/skill.md) — an area of training with a default Ability.  You add your Proficiency Bonus to a Skill Check when you have Proficiency in that Skill.

## Trades
Trades are specialized Proficiencies that let you do something you otherwise couldn't, such as repairing an Item or playing music. If an Ability Check uses both a Skill and a Trade you're Proficient with, you have Advantage on the Check.

## Initiative
When several creatures act at once, an [Initiative](../encounter/initiative.md) Check sets the order.

## Passive Checks
A passive Check represents the average result of a repeated task, or lets the DM secretly resolve a Check. A passive Check doesn't require a roll: your total is **10** plus every bonus that normally applies to the Check. Add **5** if you have Advantage on the Check, or subtract **5** if you have Disadvantage.
