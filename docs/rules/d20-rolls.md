# D20 Rolls

The three main dice rolls are ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws, collectively called d20 rolls. Effects that affect d20 rolls affect all three of these rolls. Any time you attempt a d20 roll you can choose to fail before making the roll.

Most d20 rolls add an ability and a proficiency to the roll to determine the total. The situation surrounding the d20 roll determines which ability and proficiency can be used.

## Rolling 20 or 1

When you roll a 20 on the d20, the roll succeeds automatically. Likewise, if you roll a 1 on the d20 the roll fails automatically.

Ability checks are an exception. There are some ability checks that you can never fail, and others that you can never succeed regardless of your bonus to the check.

| d20 roll | natural 20 | natural 1 |
|---|---|---|
| ability check | compare to dc | compare to dc |
| attack roll | critical hit | miss |
| saving throw | success | fail |

## Ties Go to the Player

A D20 roll succeeds when the total exceeds the target Armor Class or Difficulty Class. However, if the roll is a tie, the result depends on whether a player or the DM controls the involved creatures and effects.

The defender of a d20 roll is the creature or effect that is being attacked or resisting an ability check or saving throw.

| participants | result |
|---|---|
| player vs. dm | the player succeeds |
| player vs. player | the defender succeeds |
| dm vs. dm | the defender succeeds |

## Ability Checks

When you do something that has a chance of failure, other than attack, you attempt an ability check against the check's difficulty class to determine the outcome.

### Difficulty Class (DC)

The difficulty class of an ability check is determined by the DM or another creature's contested ability check. To succeed, your total check must meet or exceed the DC of the challenge you're facing.

### Skills

Most ability checks involve an area of instruction called a skill. Each skill is associated with a specific ability, but circumstances can pair a skill with a different ability, such as using your strength to intimidate a creature.

| ability | skills |
|---|---|
| strength | athletics |
| dexterity | acrobatics, precision, stealth |
| constitution | — |
| intelligence | arcana, history, medicine, nature, religion |
| wisdom | insight, perception, survival |
| charisma | deception, intimidation, performance, persuasion |

### Trades

Trades are specialized proficiencies that help you do something you couldn't otherwise do, such as repair an item or play music.

If an ability check uses both a skill and a trade you're proficient with, you also have advantage on the check.

### Initiative Checks

When multiple creatures want to act at the same time, Initiative checks decide who acts first. See the Initiative section for more information on Initiative.

### Passive Ability Checks

Passive ability checks represent the average result of a repeated task or can be used when the DM needs to secretly determine the result of an ability check.

Passive checks don't require a dice roll. Your total for a passive check is 10 + all bonuses that normally apply to the check. If you have advantage on the check, add 5, and if you have disadvantage on the check, subtract 5.

## Attack Rolls

When you make an attack, an attack roll is what determines whether you hit your target.

### Armor Class (AC)

A creature's armor class represents how well they avoid being wounded in combat. To hit a creature, your attack roll total must meet or exceed the target's AC.

Wearing armor and other effects can give you different ways to calculate your AC. If you have multiple ways to calculate your AC, you can only use one at a time.

### Types of Attacks

Every attack is either a weapon or spell attack, and either melee or ranged. The Range of the attack and the ability you add to its attack and damage rolls are determined by these qualities.

Attacks with weapons and unarmed strikes are usually weapon attacks, while attacks with spells and magical features are usually spell attacks.

| attack | range | attack ability | damage ability |
|---|---|---|---|
| weapon melee | your reach | strength | strength |
| weapon ranged | weapon range | dexterity | dexterity |
| spell melee | your reach | spell ability | — |
| spell ranged | effect range | spell ability | — |

### Making an Attack

The steps to make an attack are the same whether you attack with a weapon, unarmed strike, spell, or other feature.

#### Choose a Target

Choose a creature or object within your attack's Range.

#### Determine Modifiers

Your attack's Ability, your Proficiency, Cover, Spells, Items, and other features can give bonuses, penalties, Advantage, or Disadvantage to the attack roll.

#### Resolve the Attack

Attack rolls are rolled the same as other D20 Rolls.

On a hit, you usually roll damage, but some attacks have special effects in addition to or instead of damage. The weapon, spell, or feature you use to make the attack describes the dice you roll to determine the total and type of damage dealt.

### Ranged Attacks

The weapon, spell, or feature used to make a ranged attack describes its Range in feet. The first given number is the attack's Normal Range. If the Range has a second number, that is the attack's Long Range.

Attacks against a target beyond Normal Range, but within Long Range, are made with Disadvantage.

You can't hit a target beyond the attack's Long Range.

While you're within a hostile creature's Reach, your ranged attacks have disadvantage.

## Saving Throws

Saving throws represent your attempt to resist a spell, trap, poison, or other effect. You usually don't choose to attempt a save but are forced to because you are at risk of harm.

### Difficulty Class (DC)

The difficulty class of a save is determined by the effect that causes it. To succeed, your total save must meet or exceed the DC of the effect you're resisting.

### Core Saves

Most saves are fortitude, reflex, or will saves. Your primary class determines which saves you have proficiency with.

| saving throw | ability |
|---|---|
| fortitude | constitution |
| reflex | dexterity |
| will | wisdom |

### Concentration Saves

Concentration saves determine whether you lose concentration when you take damage (see the Concentration section for more information on concentration saves).

### Death Saves

Death saves determine your fate when you are dying (see the Downed condition for more information on death saves).
