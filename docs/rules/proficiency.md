# Proficiency

## Degrees of Proficiency

There are three degrees of proficiency that determine how you apply your proficiency bonus.

| degree | proficiency bonus |
|---|---|
| ½ proficiency | half, rounded up |
| proficiency | full |
| expertise | double |

### Stacking Proficiencies

If you gain proficiency in a skill or trade that you already chose to gain from another feature, you can change that choice to a different proficiency.

## Proficiency Bonus

Your proficiency bonus is based on your character level.

| character level | proficiency bonus |
|---|---|
| 1 – 4 | +2 |
| 5 – 8 | +3 |
| 9 – 12 | +4 |
| 13 – 16 | +5 |
| 17 – 20 | +6 |
