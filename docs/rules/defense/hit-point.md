# Hit Points
Your Hit Points measure how much harm you can take before you fall.
{ .subtitle }

They are an abstraction of your health: losing Hit Points doesn't always mean a wound, but can also represent spent luck, stamina, or resolve. An attack that costs you Hit Points might be a solid blow or a narrow dodge that shakes your nerve. This framing doesn't change the mechanics — rider effects still trigger no matter how the loss is described — but it explains how a seasoned hero survives blows that would kill an ordinary person many times over.

When you take damage, you lose that many Hit Points; when you heal, you regain them, up to your Hit Point Maximum. At **0** Hit Points, you are Downed.

## Hit Dice
Your Hit Dice are a reserve of latent health. You can't draw on them freely — only certain effects let you spend or restore them, most often [Resting](../rest/index.md).
