# Conditions

## Bleeding

### Bleed Die

The effect that caused this condition includes a Bleed Die.

At the start of your turn, you take one roll of the Bleed Die as Slashing damage. Each time you take this damage, the number of Bleed Die you roll increases by 1.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends when you heal, or if a creature successfully uses the Stabilize action on you.

## Blinded

### Can't See

You can't see and automatically fail checks that require sight.

### Defenseless

Attacks against you have advantage.

### Inaccurate

Your attacks have disadvantage.

## Burning

### Burn Die

The effect that caused this condition includes a Burn Die.

At the start of your turn, you take one roll of the Burn Die as Fire damage.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends after you spend 15 feet of movement while Prone, or if the fire is doused, submerged, or suffocated.

## Charmed

### False Friends

You can't target the source of this condition with damaging attacks, features, or spells.

### Enamored

The source of this condition has advantage on checks to socially interact with you.

## Corroded

### Caustic Die

The effect that caused this condition includes a Caustic Die.

At the start of your turn, you take one roll of the Caustic Die as Acid damage.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends after 1 minute, or if you are doused or submerged in clean water or a neutralizing substance, such as baking soda.

## Dazed

### Limited Action

During your turn, you can either move or take an action, not both. You must use a Main Action to take a Bonus Action, and you can't take Reactions.

## Deafened

### Can't Hear

You can't hear and automatically fail checks that require hearing.

## Downed

### Rattled

You are Dazed.

Before you take an action, you make a DC 11 Fortitude save.

**fail**: Your action has no effect.

### Prone

You are Prone. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.

### Limp

You drop anything you're holding.

### At Death's Door

At the start of your turn, you make a Death save. Track your Death save results until this condition ends.

After you fail three Death saves, you die.

After you succeed on three Death saves, you are Stabilized.

### Hemorrhaging

After you take damage, you fail a Death save. If the damage is from a Critical Hit, you fail two Death saves instead.

### Death Saves

Death saves are DC 11. You don't add an ability to the save, and most creatures don't have proficiency with death saves — for most creatures, a death save is a coin flip.

If you roll a 1 on the d20 for a death save, the failure counts as two failed death saves.

If you roll a 20 on the d20 for a death save, you immediately heal 1 hit point.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends if you become Stabilized or have more than 0 HP.

## Exhausted

### Levels of Exhaustion

Exhaustion is tracked in levels. Whenever you become Exhausted, you gain one exhaustion level.

You die if your exhaustion level is six or more.

### Weakened

Before you make a D20 Roll, reduce the total by twice your exhaustion level.

### Slowed

Your Speed is reduced by five times your exhaustion level.

### Ending the Condition

After you finish a Long Rest, you remove one exhaustion level. This condition ends when you have no exhaustion levels.

## Frightened

### Panicked

While you can see the source of this condition, your attacks and checks have Disadvantage.

### Freeze

You can't willingly move closer to the source of this condition.

## Grappled

### Immobile

You are Immobile.

### Tunnel Vision

When you attack a target other than the grappler, your attack has Disadvantage.

### Movable

The grappler can drag or carry you when it moves, but every foot of movement costs it 1 extra foot unless you are Tiny or two or more sizes smaller than it.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends when you leave the grappler's reach.

## Hidden

### Hide DC

The effect that caused this condition includes a Hide DC.

A creature whose Passive Perception is higher than your Hide DC ignores this condition.

### Concealed

Other creatures are unaware of your position.

### Surprise

You have Advantage on Initiative checks.

### Accurate

Your attacks have Advantage.

### Evasive

Attacks against you have Disadvantage.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends after you attack or when a hostile creature sees you or takes the Search action to find you and the total is higher than your Hide DC.

## Immobile

### Stationary

Your Speed is 0 and can't change.

### Unbalanced

Your Reflex saves have Disadvantage.

## Incapacitated

### Inactive

You can't take any actions.

### No Concentration

Your Concentration is broken.

### Speechless

You can't speak.

## Invisible

### Imperceptible

You and items you are wearing and carrying cannot be seen.

### Accurate

Your attacks have Advantage.

### Evasive

Attacks against you have Disadvantage.

## Paralyzed

### Incapacitated

You are Incapacitated.

### Restrained

You are Restrained.

### Helpless

Before you are hit by an attack while the attacker is within 5 feet, the attack becomes a Critical Hit.

## Petrified

### Incapacitated

You are Incapacitated.

### Restrained

You are Restrained.

### Unaware

You are Blinded, Deafened, and unaware of your surroundings.

### Resistant

You have Resistance to all damage.

### Suspended

You and nonmagical objects you are wearing or carrying are transformed into a solid, inanimate substance (usually stone). Your weight is multiplied by 10 and you cease aging.

## Poisoned

### Inaccurate

Your attacks have Disadvantage.

### Intoxicated

Your ability checks have Disadvantage.

## Prone

### Crawling

Every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot.

### Sprawled

When you are attacked while the attacker is within 5 feet, the attack has Advantage. Otherwise, the attack has Disadvantage.

### Inaccurate

Your attacks have Disadvantage.

### Unbalanced

Your Reflex saves have Disadvantage.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends after you use half your movement to stand up. Before you stand up, each creature that can reach you can use their Reaction to make an Opportunity Attack against you.

## Restrained

### Immobile

You are Immobile.

### Lagging

You automatically fail Reflex saves.

### Inaccurate

Your attacks have Disadvantage.

### Defenseless

Attacks against you have Advantage.

## Stabilized

### Rattled

You are Dazed.

Before you take an action, you make a DC 11 Fortitude save.

**fail**: Your action has no effect.

### Natural Recovery

After 1d4 hours have passed, you heal 1 HP.

### Flatlining

After you take damage, you become Downed. If the damage is from a Critical Hit, you also fail a Death save.

### Ending the Condition

This condition ends if you become Downed or have more than 0 HP.

## Stunned

### Incapacitated

You are Incapacitated.

### Restrained

You are Restrained.

## Unconscious

### Incapacitated

You are Incapacitated.

### Restrained

You are Restrained.

### Helpless

Before you are hit by an attack while the attacker is within 5 feet, the attack becomes a Critical Hit.

### Unaware

You are Blinded, Deafened, and unaware of your surroundings.

### Prone

You are Prone. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.

### Limp

You drop anything you're holding.
