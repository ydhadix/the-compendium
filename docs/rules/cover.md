# Cover

Cover makes you more difficult to harm if the harmful effect comes from the opposite side of the cover.

There are three levels of cover. If you are behind multiple sources of cover, only the strongest cover applies.

| cover | effect |
|---|---|
| half | you add +2 to your armor class and reflex saves. |
| three-quarters | you add +5 to your armor class and reflex saves. |
| full | you can't be seen or targeted directly by attacks and spells. you automatically succeed on reflex saves. |
