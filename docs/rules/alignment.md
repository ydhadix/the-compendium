# Alignment

Your alignment broadly describes your ethical attitudes and ideals.

| alignment | description |
|---|---|
| LG | Lawful Good creatures endeavor to do the right thing as expected by society. |
| NG | Neutral Good creatures do the best they can, working within rules but not feeling bound by them. |
| CG | Chaotic Good creatures act as their conscience directs with little regard for what others expect. |
| LN | Lawful Neutral creatures act in accordance with law, tradition, or personal codes and aren't swayed by the demands of those in need or the temptations of evil. |
| TN | True Neutral creatures prefer to avoid moral questions and don't take sides, doing what seems best at the time. |
| CN | Chaotic Neutral creatures follow their whims, valuing their personal freedom above all else. |
| LE | Lawful Evil creatures methodically take what they want within the limits of a code of tradition, loyalty, or order. |
| NE | Neutral Evil creatures are untroubled by the harm they cause as they pursue their desires. |
| CE | Chaotic Evil creatures act with arbitrary violence, spurred by their hatred or bloodlust. |
