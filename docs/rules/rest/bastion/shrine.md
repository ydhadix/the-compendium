#### Word of Cleansing
When a creature who meets this facility's Prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Gift of _Healing Word_.  The Gift disappears after **10** days, after you use it, or when you gain another Gift from this feature.

##### Gift of Healing Word
Boon
{ .subtitle }

This gift functions as a Spell Scroll of _Healing Word_.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Size | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Chapel | Roomy | The facility gains one Hireling and its Gift can instead be a Gift of _Prayer of Healing_. |
