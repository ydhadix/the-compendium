#### Glyph of Revelation
When a creature who meets this facility's Prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Gift of _Identify_. The Gift functions as a Spell Scroll of _Identify_ and disappears after **10** days, after you use it, or when you gain another Gift from this feature.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|
| Laboratory | 2,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Gift can instead be a Gift of _Magic Aura_. |
| Thaumatorium | 7,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Gift can instead be a Gift of _Dispel Magic_. |
