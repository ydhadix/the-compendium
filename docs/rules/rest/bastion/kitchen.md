#### Hearty Meals
When a creature finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, its Exhaustion is reduced by **1**, in addition to the Long Rest's normal Exhaustion reduction.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cafeteria | 2,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Hearty Meals also increases a creature's Hit Point Maximum by your Character Level for **24** hours. The creature gains this benefit even if it has no Exhaustion to reduce. |
| Comissary | 7,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Hearty Meals also grants Exhaustion Protection and Fear Protection for **24** hours. |
