#### Fruit of Restoration
When a creature who meets this facility's Prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Gift of _Goodberry_.  The Gift disappears after **10** days, after you use it, or when you gain another Gift from this feature.

##### Gift of Goodberry
Boon
{ .subtitle }

This gift functions as a Spell Scroll of _Goodberry_.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Size | Effect |
|---|---|---|
