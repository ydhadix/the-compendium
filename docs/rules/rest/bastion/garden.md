#### Fruit of Restoration
When a creature who meets this facility's Prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Gift of _Goodberry_. The Gift functions as a Spell Scroll of _Goodberry_ and disappears after **10** days, after you use it, or when you gain another Gift from this feature.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Greenhouse | 2,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Gift can instead be a Gift of _Lesser Restoration_. |
| Grove | 7,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Gift can instead be a Gift of _Create Food and Water_. |
