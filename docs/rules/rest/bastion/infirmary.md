#### Preventative Care
When a creature finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, one Sickness affecting it ends, and its Hit Die maximum increases by **1** for **24** hours.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic | 2,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Roomy, it gains one Hireling, and its Preventative Care also gives one additional Hit Die and maximizes all healing from Hit Dice for **24** hours. |
| Hospital | 7,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Vast, it gains one Hireling, and its Preventative Care also gives one additional Hit Die and grants Death Protection and Poison Resistance for **24** hours. |
