### Training Yard

| | |
|---|---|
| Prerequisite | Fighting Style, Unarmored Form, or Weapon Mastery |
| Size | Cramped |
| Hirelings | 1 |
| Tools | — |
| Skills | Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Performance |

#### Practiced Form
When a creature who meets this facility's Prerequisite finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, they gain a Gift of Training. The Gift grants Proficiency in one of this Facility's Skills, a Martial weapon category, or a Weapon Mastery, and disappears after **10** days or when you gain another Gift from this feature.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Arena | 2,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Roomy, and its Practiced Form can instead grant a Gift of Fighting Styles, which grants a Fighting Style Feat. |
| Gymnasium | 7,000 gold | The facility's Size becomes Vast, and its Practiced Form can instead grant a Gift of Improved Critical, which expands your Physical Attacks' Critical Hit range by **1**. |
