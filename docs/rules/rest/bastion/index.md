# Bastions
A Bastion is a location or structure you own, such as a stronghold or a sailing ship.
{ .subtitle }

A Bastion offers refuge from the dangers of adventuring and a place to conduct research, craft Items, and more.

## Gaining a Bastion
Bastions are awarded and expanded per character. You typically receive your Bastion around Character Level **5**.

Multiple Bastions can be combined into shared structures, or kept independent from one another.

You, the rest of your party, and the DM decide how your Bastion comes into your possession. Its shape, style, and function are yours to decide.

## Facilities
Every Bastion has Facilities: Basic Facilities that serve everyday needs, and Special Facilities for unique tasks.

### Facility Space
A Facility's Size sets its maximum area in **5**-foot squares. That area can spread across multiple levels, as long as they are all connected.

| Size | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Cramped | 2 squares | 4 squares |
| Roomy | 4 squares | 16 squares |
| Vast | 16 squares | 36 squares |

## Basic Facilities
A Basic Facility has no game effects, but makes a Bastion more comfortable to inhabit. A Basic Facility can resemble a Special Facility, but grants none of its mechanical benefits.

Example Basic Facilities include a barracks, bedroom, courtyard, dining room, dormitory, office, pub, or storage.

### Adding Basic Facilities
Each Bastion starts with two Basic Facilities: one Cramped and one Roomy. You can add new Basic Facilities or enlarge existing ones by spending money and time, though you don't need to be present while they are built.

Enlarging a Basic Facility costs the difference between its current Size and its new Size.

| Size | Cost |
|---|---|
| Cramped | 500 gold |
| Roomy | 1,000 gold |
| Vast | 3,000 gold |

## Special Facilities
A Special Facility is a Bastion location where certain activities yield mechanical benefits.

### Adding Special Facilities
Each Bastion starts with two Special Facilities of your choice. Unlike Basic Facilities, Special Facilities can't be bought; you gain more at certain Character Levels. When you gain a level, you can change one of your Special Facilities.

| Character Level | Special Facilities |
|---|---|
| 5 | 2 |
| 9 | 3 |
| 13 | 4 |
| 17 | 5 |

### Hirelings
Every Special Facility comes with at least one Hireling who works in and maintains it. A Hireling is proficient with any Tools or Skills tied to that Facility and can help with checks or crafting.

## Facility List
| Facility | Benefit |
|---|---|
| [Arcanum](#arcanum) | Grants a Gift of _Identify_ after a Long Rest. |
| [Garden](#garden) | Grants a Gift of _Goodberry_ after a Long Rest. |
| [Infirmary](#infirmary) | Ends a Sickness and raises Hit Die maximum after a Long Rest. |
| [Kitchen](#kitchen) | Reduces Exhaustion after a Long Rest. |
| [Outfitter](#outfitter) | A shop for Weapons and Armor. |
| [Shrine](#shrine) | Grants a Gift of _Healing Word_ after a Long Rest. |
| [Storehouse](#storehouse) | A shop for Adventuring Gear. |
| [Study](#study) | Hirelings help craft with Calligrapher's Supplies. |
| [Training Yard](#training-yard) | Grants a Gift of Training after a Long Rest. |
| [Workshop](#workshop) | Hirelings help craft with the Facility's Tools. |
