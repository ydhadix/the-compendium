## Unconscious

### Incapacitated

You are Incapacitated.

### Restrained

You are Restrained.

### Helpless

Before you are hit by an attack while the attacker is within 5 feet, the attack becomes a Critical Hit.

### Unaware

You are Blinded, Deafened, and unaware of your surroundings.

### Prone

You are Prone. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.

### Limp

You drop anything you're holding.
