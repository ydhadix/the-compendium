## Petrified

### Incapacitated

You are Incapacitated.

### Restrained

You are Restrained.

### Unaware

You are Blinded, Deafened, and unaware of your surroundings.

### Resistant

You have Resistance to all damage.

### Suspended

You and nonmagical objects you are wearing or carrying are transformed into a solid, inanimate substance (usually stone). Your weight is multiplied by 10 and you cease aging.
