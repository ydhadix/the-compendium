# Companions

Some spells and features provide magical companion creatures that travel with you and aid in combat or exploration.

## Taking Actions

Your companions share your turn in Initiative. They take actions or move only when commanded, and each companion's spell or feature determines how you command that companion.

If you don't issue a command, your companion takes the Dodge action and moves to stay within 10 feet of you and avoid danger, if possible.

If you are Incapacitated, your companions can move and act on their own and aren't limited to the Dodge action (You still control your companion).

Regardless of how many commands you issue, your companions can still only take one of each type of action per turn.

## Companion Levels

Your companions use your Character Level and Proficiency Bonus in their stat blocks.

## Communication

Your companions understand any language you speak. Some companions can also speak those languages and may know additional languages.

While within 120 feet of your companions, you can communicate with them telepathically.
