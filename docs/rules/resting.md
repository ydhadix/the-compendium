# Resting

A rest is a period of downtime where you can heal hit points and regain uses of features. You must start a rest with at least 1 hit point, and do nothing more strenuous than eating, drinking, reading, dozing, or standing watch.

After you finish a rest, you must wait at least twice the rest's normal duration before starting the same kind of rest.

## Short Rests

A short rest takes 1 hour of downtime. When you finish a short rest, you gain the following effects:

| effect | description |
|---|---|
| spend hit dice | you can spend one or more of your hit dice to heal hit points. for each hit die you spend, roll the die and add your constitution. you heal hit points equal to the total. you can decide to spend an additional hit die after each roll. |

## Travel Rests

A travel rest takes 4 hours of downtime. You must sleep for at least 3 hours and perform no more than 1 hour of light activity. When you finish a travel rest, you gain the following effects:

| effect | description |
|---|---|
| short rest | you gain the effect of a short rest. |
| hit dice | you regain up to half your total hit dice. |

## Long Rests

A long rest takes 8 hours of downtime. You must sleep for at least 6 hours and perform no more than 2 hours of light activity. When you finish a long rest, you gain the following effects:

| effect | description |
|---|---|
| heal | you heal all lost hit points. |
| hit dice | you regain all spent hit dice. |
| restored abilities | if your hit point maximum or abilities were reduced, they return to normal. |
| exhaustion | if you are exhausted, your exhaustion level decreases by 1. |

## Interruptions

A rest can be stopped by rolling initiative, casting a spell other than a cantrip, taking damage, 1 hour of walking, or other physical exertion.

You can resume a rest immediately after an interruption, but a travel or long rest requires 1 additional hour to finish each time it is interrupted.

## Sleep Deprivation

Resting isn't mandatory, but going without sleep has its consequences. If you go 24 hours without finishing a travel or long rest, you must succeed on a DC 10 fortitude save or gain 1 level of exhaustion.

Each time you make this save the DC increases by 5. The DC resets to 10 when you finish a long rest.
