##### Study
Make an Intelligence Check to recall or discern a piece of knowledge.

The subject sets the Skill that applies:

| | |
|---|---|
| Arcana | Spells, Magic Items, planes, or magical creatures |
| History | Civilizations, historic events and people, wars, or long-lived creatures |
| Medicine | Ailments, anatomy, diseases, or treatments |
| Nature | Terrain, plants, weather, or natural creatures |
| Religion | Deities, rites, holy symbols, cults, or Celestial and Fiendish creatures |
