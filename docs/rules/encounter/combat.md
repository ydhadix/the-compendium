# Combat
An Encounter always runs in Initiative order, but doesn't always involve fighting. Combat begins after a creature is attacked or targeted by a harmful Spell or effect.
{ .subtitle }

## Attacking
Dealing damage is what most sets Combat apart from other Encounters, and an [Attack](../dice/attack.md) is the most common way to do it.

### Mounted Combat
You can ride any willing creature that has suitable anatomy and is at least one Size Category larger than you.

#### Mounting and Dismounting
Once per Turn, you can spend half your movement to mount or dismount a creature within **5** feet. If your Speed is **0**, you can't mount or dismount.

#### Controlling a Mount
While mounted, your mount shares your place in Initiative order but has its own Actions and movement. You can control your mount or let it act independently. An intelligent mount decides for itself and may refuse to be controlled.

A controlled mount Moves as you direct and can only Dash, Disengage, or Dodge. An independent mount Moves and acts as it wishes, with no restrictions on its Actions.

#### Staying Mounted
If an effect Moves you against your will while you are mounted, you are dismounted.

If an effect Moves your mount against its will, or you are knocked Prone while mounted, you make a Reflex Save _(DC = **10**)_; on a failure you are dismounted and land Prone within **5** feet of your mount, and on a success you stay mounted and don't fall Prone.

If your mount is knocked Prone, you can use your Reaction to dismount safely; otherwise you are dismounted and land Prone within **5** feet of your mount.
