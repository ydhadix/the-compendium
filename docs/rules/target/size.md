# Size
Every creature and object has a **Size** that describes the space it occupies or needs to move and fight effectively.
{ .subtitle }

| Category | Rank | Space (feet) | Space (squares) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | **1** | 2.5 × 2.5 | 4 per square |
| Small | **2** | 5 × 5 | 1 × 1 |
| Medium | **3** | 5 × 5 | 1 × 1 |
| Large | **4** | 10 × 10 | 2 × 2 |
| Huge | **5** | 15 × 15 | 3 × 3 |
| Gargantuan | **6** | 20 × 20 or larger | 4 × 4 or larger |

Each Size has a **Category** that names it and a **Rank** that numbers it.  An effect that raises or lowers a Size moves it that many Ranks, and an effect that compares two creatures measures the difference between their Ranks.  A Size can't rise above Gargantuan or fall below Tiny.
