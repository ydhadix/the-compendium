# Speeds
Your Speed is the maximum distance you can move on your Turn.
{ .subtitle }

## Special Speeds
A Special Speed lets you ignore normal movement restrictions.

If you have more than one Speed, you can switch between them as you Move. The distance you travel using each Speed can't exceed that Speed's maximum, and your total distance in a Turn can't exceed the largest Speed you used that Turn.

### Climb
You can traverse vertical surfaces without spending the extra movement that climbing normally costs.

### Spider Climb
You have a Climb Speed, and you can climb with your hands free and Move along ceilings upside down.

### Fly
You can travel through the air and remain aloft. You fall if you are Incapacitated or Prone, or if your Fly Speed is **0**.

### Hover
You have a Fly Speed, you are immune to being Prone, and you don't fall if you are Incapacitated or your Hover Speed is **0**.

### Swim
You can travel through liquid without spending the extra movement that swimming normally costs.

### Burrow
You can travel through soft ground, such as earth, sand, or mud.
