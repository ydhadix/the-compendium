### Prone

- Each foot you Move costs **1** extra foot.
- Your Attack Rolls and Reflex Saves have Disadvantage.
- An attack against you has Advantage if the attacker is within **5** feet, and Disadvantage otherwise.

#### Ending the Condition
Prone ends after you spend half your Speed to stand up; before you stand, each creature within Reach can use a Reaction to make an Opportunity Attack against you _(those creatures don't need special training to make this Opportunity Attack)_.
