# Concentration

Some effects require Concentration to remain active. These factors can end your Concentration. You can also choose to end your Concentration at any time.

| factor | effect |
|---|---|
| time limit | if the effect has a maximum duration, your concentration ends when that time has passed. |
| two concentrations | if you start concentrating on a second effect, you must end one of the effects. |
| damage | after you take damage, you must succeed on a fortitude save to maintain concentration. the DC is 10 or half the damage taken (round down), whichever is higher, up to a maximum of 30. |
| incapacitated | your concentration ends if you are incapacitated or die. |
