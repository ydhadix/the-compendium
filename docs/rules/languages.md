# Languages

You begin play knowing at least three languages: common, a language provided by your background, and any standard language of your choice.

Knowledge of a language means you can communicate in that language, read it, and write it.

Standard languages are widespread in the material plane. Exotic languages are less common in the material plane but are more common in the outer planes.

Other languages exist, but knowledge of them is rare outside of their native speakers. However, your background or experiences during an adventure may allow you to learn them.
