# Movement
**Movement** is how a character gets from place to place using its Speed.
{ .subtitle }

## Speed
Your **Speed** is the maximum distance you can move on a Turn.

A creature can have additional Speeds that let it ignore normal movement restrictions.  You can switch between your Speeds as you Move, but the distance you travel using each Speed can't exceed that Speed's maximum, and your total movement in a Turn can't exceed the largest Speed you used that Turn.

#### Splitting Movement
You can move before and after any Action on your Turn.  However you break it up, your total movement that Turn can't exceed your Speed.

#### Dropping Prone
You can drop Prone without spending an Action or any movement, unless your Speed is **0**.

### Obstacles

#### Difficult Terrain
Each foot you move through or out of Difficult Terrain costs **1** extra foot.

#### Squeezing
Spaces sized for creatures one size smaller than you are Difficult Terrain.  You can't move through smaller spaces.

#### Moving Through Creatures
You can move freely through the space of Allies and Tiny creatures.  You can also move through the space of Incapacitated creatures and creatures at least two Size Categories larger or smaller than you, but that space is Difficult Terrain.

You can't willingly end your move in another creature's space. If you end your Turn in a space with another creature, the smaller creature is knocked Prone, unless either is Tiny _(If you are both the same size, both are knocked Prone)_.

### Burrowing
**Burrowing** is moving through soft ground, such as earth, sand, or mud.  A **Burrow Speed** lets you burrow, otherwise burrowing requires significant tools and time to make progress.

### Climbing
**Climbing** is moving across vertical surfaces.  You need both hands, and each foot you move costs **1** extra foot.  A **Climb Speed** lets you climb without that extra cost.

#### Spider Climbing
A **Spider Climb Speed** is a Climb Speed, and you can also climb with your hands free and move along ceilings upside down.

### Falling
**Falling** forces you to move when you are airborne with no way to stay aloft.  Falling doesn't cost any movement.

Each time you fall, you fall up to **500** feet until you land or gain a way to stay aloft.  After each of your Turns begins, if you are still falling, you fall another **500** feet.

While falling, anything you collide with makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You and whatever you struck take **1d6** Bludgeoning damage for every **10** feet you fell and are knocked Prone. The next time you collide with something, the damage is calculated from this location, not the total distance you fell. |
| Success | You continue moving until you strike something else or have moved the total falling distance. |

### Flying
**Flying** is moving through the air.  A **Fly Speed** lets you fly, but you fall if you are Incapacitated or Prone, or if your Fly Speed is **0**.

#### Hovering
A **Hover Speed** is a Fly Speed, and you gain Drifting and Prone Immunity, and you don't fall if you are Incapacitated or your Fly Speed is **0**.

### Jumping
**Jumping** is moving horizontally or vertically, avoiding most terrain effects. Your Strength determines how far or high you can jump, and each foot you jump costs **1** foot of movement.

If you don't move at least **10** feet in a straight line immediately before a jump, the distance you can jump is halved.

#### Long Jump
You can cover a horizontal distance up to **(5 × STR) + 10** feet.

#### High Jump
You can cover a vertical distance up to **3 + STR** feet. During the jump, you can extend your arms to reach up to half your height higher.

### Propelled
**Propelled** movement forces you to move against your will.  Being propelled doesn't cost any movement.

While propelled, anything you collide with makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You and whatever you struck take **1d6** Bludgeoning damage for every **10** feet of propelled movement you had remaining and are knocked Prone.  Then, you stop being propelled. |
| Success | You continue moving until you strike something else or have moved the total propelled distance. |

### Swimming
**Swimming** is moving through liquid.  You need both hands, and each foot you move costs **1** extra foot.  A **Swim Speed** lets you swim without that extra cost.
