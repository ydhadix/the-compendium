# Movement

**Movement** is how a character gets from place to place using its Speed.

#### Splitting Movement
You can move before and after any Action on your Turn.  However you break it up, your total movement that Turn can't exceed your Speed.

#### Dropping Prone
You can drop Prone without spending an Action or any movement, unless your Speed is **0**.

### Difficult Terrain
Each foot you move through or out of Difficult Terrain costs **1** extra foot.

#### Squeezing
Spaces sized for creatures one size smaller than you are Difficult Terrain.  You can't move through smaller spaces.

### Moving Through Creatures
You can move freely through the space of Allies and Tiny creatures.

You can also move through the space of Incapacitated creatures and creatures at least two Size Categories larger or smaller than you, but that space is Difficult Terrain.

You can't willingly end your move in another creature's space. If you end your Turn in a space with another creature, the smaller creature is knocked Prone, unless either is Tiny _(If you are both the same size, both are knocked Prone)_.

### Climbing
**Climbing** lets you traverse vertical surfaces.  You need both hands, and each foot you move costs **1** extra foot.

### Falling
**Falling** movement forces you to move when you are airborne with no way to stay aloft.  Falling doesn't cost any movement.

Each time you fall, you fall up to **500** feet until you land or gain a way to stay aloft.  After each of your Turns begins, if you are still falling, you fall another **500** feet.

While falling, anything you collide with makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You and whatever you struck take **1d6** Bludgeoning damage for every **10** feet you fell and are knocked Prone. The next time you collide with something, the damage is calculated from this location, not the total distance you fell. |
| Success | You continue moving until you strike something else or have moved the total falling distance. |

### Flying
**Flying** lets you move through the air.  You need a Fly Speed, otherwise you fall while airborne.

### Jumping
**Jumping** lets you move horizontally or vertically, avoiding terrain effects. Your Strength determines how far or high you can jump, and each foot you jump costs **1** foot of movement. If you don't move at least **10** feet in a straight line immediately before a jump, the distance you can jump is halved.

#### Long Jump
You can cover a horizontal distance up to **(5 × STR) + 10** feet.

#### High Jump
You can cover a vertical distance up to **3 + STR** feet. During the jump, you can extend your arms to reach up to half your height higher.

### Propelled
**Propelled** movement forces you to move against your will.  Being propelled doesn't cost any movement.

While propelled, anything you collide with makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You and whatever you struck take **1d6** Bludgeoning damage for every **10** feet of propelled movement you had remaining and are knocked Prone.  Then, you stop being propelled. |
| Success | You continue moving until you strike something else or have moved the total propelled distance. |

### Swimming
**Swimming** lets you move through liquid.  You need both hands, and each foot you move costs **1** extra foot.
