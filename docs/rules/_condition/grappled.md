##### Grappled
- You are Immobile.
- You gain Disadvantage on Attack Rolls against any creature other than the grappler.
- The grappler can propel you with it when it Moves, but each foot of that movement costs it **1** extra foot unless you are Tiny or at least **2** Sizes smaller than it.

###### Ending the Condition
This condition ends after you leave the grappler's Reach.
