# Gray Render Companion
A **Gray Render Companion** is a Primal Companion form open to a Beast Master who has bound a Gray Render to a [Belt of Pocket Monsters](/item/magic/rarity/legendary/belt-of-pocket-monsters.md). It is a smaller, leashed echo of the real thing, and it fixates on its ranger the way a wild render fixates on whatever it decides to guard.
