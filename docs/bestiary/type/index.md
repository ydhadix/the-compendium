# Bestiary by Creature Type

| Creature Type | |
|---|---|
| [Aberration](aberration.md) | **Aberrations** are alien creatures from beyond the physical realms, warped in body and mind. Their forms defy anatomy, their motives defy understanding, and few who meet one come away unchanged. |
| [Beast](beast.md) | **Beasts** are the natural animals of the world. They act on instinct rather than reason, and are commonly hunted, tamed, or ridden. |
| [Celestial](celestial.md) | **Celestials** are natives of the Upper Planes, embodiments of virtue and light. Many serve the gods as messengers and warriors, and even the least of them burns bright against the dark. |
| [Construct](construct.md) | **Constructs** are objects given animation and a purpose. They typically follow the will of their makers rather than any of their own. |
| [Dragon](dragon.md) | **Dragons** are ancient creatures of immense power and pride. The oldest command devastating magic and hoard what they covet, and their cunning grows with every century. |
| [Elemental](elemental.md) | **Elementals** are beings of raw elemental matter — fire, water, earth, air, and any combination thereof. They hail from the Elemental Planes, and rarely think or feel as mortals do. |
| [Fey](fey.md) | **Fey** are creatures of the Feywild, bound to the wild magic of nature. Whimsical and capricious, they blur the line between beauty and menace, and their bargains rarely mean what they seem. |
| [Fiend](fiend.md) | **Fiends** are natives of the Lower Planes, born of corruption and ruin. Devils bargain for souls while demons crave only destruction, and both treat mortals as playthings in their designs. |
| [Giant](giant.md) | **Giants** are towering humanoids that dwarf the common folk, heirs to an ancient and ordered world. Giants are long-lived, few in number, and boast tremendous might and long memories. |
| [Humanoid](humanoid.md) | **Humanoids** are the peoples of the world, from Primes and Elves to Orcs and Goblinoids, and every other folk who inhabit it. They raise the civilizations most adventurers call home, and stand among their most common allies and foes. |
| [Monstrosity](monstrosity.md) | **Monstrosities** are frightening creatures born of magic or mythology, uncanny yet nonetheless part of the natural world. Few can be reasoned with, and fewer still tamed. |
| [Ooze](ooze.md) | **Oozes** are amorphous masses that slither, seep, and engulf. Most lack true minds, driven by nothing but the urge to consume whatever they touch. |
| [Plant](plant.md) | **Plants** are vegetal creatures and fungal growths given strange life. Some root in place and wait, while others walk, hunt, and hunger like any predator. |
| [Undead](undead.md) | **Undead** are once-living creatures returned by necromancy or a will too restless for the grave. They shamble, hunger, and crave the living they can no longer join. |
