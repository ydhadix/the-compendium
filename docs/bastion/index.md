# Bastions
A Bastion is a location or structure you own, such as a stronghold or a sailing ship, that offers refuge from the dangers of adventuring and a place to conduct research, craft Items, and more.
{ .subtitle }

## Gaining a Bastion
Bastions are awarded to individual players. You typically receive your Bastion around Character Level **5**.

Multiple player's Bastions can be combined into shared structures, or kept independent from one another.

You, the rest of your party, and the DM decide how your Bastions come into your possession. Their shape, style, and function are yours to decide.

## Facilities
Every Bastion has Facilities: Basic Facilities that serve everyday needs, and Special Facilities for unique tasks.

#### Facility Space
A Facility's Size sets its maximum area in **5**-foot squares. That area can spread across multiple levels, as long as they are all connected.

| Size | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Cramped | 2 squares | 4 squares |
| Roomy | 4 squares | 16 squares |
| Vast | 16 squares | 36 squares |

## Basic Facilities
A Basic Facility has no explicit benefits, but makes a Bastion more comfortable to inhabit. A Basic Facility can resemble a Special Facility, but doesn't grant its benefits.

Common Basic Facilities include a barracks, bedroom, courtyard, dining room, dormitory, office, pub, or storage.

#### Adding Basic Facilities
Each Bastion starts with one Cramped and one Roomy Basic Facility. You can add new Basic Facilities or enlarge existing ones by spending money and time, though you don't need to be present while they are built.

#### Upgrading Basic Facilities
Upgrading a Basic Facility increases its Size and costs the difference between its current Size and its new Size.

| Size | Cost |
|---|---|
| Cramped | 500 gold |
| Roomy | 1,000 gold |
| Vast | 3,000 gold |

## Special Facilities
A Special Facility is a Bastion location where certain activities yield special benefits.

#### Adding Special Facilities
Each Bastion starts with two Special Facilities. Unlike Basic Facilities, Special Facilities can't be bought; you gain more at specific Character Levels.

When you gain a level, you can change one of your Special Facilities.

| Character Level | Special Facilities |
|---|---|
| 5 | 2 |
| 9 | 3 |
| 13 | 4 |
| 17 | 5 |

#### Upgrading Special Facilities
Upgrading a Special Facility increases its Size and costs the difference between its current Size and its new Size.  Each facility also gives additional benefits.

| Size | Cost |
|---|---|
| Cramped | — |
| Roomy | 2,000 gold |
| Vast | 7,000 gold |

#### Hirelings
Every Special Facility comes with at least one Hireling who works in and maintains it. A Hireling is proficient with any Tools or Skills tied to that Facility and can help with checks or crafting.
