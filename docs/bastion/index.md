# Bastions
A Bastion is a character's own stronghold, a home base they build up and put to work between adventures.
{ .subtitle }

## Gaining a Bastion
Bastions are awarded to individual players. You typically receive your Bastion around Character Level **5**.  Multiple player's Bastions can be combined into shared structures, or kept independent from one another.

You, the rest of your party, and the DM decide how your Bastions come into your possession. Their shape, style, and function are yours to decide.

## Bastion Facilities
Every Bastion has Facilities: Basic Facilities that serve everyday needs, and Special Facilities for specific tasks.

#### Facility Space
A Facility's Size is its area in **5**-foot squares. That area can spread across multiple levels, as long as they are all connected.

| Size | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Cramped | 2 squares | 4 squares |
| Roomy | 4 squares | 16 squares |
| Vast | 16 squares | 36 squares |

### Basic Facilities
A Basic Facility has no explicit benefits, but makes a Bastion more comfortable to inhabit. A Basic Facility can resemble a Special Facility, but doesn't grant its benefits.  Each Bastion starts with one Cramped and one Roomy Basic Facility. 

#### Adding Basic Facilities
You can add new Basic Facilities or enlarge existing ones by spending money.  The completed facility is available the next Day — paying the cost completes efforts that a Bastion's Hirelings have undertaken in the background.

Upgrading a Basic Facility increases its Size and costs the difference between its current Size and its new Size.

| Size | Cost |
|---|---|
| Cramped | 500 gold |
| Roomy | 1,000 gold |
| Vast | 3,000 gold |

### Special Facilities
A Special Facility is a location that helps characters work on specific tasks that help them while adventuring.  Each Bastion starts with **2** Special Facilities. 

| Facility | Prerequisite | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [Arcanum](arcanum.md) | Spellcasting with an Arcane Focus | Craft and study arcane magic and its items. |
| [Armory](armory.md) | — | Stock weapons and armor to equip your allies. |
| [Garden](garden.md) | Spellcasting with a Druidic Focus | Cultivate useful plants, herbs, and produce. |
| [Infirmary](infirmary.md) | — | Tend the wounded and recover from injury. |
| [Kitchen](kitchen.md) | — | Prepare meals that fortify your allies. |
| [Shrine](shrine.md) | Spellcasting with a Holy Symbol | A holy site for blessings and divine aid. |
| [Storehouse](storehouse.md) | — | Stockpile trade goods to buy and sell. |
| [Study](study.md) | — | A quiet place for research and scribing. |
| [Training Yard](training-yard.md) | Fighting Style, Unarmored Stance, or Weapon Mastery | Drill recruits and hone martial skill. |
| [Workshop](workshop.md) | — | Craft mundane gear and clever inventions. |

#### Hirelings
Every Special Facility comes with at least one Hireling who works in and maintains it. A facility's Hireling is proficient with any Tools or Skills tied to that Facility and can help with checks or crafting.

#### Adding Special Facilities
Unlike Basic Facilities, Special Facilities can't be bought; you gain more at specific Character Levels.

| Character Level | Special Facilities |
|---|---|
| 5 | 2 |
| 9 | 3 |
| 13 | 4 |
| 17 | 5 |

#### Changing Special Facilities
When you gain a Level, you can change one of your Special Facilities.

#### Upgrading Special Facilities
Upgrading a Special Facility increases its Size and costs the difference between its current Size and its new Size.  Each facility also gives additional benefits as its size increases.

| Size | Cost |
|---|---|
| Cramped | — |
| Roomy | 2,000 gold |
| Vast | 7,000 gold |
