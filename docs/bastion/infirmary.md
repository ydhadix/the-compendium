#### Preventative Care
When a creature finishes a Long Rest in your Bastion, one Sickness affecting it ends, and its Hit Die maximum increases by **1** for **24** hours.

#### Upgrading
| Upgrade | Size | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic | Roomy | The facility gains one Hireling, and its Preventative Care also gives one additional Hit Die and maximizes all healing from Hit Dice for **24** hours. |
| Hospital | Vast | The facility gains one Hireling, and its Preventative Care also gives one additional Hit Die and grants Death Protection and Poison Resistance for **24** hours. |
