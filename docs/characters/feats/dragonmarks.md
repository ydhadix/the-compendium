??? feats "Noble Dragonmark | No Dragonmark"

    #### Noble Dragonmark

    ###### Mark Proficiency
    You gain proficiency with one of your dragonmark's proficiencies.

    ###### Mark Spells
    You learn your dragonmark's spells at the corresponding character levels. You can cast those spells using intelligence, wisdom, or charisma as your spellcasting ability.

    ###### Free Casting
    *One use each per Long Rest*
    
    You can cast one of this feat's spells without a spell slot.

    ###### Changing Spells
    Each dragonmark has a class's spell list associated with it.

    Whenever you level up, you can change one of this feat's spells with another spell of the same level that doesn't deal damage from the associated class's spell list.

??? feats "Aberrant Dragonmark | No Dragonmark"

    #### Aberrant Dragonmark

    ###### Uncanny Fortitude
    You add 1d4 to fortitude saves.

    ###### Aberrant Spells
    You learn a cantrip and a 1st-level spell from the sorcerer spell list.  You can cast those spells using intelligence, wisdom, or charisma as your spellcasting ability.

    ###### Free Casting
    *One use per Short Rest*
    
    You can cast this feat's 1st-level spell without a spell slot.

    ###### Changing Spells

    Whenever you level up, you can change one of this feat's spells with another spell of the same level from the sorcerer spell list.

## Greater Dragonmark Boons

Greater Dragonmark boons require another [Dragonmark](dragonmarks.md#dragonmarks) as a prerequisite.  Each boon also grants the Greater Mark Spellcasting feature.

??? feats "Greater Mark Spellcasting | —"

    #### Greater Mark Spellcasting

    *One use per Long Rest*

    You can cast one spell you know without a spell slot.  The level of the spell you cast increases with your character level.

    | Character Level | Spell Level |
    |---|---|
    | 1st | 1st |
    | 3rd | 2nd |
    | 5th | 3rd |
    | 7th | 4th |
    | 9th | 5th |

??? feats "Greater Mark of Handling | Noble Dragonmark (Handling)"

    #### Greater Mark of Handling

    You gain the [Greater Mark Spellcasting](dragonmarks.md#greater-mark-spellcasting) feature.

    ###### Monstrous Instincts
    Your spells and features can treat monstrosities as beasts.

    ###### Superior Handling
    After you hit with an attack while mounted, your mount can use its reaction to move or attack.

    ###### Subdue
    *Magic Action*
    
    *Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest*
    
    You can force a beast within 30 feet to make a will save. The target has disadvantage if you or a companion dealt damage to them since the start of your previous turn.

    **Fail**
    The target is frightened for 1 minute. When the target's turn ends, it repeats this save.

    **Success**
    This effect ends on the target.

??? feats "Greater Mark of Passage | Noble Dragonmark (Passage)"

    #### Greater Mark of Passage

    You gain the [Greater Mark Spellcasting](dragonmarks.md#greater-mark-spellcasting) feature.

    ###### Fleet-Footed
    You gain a climb speed and a swim speed equal to your speed. Your movement is unaffected by difficult terrain.

    ###### Slippery
    You can use 5 feet of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints *(e.g. manacles, chains, or being grappled)*.

    ###### Rising Tide
    After an ally enters your reach for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, they gain the effect of fleet-footed and slippery until the end of their next turn.

??? feats "Greater Mark of Scribing | Noble Dragonmark (Scribing)"

    #### Greater Mark of Scribing

    You gain the [Greater Mark Spellcasting](dragonmarks.md#greater-mark-spellcasting) feature.

    ###### Scrivener
    The time it takes for you to craft spell scrolls is halved.

    ###### Quick Study
    *Bonus Action*
    
    You take the study action.

    ###### Well-Read
    *Reaction*
    
    *Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest*
    
    Before you fail a check that you don't have expertise in, you can add your proficiency bonus to the total.
