# Origin Feats

Origin feats represent an aspect of your character before they became an adventurer. Racial and Fighting Style feats are also considered Origin feats.

### Alert

##### Initiative Proficiency
You gain proficiency with Initiative checks.

##### Initiative Swap
Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally are Incapacitated.

### Crafter

##### Tool Proficiency
You gain proficiency with three of the following Artisan's Tools.

- carpenter's tools
- leatherworker's tools
- mason's tools
- potter's tools
- smith's tools
- tinker's tools
- weaver's tools
- woodcarver's tools

##### Discount
Nonmagical items and materials you buy cost 20 percent less.

##### Fast Crafting
Utilize Action, Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest: You can create a piece of nonmagical gear associated with one of the Artisan's Tools above, provided you have the Artisan's Tool in hand and proficiency with those tools.

The crafted gear can't be Armor, a Weapon (except Simple Melee Weapons), or cost more than 25 gold.

Gear crafted this way has no value to merchants and falls apart when you finish a Long Rest.

### Healer

##### Battle Medic
Utilize Action, One use of a Healer's Kit: One creature within 5 feet of you expends a Hit Dice and you roll it and add your Proficiency Bonus. You heal the creature by the total.

##### Healing Reroll
When you roll a 1 on a die to heal a creature, you can reroll the die.

### Lucky

##### Luck Points
You have Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus. When you finish a Long Rest, you regain all Luck Points.

##### Advantage
Before you roll a d20, you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself advantage on the roll.

##### Disadvantage
Before a creature rolls a d20 to attack you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose disadvantage on the roll.

### Magic Initiate
Choose Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard.

##### Spells Known
You learn two cantrips and a 1st-level spell of your choice from the chosen class's spell list. You can cast them using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

##### Free Casting
Spellcasting, Once per Long Rest: You cast this feat's 1st-level spell without a spell slot.

##### Changing Spells
When you level up, you can change one of this feat's spells with another spell of the same level from the chosen class's spell list.

##### Repeatable
You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different class each time.

### Musician

##### Instrument Training
You gain proficiency with three Musical Instruments of your choice.

##### Encouraging Song
When you finish a Short Rest, you can play a song on a musical instrument you are proficient with. Choose allies who heard the song equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Those allies gain one DM Inspiration.

### Skilled

##### Three Proficiencies
You gain proficiency with any combination of three Skills, Trades, or Martial Weapon categories of your choice.

##### Repeatable
You can take this feat more than once.

### Tough

##### Extra Hit Points Now
When you gain this feat, add twice your character level to your hit point maximum.

##### Extra Hit Points Later
When you level up, add 2 to your hit point maximum.
