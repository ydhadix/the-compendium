# Gnome

| Gnome | |
|---|---|
| abilities | dexterity and one of your choice |
| creature type | humanoid |
| size category | small |
| speed | 30 ft. |
| reach | 5 ft. |
| hit points | 6 |
| senses | darkvision 60 ft. |

### Naturally Stealthy

You can take the Hide action even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.

### Resilient Mind

You have advantage on Will saves.

### Gnomish Magic

Starting at the corresponding character levels, you learn these spells and can cast them using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

You can cast each leveled spell once without a spell slot and can do so again after you finish a Long Rest.

| Character Level | Spells Known |
|---|---|
| 1st | prestidigitation, mage hand |
| 3rd | speak with animals |
| 5th | pass without trace |
