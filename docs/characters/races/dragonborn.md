### Breath Weapon

Attack Action, Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest: You exhale elemental energy in either a 15-foot cone or a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that area must make a Reflex save. Roll 1d10 and add your Constitution.

On a failed save, a creature takes the total as your Draconic Ancestry damage type.

On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage.

You roll an additional 1d10 when you reach character level 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).

### Draconic Flight
*Prerequisite: Character Level 5*

Bonus Action, Once per Long Rest: You sprout wings on your back that last for 10 minutes or until you retract them (no action required). For the duration, you have a fly speed equal to your speed.

### Draconic Ancestry
