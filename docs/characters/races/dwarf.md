### Dwarven Toughness

When you level up, your hit point maximum increases by 1.

### Treasure Sense

Bonus Action, Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest: You can sense valuable ores for 10 minutes.

For the duration, you can sense the location of precious metals, minerals, and gems worth at least 50 gold within 30 feet of you, even through walls and physical obstacles. Your senses can identify the general category of ore but cannot distinguish between specific materials.
