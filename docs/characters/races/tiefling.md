| condition resistances | charmed |

### Silver-Tongued

You have proficiency in your choice of the Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion skills.

### Fiendish Ancestry

Choose an ancestry. Each ancestry has different spells and determines the type of your Damage Resistance.

Starting at the corresponding character levels, you learn your ancestry's spells and can cast them using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

You can cast each leveled spell once without a spell slot and can do so again after you finish a Long Rest.

#### Abyssal Ancestry

You have resistance to poison damage.

| Character Level | Spells Known |
|---|---|
| 1st | thaumaturgy, poison spray |
| 3rd | ray of sickness |
| 5th | hold person |

#### Chthonic Ancestry

You have resistance to necrotic damage.

| Character Level | Spells Known |
|---|---|
| 1st | thaumaturgy, chill touch |
| 3rd | false life |
| 5th | ray of enfeeblement |

#### Infernal Ancestry

You have resistance to fire damage.

| Character Level | Spells Known |
|---|---|
| 1st | thaumaturgy, fire bolt |

| 5th | darkness |
