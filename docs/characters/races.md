##### Divine Revelation
*Prerequisite: Character Level 3*

Bonus Action, Once per Long Rest: Choose one of the following revelations and transform for 1 minute or until you end it (no action required). You can choose a different revelation each time you use this feature.

For the duration, you have the revelation's effects.

Additionally, when you deal damage with an attack or save, you can add your Proficiency Bonus as Necrotic or Radiant damage (your choice).

| Revelation | Effect |
|---|---|
| winged | you have a fly speed equal to your speed. |
| chosen | you emit bright light to 10 feet and dim light 10 feet further. at the end of your turn, each other creature in a 10-foot emanation takes your proficiency bonus as radiant damage. |
| fallen | dc = 8 + constitution + proficiency bonus. when you choose this revelation, each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you must succeed on a will save or be frightened for the duration. |

##### Breath Weapon

Attack Action, Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest: You exhale elemental energy in either a 15-foot cone or a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that area must make a Reflex save. Roll 1d10 and add your Constitution.

On a failed save, a creature takes the total as your Draconic Ancestry damage type.

On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage.

You roll an additional 1d10 when you reach character level 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).

##### Draconic Flight
*Prerequisite: Character Level 5*

Bonus Action, Once per Long Rest: You sprout wings on your back that last for 10 minutes or until you retract them (no action required). For the duration, you have a fly speed equal to your speed.

##### Draconic Ancestry

##### Dwarven Toughness

When you level up, your hit point maximum increases by 1.

##### Treasure Sense

Bonus Action, Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest: You can sense valuable ores for 10 minutes.

For the duration, you can sense the location of precious metals, minerals, and gems worth at least 50 gold within 30 feet of you, even through walls and physical obstacles. Your senses can identify the general category of ore but cannot distinguish between specific materials.

##### Keen Senses

You have proficiency with your choice of the Insight, Perception, or Survival skill.

##### Trance

You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

You can finish a long rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in a trancelike meditation. You remain conscious.

##### Elven Ancestry

Choose an ancestry. Each ancestry has different spells and benefits.

Starting at the corresponding character levels, you learn your ancestry's spells and can cast them using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

You can cast each leveled spell once without a spell slot and can do so again after you finish a Long Rest.

##### Powerful Build

##### Large Form
*Prerequisite: Character Level 5*

Bonus Action, Once per Long Rest: You can enlarge yourself for 10 minutes or until you end this effect (no action required). For the duration, you are Large, you have advantage on strength checks, and your speed increases by 10 feet.

##### Giant Ancestry

Choose an ancestry. Each ancestry determines the type of your Damage Resistance and gives you a different Effect.

| Giant | Damage Resistance | Effect (Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest) |
|---|---|---|
| cloud | thunder | bonus action — you magically teleport up to 30 feet to a space you can see. |
| fire | fire | when you deal damage with an attack, you can add 1d10 fire damage. |
| frost | cold | when you deal damage with an attack, you can add 1d6 cold damage and reduce the target's speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. |
| hill | poison | when you deal damage with an attack, you can knock the target prone. |
| stone | poison | reaction — when you take damage, you can reduce the damage by 1d12 + your constitution. |
| storm | lightning | reaction — when you take damage from a creature within 60 feet of you, you can deal 1d8 thunder damage to the creature. |

### Synth

| | |
|---|---|
| abilities | constitution and one of your choice |
| creature type | humanoid and construct |
| size category | medium |
| speed | 30 ft. |
| reach | 5 ft. |
| hit points | 10 |
| damage resistances | poison |
| condition resistances | exhausted, poisoned |

##### Constructed Nature

You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe, and you are immune to disease.

##### Sentry's Rest

You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

You can finish a long rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state. You remain conscious.

##### Synth Ancestry

Choose an ancestry. Each ancestry has different benefits.

###### Reploid Ancestry

Choose another Race. You are visually indistinguishable from that race, and use its Size Category, Speed, and Reach, though you weigh twice as much as the race normally would.

You gain one of the chosen Race's features of your choice.

###### Android Ancestry

You have a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

You have proficiency with one Skill and one Trade of your choice.

###### Warforged Ancestry

You have a +1 bonus to Armor Class and Damage Reduction.

Your Creature Size is Large and your Reach extends by 5 feet.

Your physical attacks deal double damage to objects and structures.
