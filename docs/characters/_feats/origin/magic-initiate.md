### Magic Initiate
Choose Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard.

#### Spells Known
You learn two cantrips and a 1st-level spell of your choice from the chosen class's spell list. You can cast them using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

#### Free Casting
Spellcasting, Once per Long Rest: You cast this feat's 1st-level spell without a spell slot.

#### Changing Spells
When you level up, you can change one of this feat's spells with another spell of the same level from the chosen class's spell list.

#### Repeatable
You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different class each time.
