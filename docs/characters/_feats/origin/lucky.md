### Lucky

#### Luck Points
You have Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus. When you finish a Long Rest, you regain all Luck Points.

#### Advantage
Before you roll a d20, you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself advantage on the roll.

#### Disadvantage
Before a creature rolls a d20 to attack you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose disadvantage on the roll.
