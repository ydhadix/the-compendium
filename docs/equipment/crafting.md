# Crafting

Adventurers can create items and equipment using tools, time, and raw materials.

## Requirements

### Proficiency

The type of item determines the Artisan's Tools and Proficiency needed to craft it.

| Item Type | Typical Tool Proficiency |
|---|---|
| Armor | Leatherworker's Tools or Smith's Tools |
| Clothing | Cobbler's Tools, Jeweler's Tools, Leatherworker's Tools, or Weaver's Tools |
| Focus | Jeweler's Tools, Painter's Supplies, or Woodcarver's Tools |
| Potion | Alchemist's Supplies or Herbalism Kit |
| Scroll | Calligrapher's Supplies |
| Shield | Leatherworker's Tools, Smith's Tools, or Woodcarver's Tools |
| Weapon | Leatherworker's Tools, Smith's Tools, or Woodcarver's Tools |
| Other | Tinker's Tools or another Artisan's Tool appropriate to the item |

### Time

The time it takes to craft a nonmagical item is based on the item's worth. For every **25** gold, it takes **1** day to craft an item.  Every day of crafting requires **8** hours of work, but the total hours and days don't need to be consecutive.

Characters who meet the item's Proficiency requirements can work together to shorten the crafting time.  While working together, the time each character spends crafting the item reduces the time needed to finish as though those characters were working individually.

### Cost

The cost of crafting an item is paid in raw materials worth half the item's normal purchase cost.

## Materials

Items made with special Materials may gain additional properties. You must acquire a Material before you can craft an item with it. Crafting an item with a Material increases the item's value, but doesn't increase the time required to craft the item.

??? item "Adamantine | 500 gold"

    #### Adamantine

    Adamantine objects weigh **50**% more than normal, and have Immunity to Physical damage from non-Adamantine sources _(This Immunity does not protect the item's wielder)_.

    ###### Armor

    You are unaffected by Critical Hits from non-Adamantine sources _(Critical Hits gain no benefits, and the attack roll must beat your Armor Class to hit you)_.

    ###### Weapon

    Attacks with this weapon against objects are Critical Hits.

??? item "Heartwood | 1,000 gold"

    #### Heartwood

    Heartwood naturally mends normal wear and tear, can be a spellcasting focus for any spell, and can be used to create items normally made of metal.

    ###### Weapon

    This weapon's Physical Attack Masterwork bonus is also its Spell Attack Masterwork bonus.

??? item "Ironwill | 2,500 gold"

    #### Ironwill

    This item can be used as a spellcasting focus for any spell.

    ###### Armor

    You can use your spellcasting ability to make Fortitude saves, instead of Constitution.

    ###### Weapon

    You can use your spellcasting ability to make attacks with this weapon, instead of Strength or Dexterity.

??? item "Mithral | 500 gold"

    #### Mithral

    This item weighs **50**% less than normal.

    ###### Armor

    This Armor doesn't have a Strength requirement or Stealth penalty.

    ###### Weapon

    If this weapon is Light, it gains the Finesse property. If this weapon is not Light or Heavy, it becomes Light.

??? item "Quicksilver | 500 gold"

    #### Quicksilver

    Quicksilver is impractical for making items other than Armor and Weapons.

    ###### Armor

    _Utilize Action_

    You can don or doff this Armor.

    ###### Weapon

    _Bonus Action_

    You can change this weapon into the form of any melee weapon.

??? item "Silver | 100 gold"

    #### Silver

    Silver can't be used to make Weapons or Armor, but Weapons and Armor can be alchemically coated with it _(a coated item keeps its normal Material's benefits as well)_.

    ###### Weapon

    Before this weapon deals damage to a shapeshifted creature, add **1** damage die.

## Masterwork

Items of superior quality are more effective than their normal versions. There are different types of Masterwork bonuses, and each bonus is added to an item individually.

### Costs

The value of a Masterwork item is determined by each of the item's Masterwork bonuses, in addition to the item's other costs.  Armor Class bonuses on a set of Armor _(not a Shield)_, use the Armor column instead.

| Bonus | Cost | Armor |
|---|---|---|
| +1 | +200 gold | +2,000 gold |
| +2 | +2,000 gold | +20,000 gold |
| +3 | +20,000 gold | +200,000 gold |

### Crafting

Crafting a Masterwork item costs half as much as purchasing one, but requires Expertise in the corresponding Artisan's Tools and a minimum Character Level based on the item's bonus: Level **5** for a **+1**, Level **11** for a **+2**, and Level **17** for a **+3**.

### Physical Attack

Masterwork items with a Physical Attack bonus add that bonus to physical attack and damage rolls made using the item.

### Spell Attack

Masterwork items with a Spell Attack bonus add that bonus to spell attack rolls made using the item as a spellcasting focus _(you must be able to use the item as a spellcasting focus normally)_.

### Spell Save

Masterwork items with a Spell Save bonus add that bonus to the spell save DC of spells cast using the item as a spellcasting focus _(you must be able to use the item as a spellcasting focus normally)_.

### Armor Class

Masterwork items with an Armor Class bonus add that bonus to the item's Armor Class bonus _(an item that doesn't normally affect Armor Class doesn't gain this benefit)_.
