# Artisan's Tools

Artisan's tools are typically used to craft items or practice a profession.

### Alchemist's Supplies

**cost**: 50 GP

**ability**: intelligence

**applications**: identify a substance

**Crafting**

- acid
- alchemist's fire
- component pouch
- oil
- paper
- perfume

### Brewer's Supplies

**cost**: 20 GP

**applications**: detect poisoned drink
