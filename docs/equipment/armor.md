# Armor

## Proficiency

While you wear armor you are not proficient with, you have disadvantage on d20 rolls using strength or dexterity and can't cast spells.

## Strength Requirements

If you don't meet your armor's Strength requirement, your Speed is reduced by 15 feet.

## Damage Reduction

While wearing armor with damage reduction, the amount of bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take from attacks is reduced by that amount.

## Wearing Armor & Using Items

Your armor's weight determines how quickly you can don and doff it, as well as how many unworn items you can have readily available during combat. You can take 1 minute outside of combat to change items or restock.

| Weight | items | Don | Doff |
|---|---|---|---|
| unarmored | 6 | - | - |
| light | 6 | 1 minute | 1 minute |
| medium | 4 | 5 minutes | 1 minute |
| heavy | 2 | 10 minutes | 5 minutes |
| shield | - | utilize action | utilize action |

## Shields

Shields are extra armor that is easily donned or doffed.

### Light Shield

A light shield mounts to your forearm, leaving your hand free.

Reaction: Before you are hit by an attack while your shield hand is free, you can gain a +2 bonus to AC until the end of the current turn, potentially causing the attack to miss.

### Medium Shield

A medium shield gives +2 AC when donned and occupies one hand.

### Heavy Shield

A heavy shield gives +2 AC when donned and occupies one hand. While donned, you count as three-quarters cover for creatures of your size, and full cover for creatures smaller than you (Creatures typically count as half-cover).

## Upgrading Armor

There are three families of medium and heavy armor: chain, scale, and plate. A set of armor can be upgraded to another armor in the same family by spending gold equal to the difference in their costs, purchasing only the parts of the armor that are missing.

### Armor Families

| Family | Light | Medium | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| chain | padded | chain shirt | heavy chain |
| scale | padded | scale mail | heavy scale |
| plate | padded | breastplate, half plate | heavy plate |

### Partially Donned Armor

When donning a suit of armor, you can leave off parts of the armor to don any armor in the same family with a lesser cost instead. Leaving off parts of your armor reduces its protection but can also remove stealth penalties or reduce how long it takes to don.
