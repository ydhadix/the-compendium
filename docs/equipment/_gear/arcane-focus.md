### Arcane Focus

**cost**: varies

An Arcane Focus is carved or bejeweled to channel arcane magic.  A Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard can use an Arcane Focus as a Spellcasting Focus.

| Focus | Cost |
|---|---|
| crystal | 10 gold |
| orb | 20 gold |
| staff | 5 gold |
| rod | 10 gold |
| wand | 10 gold |
