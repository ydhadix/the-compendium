Each Ioun Stone has a Type, which determines this item's rarity and effect.  You can have up to **3** Ioun Stones orbiting your head at a time.

An orbiting stone counts as an object you wear.  It avoids contact with other creatures and objects, adjusting its orbit to thwart all attempts to attack or snatch it.

##### Toss
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can toss this stone into the air, where it orbits up to **3** feet from your head and confers its effect.

##### Stow
Utilize Action
{ .subtitle }

You can seize and stow any number of Ioun Stones orbiting your head.  After your Attunement to an orbiting stone ends, it falls as though you had dropped it.
