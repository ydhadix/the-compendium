**6** Charges (regain **1d6** per Day)

This staff holds a single Spell.  The Spell Level determines this staff's rarity.

| Rarity | Spell Level |
|---|---|
| Uncommon | Cantrip or 1st-Level |
| Rare | 2nd-Level |
| Very Rare | 3rd-Level or 4th-Level |
| Legendary | 5th-Level |

---

##### Cast
**Magic Action; 1 Charge:** You can cast the staff's Spell.
