Each Horn of Valhalla has a Metal, which determines this item's rarity, how many warriors it conjures, and the Proficiency needed to command them.

| Metal | Rarity | Berserkers | Proficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silver | Rare | **2** | — |
| Brass | Rare | **3** | Simple Weapons |
| Bronze | Very Rare | **4** | Medium Armor |
| Iron | Legendary | **5** | All Martial Weapons |

---

##### Conjure Warriors
**Magic Action; Once per 7 Days:** You can blow the horn, conjuring the listed number of Berserker Companions within **60** feet.  They gain Charm Immunity and Fear Immunity, and disappear after **1** hour or after dropping to **0** Hit Points.

Without the horn's Proficiency, the Berserkers are Enemies that Attack you instead.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the Berserkers to move and take any Action available to them.
