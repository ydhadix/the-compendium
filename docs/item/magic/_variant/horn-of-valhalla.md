Each Horn of Valhalla has a Metal, which determines this item's rarity, how many warriors it summons, and the Proficiency needed to command them.

##### Summon Warriors
Magic Action, 1 use per 7 Days
{ .subtitle }

You can blow the horn, summoning the listed number of Companion Berserkers in unoccupied spaces within **60** feet.  They gain Charm Immunity and Fear Immunity, and disappear after **1** hour or after dropping to **0** Hit Points.

Without the horn's Proficiency, the Berserkers are Enemies that Attack you instead.

###### Command
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

You can command the Berserkers to move and take any Action available to them.
