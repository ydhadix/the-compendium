This instrument comes in several types, each named for a bard college.  The college determines this item's rarity, Spell Save DC, and the Spells it can cast.

| College | Rarity | DC | Spells |
|---|---|---|---|
| All | — | — | _Fly_, _Lesser Invisibility_, _Levitate_, and _Protection from Good and Evil_ |
| Doss Lute | Uncommon | **13** | _Animal Friendship_, _Protection from Energy_ (Fire Damage only), and _Protection from Poison_ |
| Fochlucan Bandore | Uncommon | **13** | _Entangle_, _Faerie Fire_, _Shillelagh_, and _Speak with Animals_ |
| Mac-Fuirmidh Cittern | Uncommon | **13** | _Barkskin_, _Cure Wounds_, and _Fog Cloud_ |
| Canaith Mandolin | Rare | **15** | _Cure Wounds_ (3rd Level), _Dispel Magic_, and _Protection from Energy_ (Lightning Damage only) |
| Cli Lyre | Rare | **15** | _Stone Shape_, _Wall of Fire_, and _Wind Wall_ |
| Anstruth Harp | Very Rare | **17** | _Control Weather_, _Cure Wounds_ (5th Level), and _Wall of Thorns_ |

---

A creature that plays this instrument without being Attuned makes a Will Save against this item's DC.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | The instrument deals **2d4** Psychic Damage to the creature. |

##### Cast
**Magic Action; Once each per Day:** You can play the instrument to cast one of its Spells.
