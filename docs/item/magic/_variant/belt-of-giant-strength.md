Each Belt of Giant Strength has a Giant, which determines this item's rarity and Strength.

Your Strength becomes the belt's Strength, unless your Strength is already that high.

---

#### Variants

| Giant | Rarity | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Hill | Rare | **5** |
| Stone | Very Rare | **6** |
| Frost | Very Rare | **6** |
| Fire | Very Rare | **7** |
| Cloud | Legendary | **8** |
| Storm | Legendary | **9** |
