### Dwarven Thrower
Rare Weapon (Hammer), Attunement (Dwarf or Belt of Dwarvenkind)
{ .subtitle }

This weapon has Thrown _(**20**/**60** feet)_.  Its Ranged Attacks deal an additional **1d8** Force Damage, or an additional **2d8** Force Damage to Giants.  After a Ranged Attack with this weapon hits or misses, it returns to your hand.
