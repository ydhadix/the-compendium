### Shield of the Cavalier
Rare Shield (Any), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Forceful Bash
**1 Attack Action:** You can make a Melee Attack Roll using this Shield against a target within **5** feet.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **2d6 + 2 + STR** Force Damage, and you can propel the target up to **10** feet away from you or, if it is up to your Size, knock it Prone. |

##### Protective Field
**Reaction; Once per Day:** Before you or an Ally within **5** feet is attacked or makes a Saving Throw against an Area, you can cast _Resilient Sphere_ _(centered on the target, no Concentration required)_.

The triggering Attack or Area has no effect inside the sphere.
