### Shield of the Cavalier
Rare Shield (Any), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Forceful Bash
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

Before you Attack on your Turn, you can replace one Attack with a Melee Attack Roll using this Shield against a target within **5** feet.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **2d6 + 2 + STR** Force Damage, and you can propel the target up to **10** feet away from you or, if it is up to your Size, knock it Prone. |

##### Protective Field
Reaction, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

Before you or an Ally within **5** feet is attacked or makes a Saving Throw against an Area, you can create an immobile **5**-foot Aura for **1** minute _(Concentration)_.  Everything not entirely inside is propelled to the nearest unoccupied space outside it.

The triggering Attack or Area has no effect inside the Aura.  Nothing passes into or out of the Aura, and nothing inside can damage anything outside it.
