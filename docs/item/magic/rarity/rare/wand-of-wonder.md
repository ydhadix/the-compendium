### Wand of Wonder
Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

**7** Charges (regain **1d6 + 1** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

##### Wonder
Magic Action, 1 Charge
{ .subtitle }

You can choose a point within **120** feet, which becomes the origin of a random effect.  Spells cast from this wand use a DC of **15**, and their Range becomes **120** feet if it would be shorter.  Roll **1d100**.

| 1d100 | Effect |
|---|---|
| 01–20 | You cast a Spell originating from the point.  Roll **1d10**: on a **1** or **2**, _Darkness_; on a **3** or **4**, _Faerie Fire_; on a **5** or **6**, _Fireball_; on a **7** or **8**, _Slow_; on a **9** or **10**, _Stinking Cloud_. |
| 21–25 | Nothing happens at the point.  Instead, you are Stunned until the start of your next Turn, believing something awesome just happened. |
| 26–30 | You cast _Gust of Wind_, its Line extending from you to the point. |
| 31–35 | Nothing happens at the point.  Instead, you take **1d6** Psychic Damage. |
| 36–40 | Heavy rain falls for **1** minute in a **120**-foot-high, **60**-foot radius Cylinder centered on the point, making the area Lightly Obscured. |
| 41–45 | A cloud of **600** oversized butterflies fills a **60**-foot-high, **30**-foot radius Cylinder centered on the point for **10** minutes, making the area Heavily Obscured. |
| 46–50 | You cast _Lightning Bolt_, its Line extending from you to the point. |
| 51–55 | The creature nearest the point gains the Enlarge effect of _Enlarge/Reduce_.  If that creature can't be affected, you gain the effect instead. |
| 56–60 | A creature appears in the nearest unoccupied space, acts as it normally would, and disappears after **1** hour or after it drops to **0** Hit Points.  Roll **1d4**: on a **1**, a Rhinoceros; on a **2**, an Elephant; on a **3** or **4**, a Rat. |
| 61–64 | Grass covers a **60**-foot radius circle centered on the point.  Grass already there grows to ten times its size and remains overgrown for **1** minute. |
| 65–68 | An unattended object no larger than **10** feet in any dimension, within **120** feet of the point, disappears into the Ethereal Plane. |
| 69–72 | Nothing happens at the point.  Instead, you gain the Reduce effect of _Enlarge/Reduce_ for **1** minute. |
| 73–77 | Leaves grow from the creature nearest the point.  Unless picked off, they turn brown and fall off after **24** hours. |
| 78–82 | Nothing happens at the point.  Instead, a burst of shimmering light extends from you in a **30**-foot Aura.  Everything in the Aura makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **15**)_, becoming Blinded for **1** minute on a failure.  Before each of the target's Turns ends, it repeats this Saving Throw.  On a success, this effect ends on the target. |
| 83–87 | Nothing happens at the point.  Instead, you cast _Lesser Invisibility_ on yourself. |
| 88–92 | A stream of **1d4 × 10** gems worth **1** gold each shoots toward the point in a **30**-foot × **5**-foot Line.  Each gem deals **1** Bludgeoning Damage, divided equally among everything in the Line. |
| 93–97 | You cast _Polymorph_ on the creature nearest the point.  Roll **1d4**: on a **1**, a Black Bear; on a **2**, a Giant Wasp; on a **3** or **4**, a Frog. |
| 98–00 | The creature nearest the point makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **15**)_, becoming Restrained and turning to stone on a failure.  Before its next Turn ends, it repeats this Saving Throw.  On a success, this effect ends on it; on a failure, it becomes Petrified until freed by _Greater Restoration_ or similar magic. |
