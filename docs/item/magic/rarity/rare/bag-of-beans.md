### Bag of Beans
Rare Container (Bag), Consumable
{ .subtitle }

**3d4** beans
{ .subtitle }

##### Dump Beans
**Utilize Action; 1 or more beans:** You can dump beans from the bag, destroying them.  Everything in a **10**-foot radius Sphere centered on them, including you, makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **5d4** Force Damage to the target. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### Plant Bean
**1 hour; 1 bean:** You can plant a bean in dirt or sand and water it.  After **1** minute, the bean disappears and produces an effect from the ground where it was planted.  Roll **1d100**.

| 1d100 | Effect |
|---|---|
| 01 | **5d4** toadstools sprout.  After a creature eats one, roll **1d2**.  On a **1**, it makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **15**)_, taking **5d6** Poison Damage and becoming Sickened for **1** hour on a failure.  On a **2**, it gains **5d6** Temporary Hit Points for **1** hour. |
| 02–10 | A geyser erupts, spouting water, beer, tea, vinegar, wine, or oil **30** feet into the air for **1d4** minutes. |
| 11–20 | A Treant Companion sprouts. |
| 21–30 | An immobile stone statue in your likeness rises and makes verbal threats against you, describing you as a villain and directing newcomers to attack you.  It knows where you are while you are on its plane, and becomes inanimate after **24** hours. |
| 31–40 | A campfire with green flames springs forth and burns for **24** hours or until extinguished. |
| 41–50 | **3** Shrieker Fungi sprout. |
| 51–60 | **1d4 + 4** bright-pink toads crawl forth.  After a creature touches one, it becomes a Large or smaller monster for **1** minute, then disappears. |
| 61–70 | A hungry Bulette burrows up and Attacks. |
| 71–80 | A fruit tree grows bearing **1d10 + 20** fruit, **1d8** of which act as random potions.  The tree vanishes after **1** hour, and picked fruit keeps its magic for **30** days. |
| 81–90 | A nest of **1d4 + 3** rainbow-colored eggs springs up.  After a creature eats one, it makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **20**)_.  On a success, its lowest Ability permanently increases by **1**, chosen at random among equally low Abilities.  On a failure, it takes **10d6** Force Damage. |
| 91–95 | A pyramid with a **60**-foot-square base bursts upward, holding a burial chamber with an Undead and a sarcophagus of treasure. |
| 96–00 | A giant beanstalk sprouts, growing to a height and leading to a destination determined by the DM. |
