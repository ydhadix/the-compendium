### Horn of Blasting
Rare Wondrous Item (Musical Instrument)
{ .subtitle }

##### Blast
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can blow the horn, emitting a blast in a **30**-foot Cone audible out to **600** feet.  Everything in the Cone makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **5d8** Thunder Damage to the target and deafen it for **1** minute. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

You deal **10d8** Thunder Damage to unattended glass and crystal objects in the Cone.

Then, roll **1d10**.  On a **1** or a **2**, the horn explodes, dealing **10d6** Force Damage to you and destroying itself.
