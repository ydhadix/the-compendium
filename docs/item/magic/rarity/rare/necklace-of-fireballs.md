### Necklace of Fireballs
Rare Clothing (Jewelry), Consumable
{ .subtitle }

**1d6 + 3** beads
{ .subtitle }

##### Hurl Bead
**Utilize Action; 1 or more beads:** You can detach beads from this necklace and throw them up to **60** feet, creating the effect of _Fireball_ _(DC **15**)_ where they land.  Increase the Fire Damage by **1d6** for each bead after the first, to a maximum of **12d6**.

The thrown beads are destroyed.
