### Vessel of Commanding Elementals
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

Each Vessel of Commanding Elementals has an Elemental Type.

| Vessel | Elemental Type |
|---|---|
| Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals | Water Elemental |
| Brazier of Commanding Fire Elementals | Fire Elemental |
| Censer of Controlling Air Elementals | Air Elemental |
| Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals | Earth Elemental |

---

##### Conjure Elemental
**Magic Action; Once per Day:** While you are within **5** feet of the vessel, you can conjure an Elemental Companion of the vessel's Elemental Type in the nearest unoccupied space.  It disappears after **1** hour, after it drops to **0** Hit Points, or after you dismiss it.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the elemental to move and take any Action available to it, or dismiss it.
