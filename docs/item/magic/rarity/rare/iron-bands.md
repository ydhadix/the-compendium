### Iron Bands
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Bind
Magic Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

You can throw this sphere at a Huge or smaller creature within **60** feet, making a Ranged Attack Roll _(**+7** to hit)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You Restrain the target until you release it. |

After the Attack misses, or after you release the target as a Bonus Action, the bands contract into a sphere again.

###### Break Bands
Utilize Action
{ .subtitle }

A creature that can touch the bands, including the Restrained creature, can make an Athletics Check _(DC **20**)_.  On a success, this item is destroyed and the Restrained creature is freed.  On a failure, that creature's further attempts automatically fail for **24** hours.
