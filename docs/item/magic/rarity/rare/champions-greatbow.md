### Champion's Greatbow
Rare Weapon (Heavy, Ranged Weapon)
{ .subtitle }

You gain Disadvantage on this weapon's Attack Rolls if your Strength is less than **3**, and its Critical Hit Range increases by **1**.

This weapon can use a Staff as ammunition.  The weapon's Range becomes halved for that attack, and it deals an additional **STR** damage _(the stave's infusions apply, but not its masterwork or techniques, and it isn't destroyed)_.
