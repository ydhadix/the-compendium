### Portable Hole
Rare Container (Cloth)
{ .subtitle }

This cloth folds to the size of a handkerchief and unfolds into a circular sheet **6** feet in diameter.  It weighs next to nothing no matter what it holds, and holds enough air for **1** hour of breathing while closed, divided by the number of breathing creatures inside.

##### Open
**Magic Action:** You can unfold this cloth against a solid surface, creating an extradimensional hole **10** feet deep.  A creature inside can climb out.

##### Close
**Magic Action:** You can fold this cloth up, closing the hole.  Anything inside remains in the extradimensional space.

###### Force Out
Any creature in the extradimensional space can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature makes an Athletics Check _(DC **10**)_.  On a success, it appears within **5** feet of this item.
