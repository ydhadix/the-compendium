### Bolt Rifle
Rare Weapon (Loading, Ranged Weapon), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

**3** Charges (regain **1d3** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

This weapon deals an additional **1d6** Lightning Damage.

You gain Advantage on Attack Rolls with this weapon against targets made of metal or wearing metal Armor.

After you make a Ranged Attack with this weapon, the path of the attack emits Bright Light out to **20** feet for an instant.

##### Lightning Strike
**Free Action; 1 or more Charges:** After you roll an Attack Roll with this weapon, you can increase the d20 roll by **1** for each Charge expended.  After the Attack, each Creature and Object you choose in a **10**-foot Sphere centered on the target makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Failure | You deal **2d6** Lightning Damage for each Charge expended. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |
