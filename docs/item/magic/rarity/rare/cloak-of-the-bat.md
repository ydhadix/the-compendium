### Cloak of the Bat
Rare Clothing (Cloak), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

Your Stealth Checks gain Advantage.

While you grip the edges of this cloak in Dim Light or Darkness, you gain a **40**-foot Fly Speed.

##### Cast
Magic Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

While in Dim Light or Darkness, you can cast _Polymorph_ on yourself, becoming a Bat and retaining your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
