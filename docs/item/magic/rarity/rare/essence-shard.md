### Essence Shard
Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement (Sorcerer)
{ .subtitle }

Each Essence Shard contains the essence of a planar element.  Before you use a Metamagic, you can use this item's effect.

---

#### Variants

| Element | Effect |
|---|---|
| Air     | You Fly up to **60** feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks. |
| Earth   | Choose a Damage Type.  You gain Resistance to the chosen Damage Type until the start of your next Turn. |
| Fire    | Choose one of the spell's targets.  You burn _(**2d10**)_ the target until the start of your next Turn. |
| Water   | You deal **2d6** Cold Damage to anything you choose within a **10**-foot Aura.  Then, each target makes a Fortitude Save or is propelled up to **10** feet away and knocked Prone. |
| Lawful  | Choose Charm, Blindness, Deafness, Fear, Sickness, or Stun. You end the chosen Condition on a creature within **30** feet. |
| Chaotic | Choose one of the spell's targets.  The target's Attack Rolls and Ability Checks gain Disadvantage until the start of your next Turn. |
| Good    | One creature within **30** feet gains **3d6** Temporary Hit Points. |
| Evil    | Choose one of the spell's targets.  Before the chosen target takes Damage from the spell, you deal **3d6** Necrotic Damage to the target. |
| Astral  | You teleport up to **30** feet. |
