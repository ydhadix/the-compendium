### Marble Elephant Figurine
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Summon Elephant
**Magic Action; Once per 7 Days:** You can throw the figurine to a point on the ground within **60** feet, where it transforms into an Elephant Companion.  It stays transformed for **24** hours, until it drops to **0** Hit Points, or until you use this Action again.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the elephant to move and take any Action available to it.
