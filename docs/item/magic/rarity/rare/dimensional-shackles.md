### Dimensional Shackles
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Shackle
**Utilize Action:** You can place these shackles on an Incapacitated Small, Medium, or Large creature.  The target can't teleport or travel to another plane, but can still pass through an interdimensional portal.

You and any creature you designate can use this Action to remove the shackles.

###### Break Free
Any shackled creature can take this Action.

**Utilize Action; Once per 30 Days:** The creature makes an Athletics Check _(DC **30**)_.  On a success, it breaks free and destroys the shackles.
