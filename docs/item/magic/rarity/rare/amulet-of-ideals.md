### Amulet of Ideals
Rare Spellcasting Focus (Holy Symbol), Attunement (Cleric or Paladin)
{ .subtitle }

**5** Charges (regain **1d4 + 1** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

##### Devotion
**Free Action; Once per Day:** You can use your Channel Divinity without expending one of its uses.

##### Ideation
**Free Action; 1 or more Charges:** Before you cast a Domain Spell, you can reduce the Spell Slot Level required by **1** for each Charge expended _(this doesn't change the Spell's Level)_.  You can't use this effect to cast a Spell at a Level you don't have Spell Slots for, or at **6th** Level or higher.
