### Rod of Rulership
Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Command Obedience
Magic Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

Each creature you choose within **120** feet makes a Will Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You Charm the target for **8** hours, and it regards you as its trusted leader. |
| Success | No effect. |

After you or your Allies harm the target, or after you command it to act against its nature, this effect ends on it.
