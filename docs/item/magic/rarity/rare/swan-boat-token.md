### Swan Boat Token
Rare Wondrous Item, Consumable
{ .subtitle }

##### Summon Boat
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can touch this token to a body of water at least **60** feet across, destroying it and summoning a self-propelled swan-shaped boat **50** feet long and **20** feet wide.  The boat moves across water at **6** miles per hour and disappears after **24** hours or after you dismiss it.

###### Steer
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

While aboard, you can command the boat to move or to turn up to **90** degrees, or dismiss it.
