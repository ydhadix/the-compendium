### Whip Token
Rare Wondrous Item, Consumable
{ .subtitle }

##### Conjure Whip
**Magic Action:** You can throw this token to a point within **10** feet, destroying it and conjuring a floating whip.  The whip disappears after **1** hour, after you dismiss it, or after you die or become Incapacitated.

###### Lash
**Bonus Action:** You can make a Melee Spell Attack Roll _(**+7** to hit)_ against a creature within **10** feet of the whip, first moving the whip up to **20** feet.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **1d6 + 5** Force Damage. |
