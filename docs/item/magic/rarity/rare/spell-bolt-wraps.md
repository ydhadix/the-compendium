### Spell Bolt Wraps
Rare Clothing (Gloves), Attunement (Monk)
{ .subtitle }

These armwraps are inscribed with a Spell: _Chill Touch_, _Fire Bolt_, _Ray of Frost_, or _Shocking Grasp_.

**Once per Turn:** Before you make an Unarmed Strike with Flurry of Blows, you can replace the attack with casting the inscribed spell.  Your Monk Ability is your Spellcasting Ability.
