### Cube of Summoning
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Wind Crank
**Magic Action; Once per Day:** You can wind this cube's crank, randomly casting one of the following Spells at 5th Level _(no Concentration required, DC **15**, **+7** to hit)_.

| 1d6 | Spell |
|---|---|
| 1 | _Summon Aberration_ |
| 2 | _Summon Beast_ |
| 3 | _Summon Construct_ |
| 4 | _Summon Dragon_ |
| 5 | _Summon Elemental_ |
| 6 | _Summon Fey_ |
