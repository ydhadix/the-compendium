### Tentacle Rod
Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Lash
**Magic Action:** You can direct this rod's **3** tentacles to each Attack a creature within **15** feet, making a Melee Attack Roll _(**+7** to hit)_ for each.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **1d6** Psychic Damage. |

After all **3** tentacles hit the same target, it makes a Reflex Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You restrain the target.<br>After the target's Turn starts, you deal **3d6** Psychic Damage to it. |
| Success | No effect. |

This effect ends after you become Incapacitated, after you release the target as a Bonus Action, or after the target is more than **15** feet from you.

Before the target's Turn ends, it repeats this Saving Throw.  On a success, this effect ends on the target.
