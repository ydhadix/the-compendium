### Onyx Dog Figurine
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Animate Mastiff
**Magic Action; Once per 7 Days:** You can throw the figurine to a point on the ground within **60** feet, where you animate it as a Mastiff Companion.  It stays animated for **6** hours, until it drops to **0** Hit Points, or until you use this Action again.  The mastiff has an Intelligence of **-1**, speaks Common, and has **60** feet of Blindsight.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the mastiff to move and take any Action available to it.
