### Docent
Rare Wondrous Item, Attunement (Construct Companion)
{ .subtitle }

**4** uses
{ .subtitle }

Your companion gains the following effects:

- Its Intelligence becomes **3**, its Wisdom becomes **2**, and its Charisma becomes **2**, unless those Abilities are already higher.
- It gains Expertise in **2** of Arcana, History, Medicine, Nature, or Perception.
- It can speak any language it understands.

##### Learn Language
Your companion can take this Reaction.

**Reaction; 1 use:** After the companion hears or reads a language it doesn't know, it instantly learns that language.

##### Cast
Your companion can take this Action.

**Magic Action:** The companion casts _Detect Magic_ without a Spell Slot or Components.
