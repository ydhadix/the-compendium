### Ebony Fly Figurine
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Animate Giant Fly
**Magic Action; Once per 2 Days:** You can throw the figurine to a point on the ground within **60** feet, where you animate it as a Giant Fly Companion.  It stays animated for **12** hours, until it drops to **0** Hit Points, or until you use this Action again.  You can ride the fly as a mount.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the fly to move and take any Action available to it.
