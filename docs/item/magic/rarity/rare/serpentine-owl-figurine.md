### Serpentine Owl Figurine
Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Summon Giant Owl
**Magic Action; Once per 2 Days:** You can throw the figurine to a point on the ground within **60** feet, where it transforms into a Giant Owl Companion.  It stays transformed for **8** hours, until it drops to **0** Hit Points, or until you use this Action again.  You and the owl gain Telepathy with each other at any range on the same plane.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the owl to move and take any Action available to it.
