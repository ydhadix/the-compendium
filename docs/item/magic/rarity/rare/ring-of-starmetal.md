### Ring of Starmetal
Rare Clothing (Jewelry), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

After a Short Rest, you can bond an object weighing no more than **25** pounds that is at least **50**% metal to the ring.  Only one item can be bound to the ring at a time.

##### Summon
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

While the bound item is within **60** feet and unobstructed, you can pull it to your hand.  The returning item bounces harmlessly against creatures and objects as it passes by.
