### Flame Tongue
Rare Weapon (Melee), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Ignite
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You can engulf this weapon in flames until you use this Action again or until you drop or stow the weapon.

While ablaze, this weapon emits Bright Light out to **40** feet and Dim Light **40** feet further, and deals an additional **2d6** Fire Damage.
