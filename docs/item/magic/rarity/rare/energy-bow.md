### Energy Bow
Rare Weapon (Bow), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

This weapon needs no ammunition, conjuring an arrow of golden energy for each Attack.  Its arrows deal Force Damage instead of Piercing Damage, and emit Bright Light out to **20** feet and Dim Light **20** feet further until they hit or miss.

##### Arrow of Restraint
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

Before you make a Ranged Attack with this weapon, you can forgo its damage.  After the Attack hits, the target makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You Restrain the target for **1** minute. |
| Success | No effect. |

###### Break Free
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

A creature Restrained this way can make an Athletics Check _(DC **20**)_.  On a success, this effect ends on it.

##### Arrow of Transport
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can fire an arrow at an Ally, or at an unattended object that fits in a **5**-foot Cube, within **60** feet.  The target teleports to an unoccupied space within **10** feet of you.

##### Energy Ladder
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can fire a flurry of arrows at a wall within **60** feet, forming a ladder of glowing rungs up to **60** feet long on the wall for **1** minute.
