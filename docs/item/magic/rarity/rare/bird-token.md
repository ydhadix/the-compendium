### Bird Token
Rare Wondrous Item, Consumable
{ .subtitle }

##### Summon Bird
**Magic Action:** You can toss this token **5** feet into the air, destroying it and summoning a Roc Companion that can't Attack.  It carries up to **500** pounds at its full Fly Speed, or up to **1,000** pounds at half that Speed.  It disappears after flying its maximum distance for a day, after it drops to **0** Hit Points, or after you dismiss it.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the bird to move, or dismiss it.
