### Frost Brand
Rare Weapon (Blade or Fence), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

This weapon deals an additional **1d6** Cold Damage, and you gain Fire Resistance.

In freezing temperatures, this weapon emits Bright Light out to **10** feet and Dim Light **10** feet further.

##### Quench
Free Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

After you draw this weapon, you can extinguish all nonmagical flames within **30** feet.
