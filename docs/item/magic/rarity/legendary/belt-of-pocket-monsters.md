### Belt of Pocket Monsters
Legendary Clothing (Belt), Attunement (Beast Master Ranger)
{ .subtitle }

This exotic leather belt bears six talon-shaped claws, each gripping a **3**-inch stone-grey ball. Each ball is divided into two hemispheres by a thin seam and hinge with a small button at its center.  Only an attuned creature can attach or remove a ball from the belt.

#### Linking Monsters
Each ball can house the spirit of a Beast or Monstrosity, which can be conjured as a Primal Companion (called a _monster_).  A ball that remains more than **30** feet from the belt for **1** minute vanishes and reappears in its place on the belt.

After you attune to the belt, each of your Primal Companion forms becomes linked to a ball.  While linked, a ball's color and pattern change to reflect the monster bound within it _(you determine its appearance)_.

For each linked ball, reduce your daily Gotcha! uses by **1**.

#### Conjuring Monsters
Whenever you would conjure a Primal Companion or change its form, you can instead choose any monster linked to the belt.

All monsters linked to the belt share the same Hit Points, Attunement Slots, and equipment.  After you conjure a monster:

- It appears wearing any equipment worn by the previous monster.
- Any equipment that can't be worn due to the new monster's anatomy falls to the ground at the monster's feet.
- If the new monster's Hit Point Maximum is lower than the previous monster's current Hit Points, the new monster's Hit Points equal its Hit Point Maximum.

##### Command Word
**Magic Action:** If you have a free hand, you can speak **1** of the following Command Words to use its effect.

| Command | |
|---|---|
| I Choose You! | You throw a linked ball up to **30** feet, conjuring its monster where the ball lands _(or the nearest unoccupied space large enough to hold it)_.  If you already have a monster conjured, it immediately returns to its ball _(no action required)_. |
| Return! | Your conjured monster returns to its ball, along with anything it is wearing.  Anything the monster was carrying falls to the ground in its previous space. |
| Gotcha! | **6 times per Day:** You throw an unlinked ball at a Beast or Monstrosity within **30** feet.  The target makes a Capture Save _(DC **Ranger Spell Save DC**)_; on a failure, the creature's body vanishes and its spirit becomes bound to the thrown ball. |
| Release! | You release a linked monster.  The creature's original body reappears within **5** feet, healed to its Hit Point Maximum.  A released creature behaves according to its nature and can't be captured again by the belt. |

###### Capture Saves
A Capture Save adds the creature's Level to the total instead of an Ability or Proficiency Bonus.  The creature automatically succeeds unless it is Incapacitated, Downed, Stabilized, or an Ally, and gains Advantage on the Save if it has more than **0** Hit Points.

#### Ending Attunement
After your attunement to the belt ends:

- Your normal Primal Companion forms become unlinked from the belt.
- If your currently conjured monster is not one of your normal Primal Companion forms, it vanishes and is replaced by a Primal Companion of your choice.

If the belt remains unattuned for **24** hours, all linked monsters are released as though by the Release! command.
