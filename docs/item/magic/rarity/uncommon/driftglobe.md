### Driftglobe
Uncommon Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Command Word
**Free Action:** You can speak **1** of the following Command Words to use its effect.

| Command | |
|---|---|
| Illuminate | This item gains the effect of _Light_ for **1** hour, or until you speak this Command Word again. |
| Shine | **Once per Day:** This item gains the effect of _Daylight_ for **1** hour. |
| Follow | The illuminated globe rises and floats up to **5** feet above the ground, following you by the shortest route until you or another creature grasps it. If it can't move, it sinks to the ground and its light goes out. |
