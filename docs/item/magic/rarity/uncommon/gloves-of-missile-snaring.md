### Gloves of Missile Snaring
Uncommon Clothing (Gloves), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Catch Missile
Reaction
{ .subtitle }

Before you are hit by a Ranged Weapon Attack, if you have a free hand, you can reduce its damage by **1d10 + DEX**. If you reduce the damage to **0**, you catch the ammunition or weapon, provided it is small enough to hold in that hand.
