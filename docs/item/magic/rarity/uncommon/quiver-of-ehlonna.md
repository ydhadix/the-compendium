### Quiver of Ehlonna
Uncommon Container (Quiver)
{ .subtitle }

This quiver's **3** compartments are larger than their outside dimensions.  The quiver can hold up to **60** Arrows or Bolts, **18** Javelins or similar objects, and **6** long objects such as Bows or Staves.  You can draw any item it holds as if from an ordinary quiver.
