### First Mate's Scabbard
Uncommon Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Strike First
**Bonus Action; Once per Day:** You can choose yourself or an Ally within **30** feet.  You attack anything within the chosen creature's Reach using the weapon sheathed in this scabbard.  You gain Advantage on the Attack Roll if the target is unaware of this item's effect.

After the Attack, the weapon flies to the open hand of the chosen creature or lands on the ground at their feet.
