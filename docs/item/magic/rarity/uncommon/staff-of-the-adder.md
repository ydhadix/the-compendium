### Staff of the Adder
Uncommon Weapon (Staff), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Animate Head
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You can transform the staff's head into an animate, venomous snake.  It stays transformed for **1** minute, or until you use this Action again.

The transformed head has AC **15**, **20** Hit Points, and Poison and Psychic Immunity. If the snake drops to **0** Hit Points, the staff is destroyed; if it reverts to inanimate form before losing all its Hit Points, it regains them all.

###### Strike
Free Action, 1 use per Turn
{ .subtitle }

After you Attack, you can make a Melee Weapon Attack using the snake head.  The attack uses your Wisdom, Proficiency Bonus, and Reach.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **1d6** Piercing Damage and **3d6** Poison Damage. |
