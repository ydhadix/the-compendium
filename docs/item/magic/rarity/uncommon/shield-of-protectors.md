### Shield of Protectors
Uncommon Shield (Any)
{ .subtitle }

##### Protector's Ward
Reaction, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

Before a creature within **15** feet is hit with an Attack, the target makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You teleport to the target's space and propel them to an unoccupied space within **5** feet.  Then, the Attack hits you instead _(even if the Attack Roll doesn't meet or exceed your AC)_. |
| Success | You teleport to an unoccupied space within **5** feet of the target. |
