### Shield of Protectors
Uncommon Shield (Any)
{ .subtitle }

##### Protector's Ward
**Reaction; Once per Day:** Before a creature within **15** feet is hit with an Attack, you can force the target to make a Fortitude Save _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You teleport to the target's space and propel them **5** feet away from you.  Then, the Attack hits you instead _(even if the Attack Roll doesn't meet or exceed your AC)_. |
| Success | You teleport to within **5** feet of the target. |
