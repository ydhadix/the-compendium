### Javelin of Lightning
Uncommon Weapon (Staff)
{ .subtitle }

This weapon can deal Lightning Damage instead of its normal Damage Type.

##### Lightning Bolt
**Magic Action; Once per Day:** You can hurl the weapon at one creature within **120** feet as a bolt of lightning, forming a **5**-foot-wide Line between you and the target. Everything in the Line makes a Reflex Save _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **4d6** Lightning Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

Then, the weapon reappears in your hand.
