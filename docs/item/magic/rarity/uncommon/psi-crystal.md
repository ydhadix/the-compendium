### Psi Crystal
Uncommon Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

You gain Telepathy and the crystal emits light.  The range of the Telepathy and light increases with your Intelligence.

| Intelligence | Telepathy | Bright Light | Dim Light |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **15** feet | — | **5** feet |
| **1** | **30** feet | **5** feet | **5** feet |
| **2** | **60** feet | **10** feet | **10** feet |
| **3+** | **120** feet | **15** feet | **15** feet |
