### Eversmoking Bottle
Uncommon Container (Flask)
{ .subtitle }

##### Open
**Utilize Action:** You can open the bottle, causing thick smoke to billow in a **60**-foot Aura around it, Heavily Obscuring the area and growing **10** feet each minute up to **120** feet.

##### Close
**Utilize Action:** You can close the bottle, fixing the smoke in place until it disperses after **10** minutes; a strong wind disperses it after **1** minute.
