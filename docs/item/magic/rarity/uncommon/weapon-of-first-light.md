### Weapon of First Light
Uncommon Weapon (Sidearm, Foil, or Blade), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

You gain Advantage on Initiative Checks.

After you roll Initiative, if you are first in the Initiative Order, your Speed increases by **15** feet until the end of your next Turn.

##### First Light
**Bonus Action:** You can give this item the effect of _Light_ for **1** hour, or until you use this Action again.  While the weapon is glowing, it can deal Radiant Damage instead of its normal Damage Type.
