### Berserker Axe
Cursed Weapon (Blade or Polearm), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

Your Hit Point Maximum increases by **LV**.

You can't willingly part with this weapon, keeping it within Reach at all times, and your Attack Rolls with other weapons gain Disadvantage.

##### Berserk
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After a creature deals damage to you while you carry this weapon, you make a Will Save _(DC **15**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You go berserk until you start a Turn with no creature within **60** feet. |
| Success | No effect. |

While berserk, you target the nearest creature, choosing at random between equally near creatures.  While it is your Turn, you move as close to the target as possible and Attack it with this weapon.  After the target dies or moves more than **60** feet away, you target the next nearest creature.
