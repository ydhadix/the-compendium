### Ammunition of Slaying
Very Rare Weapon (Ammunition), Consumable
{ .subtitle }

Each Ammunition of Slaying has a Creature Type.

##### Slay
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After this ammunition damages a creature of its Creature Type, the target makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **17**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal an additional **6d10** Force Damage to the target. |
| Success | You deal half as much additional damage. |

Then, this ammunition becomes nonmagical.
