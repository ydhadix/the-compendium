### Obsidian Steed Figurine
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Animate Nightmare
**Magic Action; Once per 5 Days:** You can throw the figurine to a point on the ground within **60** feet, where you animate it as a Nightmare Companion that fights only to defend itself.  Then, roll **1d10**.  On a **1**, the nightmare becomes an Enemy and ignores your commands; while you ride it, it instead transports you both to a random location on Hades, then reverts to figurine form.

It stays animated for **24** hours, until it drops to **0** Hit Points, or until you use this Action again.

###### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the nightmare to move and take any Action available to it.
