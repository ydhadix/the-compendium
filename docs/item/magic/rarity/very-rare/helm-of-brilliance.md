### Helm of Brilliance
Very Rare Clothing (Hat), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

This helm is set with **1d10** diamonds, **2d10** rubies, **3d10** fire opals, and **4d10** opals.  A gem pried from the helm crumbles to dust, and the helm loses its magic after all its gems are removed or destroyed.

While the helm has at least one ruby, you gain Fire Resistance.

While the helm has at least one diamond, it emits a **30**-foot Aura.  While at least one Undead is in the Aura, the Aura fills with Dim Light and deals **1d6** Radiant Damage to each Undead that starts its Turn there.

##### Ignite Weapon
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

While the helm has at least one fire opal, you can engulf one weapon you hold in harmless flames that emit Bright Light out to **10** feet and Dim Light **10** feet further.  While ablaze, that weapon deals an additional **1d6** Fire Damage.  The flames last until you extinguish them as a Bonus Action or until you drop or stow the weapon.

##### Cast
You can cast the following Spells _(DC **17**)_, destroying the listed gem:

| Spell | Action | Gem |
|---|---|---|
| _Daylight_ | Magic Action | Opal |
| _Fireball_ | Magic Action | Fire Opal |
| _Wall of Fire_ | Magic Action | Ruby |
| _Prismatic Spray_ | Magic Action | Diamond |

##### Overload
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

After you fail a Saving Throw against a Spell that deals Fire Damage to you, roll **1d20**.  On a **1**, the helm emits beams from its remaining gems and is destroyed.  Everything in a **60**-foot Aura makes a Reflex Save _(DC **17**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal Radiant Damage to the target equal to the number of gems in the helm. |
| Success | No effect. |
