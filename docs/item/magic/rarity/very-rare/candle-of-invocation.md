### Candle of Invocation
Very Rare Wondrous Item, Consumable, Attunement
{ .subtitle }

This candle burns for a total of **4** hours before it is destroyed.

While lit, it emits Dim Light out to **30** feet.  In that light, your d20 rolls gain Advantage, and a Cleric or Druid can cast their prepared 1st-Level Spells without expending a Spell Slot.

##### Light
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can light or snuff out this candle, deducting the time it burned in increments of **1** minute.
