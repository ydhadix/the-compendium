### Candle of Invocation
Very Rare Wondrous Item (Candle), Consumable, Attunement
{ .subtitle }

This candle burns for a total of **4** hours, and is destroyed once that time runs out.  While lit, it emits Dim Light out to **30** feet.

In that light, you gain Advantage on Ability Checks, Attack Rolls, and Saving Throws, and a Cleric or Druid can cast their prepared 1st-Level Spells without expending a Spell Slot.
