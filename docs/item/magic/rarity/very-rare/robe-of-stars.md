### Robe of Stars
Very Rare Clothing (Clothes), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

**6** Charges (regain **1d6** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

Your Saving Throws increase by **1**.

##### Cast
**Magic Action; 1 Charge:** You can cast _Magic Missile_ at 5th Level.

##### Enter the Astral Plane
**Magic Action:** You can enter the Astral Plane with everything you wear and carry, remaining until you use this Action again.  You reappear in the space you left, or the nearest unoccupied space.
