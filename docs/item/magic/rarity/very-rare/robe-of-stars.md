### Robe of Stars
Very Rare Clothing (Clothes), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

**6** Charges (regain **1d6** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

Your Saving Throws increase by **1**.

##### Cast
Magic Action, 1 Charge
{ .subtitle }

You can pluck one of the six large stars from this robe to cast _Magic Missile_ at 5th Level.

##### Enter the Astral Plane
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can enter the Astral Plane with everything you wear and carry, remaining until you use this Action again.  You reappear in the space you left, or the nearest unoccupied space.
