### Oathbow
Very Rare Weapon (Bow), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

You gain Advantage on Ranged Attack Rolls with this weapon against a creature you Marked, and those Attacks ignore Long Range penalties and Cover and deal an additional **3d6** Piercing Damage.

While you have a creature Marked, you gain Disadvantage on Attack Rolls with other weapons.

##### Swear Enmity
**Free Action:** Before you make a Ranged Attack with this weapon, you can Mark the target for **7** days or until it dies.  You can have only one creature Marked this way at a time.
