### Oathbow
Very Rare Weapon (Bow), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

Your Ranged Attack Rolls with this weapon against a creature you Marked gain Advantage, ignore Long Range penalties and Cover, and deal an additional **3d6** Piercing Damage.

While you have a creature Marked, your Attack Rolls with other weapons gain Disadvantage.

##### Swear Enmity
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

Before you make a Ranged Attack with this weapon, you can mark the target for **7** days or until it dies.  You can have only one creature Marked this way at a time.
