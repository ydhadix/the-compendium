### Manual of Golems
Very Rare Wondrous Item, Consumable
{ .subtitle }

Each Manual of Golems has a Golem, which determines the time and gold needed to build it.

A creature that reads this manual without being a Spellcaster with two 5th-Level Spell Slots takes **6d6** Psychic Damage.

##### Build Golem
You can work without interruption, resting no more than **8** hours per day, for the listed Time and Cost.  Then, this manual is consumed in eldritch flames, and sprinkling its ashes on the golem animates it as your Companion.
