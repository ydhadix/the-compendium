### Manual of Golems
Very Rare Wondrous Item, Consumable
{ .subtitle }

Each Manual of Golems has a Golem, which determines the time and gold needed to build it.

| Golem | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Clay | **30** days | **65,000** gold |
| Flesh | **60** days | **50,000** gold |
| Stone | **90** days | **80,000** gold |
| Iron | **120** days | **100,000** gold |

---

A creature that reads this manual without being a Spellcaster with two 5th-Level Spell Slots takes **6d6** Psychic Damage.

#### Build Golem
You can work without interruption, resting no more than **8** hours per day, for the listed Time and Cost.  After that time, this manual is destroyed and the golem animates as your Companion.
