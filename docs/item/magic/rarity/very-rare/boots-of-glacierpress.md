### Boots of Glacierpress
Very Rare Clothing (Boots)
{ .subtitle }

**3** Charges (regain **1d3** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks, and you can move in a straight line through the space of any Huge or smaller creature.

##### Glacial Surge
Free Action, 1 Charge
{ .subtitle }

Before you move into another creature's space, you can force the target to make a Fortitude Save _(DC 17)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | While you move in a straight line, the target is propelled along the ground ahead of you until the end of the turn or you end this effect _(no action required)_. |
| Success | No effect. |
