### Bag of Devouring
Very Rare Container (Bag)
{ .subtitle }

This bag resembles a Bag of Holding, but is the feeding orifice of a gigantic extradimensional creature.  Turning it inside out closes the orifice.

Animal or vegetable matter placed wholly inside is devoured and lost forever.  The bag holds a cubic foot of inanimate objects, but once per Day it swallows them and spits them out onto another plane.

If this bag is pierced or torn, it is destroyed, and everything inside travels to a random location on the Astral Plane.

##### Reach Inside
**Utilize Action:** You can reach into the bag.  Roll **1d10**.  On a **5** or lower, the bag pulls you inside.

After the end of a creature inside the bag's Turn, the creature is devoured and destroyed.

###### Escape
Any creature inside the bag can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature makes an Athletics Check _(DC **17**)_.  On a success, it climbs out.

###### Extract
Any creature outside the bag can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature makes an Athletics Check _(DC **20**)_, provided the bag doesn't propel it inside first.  On a success, it pulls one creature out of the bag.
