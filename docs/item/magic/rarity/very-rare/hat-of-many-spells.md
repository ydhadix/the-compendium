### Hat of Many Spells
Very Rare Clothing (Hat), Attunement (Wizard)
{ .subtitle }

You can use this hat as a Spellcasting Focus for your Wizard Spells.  Spells you cast using it gain a Somatic component: you reach into the hat and draw the Spell out.

##### Unknown Spell
**Free Action; 3 times per Day:** You can choose a 1st-Level or higher Wizard Spell that you don't know, of a Level you can cast and without Material components costing more than **1,000** gold.  You expend a Spell Slot of the Spell's Level and make an Arcana Check _(DC **10 + Spell Level**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Success | You cast the Spell using its normal Casting Time. |
| Fail | The casting fails, and you roll **1d100** on the following table instead. |

| 1d100 | Effect |
|---|---|
| 01–50 | You cast a random Spell.  Roll **1d10**: on a **1**, _Enlarge/Reduce_ (Enlarge); on a **2**, _Enlarge/Reduce_ (Reduce); on a **3**, _Faerie Fire_; on a **4**, _Fireball_; on a **5**, _Gust of Wind_; on a **6**, _Lesser Invisibility_ on yourself; on a **7**, _Lightning Bolt_; on an **8**, _Phantasmal Force_; on a **9**, _Polymorph_; on a **10**, _Stinking Cloud_. |
| 51–55 | You are Stunned until the end of your next Turn, believing something awesome just happened. |
| 56–60 | A harmless swarm of butterflies fills a **10**-foot Cube within **30** feet, dispersing after **1** minute. |
| 61–65 | You pull a nonmagical object from the hat.  Roll **1d4**: on a **1**, a Vial of Acid; on a **2**, a Flask of Alchemist's Fire; on a **3**, a Crowbar; on a **4**, a lit Torch. |
| 66–70 | You are Sickened for **1** hour. |
| 71–75 | You are Petrified until the end of your next Turn. |
| 76–80 | You pull a nonmagical object from the hat.  Roll **1d4**: on a **1**, a Dagger; on a **2**, a Rope with a Grappling Hook tied to one end; on a **3**, a bag of Caltrops; on a **4**, a gem worth **50** gold. |
| 81–85 | A creature appears in the nearest unoccupied space, acts as it normally would, and disappears after **1** hour or after it drops to **0** Hit Points.  Roll **1d4**: on a **1**, a Camel; on a **2**, a Constrictor Snake; on a **3**, an Elephant; on a **4**, a Mule. |
| 86–90 | An Enemy Level 2 Swarm of Bats flies out of the hat, occupies your space, and Attacks you. |
| 91–95 | A vertical **10**-foot-diameter two-way portal to another plane opens within **30** feet and remains until the end of your next Turn. |
| 96–00 | You pull a magic item from the hat, which disappears after **1** hour unless consumed or destroyed.  Roll **1d6**: on a **1** to **3**, a Common item; on a **4** or **5**, an Uncommon item; on a **6**, a Rare item. |
