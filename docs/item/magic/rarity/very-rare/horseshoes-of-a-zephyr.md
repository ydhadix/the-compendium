### Horseshoes of a Zephyr
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

These horseshoes come in a set of four.  While all four are affixed to the same creature, it floats **4** inches above any surface, letting it cross or stand above nonsolid and unstable surfaces.  It leaves no tracks, ignores Difficult Terrain, and can travel up to **12** hours a day without gaining Exhaustion levels.

##### Affix
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can affix one horseshoe to the hoof of a creature within Reach, or remove one.
