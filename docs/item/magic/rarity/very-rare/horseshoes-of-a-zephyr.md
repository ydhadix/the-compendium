### Horseshoes of a Zephyr
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

These horseshoes come in a set of **4**.  While all of them are affixed to the same creature, it gains the following effects:

- It floats **4** inches above any surface, letting it cross or stand above nonsolid and unstable surfaces.
- It leaves no tracks.
- It ignores Difficult Terrain.
- It can travel up to **12** hours a day without gaining Exhaustion levels.

##### Affix
**1 minute:** You can affix all **4** horseshoes to the hooves of a creature within Reach, or remove them.
