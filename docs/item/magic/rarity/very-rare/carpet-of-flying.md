### Carpet of Flying
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

Each Carpet of Flying has a Size, which determines its Capacity and Fly Speed.  A carpet can carry up to twice its Capacity, halving its Fly Speed.

##### Command Word
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can make this carpet hover and fly.  It moves according to your directions while you are within **30** feet of it.
