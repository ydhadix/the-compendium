### Carpet of Flying
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

Each Carpet of Flying has Dimensions, which determine its Capacity and Hover Speed.

| Dimensions | Capacity | Hover Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **3** ft. × **5** ft. | **200** lb. | **80** feet |
| **4** ft. × **6** ft. | **400** lb. | **60** feet |
| **5** ft. × **7** ft. | **600** lb. | **40** feet |
| **6** ft. × **9** ft. | **800** lb. | **30** feet |

---

A carpet can carry up to twice its Capacity, halving its Hover Speed.

##### Command Word: Fly
**Magic Action:** You can make this carpet hover until you use this Action again.  While you are within **30** feet, the carpet moves according to your directions.
