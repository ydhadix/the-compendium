### Crack of Thunder
Very Rare Weapon (Staff, Fence, or Blade)
{ .subtitle }

**3** Charges (regain **3** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

You gain Lightning Resistance.

This weapon deals an additional **1d6** Lightning damage.

After you take **10** or more Lightning damage _(before Resistance or Immunity)_, this weapon regains **1** Charge.

##### Surge
**Free Action; 1 Charge:** Before you make a Melee Attack using this weapon, you can increase the attack's Reach by **15** feet and increase its damage by **2d6** Lightning damage.  After the attack, each Creature and Object you choose within **10** feet of the target makes a Fortitude Save _(DC 17)_.  The target of the attack gains Disadvantage on the Saving Throw.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **3d6** Thunder damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### Lightning Rod
**Reaction:** Before a Creature or Object within **60** feet takes Lightning damage, you can take any of that damage instead.
