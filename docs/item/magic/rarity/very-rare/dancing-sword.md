### Dancing Sword
Very Rare Weapon (Blade or Fence), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

##### Dance
**Bonus Action:** You can toss this weapon into the air, where it hovers.  You can move the weapon up to **30** feet, then Attack one creature within **5** feet of it.  The weapon uses your Attack Roll and adds your Ability to its Damage Rolls.

While it hovers, you can use this Action again to move the weapon up to your Speed to a space within **30** feet of you, then Attack one creature within **5** feet of it.

After this weapon Attacks a fourth time, after you grasp it, or after you move more than **30** feet from it, it stops hovering and returns to your hand.  Without a free hand, it falls to the ground in your space.
