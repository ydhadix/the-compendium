### Rod of Security
Very Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus)
{ .subtitle }

##### Retreat
Magic Action, 1 use per 10 Days
{ .subtitle }

You can transport yourself and up to **199** Allies within **100** feet to a demiplane whose form you choose.  It holds enough food and water to sustain its visitors and can't harm them, and anything else there exists only there.

Each hour spent there heals a visitor as though it had spent **1** Hit Die, and visitors don't age.  They can remain for **200** days divided by the number of creatures present, rounded down.

After the time runs out, or after you use this Action again, every visitor reappears in the space it left or the nearest unoccupied space.
