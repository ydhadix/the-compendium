### Efreeti Bottle
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

##### Unstopper
Magic Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

You can remove this bottle's stopper, releasing a cloud of smoke.  Before your Turn ends, the smoke vanishes and an Efreeti appears in an unoccupied space within **30** feet.

The first time you open this bottle, roll **1d10** to determine the Efreeti's response.

| 1d10 | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Efreeti is an Enemy and Attacks you.  After **5** Rounds it disappears, and this bottle loses its magic. |
| 2–9 | The Efreeti is a Companion for **1** hour, then returns to the bottle.  The stopper can't be removed for **24** hours.  This happens the next two times the bottle is opened; on the fourth, the Efreeti escapes and this bottle loses its magic. |
| 10 | The Efreeti casts _Wish_ once for you, then disappears, and this bottle loses its magic. |
