### Mirror of Life Trapping
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

This mirror is **4** feet tall and **2** feet wide, and has Armor Class **11**, **10** Hit Points, Poison and Psychic Immunity, and Bludgeoning Vulnerability.  It shatters and is destroyed at **0** Hit Points, freeing everything trapped inside.

This mirror holds twelve extradimensional cells, each an infinite fog-filled expanse with visibility out to **10** feet.  A creature trapped in a cell doesn't age and doesn't need to eat, drink, or sleep, and can escape only through magic that permits planar travel.

#### Command Word
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

##### Awaken / Sleep
While within **5** feet of this mirror, you can activate or deactivate it.

While active, each creature other than you that sees its reflection within **30** feet makes a Will Save _(DC **17**)_.  A creature that knows this mirror's nature gains Advantage, and a Construct automatically succeeds.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | The target is trapped in one of the mirror's cells with everything it wears and carries. |
| Success | No effect. |

If every cell is occupied, the mirror frees one trapped creature at random, which appears in an unoccupied space in sight of the mirror and facing away from it.

###### Appear
You can name one creature trapped in this mirror or call out a cell by number.  That creature appears on the mirror's surface, and you can communicate with it.

###### Release
You can free one creature trapped in this mirror.  It appears with its possessions in the nearest unoccupied space, facing away from the mirror.
