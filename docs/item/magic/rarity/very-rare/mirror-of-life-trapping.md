### Mirror of Life Trapping
Very Rare Wondrous Item
{ .subtitle }

This mirror is **4** feet tall and **2** feet wide, and has Armor Class **11**, **10** Hit Points, and Bludgeoning Vulnerability.  It shatters and is destroyed at **0** Hit Points, freeing everything trapped inside.

This mirror holds twelve extradimensional cells, each an infinite fog-filled expanse with visibility out to **10** feet.  A creature trapped in a cell doesn't age and doesn't need to eat, drink, or sleep, and can escape only through magic that permits planar travel.

##### Command Word
**Magic Action:** You can speak **1** of the following Command Words to use its effect.

| Command | |
|---|---|
| Awaken | While within **5** feet of this mirror, you activate it.  While active, each creature other than you that sees its reflection within **30** feet makes a Will Save _(DC **17**)_.  A creature that knows this mirror's nature gains Advantage, and a Construct automatically succeeds.  On a failure, the target is trapped in one of the mirror's cells with everything it wears and carries.<br>If every cell is occupied, the mirror frees one trapped creature at random, which appears in sight of the mirror and facing away from it. |
| Sleep | While within **5** feet of this mirror, you deactivate it. |
| Appear | You name one creature trapped in this mirror or call out a cell by number.  That creature appears on the mirror's surface, and you can communicate with it. |
| Release | You name one creature trapped in this mirror or call out a cell by number.  That creature appears with its possessions in the nearest unoccupied space, facing away from the mirror. |
