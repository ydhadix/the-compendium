### Cauldron of Rebirth
Very Rare Spellcasting Focus (Any), Attunement (Druid or Warlock)
{ .subtitle }

This cauldron serves as a Material component for _Scrying_.

##### Brew Potion
1 minute, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

After you finish a Long Rest, you can brew a Potion of Greater Healing.  It loses its magic after **24** hours if not consumed.

##### Resize
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can grow this cauldron large enough for a Medium creature to crouch within, or revert it, harmlessly shunting anything that can't fit to the nearest unoccupied space.

##### Raise Dead
8 hours, 1 use per 7 Days
{ .subtitle }

You can place a Humanoid corpse in the enlarged cauldron and cover it with **200** pounds of salt worth **10** gold.  The salt is consumed, and after the next Long Rest the creature returns to life as though by _Raise Dead_.
