### Cauldron of Rebirth
Very Rare Wondrous Item, Attunement (Druid or Warlock)
{ .subtitle }

You can use this cauldron as a Spellcasting Focus, and it serves as a Material component for _Scrying_.

##### Brew Potion
**1 minute; Once per Day:** You can brew a Potion of Greater Healing.  It loses its magic after **24** hours if not consumed.

##### Resize
**Magic Action:** You can transform this cauldron into one large enough for a Medium creature to crouch within, or revert it, harmlessly shunting anything that can't fit to the nearest unoccupied space.

##### Raise Dead
**8 hours; Once per 7 Days:** You can place a Humanoid corpse in the enlarged cauldron and cover it with **200** pounds of salt worth **10** gold.  The salt is consumed, and after the next Long Rest the creature returns to life as though by _Raise Dead_.
