### Kinetic Gi
Very Rare Clothing (Clothes)
{ .subtitle }

While wearing the gi and Unarmored, you gain the following benefits:

- Your Speed increases by **10** feet.
- Opportunity Attacks against you gain Disadvantage.  After you move at least **15** feet in a line, you don't provoke Opportunity Attacks until you move in a different direction.
- After you move at least **15** feet in a line, your Acrobatics checks increase by **1d6** until you stop moving or move in a different direction.  If you're a Monk, your Acrobatics checks increase by your **Martial Arts Die** instead.
- After you move at least **15** feet in a line, your next Melee Attack deals an additional **1d6** Force damage for every **15** feet you moved without stopping or moving in a different direction.
