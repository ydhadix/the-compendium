### Rod of Alertness
Very Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

Your Initiative and Perception Checks gain Advantage.

##### Cast
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can cast the following Spells:

| Spell  | Charges |
|---|---|
| _Detect Evil and Good_ | — |
| _Detect Magic_ | — |
| _Detect Poison and Disease_ | — |
| _See Invisibility_ | — |

##### Protective Aura
Magic Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

You can plant this rod in the ground, where its head emits Bright Light out to **60** feet and Dim Light **60** feet further for **10** minutes.  In that Bright Light, you and your Allies gain **1** to Armor Class and Saving Throws and sense the location of any Invisible creature there.

This effect ends early after a creature removes the rod from the ground as a Magic Action.
