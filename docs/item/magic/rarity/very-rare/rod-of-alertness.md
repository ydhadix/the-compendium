### Rod of Alertness
Very Rare Spellcasting Focus (Arcane Focus), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

You gain Advantage on Initiative and Perception Checks.

##### Cast
**Magic Action:** You can cast _Detect Evil and Good_, _Detect Magic_, _Detect Poison and Disease_, or _See Invisibility_.

##### Protective Aura
**Magic Action; Once per Day:** You can plant this rod in the ground, where its head emits Bright Light out to **60** feet and Dim Light **60** feet further for **10** minutes.  In that Bright Light, the Armor Class and Saving Throws of you and your Allies increase by **1**, and each of you senses the location of any Invisible creature there.

This effect ends early after a creature removes the rod from the ground as a Magic Action.
