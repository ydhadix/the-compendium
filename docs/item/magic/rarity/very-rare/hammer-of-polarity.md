### Hammer of Polarity
Very Rare Weapon (Heavy, Bludgeoning Weapon), Attunement (Spellcaster)
{ .subtitle }

**7** Charges (regain **1d4 + 3** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

You can use this weapon as a Spellcasting Focus. Spells you cast add your Spellcasting Ability to Lightning and Thunder Damage.

This weapon deals an additional **1d6** Lightning Damage and has Thrown _(**20**/**60** feet)_. After a Ranged Attack with this weapon hits or misses, it returns to your hand.

##### Magnetic Compass
**Magic Action:** You can make this weapon hover within your Reach, remaining upright and leaving your hands free. The weapon moves around you to indicate magnetic north until you use this Action again.

##### Attraction Point
**Free Action; Once per Turn:** After you hit a target up to your Size with this weapon, you can propel the target up to **10** feet closer or knock the target Prone.

##### Cast
**Reaction; 1 Charge:** You can cast _Shield_.

##### Emergency Repulsion
**Reaction; 4 Charges:** Before you or a creature within **5** feet of you takes damage, you can cast _Resilient Sphere_ _(centered on the target, no Concentration required)_.
