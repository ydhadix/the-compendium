# Magic Items

## Using Magic Items
**Magic Items** are ordinary gear powered by **Infusions**, magical properties beyond what mundane craftsmanship can achieve.

Magic Items usually require wearing or wielding them properly, just like normal items. Magic Items that don't fit the standard [Item Types](/item/index.md#item-types) are called Wondrous Items, and their usage varies.

### Identification
Some Magic Items are indistinguishable from their nonmagical counterparts, while others are obviously magical.

- _Detect Magic_ is the fastest way to determine whether an item is infused with magic.
- _Identify_ is the fastest way to reveal an item's infused properties.
- Wearing or attempting to use an item can offer hints about its magical nature. For Potions, a little taste is enough to tell you what a Potion does.
- You can spend a Short Rest focused on only that item while in physical contact with it _(this can't be the same Short Rest used to Attune to the item)_. After the Short Rest, you learn the item's properties and how to use them.

### Attunement
**Attunement** is a special bond some Magic Items require before you can use their infused properties.

#### Attunement Slots
Each item you attune to occupies one of your **Attunement Slots**.  You have **3** Attunement Slots.  You must have a free slot to attune to another item.

#### Harmonic Infusions
A **Harmonic** infusion requires attunement, but doesn't occupy an Attunement Slot.  When you attune to an item with multiple Harmonic and Attunement infusions, you attune and deattune to all of them at the same time.

#### Becoming Attuned
You can Attune to an item by spending a Short Rest focused on only that item while in physical contact with it _(this can't be the same Short Rest used to identify the item)_.  After the Short Rest, you become Attuned to the item and can use its full magical capabilities.

#### Ending Attunement
You can voluntarily end Attunement by spending another Short Rest focused on the item. Your Attunement also ends if:

- the item is more than **100** feet away from you for **24** hours.
- another creature Attunes to the item.
- you die.

### Charges
An item with **Charges** lists its maximum number of Charges alongside how many it regains. Using certain effects expends a number of Charges, and an effect can't be used while the item lacks the Charges to pay for it.

#### Regaining Charges
An item regains Charges each Day, either a fixed number or a rolled amount noted in parentheses. An item never exceeds its maximum number of Charges.

### Command Words
A **Command Word** is a word or phrase you must audibly speak aloud to use some of this item's effects, similar to a Spell's Verbal Component.

### Cursed Items
**Cursed** items have a hidden, usually detrimental effect. Most methods of identifying an item don't reveal its curse.

Every Cursed item requires Attunement. You can't voluntarily end your Attunement to a Cursed item without _Remove Curse_ or similar magic.

Cursed infusions can't be crafted, transferred, or removed without specific methods determined by your DM.

### Infusing Items
**Infusing** is how a character weaves magic into an item.  An item can have up to **2** Infusions at a time, and only **1** of them can require an Attunement Slot.

#### Requirements
You can only infuse an item if you have Proficiency in Arcana and, if the Infusion casts or replicates any Spells, know those Spells each day you spend infusing the item.

#### Time and Cost
The Cost of infusing an item is paid in raw materials and time based on the Infusion's Rarity. Consumable items require half as much time and gold.

| Rarity | Rank | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | **1** | **5** days | **50** gold |
| Uncommon | **2** | **10** days | **200** gold |
| Rare | **3** | **50** days | **2,000** gold |
| Very Rare | **4** | **125** days | **20,000** gold |
| Legendary | **5** | **250** days | **100,000** gold |

Every day of infusing requires **8** hours of work, but the total hours and days don't need to be consecutive.

##### Working Together
You can work with others to shorten the infusing time, provided each contributor has the appropriate Proficiencies and Spells. Each contributor reduces the total time as though they were working independently.

#### Making Infusions Harmonic
You can't remove an infusion's Attunement requirement, but you can reduce the number of Attunement Slots needed by making an infusion Harmonic.  Making an Attunement item Harmonic increases its rarity by **1** Rank, and the time and cost required is based on the difference in its new rarity and old rarity.

#### Removing Infusions
You can permanently remove an Infusion from a Magic Item with **8** hours of work, provided you have Proficiency in Arcana and know _Dispel Magic_. Cursed and Artifact Infusions can't be removed without special methods unique to the item.

#### Transferring Infusions
You can transfer an Infusion to another appropriate item, provided you have Proficiency in Arcana. Doing so costs no gold and takes half the time a new Infusion of the same Rarity would take.
