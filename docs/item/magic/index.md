### Using Magic Items
Magic Items usually require wearing or wielding them properly, just like normal items. Magic Items that don't fit the standard [Item Types](/item/equip.md#item-types) are called Wondrous Items, and their usage varies.

### Identification
Some Magic Items are indistinguishable from their nonmagical counterparts, while others are obviously magical.

The _Detect Magic_ Spell is the fastest way to determine whether an item is infused with magic, and the _Identify_ Spell is the fastest way to reveal an item's infused properties.

Wearing or attempting to use an item can also offer hints about its magical nature. For Potions, a little taste is enough to tell you what a Potion does.

Alternatively, you can spend a Short Rest focused on only that item while in physical contact with it _(this can't be the same Short Rest used to Attune to the item)_. After the Short Rest, you learn the item's properties and how to use them.

### Infusion Properties
Many Infusions share common properties.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| [Attunement](#attunement) | A special bond required to use some infusions. |
| [Charges](#charges) | Some infusions hold Charges that power their effects. |
| Command Word | You must audibly speak a Command Word to use some of this item's effects. |
| [Consumable](/item/equip.md#consumables) | You can use this item a limited number of times before it loses its magic. |
| Cursed | This item has a hidden, detrimental effect. Most methods of identifying items don't reveal curses, and you can't voluntarily end your Attunement to it. |

##### Attunement
Some Magic Items require a special bond — called Attunement — before you can use their infused properties.

###### Attunement Slots
You can be Attuned to up to **3** Magic Items at a time. Attuning to a fourth item fails; you must end another Attunement first.

###### Becoming Attuned
You can Attune to an item by spending a Short Rest focused on only that item while in physical contact with it _(this can't be the same Short Rest used to identify the item)_.  After the Short Rest, you become Attuned to the item and can use its full magical capabilities.

###### Ending Attunement
You can voluntarily end Attunement by spending another Short Rest focused on the item. Your Attunement also ends if:

- the item is more than **100** feet away from you for **24** hours.
- another creature Attunes to the item.
- you die.

##### Charges
An item with Charges lists its maximum number of Charges alongside how many it regains. Using certain effects expends a number of Charges, and an effect can't be used while the item lacks the Charges to pay for it.

###### Regaining Charges
An item regains Charges each Day, either a fixed number or a rolled amount noted in parentheses, such as **1d6 + 4** per Day. An item never exceeds its maximum number of Charges.
