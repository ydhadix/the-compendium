### Eversmoking Bottle
Uncommon Container (Flask)
{ .subtitle }

#### Open or Close
Utilize Action
{ .subtitle }

You can open or close the bottle.

##### Open
Opening the bottle billows thick smoke in a **60**-foot Aura around it, Heavily Obscuring the area and growing **10** feet each minute up to **120** feet.

##### Close
Closing the bottle fixes the cloud in place until it disperses after **10** minutes; a strong wind disperses it after **1** minute.
