### Decanter of Endless Water
Uncommon Container (Flask)
{ .subtitle }

#### Command Word
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can remove the stopper, choose fresh or salt water, and speak one of the following Command Words.

##### Splash
The decanter produces **1** gallon of water.

##### Fountain
The decanter produces **5** gallons of water.

##### Geyser
The decanter produces **30** gallons of water in a **30**-foot × **1**-foot Line until the start of your next Turn.

###### Aim Geyser
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

One creature in the Line makes a Fortitude Save _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **1d4** Bludgeoning Damage to the target and knock it Prone. |
| Success | No effect. |
