### Ring of Mind Shielding
Uncommon Clothing (Jewelry), Attunement
{ .subtitle }

You gain Immunity to magic that reads your thoughts, determines whether you are lying, or knows your alignment or creature type. Creatures can communicate telepathically with you only if you allow it.

After you die while attuned to the ring, your soul enters it, provided the ring doesn't already house a soul.  While your soul is inside, you can communicate telepathically with whoever wears the ring, and you can choose to remain or depart for the afterlife.

#### Conceal Ring
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

The ring becomes Invisible until you use this Action again, remove the ring, or die.
