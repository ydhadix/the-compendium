### Amulet of Ideals
Rare Spellcasting Focus (Holy Symbol), Attunement (Cleric or Paladin)
{ .subtitle }

**5** Charges (regain **1d4 + 1** per Day)
{ .subtitle }

#### Devotion
Free Action, 1 use per Day
{ .subtitle }

You can use your Channel Divinity without spending a use.

#### Ideation
Free Action, 1 or more Charges
{ .subtitle }

Before you cast a Domain Spell, you can reduce the spell slot level required by **1** for each Charge expended _(this doesn't change the level of the spell)_.  You can't use this effect to cast a spell at a level you don't have spell slots for, or at **6th** level or higher.
