### Belt of Pocket Monsters
Legendary Clothing (Belt), Attunement (Beast Master Ranger)
{ .subtitle }

This exotic leather belt bears six talon-shaped claws, each gripping a **3**-inch stone-grey ball. Each ball is divided into two hemispheres by a thin seam and hinge with a small button at its center.  Only an attuned creature can attach or remove a ball from the belt.

#### Linking Monsters
Each ball can house the spirit of a Beast or Monstrosity, which can be summoned as a Primal Companion (called a _monster_).

After you attune to the belt, each of your Primal Companion forms becomes linked to a ball.  While linked, a ball's color and pattern change to reflect the monster bound within it _(you determine its appearance)_.

For each linked ball, reduce your daily [Gotcha!](#gotcha) uses by **1**.

Whenever you would summon a Primal Companion or change its form, you can instead choose any monster linked to the belt.

After throwing a ball, it immediately returns to its place on the belt.  A ball that remains more than **30** feet from the belt for **1** minute vanishes and reappears in its place on the belt.

#### Using the Belt
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

If you have a free hand, you can speak one of the following Command Words to use its effect:

##### I Choose You!
You throw a linked ball up to **30** feet, summoning its monster where the ball lands _(or the nearest unoccupied space large enough to hold it)_.  If you already have a monster summoned, it immediately returns to its ball _(no action required)_.

All monsters linked to the belt share the same Hit Points, Attunement Slots, and equipment.  After you summon a monster:

- It appears wearing any equipment worn by the previous monster.
- Any equipment that can't be worn due to the new monster's anatomy falls to the ground at the monster's feet.
- If the new monster's Hit Point Maximum is lower than the previous monster's current Hit Points, the new monster's Hit Points equal its Hit Point Maximum.

##### Return!
Your summoned monster returns to its ball, along with anything it is wearing.  Anything the monster was carrying falls to the ground in its previous space.

##### Gotcha!
6 uses per Day
{ .subtitle }

You throw an unlinked ball at a Beast or Monstrosity within **30** feet.  The target makes a Capture Save _(DC = **Ranger Spell Save DC**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | The creature's body vanishes and its spirit becomes bound to the thrown ball. |
| Success | No effect. |

###### Capture Saves
- A Capture Save adds the creature's Challenge Rating to the total instead of an Ability or Proficiency Bonus.
- The creature automatically succeeds unless it is Incapacitated, Downed, Stabilized, or an Ally.
- The Capture Save gains Advantage if the creature has more than **0** Hit Points.

##### Release!
You release a linked monster.  The creature's original body reappears in an unoccupied space within **5** feet, healed to its Hit Point Maximum.  A released creature behaves according to its nature and can't be captured again by the belt.

#### Ending Attunement
After your attunement to the belt ends:

- Your normal Primal Companion forms become unlinked from the belt.
- If your currently summoned monster is not one of your normal Primal Companion forms, it vanishes and is replaced by a Primal Companion of your choice.

If the belt remains unattuned for **24** hours, all linked monsters are automatically released as though by the [Release!](#release) command.
