### Manacles
Tool, 2 gold
{ .subtitle }

A set of Manacles comes with a key used to lock or unlock them.

##### Bind
**Utilize Action:** You can choose a Small or Medium Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained creature within Reach and make a Precision Check _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Success** | You bind the target's hands. While bound, the target gains Disadvantage on Attack Rolls, can't provide Somatic Components, and is Restrained if the Manacles are attached to an Anchored Chain or hook. |

###### Escape
Any bound creature can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature makes a Precision Check _(DC **20**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Success** | The creature escapes. |

###### Break Free
Any creature that can reach a bound target can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature makes an Athletics Check _(DC **25**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Success** | The Manacles are destroyed. |

###### Pick Lock
Any creature without a key that can reach the Manacles can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The creature makes a Precision Check _(DC **20**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Success** | The creature chooses whether the Manacles are locked or unlocked. |
