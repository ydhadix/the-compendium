### Potion of Longevity
Potion, Consumable, 20,000 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion comes in a Vial.

After consuming this potion, your age decreases by **1d6 + 6** years, to a minimum of **13** years.

Each Potion of Longevity you consume after the first has a cumulative **10**% chance to increase your age by **1d6 + 6** years instead.
