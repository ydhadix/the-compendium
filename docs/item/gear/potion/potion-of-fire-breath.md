### Potion of Fire Breath
Potion (Ingested), Consumable, 200 gold
{ .subtitle }

The target gains the following Action for **1** hour.

##### Breathe Fire
**Bonus Action; 3 uses:** You can exhale fire at one creature within **30** feet.  That creature makes a Reflex Save _(DC **13**)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **4d6** Fire Damage to the target. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |
