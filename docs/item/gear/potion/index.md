# Potions
A **Potion** is a fluid with special properties.

## Delivery
A Potion has a single effect, which it applies to **the target** — whatever a delivery method brings it into contact with.  Its **delivery types** determine which methods carry that effect intact.

| Delivery | The target is… |
|---|---|
| Ingested | a creature that swallows the potion |
| Contact | anything the potion touches |
| Injury | a creature damaged by a weapon coated with the potion |

A sealed container doesn't expose you to a potion — only spilling, breaking, or splashing it does.  Carrying a Vial of Alchemist's Fire is safe; dropping it isn't.

A potion may support several deliveries, or none.  A potion with no delivery type is used only through an action on its own card.

## Using Potions

##### Administer
**Bonus Action or Utilize Action:** You can administer an Ingested potion to a target — yourself, an Ally within Reach, or an Incapacitated or Restrained creature within Reach.  If you Utilize, any dice the potion rolls to determine its effect are maximized.

##### Throw
A glass container filled with a Contact or Injury potion can be wielded as a Simple Melee Weapon with Thrown _(**20**/**60** feet)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hit** | You deal **1** Piercing Damage, and the potion affects the target and the space it occupies. |

After the Attack, the container and its contents are destroyed.  A potion delivered this way doesn't cut as deep as a coated weapon, so the target gains Advantage on Saving Throws against an Injury potion's effects.

##### Pour
**Utilize Action:** You can pour a Contact potion across a **10**-foot Square within Reach, targeting the area rather than the creatures standing in it.  The container isn't destroyed.

##### Coat Weapon
**Bonus Action; 1 Injury Potion:** You can coat **1** weapon that deals Piercing or Slashing Damage, or up to **3** pieces of ammunition.  Once applied, the potion retains potency for **1** minute or until it strikes a creature, whichever comes first.
