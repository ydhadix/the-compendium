### Acid
Potion, Consumable, 25 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion comes in a Vial.

##### Throw
A Vial filled with Acid can be wielded as a Simple Melee Weapon with Thrown _(**20**/**60** feet)_.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hit** | You deal **2d6** Acid Damage. |

After the Attack, the Acid is consumed and the Vial is destroyed.
