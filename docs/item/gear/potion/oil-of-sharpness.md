### Oil of Sharpness
Potion, Consumable, 20,000 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion comes in a Vial.

##### Apply
1 minute
{ .subtitle }

You can apply this oil to a nonmagical Melee weapon that deals Piercing or Slashing Damage, or to twenty pieces of nonmagical ammunition, within Reach.  The coated item's Physical Masterwork bonus becomes **+3**.
