### Oil of Sharpness
Potion (Contact), Consumable, 20,000 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion has no effect unless it is applied.

##### Apply
**1 minute:** You can apply this oil to a nonmagical Melee weapon that deals Piercing or Slashing Damage, or to twenty pieces of nonmagical ammunition, within Reach.  The target's Physical Masterwork bonus becomes **+3** for **1** hour.
