### Oil of Slipperiness
Potion (Contact), Consumable, 200 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion gives the target the effect of _Grease_ for **8** hours.

##### Apply
**10 minutes:** You can apply this oil to a creature within Reach.  The target gains the effect of _Freedom of Movement_ for **8** hours.

One vial coats a Medium or smaller creature and its equipment; each Size above Medium needs another vial.
