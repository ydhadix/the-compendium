### Oil of Etherealness
Potion (Contact), Consumable, 2,000 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion has no effect unless it is applied.

##### Apply
**10 minutes:** You can apply this oil to a creature within Reach.  The target gains the effect of _Etherealness_ for **1** hour.

One vial coats a Medium or smaller creature and its equipment; each Size above Medium needs another vial.
