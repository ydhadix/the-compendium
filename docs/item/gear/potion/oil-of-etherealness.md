### Oil of Etherealness
Potion, Consumable, 2,000 gold
{ .subtitle }

This potion comes in a Vial.

##### Apply
10 minutes
{ .subtitle }

You can apply this oil to a creature within Reach.  The creature gains the effect of _Etherealness_ for **1** hour.

One vial coats a Medium or smaller creature and its equipment; each Size above Medium needs another vial.
