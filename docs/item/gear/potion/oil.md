### Oil
Potion (Contact), Consumable, 1 silver
{ .subtitle }

This potion covers the target in Oil for **1** minute.  A target covered in Oil takes an additional **5** damage from any Fire Damage dealt to it.

##### Fuel
**Utilize Action:** You can light or extinguish Oil in a Lamp or Lantern. Oil in a Lamp or Lantern burns for a total of **6** hours before being consumed.
