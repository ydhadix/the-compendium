### Dragon Scale
Material (Other), Consumable, 4,000 gold
{ .subtitle }

Dragon Scale is especially resilient and can be used to create items normally made of metal.  Each Dragon Scale has a Dragon Type, which determines its Damage Type.

| Dragon Type | Damage Type |
|---|---|
| Black | Acid |
| Blue | Lightning |
| Brass | Fire |
| Bronze | Lightning |
| Copper | Acid |
| Gold | Fire |
| Green | Poison |
| Red | Fire |
| Silver | Cold |
| White | Cold |

##### Sense Dragon
**Magic Action; Once per Day:** You can learn the distance and direction to the closest dragon of the scale's Dragon Type within **30** miles.

##### Weapon
This weapon deals an additional **1d6** damage of its scale's Damage Type and can deal that Damage Type instead of its normal Damage Type.

##### Armor
You gain Breath Weapon Protection and Resistance to the scale's Damage Type.
