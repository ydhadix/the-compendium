### Spell Scroll
Scroll, Consumable, Value Varies
{ .subtitle }

This Scroll holds a single Spell.  The Spell Level determines this item's Crafting Time and Value.

| Spell Level | Crafting Time | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Cantrip | **1** day | **50** gold |
| 1st | **1** day | **200** gold |
| 2nd | **5** days | **500** gold |
| 3rd | **10** days | **2,000** gold |
| 4th | **40** days | **20,000** gold |
| 5th | **60** days | **50,000** gold |
| 6th | **120** days | **100,000** gold |

#### Cast
If the scroll's Spell is on your Spell List, you can cast the spell without Material components using the Spell's normal Casting Time.  Then, the Scroll crumbles to dust.

Casting the Spell above the highest Spell Level you can normally cast requires an Ability Check using your Spellcasting Ability _(DC **10 + Spell Level**)_.  On a failure, the Scroll crumbles to dust with no other effect.

#### Copy
A Wizard can make an Arcana Check _(DC **10 + Spell Level**)_.  On a success, you copy a Wizard Spell from the Scroll into a spellbook.  Whether the Check succeeds or fails, the Scroll is consumed.
