### Hooded Lantern
Other, 5 gold
{ .subtitle }

A Hooded Lantern burns Oil as fuel. While lit, a Hooded Lantern emits Bright Light out to **30** feet and Dim Light **30** feet further.

###### Adjust Hood
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You can lower or raise the hood. While lowered, the light is restricted to Bright Light out to **5** feet and Dim Light **5** feet further.
