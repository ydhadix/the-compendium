# Poisons
A **Poison** is a consumable that inflicts Poison Damage, Sickness, or both, and sometimes additional damage or conditions.

## Using Poisons
Each type of poison has a different method to deliver its effects:

| Type | Usage | Typical Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Injury | Affects a creature it damages | Coat a weapon or ammunition |
| Ingested | Affects a creature that swallows it | Add to food or drink |
| Contact | Affects a creature that touches it | Splash onto a surface or creature |

#### Coat Weapon
Bonus Action, 1 Injury Poison
{ .subtitle }

You can coat **1** weapon or up to **3** pieces of ammunition. Once applied, the poison retains potency for **1** minute or until its strikes a creature, whichever comes first.

#### Splash
A Vial filled with Contact Poison can be wielded as a Simple Melee Weapon.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hit** | If the target is a creature, you inflict the Poison's effect.  If the target is a surface, you cover a **5**-foot Square area; the poison retains potency for **1** minute or until a creature touches it, whichever comes first. |

After the attack, the Poison is consumed.
