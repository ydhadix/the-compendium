# Poisons
A **Poison** is a consumable that inflicts Poison Damage, Sickness, or both, and sometimes additional damage or conditions.
{ .subtitle }

Each poison has a different method to deliver it:

| Type | Usage |
|---|---|
| Injury | Coat a weapon or ammunition; the Poison affects a creature it damages. |
| Contact | Smear onto a surface or creature; the Poison affects a creature that touches it. |
| Ingested | Add to food or drink; the Poison affects a creature that swallows it. |
