# Armor
**Armor** is the protective gear a character dons to guard against attacks.
{ .subtitle }

## Wearing Armor
Your armor's weight determines how quickly you can don and doff it.

| Weight | Don | Doff |
|---|---|---|
| [Unarmored](#unarmored) | - | - |
| [Light](#light-armor) | 1 minute | 1 minute |
| [Medium](#medium-armor) | 5 minutes | 1 minute |
| [Heavy](#heavy-armor) | 10 minutes | 5 minutes |
| [Shield](#shields) | Utilize Action | Utilize Action |

#### Strength Requirements
If you don't meet your armor's Strength Requirement, your Speed decreases by **15** feet.

#### Item Capacity
Each armor has a different capacity for carrying Containers and Consumable items into combat. You can take **1** minute outside of combat to change items.

#### Upgrading Armor
A set of armor can be upgraded to another armor in the same family by spending gold equal to the difference in their Values, purchasing only the parts of the armor that are missing.

| Family | Light | Medium | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chain | Padded Armor | Chain Shirt | Heavy Chain |
| Scale | Padded Armor | Scale Mail | Heavy Scale |
| Plate | Padded Armor | Breastplate, Half Plate | Full Plate |

#### Partially Donned Armor
While donning a suit of armor, you can leave off parts of the armor to treat it as an armor in the same family with a lesser Value instead. Leaving off parts of your armor reduces its protection but can also remove Stealth penalties or reduce how long it takes to don.

### Unarmored
While **Unarmored**, your Armor Class is only based on your Dexterity and Proficiency Bonus.

| Name | Armor Class | Strength Requirement | Damage Reduction | Item Capacity | Stealth Penalty | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unarmored | 8 + DEX | — | — | 4 | — | — |

#### Natural Armor
**Natural Armor** is part of a creature's body that makes them particularly hard to hit.  Natural Armor usually uses the defenses of Light, Medium, or Heavy Armor, but the creature is still considered Unarmored.

### Light Armor
**Light Armor** keeps your full Dexterity bonus to Armor Class and has no Stealth penalty.

| Name | Armor Class | Strength Requirement | Damage Reduction | Item Capacity | Stealth Penalty | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Padded Armor | 9 + DEX | — | — | 6 | — | 10 gold |
| Leather Armor | 10 + DEX | — | — | 6 | — | 45 gold |

### Medium Armor
**Medium Armor** caps the Dexterity you add to your Armor Class, but also grants Damage Reduction.

| Name | Armor Class | Strength Requirement | Damage Reduction | Item Capacity | Stealth Penalty | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chain Shirt | 12 + DEX (max 2) | — | STR (max 1) | 4 | — | 50 gold |
| Scale Mail | 13 + DEX (max 2) | — | STR (max 2) | 4 | Disadvantage | 50 gold |
| Breastplate | 13 + DEX (max 2) | — | STR (max 1) | 4 | — | 400 gold |
| Half Plate | 14 + DEX (max 2) | — | STR (max 2) | 4 | Disadvantage | 750 gold |

### Heavy Armor
**Heavy Armor** offers the highest Armor Class and Damage Reduction, but requires Strength and penalizes Stealth.

| Name | Armor Class | Strength Requirement | Damage Reduction | Item Capacity | Stealth Penalty | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy Chain | 15 | 1 | STR (max 2) | 2 | Disadvantage | 75 gold |
| Heavy Scale | 16 | 2 | STR (max 3) | 2 | Disadvantage | 200 gold |
| Full Plate | 17 | 3 | STR (max 3) | 2 | Disadvantage | 1500 gold |

### Shields
A **Shield** is carried in one hand, adding to a character's Armor Class at the cost of a free hand.

| Name | Armor Class | Strength Requirement | Stealth Penalty | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Light Shield](#light-shield) | +0 | — | — | 5 gold |
| [Medium Shield](#medium-shield) | +2 | — | — | 10 gold |
| [Heavy Shield](#heavy-shield) | +2 | 2 | Disadvantage | 50 gold |

##### Light Shield
Shield, 5 gold
{ .subtitle }

A Light Shield leaves your hand free.

###### Block
Reaction
{ .subtitle }

Before you are hit by an Attack, you can add **2** to your Armor Class until the end of the Turn, while your shield hand is free.

##### Medium Shield
Shield, 10 gold
{ .subtitle }

While donned, a Medium Shield occupies one hand, and your Armor Class increases by **2**.

##### Heavy Shield
Shield, 50 gold
{ .subtitle }

While donned, a Heavy Shield occupies one hand, and you count as Three-Quarters Cover for creatures your size, or Full Cover for creatures smaller than you _(creatures typically count as Half Cover)_.  Your Armor Class increases by **2**.
