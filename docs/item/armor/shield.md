# Shields

Shields are extra armor that is easily donned or doffed.

| Name | Armor Class | Strength Requirement | Stealth Penalty | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Light Shield](#light-shield) | +0 | — | — | 5 gold |
| [Medium Shield](#medium-shield) | +2 | — | — | 10 gold |
| [Heavy Shield](#heavy-shield) | +2 | 2 | Disadvantage | 50 gold |

### Light Shield
5 gold
{ .subtitle }

A Light Shield leaves your hand free.

###### Block
Reaction
{ .subtitle }

Before you are hit by an Attack, you can add **+2** to your Armor Class until the end of the Turn, while your shield hand is free.

### Medium Shield
10 gold
{ .subtitle }

While donned, a Medium Shield adds **+2** to your Armor Class and occupies one hand.

### Heavy Shield
50 gold
{ .subtitle }

While donned, a Heavy Shield adds **+2** to your Armor Class, occupies one hand, and you count as Three-Quarters Cover for creatures of your size, or Full Cover for creatures smaller than you _(creatures typically count as Half Cover)_.
