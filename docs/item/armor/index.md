# Armor

Armor protects you in combat, raising your Armor Class and, for heavier sets, reducing the damage you take.

## Wearing Armor
Your armor's weight determines how quickly you can don and doff it.

| Weight | Don | Doff |
|---|---|---|
| Unarmored | - | - |
| Light | 1 minute | 1 minute |
| Medium | 5 minutes | 1 minute |
| Heavy | 10 minutes | 5 minutes |
| Shield | Utilize Action | Utilize Action |

#### Partially Donned Armor
While donning a suit of armor, you can leave off parts of the armor to treat it as an armor in the same family with a lesser Value instead. Leaving off parts of your armor reduces its protection but can also remove Stealth penalties or reduce how long it takes to don.

#### Proficiency
While you have armor donned that you don't have Proficiency with, you have Disadvantage on D20 Tests using Strength or Dexterity and can't cast Spells.

#### Strength Requirements
If you don't meet your armor's Strength Requirement, your Speed is reduced by **15** feet.

#### Item Capacity
Each armor has a different capacity for carrying readily accessible items into combat. You can take **1** minute outside of combat to change items.
