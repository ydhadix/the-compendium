### Version 1.7.3
August 2, 2026
{ .subtitle }

##### General
###### Navigation
- Flattened and reordered the [Rules Rolodex](/rules/index.md); the Adventuring and Positioning groupings are dissolved, and each rule now sits directly in its own rules page.
- Removed the in-page tables of contents.  The left navigation and mega-menu handle page-to-page navigation; the alphanumeric jump rails (the keyword index and catalog pages) remain, and now scroll rather than wrap.
- Added a floating back-to-top button.
###### Rules Organization
- Merged Visibility (Obscurement, Lighting, and Senses) into [Targeting](/rules/target.md).
- Merged Time into [Resting](/rules/rest.md), adding the [Week](/rules/rest.md#time), [Tenday](/rules/rest.md#time), and [Month](/rules/rest.md#time).
- Folded Abilities and Proficiency into the [Dice Rolls](/rules/dice/index.md#abilities) overview.

##### Conditions
- [Prone](/rules/condition.md#prone) no longer triggers an **Opportunity Attack** on standing up.

##### Races
- The [Races](/character/race/index.md) table now lists each race's Abilities, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Reach, Hit Points, and Senses.

##### Classes
- Renamed Feature Ability back to [Class Ability](/character/class/index.md#class-ability).

##### Bestiary
- Moved [Non-Player Characters](/bestiary/index.md#non-player-characters) (Disposition, Companions, Familiars, and Mounts) into the Creature Codex.

##### Spells
- [Booming Blade](/spell/level/0/booming-blade.md) now triggers when the target _expends movement_, not when the target moves; this means that standing up from Prone will trigger the rider, but being propelled or teleporting won't.
