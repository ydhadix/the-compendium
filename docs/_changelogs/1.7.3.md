### Version 1.7.3
August 2, 2026
{ .subtitle }

##### General
###### Navigation
- Flattened and reordered the [Rules Rolodex](/rules/index.md); the Adventuring and Positioning groupings are dissolved, and each rule now sits directly in its own rules page.
- Removed the in-page tables of contents.  The left navigation and mega-menu handle page-to-page navigation; the alphanumeric jump rails (the keyword index and catalog pages) remain, and now scroll rather than wrap.
- Added a floating back-to-top button.
###### Rules Organization
- Merged Visibility (Obscurement, Lighting, and Senses) into [Targeting](/rules/target.md).
- Merged Time into [Resting](/rules/rest.md), adding the [Week](/rules/rest.md#time), [Tenday](/rules/rest.md#time), and [Month](/rules/rest.md#time).
- Folded Abilities and Proficiency into the [Dice Rolls](/rules/dice/index.md#abilities) overview.

##### Feats
- Overhaul of Fighting Style & Weapon Mastery systems.
- Weapon Masteries are now [Weapon Techniques](/item/weapon.md#techniques), and are made available through [Fighting Style](/character/feat/fighting-style/index.md) feats.
- Some weapon & armor related General Feats have been turned into Fighting Styles (Lightly Armored, Charger, etc.).
- Features and Classes that used to grant Weapon Masteries now grant Fighting Style feats, and general distribution of Fighting Styles has increased.

- This doesn't count other features that may grant specific weapon techniques (e.g. Fighter's [Tactical Master](/character/class/fighter/index.md#7-tactical-master) or Monk's [Martial Arts](/character/class/monk/index.md#1-martial-arts)).
- **Notes**
    - **Barbarian** is the baseline martial progression.
    - **Rogue** is defaulting to Barbarian's progression *for now*.  When Rogue is fully ported, it may be brought to a different level.
    - **Fighter** starts higher as the archetypal flexible martial class.
    - **Ranger** and **Paladin** start at the same baseline but progress slower, since they get Spellcasting at level 2.
    - **Artificer** starts even lower and a level later, since Fighting Styles are an accent to their kit, not foundational, and their Spellcasting includes Cantrips at level 1.  Ranger and Paladin can functionally choose to drop down to this tier by taking Blessed/Druidic Warrior.
    - **Monk** uses the Artificer's scaling for similar reasons: most Monk features and subclasses include unique "Weapon Techniques" for Unarmed Strikes/Monk Weapons, so general Fighting Styles are accents.  A weapons master subclass, like Kensei, would likely receive additional Fighting Styles to fit their theme.
    - **Bladesinger** includes "Full Spellcasters with Martial Subclasses" like Bladesinger, Swords Bard, etc.  They mirror Third-Caster subclasses for Martials that get a taste of simple magic, but not true caster progression.

###### Updated
- Renamed Fey Touched and Shadow Touched to [Touch of the Fey](/character/feat/general/index.md#touch-of-the-fey) and [Touch of the Shadows](/character/feat/general/index.md#touch-of-the-shadows) (opening up room for other, easily searchable, "Touch of the X" feats later).
- [Fighting Styles](/character/feat/fighting-style/index.md) have been overhauled into three tiers: [Initiate](/character/feat/fighting-style/initiate/index.md), [Expert](/character/feat/fighting-style/expert/index.md), and [Master](/character/feat/fighting-style/master/index.md).
    - Initiate Fighting Styles are generically useful and/or provide the Proficiencies to take other Fighting Styles, and usually have no prerequisites _(except Class-specific Fighting Styles)_.
    - Expert Fighting Styles enhance a wide array of builds that span weapon types, and often have an Ability prerequisite.
    - Master Fighting Styles become available at Level **4** and require a specific Expert Fighting Style and/or Proficiency that they enhance.
    - [Fighting Initiate](/character/feat/general/index.md#fighting-initiate) makes it easier for a non-martial character to gain a Weapon proficiency and a corresponding Technique/Fighting Style.

###### Removed
- **Poisoner**.  Redundant with better poisons, no Poisoner's Kit trade, and the [Elemental Adept](/character/feat/general/index.md#elemental-adept) feat.
- **Weapon Master**.  Redundant with Weapon Masteries becoming Fighting Styles.

##### Conditions
- [Prone](/rules/condition.md#prone) no longer triggers an **Opportunity Attack** on standing up.

##### Races
- The [Races](/character/race/index.md) table now lists each race's Abilities, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Reach, Hit Points, and Senses.

##### Classes
- Renamed Feature Ability back to [Class Ability](/character/class/index.md#class-ability).
###### Monk
- Slow Fall wrapped into [Unarmored Stance](/character/class/monk/index.md#1-unarmored-stance) to make room for [Fighting Styles](/character/class/monk/index.md#2-fighting-style).
- [Martial Arts](/character/class/monk/index.md#1-martial-arts) now builds on the [Striking](/character/feat/fighting-style/index.md#striking) fighting style, rather than duplicating a slightly parallel effect.

##### Bestiary
- Moved [Non-Player Characters](/bestiary/index.md#non-player-characters) (Disposition, Companions, Familiars, and Mounts) into the Creature Codex.

##### Spells
- [Booming Blade](/spell/level/0/booming-blade.md) now triggers when the target _expends movement_, not when the target moves; this means that standing up from Prone will trigger the rider, but being propelled or teleporting won't.
