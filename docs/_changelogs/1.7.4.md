### Version 1.7.4
August 12, 2026
{ .subtitle }

##### General
- Updated [Critical Hit Table](/rules/dice/attack.md#player-critical-hits): now uses more restrained durations and conditions, but expanded effectiveness vs. specific armor types and creature types.  Aberrations and Dragons don't have any effective damage types.  Medium Armor, Heavy Armor, and Beasts are the only types that have multiple effective damage types, due to how common they are.

| Damage Type | Effective Against... | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Bludgeoning | Heavy Armor | Sundering plated defenses |
| Piercing    | Medium Armor | Piercing gaps in armor |
| Slashing    | Unarmored, Light Armor | Slashing non-metal material |
| Acid        | Constructs | Melting inorganic material |
| Cold        | Oozes | Low temperatures restrict creatures that are mostly fluid |
| Fire        | Beasts, Plants | Natural creatures have few protections from flames |
| Lightning   | Medium Armor, Heavy Armor | Metallic armor conducts electricity |
| Poison      | Giants, Humanoids | Standard biology is susceptible to poison |
| Thunder     | Elementals | Concussive force against indeterminate forms |
| Force       | — | Most neutral damage type |
| Necrotic    | Celestials | Evil is effective against Good |
| Psychic     | Beasts, Monstrosities | Simple minds are easily manipulated/disturbed |
| Radiant     | Fiends, Undead | Good is effective against Evil |

##### Conditions
- Added [Withering](/rules/condition.md#withering): a necrotic DoT that reduces max HP.  Currently only inflicted by Critical Hits.

##### Bastions
- Added [Studio](/bastion/studio.md) for Bardic Bastions.

##### Magic Items
- Added section sorted by [item type](/item/magic/rarity/index.md), in addition to rarity.
- Added the [Rare](/item/magic/rarity/rare.md) and [Very Rare](/item/magic/rarity/very-rare.md) tiers.
- Added [Champion's Weapon](/item/magic/rarity/uncommon/champions-weapon.md), [Weapon of Elements](/item/magic/rarity/uncommon/weapon-of-elements.md).
- Updated [Medusan Signet](/item/magic/rarity/uncommon/medusan-signet.md) to be non-attunement, but only 1 use.
- Added [Cursed](/item/magic/rarity/cursed.md) as a rarity: Cursed items can't be crafted or infused, always require Attunement, and share one rule for ending that Attunement.

###### Updates
- A number of items from Rare and Very Rare have been downgraded to Uncommon or Rare, respectively.  Many of these are items whose bonuses to Attack, Damage, Spell Save DC, or AC are now covered by [Masterwork](/item/index.md#masterwork) and had that bonus stripped.
- Split the Feather Token into the [Anchor](/item/magic/rarity/uncommon/anchor-token.md), [Fan](/item/magic/rarity/uncommon/fan-token.md), and [Tree](/item/magic/rarity/uncommon/tree-token.md) Tokens, joined by the [Bird](/item/magic/rarity/rare/bird-token.md), [Swan Boat](/item/magic/rarity/rare/swan-boat-token.md), and [Whip](/item/magic/rarity/rare/whip-token.md) Tokens.

##### Items
- Rare and Very Rare [Potions](/item/gear/potion/index.md) and [Scrolls](/item/gear/scroll/index.md) have been ported over.
- Added the [Potion of Giant Strength](/item/gear/potion/potion-of-giant-strength.md), replacing the Potion of Hill Giant Strength with a single card covering every giant.

##### Races
- Added [Elven Name Generator](/character/race/name/elf.md) and [Dark Elven Name Generator](/character/race/name/dark-elf.md).
