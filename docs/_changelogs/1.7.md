### Version 1.7
July 13, 2026
{ .subtitle }

##### General
###### New
- [Campaign Playlists](/archive/index.md) added.
- Enabled heading **⚓︎** permalinks for quick copy-pasting URLs.

##### Feats
###### New
- Added Strike of the Giants feats as Goliath [Ancestry Feats](/character/feat/ancestry.md).

##### Magic Items
###### New
- [Common](/item/magic/common.md) and [Uncommon](/item/magic/uncommon.md) Infusions added.
- All homebrew, custom, and higher-rarity Infusions encountered during the campaign added.
- [Boots of the Savannah](/item/magic/uncommon/boots-of-the-savannah.md), paralleling the [Boots of the Winterlands](/item/magic/uncommon/boots-of-the-winterlands.md)
###### Updates
- Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location -> [Amulet of Nondetection](/item/magic/uncommon/amulet-of-nondetection.md)
- [Spell Scroll](/item/gear/scroll/spell-scroll.md) scaling changed; caps at 6th-level, and rarities for 3rd-level and higher scrolls increased.  [Crafting](/item/gear/scroll/spell-scroll.md) costs and times adjusted accordingly (generally, scrolls are more expensive).
- Enspelled Items ([Armor](/item/magic/uncommon/enspelled-armor.md), [Staff](/item/magic/uncommon/enspelled-staff.md), [Weapon](/item/magic/uncommon/enspelled-weapon.md)) are likewise scaled, capping at 5th-level.
- [Rod of the Pact Keeper](/item/magic/common/rod-of-the-pact-keeper.md) and [Wraps of Unarmed Power](/item/magic/common/wraps-of-unarmed-power.md) become Common items and lose their bonus to attack/save DCs (in favor of the [Masterwork](/item/index.md#masterwork) system).
- Unified some wording and mechanics on summoning items ([Bag of Tricks](/item/magic/uncommon/bag-of-tricks.md), [Dancing Broom](/item/magic/uncommon/dancing-broom.md), [Elemental Gem](/item/magic/uncommon/elemental-gem.md), [Silver Raven Figurine](/item/magic/uncommon/silver-raven-figurine.md), [Staff of the Python](/item/magic/uncommon/staff-of-the-python.md)) to use companion rules.
- Items that destroy themselves randomly now don't.  Items that were gated behind a random chance per use now have a set number of uses each day ([Wind Fan](/item/magic/uncommon/wind-fan.md))

##### Spells
###### New
- [4th-Level Dragonmark Spells](/spell/level/4/index.md) added.
