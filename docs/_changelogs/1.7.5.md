### Version 1.7.5
August x, 2026
{ .subtitle }

##### General
- Codified [Asleep](/rules/condition.md#asleep) as a condition: it's Unconscious, but with an early exit condition.
- Codified Creatures, Objects, and Locations as the three valid types of [Target](/rules/target/index.md#types-of-targets).  Also defined [Animation](/rules/target/index.md#animating), [Corpses](/rules/target/index.md#corpses), and [Conjuring](/rules/target/index.md#conjuring).
- Targeting rules expanded back into separate [Cover](/rules/target/cover.md), [Range and Area](/rules/target/range.md), [Sight](/rules/target/sight.md), and [Size](/rules/target/size.md) pages, with general procedure on the [main index](/rules/target/index.md).

##### Spells
- Updated the Spell Card template.
- Codified [Dispelling](/spell/index.md#dispelling) and [Countering](/spell/index.md#countering).
- Defined [Spell Levels](/spell/index.md#spell-level-of-non-spell-effects) for non-Spell magical effects: Features use the source creature's Proficiency Bonus (minus 1), while magic items use the item's rarity.  Only a spell that was cast keeps its original spell level.
- Targets and Areas for spells are now declared independently on spell cards.  For area spells, the target row works like a filter for "things that become targets while in the area".
- Added [6th-Level Spells](/spell/level/6/index.md).
- Renamed Giant Insect to [Summon Giant Insect](/spell/level/4/summon-giant-insect.md).
- Renamed Dispel Evil and Good to [Dispel Good and Evil](/spell/level/5/dispel-good-and-evil.md).
- Added keywords for [Contingent Spells](/spell/level/6/contingency.md#contingent-spells), [Recall Sanctuaries](/spell/level/6/word-of-recall.md#recall-sanctuaries), and [Scrying Sensors](/spell/level/5/scrying.md#scrying-sensors).
- Many spell effects that resemble other spells now directly reference those spells (such as [Magic Circle](/spell/level/3/magic-circle.md) referencing [Protection from Good and Evil](/spell/level/1/protection-from-good-and-evil.md)).

##### Items
- Combined Poisons and [Potions](/item/gear/potion/index.md) into one category with shared Ingested/Contact/Injury mechanics.
- Many item effects that resemble spells now directly reference those spells (such as [Ring of Free Action](/item/magic/rare/ring-of-free-action.md) referencing [Freedom of Movement](/spell/level/4/freedom-of-movement.md)).

##### Bestiary
- Added each [Beast](/bestiary/type/beast.md) from the 2024 Monster Manual.
- Added creatures summoned by spells and magic items (such as [Zombies](/bestiary/level/2/zombie.md) and [Elementals](/bestiary/level/8/air-elemental.md)).
- Challenge Rating is gone as a concept - creatures now have their own level, and their stats are calculated the same as a player character's.
    - CR 0 -> Level 0
    - CR 1/8 -> Level 1
    - CR 1/4 -> Level 2
    - CR 1/2 -> Level 3
    - CR N -> Level N + 3
    _(This also makes the functional level cap for an enemy monster **33**)_
- Codified [Planar](/bestiary/index.md#planar-creatures) Creatures: Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, and Undead.
- Codified [Recharge](/bestiary/index.md#recharge): recharge now triggers at the *end* of the creature's turn, giving a round to prepare for the recharged effect.
- Codified [Swarms](/bestiary/index.md#swarm): instead of bespoke stats, Swarms now have a set of rules for how to treat a Tiny or Small creature as a swarm.
- Rebalanced [Summon](/bestiary/summon/index.md) stats against Armor/AC calculations.  Made armor calculations and weights explicit.
