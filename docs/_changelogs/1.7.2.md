### Version 1.7.2
July 20, 2026
{ .subtitle }

#### General
- Codified [Nonlethal Attacks](/rules/dice/attack.md#nonlethal-attacks).  Any Melee attack can now be declared Nonlethal, not just weapon attacks.
- Cleaned up the [Keyword Index](/rules/keyword.md).  Fixed misaligned links, repeated keywords, etc.
- Removed the Stabilize Action; its functionality is scoped to the [Downed](/rules/adventure/condition.md#down) condition.
- Removed Carry Capacity; Powerful Build ([Ranger Imprint](/character/class/ranger/imprint/index.md#powerful-build), [Goliath Feature](/character/race/goliath.md#powerful-build)) now doubles Jump distance instead of carry capacity.
- Created a new [Positioning](/rules/position/index.md) section in the Rules Rolodex covering [Movement](/rules/position/movement.md), [Cover](/rules/position/cover.md), [Visibility](/rules/position/visibility.md), [Range & Targeting](/rules/position/target.md), and [Areas of Effect](/rules/position/area.md).

#### Spells
- Moved rules for [Targeting](/rules/position/target.md) and [Areas of Effect](/rules/position/area.md) to the new general [Positioning](/rules/position/index.md) section.

#### Classes
- Renamed Class Ability to [Starting Ability](/character/class/index.md#starting-ability) and Secondary Ability to [Feature Ability](/character/class/index.md#feature-ability); the old names were confusing alongside Primary and Secondary Class.
