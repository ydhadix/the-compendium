### Version 1.7.2
July 25, 2026
{ .subtitle }

##### General
###### New
- Codified [Nonlethal Attacks](/rules/dice/attack.md#nonlethal-attacks).  Any Melee attack can now be declared Nonlethal, not just weapon attacks.
- Codified [Bloodied](/rules/dice/damage.md#bloodied) state; not a condition, unlike its siblings [Downed](/rules/condition.md#downed) and [Stable](/rules/condition.md#stable), since Bloodied doesn't have any inherent rules of its own.
- Created a new Positioning section in the Rules Rolodex covering [Movement](/rules/movement.md), [Cover](/rules/target.md#cover), [Visibility](/rules/target.md#obscurement), [Range & Targeting](/rules/target.md), and [Areas of Effect](/rules/target.md#areas).
- Added **Wall** shape to [Areas of Effect](/rules/target.md#areas): a rectangular length × width × height.  On a 5-foot grid, walls that are meant to occupy tiles ([Wall of Water](/spell/level/3/wall-of-water.md), [Wall of Fire](/spell/level/4/wall-of-fire.md)) now fill entire spaces on a grid.  Walls that are meant to be barriers ([Wall of Force](/spell/level/5/wall-of-force.md), [Wall of Stone](/spell/level/5/wall-of-stone.md)) are drawn along the boundaries of the grid.

###### Updates
- Moved the [Keyword Index](/rules/index.md) into the main Rules Rolodex page.  Fixed misaligned links, repeated keywords, etc.
- Moved [Character Creation](/character/index.md#character-creation) into the main Hero Handbook Page.
- Removed the Stabilize Action; its functionality is scoped to the [Downed](/rules/condition.md#downed) condition.
- Removed Carry Capacity; Powerful Build ([Ranger Imprint](/character/class/ranger/imprint/index.md#powerful-build), [Goliath Feature](/character/race/goliath.md#powerful-build)) now doubles Jump distance instead of carry capacity.

##### Items
- Recategorized [Potions](/item/gear/potion/index.md), [Poisons](/item/gear/poison/index.md), [Scrolls](/item/gear/scroll/index.md), and [Materials](/item/gear/material/index.md) as their own Adventuring Gear categories.
- Consolidated rules for [Crafting](/item/index.md#crafting-items) and [Masterwork](/item/index.md#masterwork) to the general Item Index page.
- Consolidated rules for [Weapon Types](/item/weapon.md) to the general Weapons page.  [Weapon Properties](/item/weapon.md) and [Weapon Masteries](/item/weapon.md#techniques) are still their own pages.
- Consolidated rules for [Armor Types](/item/armor.md) and [Shields](/item/armor.md#shields) to a single page.
- Consolidated rules for [Infusing Items](/item/magic/index.md) to the general Magic Items page.
- Added definitions for [Natural Weapons](/item/weapon.md#natural-weapons) and [Natural Armor](/item/armor.md#natural-armor).

##### Spells
###### New
- Added [5th-Level Spells](/spell/level/5/index.md).
###### Updates
- [Animate Objects](/spell/level/3/animate-objects.md), [Banishing Smite](/spell/level/3/banishing-smite.md), [Conjure Volley](/spell/level/3/conjure-volley.md), [Destructive Wave](/spell/level/3/destructive-wave.md), [Fabricate](/spell/level/3/fabricate.md), [Swift Quiver](/spell/level/3/swift-quiver.md), [Tree Stride](/spell/level/3/tree-stride.md) are moved to 3rd Level for Artificer, Paladin, and Ranger access.
- [Animate Objects](/spell/level/3/animate-objects.md), [Fabricate](/spell/level/3/fabricate.md), and [Summon Construct](/spell/level/3/summon-construct.md) are now Artificer-exclusive, to keep the Wizard from completely subsuming the magical crafting archetype.
- Augury, Divination, Commune, Contact Other Plane, and Legend Lore are removed; their function is encompassed by INT checks or the corresponding [Cleric](/character/class/cleric/index.md#8-commune)/Warlock features.
- Increased [Insect Plague](/spell/level/5/insect-plague.md)'s damage to **6d10**.
- [Revivify](/spell/level/3/revivify.md) and [Raise Dead](/spell/level/5/raise-dead.md) now give Exhaustion levels.

##### Classes
###### Updates
- Renamed Class Ability to [Starting Ability](/character/class/index.md#starting-ability) and Secondary Ability to [Feature Ability](/character/class/index.md#class-ability); the old names were confusing alongside Primary and Secondary Class.
