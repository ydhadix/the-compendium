## Range
A Spell's Range is how far from you its effect can reach, given as a distance in feet or as your Reach. For a Spell with an [Area](area.md), the Range is the distance to the Area's Origin. For a Spell without an Area, the Range is the distance to its individual Targets.

Once placed, a Spell's effect can extend beyond its Range, and movable effects aren't restricted by Range unless the Spell says otherwise.

## Line of Effect
You must have a clear path to a Target, so it can't be behind [Full Cover](../../rules/adventure/cover.md).

## Targeting Yourself
A Spell with a Target of Self can only target you. A Spell that can target a creature can also target you, unless its description says otherwise.

## Worn or Held Objects
A Spell can't target an object being worn or held, such as Armor or a Weapon, even when the Spell targets everything in an Area.

## Invalid Targets
If a Target can't be affected by a Spell, nothing happens to it, but you still cast the Spell as normal.

If a Spell normally has no effect on a Target that succeeds on a Saving Throw, an invalid Target appears to have succeeded on that Save, giving no hint that it was an invalid Target.
