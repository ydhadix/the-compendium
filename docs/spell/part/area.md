# Area of Effect
An Area of Effect is the shape of space a Spell fills, spreading from an Origin point.
{ .subtitle }

Every Area has an Origin — the point its effect erupts from in straight lines. If a straight line from the Origin to a location in the Area is blocked by [Full Cover](../../rules/adventure/cover.md), that location is excluded from the Area.

An Area usually takes one of the following shapes.

| Area | Origin | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Aura | your space | radius from the Origin |
| Cone | your space | length from the Origin; its width at any point equals that distance |
| Cube | your space | length of each side |
| Cylinder | center of the top or bottom circle | radius and height, plus how far the Origin can be from you |
| Line | your space | length × width |
| Sphere | its center point | radius, plus how far the Origin can be from you |
| Square | its center point, at ground level | length of each side, plus how far the Origin can be from you |
