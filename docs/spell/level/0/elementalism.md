### Elementalism
Transmutation Cantrip
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Target | Special |
| Duration | 1 hour |

##### Spell Effect
Choose one of the following effects:

| | |
|---|---|
| Fire Play | You light or snuff out a candle, torch, or small campfire. |
| Control Flames | Choose a flame. You can double or halve the light it casts, change its color, cause simple shapes to appear and animate within the flames, or any combination of these effects. |
| Beckon Air | You create a breeze strong enough to ripple cloth, stir dust, rustle leaves, and close open doors and shutters in the area. |
| Gust | You move a Medium or smaller creature or object up to **5** feet away from you. A creature can make a Fortitude Save to avoid being moved. |
| Excavate | Choose a **5**-foot Cube or smaller portion of dirt. You excavate it, move it along the ground, and deposit it up to **5** feet away. |
| Mold Earth | Choose a **5**-foot Square or smaller portion of dirt or stone. You change it to Difficult Terrain or normal terrain. |
| Beckon Water | You create a spray of cool mist that lightly dampens everything in a **5**-foot Cube. |
| Shape Water | Choose a **5**-foot Cube or smaller portion of water. You can change its color or opacity, cause it to form into simple shapes and animate, freeze or unfreeze it, or any combination of these effects. |
