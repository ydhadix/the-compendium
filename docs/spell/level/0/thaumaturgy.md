### Thaumaturgy
Transmutation Cantrip
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric |
| Components | V |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Target | Special |
| Duration | 1 minute |

Choose one of the following effects. If you cast this Spell multiple times, you can have up to **3** of its effects active at once:

- **Altered Eyes.** You change the appearance of your eyes.
- **Booming Voice.** Your voice booms up to three times as loud as normal, and you have Advantage on Intimidation Checks.
- **Fire Play.** You light or snuff out a candle, torch, or small campfire.
- **Invisible Hand.** You cause an unlocked door or window to fly open or slam shut.
- **Phantom Sound.** A sound emits from a place in range, such as a rumble of thunder, the cry of a raven, or ominous whispers.
- **Tremors.** You cause harmless tremors in the ground.
