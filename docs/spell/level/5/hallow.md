### Hallow
5th-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric |
| Components | V, S, M _(consumes incense worth 1,000 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 24 hours |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | up to a 60-ft. radius Sphere |
| Duration | Until dispelled |

##### Spell Effect
Choose any of these Creature Types: Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, or Undead.  Creatures of the chosen types can't willingly enter the area, and while in the area, a creature isn't possessed, Charmed, or Frightened by them.

Choose one of the following effects:

| | |
|---|---|
| Courage | Creatures in the area gain Fear Immunity. |
| Darkness | Magical Darkness fills the area. |
| Daylight | Sunlight fills the area. |
| Peaceful Rest | Dead bodies in the area can't become Undead. |
| Extradimensional Interference | Creatures of the chosen types can't enter or leave the area by teleportation or interplanar travel. |
| Fear | While in the area, the area frightens creatures of the chosen types. |
| Resistance | Choose any Damage Type.  Creatures in the area gain Resistance to the chosen Damage Type. |
| Silence | No sound can pass into or out of the area. |
| Tongues | Creatures in the area can communicate with any other creature in the area, even without a shared language. |
| Vulnerability | Creatures of the chosen types have Vulnerability to damage that originates from the area. |

If you cast this Spell on the same area every day for **365** days, the effect lasts until dispelled.
