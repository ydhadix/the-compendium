### Dream
5th-Level Illusion
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a handful of sand)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | — |
| Target | One creature you know on your plane |
| Duration | 8 hours |

##### Spell Effect
You, or an Ally within Reach, becomes a dream messenger, entering a trance in which it is Incapacitated and Immobile.

While the target is asleep, the messenger appears in its dreams, can converse with it, and can shape the dream's environment.  The target recalls the dream on waking.

The messenger can end this Spell at any time _(no action required)_.

You can make the messenger terrifying.  If you do, it delivers a message of ten words or fewer, and the target makes a Will Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | The target gains no benefit from its rest and takes **3d6** Psychic Damage when it wakes. |
| Success | No effect. |
