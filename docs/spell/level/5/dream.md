### Dream
5th-Level Illusion
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | The same Plane |
| Area | — |
| Target | **1** Creature you are familiar with |
| Duration | 8 hours |

---

You, or an Ally within Reach, becomes a Dream Messenger to the target.  You incapacitate and immobilize the messenger.

##### Haunt
**Free Action; Once per Casting:** You can force an Asleep target to make a Will Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | The target gains no benefit from its rest.  After it wakes, you deal **3d6** Psychic Damage. |
| Success | No effect. |

#### Ending Early
The messenger can end this Spell at any time _(no action required)_.

---

#### Dream Messengers
A **Dream Messenger** is a creature that walks in another creature's dreams.

While a Dream Messenger's target is Asleep, the Dream Messenger can appear in its dreams, converse with it, and shape the dream's environment.  The sleeping creature recalls the dream on waking.
