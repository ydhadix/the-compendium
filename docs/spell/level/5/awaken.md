### Awaken
5th-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Druid |
| Components | V, S, M _(consumes an agate worth 1,000 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 8 hours |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | One Beast or Plant with an Intelligence of -4 or less, or one natural plant |
| Duration | — |

##### Spell Effect
The target's Intelligence becomes **0** and learns to speak and understand one language you know.  A natural plant becomes a Plant creature that can move and gains senses like a human's, using statistics the DM chooses.

You charm the target for **30** days, or until you or an Ally deals damage to it.  After the Charm ends, the target chooses its attitude toward you.
