### Wall of Force
5th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Area | Special |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

---

Choose **1** of the following forms:

| Form | |
|---|---|
| Wall | A flat surface of up to **10** contiguous **10**-foot-by-**10**-foot panels, in any orientation |
| Dome | Around all or part of up to a **10**-foot-radius Sphere |

You conjure a **¼**-inch thick barrier of magical force in the chosen form.

The barrier has the following effects:

| Effect | |
|---|---|
| Invisible | The barrier is Invisible. |
| Immovable | The barrier is Anchored. |
| Impervious | The barrier has Immunity to all damage and can't be dispelled. |
| Impassable | Nothing can pass through the barrier, including Attacks and other effects. |
| Ward Travel | The barrier blocks teleportation and travel through the Ethereal Plane. |
| Ward Targeting | A magical effect can't target anything in the area from outside the barrier. |
| Ward Area | The area is excluded from every magical effect's area. |

Before the barrier appears, you propel each Creature and Object in the barrier's space to either side of it.
