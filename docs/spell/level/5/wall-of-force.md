### Wall of Force
5th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a shard of glass)_ |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You create an Invisible wall of force.  The wall can be a dome or globe up to a **10**-foot-radius Sphere, or a flat surface of ten contiguous **10**-foot-by-**10**-foot panels, in any orientation.

The wall is a quarter inch thick and lasts for the duration.  A creature in the wall's space when it appears is moved to one side of your choice.

Nothing can pass through the wall, and it also blocks teleportation and travel through the Ethereal Plane.  It is immune to all damage and can't be dispelled by _Dispel Magic_, but a _Disintegrate_ Spell destroys it instantly.
