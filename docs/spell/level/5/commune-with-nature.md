### Commune with Nature
5th-Level Divination
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Ranger |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | 1 minute, or Ritual |
| Range | — |
| Target | a 3-mile Aura, or a 300-ft. Aura while underground |
| Duration | — |

##### Spell Effect
You gain knowledge of the natural land in the area.  This Spell doesn't function where nature has been replaced by construction.

Choose three of the following facts to learn about the area:

- The locations of settlements.
- The locations of portals to other planes.
- The location of one Level 12 or higher Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, or Undead _(DM's choice)_.
- The most prevalent kind of plant, mineral, or Beast _(your choice)_.
- The locations of bodies of water.
