### Teleportation Circle
5th-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, M _(consumes rare inks worth 50 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | 10 ft. |
| Target | a 5-ft. radius Circle |
| Duration | Until the end of your next Turn |

##### Teleportation Sigils
Permanent teleportation circles are identified by a unique series of arcane symbols and runes.

The first time you learn or prepare this Spell, you learn the sigils for **2** permanent teleportation circles.  If you encounter additional sigils, you can learn them by spending **1** minute studying.

##### Spell Effect
Choose one of your known sigils.

The area becomes a portal to the chosen sigil's teleportation circle; anything that enters the area is teleported within **5** feet of the destination circle, or vice-versa.

By casting this Spell on the same area every day for **365** days, you create a permanent teleportation circle there.
