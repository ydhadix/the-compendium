### Teleportation Circle
5th-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, M _(consumes rare inks worth 50 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | 10 ft. |
| Area | A 5-ft. radius Circle |
| Target | — |
| Duration | Until the end of your next Turn |

---

Choose **1** of your known Teleportation Sigils.

The area becomes a portal to the chosen sigil's teleportation circle; anything that enters the area is teleported within **5** feet of the destination circle, or the reverse.

#### Permanence
If you cast this Spell on the same area every day for **365** days, the teleportation circle becomes permanent and you learn its sigil.

---

#### Teleportation Sigils
**Teleportation Sigils** are unique series of arcane symbols and runes that identify a permanent teleportation circle.

The first time you learn or prepare _Teleportation Circle_, you learn **2** sigils.  If you encounter additional sigils, you can learn them by spending **1** minute studying.
