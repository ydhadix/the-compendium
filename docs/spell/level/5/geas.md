### Geas
5th-Level Enchantment
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Cleric, Druid, Wizard |
| Components | V |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | 60 ft. |
| Target | One creature |
| Duration | 30 days |

##### Spell Effect
You give the target a verbal command to carry out a service, or to refrain from some action or course of activity.  It makes a Will Save, and automatically succeeds if it can't understand your command.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You charm the target.  While Charmed, whenever the target acts in a manner directly counter to your command, you deal **5d10** Psychic Damage to it, no more than once per day. |
| Success | No effect. |

You can't command the target to take a suicidal action; doing so ends this Spell.  A _Remove Curse_ or _Greater Restoration_ also ends it.

##### At Higher Levels
When Upcast to 7th-Level, the duration is **365** days.

When Upcast to 9th-Level, the target remains Charmed until this Spell is dispelled.
