### Arcane Hand
5th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(an eggshell and a glove)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You create a hand of magical energy in an unoccupied space.  It is a Large object with AC **20**, **your Hit Point Maximum** Hit Points, and doesn't occupy its space.

This Spell ends if the hand drops to **0** Hit Points.

###### Command
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You move the hand up to **60** feet, then choose one effect:

| | |
|---|---|
| Clenched Fist | You make a Melee Spell Attack against a creature within **5** feet of the hand; on a hit, you deal **5d8** Force Damage. |
| Forceful Hand | A Huge or smaller creature within **5** feet of the hand makes a Fortitude Save; on a failure, you propel the target **5 × (Spellcasting Ability + 1)** feet away from the hand, and the hand moves with it. |
| Grasping Hand | A Huge or smaller creature within **5** feet of the hand makes a Reflex Save; on a failure, the hand grapples the target _(DC **Spell Save DC**)_.  While the hand is grappling, you can use a Bonus Action to crush the target, dealing **4d6 + Spellcasting Ability** Bludgeoning Damage. |
| Interposing Hand | The hand counts as Half Cover for you against Attacks and effects that originate from or pass through its space, and its space is Difficult Terrain for your Enemies. |

##### At Higher Levels
Add **2d8** to the Clenched Fist damage and **2d6** to the Grasping Hand damage per additional Spell Level.
