### Arcane Hand
5th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Area | — |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

---

You create a hand of magical energy.  It is a Large, intangible object with Armor Class **20** and **your Hit Point Maximum** Hit Points.

##### Command
**Bonus Action:** You can move the hand up to **60** feet, then choose **1** of the following effects:

| Effect | |
|---|---|
| Clenched Fist | You make a Melee Spell Attack against a Creature or Object within **5** feet of the hand; on a hit, you deal **5d8 + Spellcasting Ability** Force Damage. |
| Pushing Hand | A Huge or smaller Creature or Object within **5** feet of the hand makes a Fortitude Save; on a failure, you propel the target **5 × (Spellcasting Ability + 1)** feet away from the hand, and the hand moves with it. |
| Grappling Hand | A Huge or smaller Creature or Object within **5** feet of the hand makes a Reflex Save; on a failure, the hand grapples the target _(DC **Spell Save DC**)_. |
| Crushing Hand | While the hand is grappling, you can deal **4d6 + Spellcasting Ability** Bludgeoning Damage to the Grappled target. |
| Covering Hand | The hand counts as Half Cover for you against Attacks and effects, and its space is Difficult Terrain for your Enemies. |

#### Ending Early
This Spell ends if the hand drops to **0** Hit Points.

#### At Higher Levels
Add **2d8** to the Clenched Fist damage and **2d6** to the Crushing Hand damage per additional Spell Level.
