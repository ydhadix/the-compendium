### Dispel Good & Evil
5th-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | — |
| Area | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

---

You gain the effect of _Protection from Good & Evil_.

#### Ending Early
This Spell ends after you use either of the following Actions.

##### Break Enchantment
**Magic Action:** You can choose a Creature within Reach that is possessed, Charmed, or Frightened by a Planar Creature.

You end those effects on the target.

##### Banishment
**Magic Action:** You can force a Planar Creature within Reach to make a Will Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | If the target is not on its home plane, you teleport it there.<br>Otherwise, you teleport it to a plane associated with its Planar Creature Type. |
| Success | No effect. |
