### Creation
5th-Level Illusion
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a paintbrush)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Target | a 5-ft. Cube |
| Duration | Special |

##### Spell Effect
You create a nonliving object of vegetable or mineral matter in the area and of a form and material you have seen.  The object's duration depends on its material; for an object of multiple materials, use the shortest.

| Material | Duration |
|---|---|
| Vegetable matter | 24 hours |
| Stone or crystal | 12 hours |
| Precious metals | 1 hour |
| Gems | 10 minutes |
| Adamantine or mithral | 1 minute |

##### At Higher Levels
Add **5** feet to the Area per additional Spell Level.
