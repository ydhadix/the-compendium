### Reincarnate
5th-Level Necromancy
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid |
| Components | V, S, M _(consumes rare oils worth 1,000 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 hour |
| Range | Reach |
| Area | — |
| Target | **1** Humanoid Corpse that died within the past 10 days |
| Duration | — |

---

Randomly choose a non-Construct Race.

You resurrect each target into a new body of the chosen Race.  Each target retains its memories and capabilities, but loses the traits of its previous Race and gains those of its new one.
