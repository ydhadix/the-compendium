### Reincarnate
5th-Level Necromancy
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid |
| Components | V, S, M _(consumes rare oils worth 1,000 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 hour |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | One dead Humanoid that died within the past 10 days |
| Duration | — |

##### Spell Effect
You form a new body for the target and call its soul to enter it.  Roll **1d10** for the new body's Race, or the DM chooses one.

| 1d10 | Race |
|---|---|
| 1 | Aasimar |
| 2 | Dragonborn |
| 3 | Dwarf |
| 4 | Elf |
| 5 | Gnome |
| 6 | Goliath |
| 7 | Halfling |
| 8 | Human |
| 9 | Orc |
| 10 | Tiefling |

The target retains its memories and capabilities, but loses the traits of its previous Race and gains those of its new one.
