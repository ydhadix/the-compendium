### Detect Poison & Disease
1st-Level Divination
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action, or Ritual |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You sense the location of any poisons, poisonous or venomous creatures, and magical contagions, and can identify the kind of poison, creature, or contagion.

This Spell can't sense through **1** foot of stone, dirt, or wood; **1** inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
