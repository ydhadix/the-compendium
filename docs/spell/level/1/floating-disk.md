### Floating Disk
1st-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Normal Action, or Ritual |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 1 hour |

##### Spell Effect
You create a circular, horizontal plane of force that floats **3** feet above the ground. The disk is **3** feet in diameter and **1** inch thick.

- While you are within **20** feet of it, the disk is immobile.
- If you move more than **20** feet from it, the disk follows you so it remains within **20** feet. The disk can move across uneven terrain, stairs, slopes, and the like, but it can't cross an elevation change of **10** feet or more.

This Spell ends if the disk holds more than **500** pounds or you are more than **100** feet from it.
