### Zephyr Strike
1st-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Ranger |
| Components | V |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

Your Speed increases by **10** feet, and your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.

On your Turn, you can shorten this Spell's Duration to the end of your Turn. If you do, your Speed increases by an additional **30** feet, and the next Weapon Attack you make has Advantage and deals an additional **1d8** Force Damage.
