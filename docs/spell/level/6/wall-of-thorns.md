### Wall of Thorns
6th-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Area | A 60-ft. × 5-ft. × 10-ft. Wall, or a 10-ft.-radius, 20-ft. Cylinder |
| Target | Each Creature and Object |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

---

The area is Heavily Obscured, and each foot a creature moves through it costs **3** extra feet.

After a target enters the area _(including when this Spell is cast)_, it makes a Reflex Save.  After your Turn starts, each target in the area also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **7d8** Piercing Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

#### At Higher Levels
Add **1d8** damage per additional Spell Level.
