### Move Earth
6th-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a miniature shovel)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Target | A 5- to 40-ft. Square |
| Duration | 2 hours (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You reshape the dirt, sand, and clay in the area however you choose: raising or lowering its elevation, digging or filling a trench, or raising or flattening a wall or pillar.  No change can exceed half the area's largest dimension, and each takes **10** minutes to complete.

The transformation is slow enough that it doesn't trap or injure creatures in the area.  After every **10** minutes of Concentration, you can choose a new area within range.

This Spell can't manipulate natural stone or stone construction, and moved earth carries its plants along with it.  Rocks and structures shift to accommodate the new terrain, and an unstable structure might collapse.
