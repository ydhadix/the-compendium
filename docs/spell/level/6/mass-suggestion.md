### Mass Suggestion
6th-Level Enchantment
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, M _(a snake's tongue)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 60 ft. |
| Target | up to 12 creatures who understand you |
| Duration | 24 hours |

##### Spell Effect
You suggest a course of activity to the targets in **25** words or fewer.  The suggestion must sound achievable and must not obviously damage a target or its Allies.  Each target makes a Will Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You charm the target. While Charmed, it follows the suggestion to the best of its ability. This Spell ends on the target after it completes the suggested activity, or after you or an Ally damage it. |
| Success | No effect. |

##### At Higher Levels
When Upcast, the duration is **10** days _(7th-Level)_, **30** days _(8th-Level)_, or **366** days _(9th-Level)_.
