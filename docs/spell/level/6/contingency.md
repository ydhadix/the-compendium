### Contingency
6th-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a gem-encrusted statuette of yourself worth 1,500 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 10 minutes |
| Range | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 10 days |

##### Spell Effect
Choose a Spell of 5th-Level or lower that you can cast, that has a Casting Time of a Magic Action, and that can target you.  You cast that Spell as part of casting this one, expending both Spell Slots, and you describe a trigger for it.

The contingent Spell takes effect on you alone the first time the trigger occurs, whether or not you want it to, and this Spell then ends.

This Spell also ends if you cast it again or its Material Component leaves your person.
