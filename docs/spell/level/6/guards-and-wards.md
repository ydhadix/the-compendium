### Guards and Wards
6th-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a silver rod worth 10 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 hour |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | A 5- to 50-ft. Cube |
| Duration | 24 hours |

##### Spell Effect
You can specify creatures that this Spell doesn't affect, and a password that leaves a creature speaking it within **5** feet of the area unaffected.

The area gains all of the following effects:

| | |
|---|---|
| Corridors | Fog makes the corridors in the area Heavily Obscured.  At each intersection or branching passage, a creature other than you has a **50**% chance of believing it is going in the opposite direction from the one it chooses. |
| Doors | Doors in the area are locked as if sealed by _Arcane Lock_, and you can cover up to **10** of them with an illusion of plain wall. |
| Stairs | Webs fill the stairs in the area, as _Web_.  Destroyed strands regrow in **10** minutes. |
| Other Effect | Choose one: _Dancing Lights_ repeating in **4** corridors, _Magic Mouth_ in **2** locations, _Stinking Cloud_ in **2** locations _(dispersed vapors return in **10** minutes)_, _Gust of Wind_ blowing continuously in **1** corridor or room, or _Suggestion_ delivered to any creature entering a **5**-foot Square. |

_Dispel Magic_ has no effect on this Spell, but each effect above can be dispelled on its own; this Spell ends after all **4** are dispelled.

If you cast this Spell on the same area every day for **365** days, the effect lasts until dispelled.
