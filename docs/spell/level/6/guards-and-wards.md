### Guards and Wards
6th-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a silver rod worth 10 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 hour |
| Range | Reach |
| Area | Up to a 50-ft. Cube |
| Target | Each Creature and Object |
| Duration | 24 hours |

---

The area gains the following effects.  This Spell can't be dispelled by another effect, but each of its effects can; after each of its effects is dispelled, this Spell is dispelled.

| Effect | |
|---|---|
| Fogged Corridors | The corridors in the area gain the effect of _Fog Cloud_ _(dispersed fog returns in **10** minutes)_.  At each intersection or branching passage, a target other than you has a **50**% chance of believing it is going in the opposite direction from the one it chooses. |
| Locked Doors | Doors in the area gain the effect of _Arcane Lock_, and you can cover up to **10** of them with an illusion of plain wall. |
| Webbed Stairs | The stairs in the area gain the effect of _Web_ _(destroyed strands regrow in **10** minutes)_. |

The area gains **1** of the following effects:

| Effect | |
|---|---|
| Dancing Lights | **4** corridors in the area gain the effect of _Dancing Lights_. |
| Gust of Wind | **1** corridor or room in the area gains the effect of _Gust of Wind_. |
| Magic Mouth | **2** locations in the area gain the effect of _Magic Mouth_. |
| Stinking Cloud | **2** locations in the area gain the effect of _Stinking Cloud_ _(dispersed vapors return in **10** minutes)_. |
| Suggestion | A target entering a **5**-foot Square you choose gains the effect of _Suggestion_. |

#### Password
After you cast this Spell, you can choose a password.  After a creature speaks this password, or presents it visually, the creature ignores this Spell's effects.

#### Permanence
If you cast this Spell on the same area every day for **365** days, the effect lasts until dispelled.
