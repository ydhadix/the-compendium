### Magic Jar
6th-Level Necromancy
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a gem, crystal, or reliquary worth 500 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | Until dispelled |

##### Spell Effect
Your soul leaves your body and enters the Material Component, which becomes its container.  While your soul is elsewhere, your body is Incapacitated.

While your soul is in the container, you sense your surroundings from the container's space, and you can't Move or take Reactions.

Your soul returns to your body after the container is destroyed or this Spell ends; if your body is dead or more than **100** feet away, you die instead.  If another creature's soul is in the container when it is destroyed, that soul returns to its body if the body is alive and within **100** feet; otherwise, that creature dies.

The container is destroyed when this Spell ends.

###### Project
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can project your soul up to **100** feet from the container, either returning to your body _(ending this Spell)_ or possessing one Humanoid.  A creature warded by _Protection from Good & Evil_ or _Magic Circle_ can't be possessed.

The Humanoid makes a Will Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | Your soul enters the target's body and you control it, and its soul becomes trapped in the container, where it perceives with its own senses but is Incapacitated.  Your Hit Points, Hit Dice, Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Speed, and Senses are replaced by the target's; you keep your other statistics. |
| Success | You can't attempt to possess the target again for **24** hours. |

###### Withdraw
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

While possessing a body, you can return to the container if it is within **100** feet, returning the host's soul to its body.

If the host body dies while you possess it, the host dies and you make a Will Save against your Spell Save DC.  On a success, you return to the container if it is within **100** feet; otherwise, you die.
