### Wall of Ice
6th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a piece of quartz)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You create a wall of ice on a solid surface.  The wall can be a dome or globe up to a **10**-foot-radius Sphere, or a flat surface of **10** contiguous **10**-foot-by-**10**-foot panels, in any orientation.

The wall is **1** foot thick and lasts for the duration.  Before the wall appears, you propel everything in the wall's space to one side of the wall, and the target makes a Reflex save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **10d6** Cold Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

Each panel is an object with AC **12**, **30** Hit Points, Immunity to Cold, Poison, and Psychic Damage, and Vulnerability to Fire Damage.  A panel with **0** Hit Points is destroyed, leaving a sheet of frigid air in its space.

After a creature enters the frigid air, it makes a Fortitude Save.  A creature only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **5d6** Cold Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### At Higher Levels
Add **2d6** damage when the wall appears and **1d6** damage from the frigid air per additional Spell Level.
