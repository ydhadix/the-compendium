### Wall of Ice
6th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Area | Special |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

---

Choose **1** of the following forms:

| Form | |
|---|---|
| Wall | A flat surface of up to **10** contiguous **10**-foot-by-**10**-foot panels, in any orientation |
| Dome | All or part of up to a **10**-foot-radius Sphere _(the barrier is a single panel)_ |

You conjure a **1**-foot thick barrier of ice on a solid surface in the chosen form.

Before the barrier appears, you propel each Creature and Object in the barrier's space to either side of it.  Then, each target makes a Reflex Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **10d6** Cold Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

Each panel is an object with Armor Class **12**, **30** Hit Points, Cold Immunity, and Fire Vulnerability.

A panel with **0** Hit Points is destroyed, leaving a sheet of frigid air in its space.  After a target enters the frigid air, it makes a Fortitude Save.  After your Turn starts, each target in the frigid air also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **5d6** Cold Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

#### At Higher Levels
Add **2d6** damage when the barrier appears and **1d6** damage from the frigid air per additional Spell Level.
