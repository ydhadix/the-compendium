### Freezing Sphere
6th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a miniature crystal sphere)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 300 ft. |
| Target | Each creature in a 60-ft. radius Sphere |
| Duration | — |

##### Spell Effect
Each target makes a Fortitude Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **10d6** Cold Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

A body of water in the area freezes **6** inches deep across a **30**-foot Square for **1** minute.  A creature swimming on the surface is Restrained until it succeeds on an Athletics Check against your Spell Save DC.

You can instead hold the globe, which you or a creature you give it to can throw up to **40** feet or hurl with a Sling.  It shatters on impact with the same effect as this Spell, and explodes on its own after **1** minute.

##### At Higher Levels
Add **1d6** damage per additional Spell Level.
