### Stone Shape
3rd-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(soft clay)_ |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | One Medium or smaller stone object, or a 5-ft. section of stone |
| Duration | — |

##### Spell Effect
You reshape the target into any form you like, such as a weapon, a container, a sealed door, or a passage through stone up to **5** feet thick.  The new form can have up to two hinges and a latch, but no finer mechanical detail.
