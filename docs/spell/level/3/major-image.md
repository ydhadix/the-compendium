### Major Image
3rd-Level Illusion
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Area | A 20-ft. Cube |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

---

You conjure the image of an object, creature, or other visible phenomenon.  It seems real, including sounds, smells, and temperatures, but it can't deal damage or cause Conditions.

##### Move
**Magic Action:** You can move the image up to **120** feet.  As it moves, you can alter its appearance and the sounds it makes so they seem natural.

#### At Higher Levels
When Upcast to 4th-Level or higher, this Spell lasts until dispelled and doesn't require Concentration.
