### Wind Wall
3rd-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Ranger |
| Components | V, S, M _(a fan and a feather)_ |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You create a wall of strong wind on the ground, up to **50** feet long, **15** feet high, and **1** foot thick, shaped into any continuous path.

When the wall appears, each creature in its area makes a Fortitude Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **4d8** Bludgeoning Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

The wall keeps fog, smoke, and gases at bay.  Small or smaller flying creatures and objects can't pass through it, nor can a creature in _Gaseous Form_.  Ordinary projectiles launched through the wall automatically miss, though boulders and siege-engine projectiles are unaffected.
