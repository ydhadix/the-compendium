### Plant Growth
3rd-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Druid, Ranger |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Magic Action, or 8 hours |
| Range | 150 ft. |
| Area | Special |
| Target | — |
| Duration | **1** year |

---

If you cast this Spell with its full Casting Time, choose a **½**-mile radius Sphere.  All plants in the area yield twice the food.

Otherwise, choose a **100**-foot radius Sphere.  You can exclude any part of the area.  All normal plants in the area become thick and overgrown, and the area gains the following effects:

- The area is Heavily Obscured.
- Each foot of movement in the area costs **3** extra feet.
