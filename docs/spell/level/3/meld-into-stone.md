### Meld into Stone
3rd-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric, Druid, Ranger |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Magic Action, or Ritual |
| Range | Reach |
| Area | — |
| Target | **1** stone Object large enough to contain your body |
| Duration | 8 hours |

---

You gain the following effects:

- You merge seamlessly with the target.
- You are Blinded and gain Disadvantage on Perception Checks to hear beyond the target, though you can cast Spells targeting only yourself without Verbal or Somatic components.

After the target is partially destroyed or changed so that you no longer fit within it, this Spell ends, you are expelled, take **6d6** Force Damage, and fall Prone.  Minor damage to the target doesn't harm you.

After the target is completely destroyed or changed, this Spell ends, you are expelled, take **50** Force Damage, and fall Prone.

##### Emerge
**Free Action; 5 feet of movement:** You can leave the stone, ending this Spell.
