### Silver Viper
3rd-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range | — |
| Area | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 1 hour |

---

You gain **15** Temporary Hit Points and a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.  After you lose your last Temporary Hit Point, this Spell ends.

##### Viper Strike
**Magic Action:** You can make a Ranged Spell Attack against a Creature or Object within **60** feet.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **1d6** Poison Damage, and you sicken the target until the end of your next Turn. While Sickened by this Spell, a target is Incapacitated. |

#### At Higher Levels
Add **5** Temporary Hit Points and **1d6** damage per additional Spell Level.
