### Glyph of Warding
3rd-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(consumes powdered diamond worth 200 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 hour |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | — |
| Duration | Until dispelled or triggered |

##### Spell Effect
You inscribe a glyph on a surface or inside an object that can be closed, covering an area up to **10** feet across.  If the surface or object moves more than **10** feet from where you cast this Spell, the glyph breaks and this effect ends.

A creature can make a Perception Check against your Spell Save DC to notice the glyph.

When you inscribe the glyph, you set its trigger — such as touching the glyph, opening the object, or approaching within a set distance — and choose Explosive Rune or Spell Glyph.  You can limit the trigger to certain Creature Types, or exempt creatures that speak a password.  The glyph triggers once, then this effect ends.

##### Explosive Rune
The glyph erupts in a **20**-foot radius Sphere.  Each creature in the area makes a Reflex Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **5d8** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder Damage. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### Spell Glyph
You store a Spell of 3rd-Level or lower in the glyph by casting it as part of the inscription, targeting a single creature or an area.  The stored Spell has no effect until the glyph triggers.

When the glyph triggers, the stored Spell takes effect, targeting the creature that triggered it or centering its area on that creature.  Any Hostile creatures or harmful effects it creates appear as close as possible to that creature and act against it.  A stored Spell requiring Concentration lasts its full Duration.

##### At Higher Levels
Add **1d8** to the Explosive Rune's damage per additional Spell Level.  A Spell Glyph can store a Spell up to the Level of the Slot used.
