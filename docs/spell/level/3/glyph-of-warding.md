### Glyph of Warding
3rd-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(consumes powdered diamond worth 200 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | 1 hour |
| Range | Reach |
| Area | Up to a 10-ft. radius Circle |
| Target | Up to **1** Object that can be closed |
| Duration | Until dispelled |

---

Choose a perceivable trigger, and one of the following glyphs:

| Glyph | Effect |
|---|---|
| Contingent | Choose a lower-Level Spell that you can cast, that has a Casting Time of a Magic Action or a Bonus Action, and that can target **1** Creature or an Area.<br>You cast that Spell as part of casting this one, expending both Spell Slots, but the chosen Spell's effect is delayed.<br>After this glyph triggers, the Spell takes effect _(no Concentration required)_, targeting the triggering Creature or an Area centered on it. |
| Explosive | Choose an Elemental Damage Type.<br>After this glyph triggers, each Creature and Object in a **20**-foot radius Sphere makes a Reflex Save.<br>**Fail.** You deal **5d8** damage of the chosen type.<br>**Success.** You deal half as much damage. |

You inscribe the chosen glyph on the target.

You can choose a password.  Before a Creature triggers the glyph, if it speaks this password, the glyph doesn't trigger.

#### Ending Early
This Spell ends after the trigger occurs or the target moves more than **10** feet from where you cast this Spell.

#### At Higher Levels
Add **1d8** to the Explosive Glyph's damage per additional Spell Level.
