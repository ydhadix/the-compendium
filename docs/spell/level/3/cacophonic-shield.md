### Cacophonic Shield
3rd-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Anything you choose in a 10-ft. Aura |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You gain Thunder Resistance, and Ranged Attacks gain Disadvantage if they pass through the area.

After a target enters the area _(including when this Spell is cast or the area moves)_, it makes a Fortitude Save.  After your Turn starts, each target also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **3d6** Thunder Damage, and the target is Deafened until the start of its next Turn. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### At Higher Levels
Add **1d6** damage per additional Spell Level.
