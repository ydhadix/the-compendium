### Spirit Guardians
3rd-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Each creature in a 15-ft. Aura |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

Choose Radiant or Necrotic. Creatures you choose have their Speed halved while in the area.

A creature you choose that enters the area (including when this Spell is cast or the area moves) or ends its Turn there makes a Will Save, no more than once per Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **3d8** damage of the chosen type. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### At Higher Levels

Add **1d8** damage per additional Spell Level.
