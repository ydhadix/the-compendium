### Spirit Guardians
3rd-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Anything you choose in a 15-ft. Aura |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
Choose Radiant or Necrotic.

Each target's Speed becomes halved while in the area.

After a target enters the area _(including when this Spell is cast or the area moves)_, it makes a Will Save.  After your Turn starts, each target also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **3d8** damage of the chosen type. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |

##### At Higher Levels
Add **1d8** damage per additional Spell Level.
