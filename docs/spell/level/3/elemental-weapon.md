### Elemental Weapon
3rd-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Druid, Paladin, Ranger |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | One weapon |
| Duration | 1 hour (Concentration) |

Choose an Elemental Damage Type. The weapon gains a **+1** bonus to Attack and Damage Rolls and deals an additional **1d4** damage of the chosen type.

##### At Higher Levels

Add **1d4** damage per additional Spell Level.

When Upcast to 5th- or 6th-Level, the bonus to Attack and Damage Rolls becomes **+2**.

When Upcast to 7th-Level or higher, the bonus becomes **+3**.
