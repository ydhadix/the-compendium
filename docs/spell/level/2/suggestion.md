### Suggestion
2nd-Level Enchantment
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Area | |
| Target | **1** Creature who understands you |
| Duration | 8 hours (Concentration) |

---

You suggest a course of activity to the target in **25** words or fewer.  The suggestion must sound achievable and must not obviously damage the target or its Allies.

Each target makes a Will Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You charm the target. While Charmed, it follows the suggestion to the best of its ability. |
| Success | No effect. |

#### Ending Early
This Spell ends on a target after it completes the suggested activity, or after you or an Ally damage it.

#### At Higher Levels
Add **1** target per additional Spell Level.
