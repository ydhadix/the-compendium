### Locate Animals or Plants
2nd-Level Divination
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Druid, Ranger |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Normal Action, or Ritual |
| Range | 5 miles |
| Target | Special |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
Choose a specific kind of Beast, plant creature, or plant.

You sense the direction to the nearest chosen target. If it is moving, you know the direction of its movement.

This Spell is blocked by any amount of lead.

##### At Higher Levels
Add **1** target per additional Spell Level.
