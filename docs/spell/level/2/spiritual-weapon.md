### Spiritual Weapon
2nd-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range | 60 ft. |
| Area | |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

---

You create an intangible, floating, spectral force resembling a weapon.  The weapon is **2** Sizes smaller than you.

While the weapon shares your space, it moves with you.

##### Attack
**Bonus Action:** You can move the weapon up to your Speed, then make a Melee Attack against a Creature or Object within **5** feet of it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit | You deal **1d8 + Spellcasting Ability** Force Damage. |

#### At Higher Levels
Add **1d8** damage per additional Spell Level.
