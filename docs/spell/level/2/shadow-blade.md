### Shadow Blade
2nd-Level Illusion
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You create a sword of solidified shadow in your hand. While in Dim Light or Darkness, you have Advantage on Attack Rolls with the sword. It vanishes after you drop or throw it.

The sword is a Simple Melee Weapon with Finesse, Light, and Thrown (20/60 ft.). Attacks using it can use your Spellcasting Ability for Attack and Damage Rolls, and its Damage Die is **2d8** Psychic Damage.

As a Bonus Action, you resummon the sword to your hand.

##### At Higher Levels
Add **1d8** Psychic Damage per additional Spell Level.
