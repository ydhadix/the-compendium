### Find Steed
2nd-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Paladin |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Area | — |
| Target | — |
| Duration | Until dispelled |

---

Choose a Creature Type: Celestial, Fey, or Fiend.

You conjure an Otherworldly Steed Companion with the chosen Creature Type that resembles a rideable Beast.  If you already have a steed from this Spell, it adopts the chosen form and Creature Type instead.

You can also use this Spell to turn an Ally Beast into a steed.  It becomes a Companion to you and gains the chosen Creature Type.

After the steed drops to **0** Hit Points or you die, this Spell ends.

##### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the steed to move and take any Action available to it.

#### At Higher Levels
When Upcast at 4th-Level or higher, the steed gains a **60**-foot Fly Speed.
