##### Spell Effect
Choose a Large, rideable Beast and a Creature Type: Celestial, Fey, or Fiend.

You summon a companion Otherworldly Steed with the chosen Creature Type that resembles the chosen creature.  If you already have a steed from this spell, it adopts the chosen form and Creature Type instead.

You can also use this Spell to turn an allied Beast into a steed.  It becomes a companion and gains the chosen Creature Type.

This spell ends if the steed drops to **0** Hit Points or you die; the steed vanishes, leaving behind anything it was wearing or carrying.

###### Command
Free Action
{ .subtitle }

You can command the steed to move and take any Action available to it.

##### At Higher Levels
The Spell Level determines the steed's Armor Class, Hit Points, and attack damage.

When cast at 4th-Level or higher, the steed gains a **60** foot Fly Speed.

##### Otherworldly Steed
Large Celestial, Fey, or Fiend, Neutral
