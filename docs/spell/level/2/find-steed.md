### Find Steed
2nd-Level Conjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Paladin |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Area | |
| Target | — |
| Duration | — |

---

Choose a Large, rideable Beast and a Creature Type: Celestial, Fey, or Fiend.

You conjure an Otherworldly Steed Companion with the chosen Creature Type that resembles the chosen creature.  If you already have a steed from this Spell, it adopts the chosen form and Creature Type instead.

You can also use this Spell to turn an allied Beast into a steed.  It becomes a Companion to you and gains the chosen Creature Type.

##### Command
**Free Action:** You can command the steed to move and take any Action available to it.

#### Ending Early
This Spell ends if the steed drops to **0** Hit Points or you die; the steed vanishes, leaving behind anything it was wearing or carrying.

#### At Higher Levels
The Spell Level determines the steed's Armor Class, Hit Points, and attack damage.

When cast at 4th-Level or higher, the steed gains a **60**-foot Fly Speed.
