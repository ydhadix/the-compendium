### Prayer of Healing
2nd-Level Abjuration
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric, Paladin |
| Components | V |
| Casting Time | 10 minutes |
| Range | 30 ft. |
| Target | 5 creatures |
| Duration | — |

You heal the target by **2d8**, and the target gains the benefit of a Short Rest.

A target can't be affected by this Spell again until it finishes a Long Rest.

##### At Higher Levels

Add **1d8** to the healing per additional Spell Level.
