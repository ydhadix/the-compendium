### Magic Aura
2nd-Level Illusion
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | One creature or object |
| Duration | 24 hours |

You alter how the target appears to Spells and magical effects.

- **Creature.** Choose one or two Creature Types. The target is treated as a creature of the chosen types instead of its usual type. Spells and effects that affect only the chosen types can affect the target.
- **Object.** Choose one School of Magic, or none. The target appears to contain magic of the chosen School, or to be nonmagical.

##### At Higher Levels

Add **1** target per additional Spell Level.
