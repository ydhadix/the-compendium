### Gust of Wind
2nd-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | Reach |
| Area | A 60-ft. × 10-ft. Line |
| Target | Each Creature and Object |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

---

The area blasts strong wind and moves with you.  Each foot of movement in the area costs **1** extra foot moving towards you.

After a target enters the area _(including when this Spell is cast or the area moves)_, it makes a Fortitude Save.  After your Turn starts, each target also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You propel the target **15** feet away from you. |
| Success | No effect. |

##### Rotate
**Bonus Action:** You can rotate the area around you.
