### Gust of Wind
2nd-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | Reach |
| Target | Everything in a 10-ft. × 60-ft. Line |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
The area blasts strong wind away from you and moves with you. A creature in the area that moves closer to you spends **1** extra foot of movement for each foot moved.

After your Turn starts, each target makes a Fortitude Save.  After a target enters the area (including when this Spell is cast or the area moves), it also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You propel the target **15** feet away from you. |
| Success | No effect. |

###### Rotate
Bonus Action
{ .subtitle }

You rotate the area's direction around you.
