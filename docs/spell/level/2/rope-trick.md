### Rope Trick
2nd-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Artificer, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(rope worth 1 Gold)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | Reach |
| Area | — |
| Target | — |
| Duration | 1 hour |

---

One end of the Material Component hovers perpendicular to the ground, up to its length or until it reaches a ceiling.  At the top, an invisible portal large enough for a Medium creature opens to an extradimensional space reachable by climbing the rope.

The space can hold up to **8** Medium or smaller creatures.  Attacks, Spells, and other effects can't pass into or out of the space, but creatures inside can see through the portal.  After this Spell ends, anything inside drops out.
