### Flame Blade
2nd-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Druid, Sorcerer |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 1 minute (Concentration) |

You create a sword of solidified flame in your hand. It emits Bright Light out to **10** feet and Dim Light for an additional **10** feet, and vanishes after you drop or throw it.

The sword is a Simple Melee Weapon with Finesse, Light, and Thrown (20/60 ft.). Attacks using it can use your Spellcasting Ability for Attack and Damage Rolls, and its Damage Die is **3d6** Fire Damage.

As a Bonus Action, you resummon the sword to your hand.

##### At Higher Levels

Add **1d6** Fire Damage per additional Spell Level.
