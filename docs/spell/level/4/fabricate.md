### Fabricate
4th-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Wizard |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | 10 minutes |
| Range | 120 ft. |
| Target | — |
| Duration | — |

##### Spell Effect
You convert raw materials into a finished product of the same material, provided you have enough of it.  You can fabricate a Large or smaller object, or a Medium or smaller object from metal, stone, or another mineral.

The object's quality matches the raw materials.  You can't create creatures or magic items, nor items requiring significant skill — such as weapons or armor — unless you have Proficiency with the relevant Artisan's Tools.
