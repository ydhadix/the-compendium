### Control Water
4th-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric, Druid, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M _(a mixture of water and dust)_ |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 300 ft. |
| Target | Water in a 100-ft. Cube |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You control the water in the area, choosing one of the effects below.

###### Change Effect
Magic Action
{ .subtitle }

You can repeat the current effect or choose a different one.

##### Flood
You raise the water level of all standing water in the area by up to **20** feet.  In a large body of water, you instead create a **20**-foot wave that crosses the area and crashes.  A Huge or smaller vehicle in the wave's path is carried along; a Huge or smaller vehicle the wave strikes has a **25**% chance of capsizing.  While this effect lasts, the wave repeats after your Turn starts.

##### Part Water
You part the water in the area into a trench, the separated water forming a wall to either side.  When you end this effect, the water fills the trench over the next Round.

##### Redirect Flow
You cause flowing water in the area to move in a direction you choose, even over obstacles or up walls.  Beyond the area, it resumes its natural flow.

##### Whirlpool
The area must be at least **50** feet square and **25** feet deep to use this effect.

You summon a whirlpool **5** feet wide at the base, up to **50** feet wide at the top, and **25** feet tall.  Then, you propel each creature in the water within **25** feet of the whirlpool **10** feet toward it.

Creatures in the area are immobilized.  An immobilized creature can Utilize to make an Athletics check against your Spell Save DC; on a success, it ignores this effect until the end of the Turn.

After a target enters the area __(including when the whirlpool is summoned)__, it makes a Fortitude Save.  After your Turn starts, you propel everything in the water within **25** feet of the whirlpool **10** feet toward it, then everything in the whirlpool also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You deal **4d8** Bludgeoning Damage and the target can't hold their breath until the start of your next Turn. |
| Success | You deal half as much damage. |
