### Control Water
4th-Level Transmutation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Cleric, Druid, Wizard |
| Components | V, S, M |
| Casting Time | Magic Action |
| Range | 300 ft. |
| Area | Up to a 100-ft. Cube |
| Target | Each Creature and Object |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

---

You control water in the area, choosing **1** of the following effects.

| Effect | |
|---|---|
| Flood | You raise the water level of all standing water in the area by up to **20** feet.  In a large body of water, you instead transform the water into a **20**-foot wave that crosses the area and crashes.  A Huge or smaller vehicle in the wave's path is carried along; a Huge or smaller vehicle the wave strikes has a **25**% chance of capsizing.  After your Turn starts, the wave repeats until you choose another effect. |
| Part Water | You part the water in the area into a trench, the separated water forming a wall to either side.  After you end this effect, the water fills the trench over the next Round. |
| Redirect Flow | You cause flowing water in the area to move in a direction you choose, even over obstacles or up walls.  Beyond the area, it resumes its natural flow. |
| Whirlpool | The water in the area must be at least **50** feet square and **25** feet deep to use this effect.  You transform the water into a whirlpool **5** feet wide at the base, up to **50** feet wide at the top, and **25** feet tall.<br>You immobilize each target in the whirlpool.<br>After you transform the water and after your Turn starts, you propel each target within **25** feet of the whirlpool **10** feet toward it.<br>After a target enters the whirlpool _(including when the water is transformed)_, it makes a Fortitude Save.  After your Turn starts, each target in the whirlpool also makes this Saving Throw.  A target only makes this Saving Throw once until the start of your next Turn.<br>**Fail.** You deal **4d8** Bludgeoning Damage and the target can't hold its breath until the start of your next Turn.<br>**Success.** You deal half as much damage. |

##### Break Free
Any Immobilized target can take this Action.

**Utilize Action:** The target makes an Athletics Check against your Spell Save DC.

| | |
|---|---|
| Success | The target ignores the whirlpool's movement penalties until the end of the Turn. |

##### Change Effect
**Magic Action:** You can change this Spell's chosen effect.
