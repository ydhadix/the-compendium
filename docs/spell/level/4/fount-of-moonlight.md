### Fount of Moonlight
4th-Level Evocation
{ .subtitle }

| | |
|---|---|
| Classes | Bard, Druid |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Normal Action |
| Range | — |
| Target | Self |
| Duration | 10 minutes (Concentration) |

##### Spell Effect
You emit Bright Light out to **20** feet and Dim Light **20** feet further.

You gain Radiant Resistance.

Your Melee Attacks deal an additional **2d6** Radiant Damage.

###### Flare
Reaction, when you take damage from a creature within **60** feet
{ .subtitle }

The creature makes a Fortitude Save.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fail | You blind the creature until the end of your next Turn. |
| Success | No effect. |
