## Spellcasting Ability
The way you cast a Spell sets which Ability you use as its Spellcasting Ability. You use your Spellcasting Ability to determine your Spell Attacks and your Spell Save DC for the Spells you cast.

## Spell Slots
Casting a Spell usually expends a Spell Slot, a reserve of magical energy granted by the Spellcasting feature. Spell Slots have Levels from **1** to **9**, matching the Spell Levels.

To cast a Spell with a Spell Slot, you expend a Slot of the Spell's Level or higher. You can use your Spell Slots to cast any Spell you have Prepared or Learned.

### One Spell Slot per Turn
You can expend only one Spell Slot to cast a Spell on each of your Turns. Spell Slots spent on other effects don't count against this limit.

### Upcasting
When you cast a Spell using a Spell Slot higher than the Spell's Level, you Upcast it, and the Spell takes on the Level of the Slot for that casting. Some Spells have stronger effects when Upcast, described in the Spell's **At Higher Levels** entry.

## Casting Without a Slot
Some Spells can be cast without expending a Spell Slot:

- **Cantrips.** A Cantrip never expends a Spell Slot.
- **Rituals.** A Spell with a Ritual [Casting Time](part/casting-time.md) can be cast as a Ritual, expending no Spell Slot.
- **Features.** A feature that grants a Spell sometimes lets you cast it without a Spell Slot.
- **Items.** An Item that casts a Spell expends no Spell Slot; many instead expend Charges.
