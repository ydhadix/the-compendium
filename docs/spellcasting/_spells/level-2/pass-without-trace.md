### *Pass Without Trace*

| 2nd-Level Abjuration | *druid, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | - |
| target | creatures in a 30-foot aura |
| duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

targets you choose have a +10 bonus to stealth checks and leave no tracks.
