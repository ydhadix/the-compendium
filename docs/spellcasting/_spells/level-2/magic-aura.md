### *Magic Aura*

| 2nd-Level Illusion | *artificer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one creature or object |
| duration | 24 hours |

you alter how the target appears to spells and magical effects.

creature: choose one or two creature types. the target is treated as if it is a creature of the chosen types, instead of its usual type. (spells and effects that only affect the chosen creature types can affect the target.)

object: choose one school of magic, or none. the target appears as if it contains magic of the chosen school, or as if it is nonmagical.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
