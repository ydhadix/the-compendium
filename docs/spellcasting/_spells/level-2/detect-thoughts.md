    #### Detect Thoughts

    | 2nd-Level Divination | *bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    You sense the presence of any creatures that understand language.

    This spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.

    Magic action: you mentally target a creature that understands language. you learn what is most on the target's mind right now.

    Magic action: you mentally target a creature that understands language. that target makes a will save.

    Fail: you learn the target's reasoning, emotional state, and something that looms large in its mind. when the target's turn ends, it repeats the save.

    Success: this spell ends.
