### *Calm Emotions*

| 2nd-Level Enchantment | *bard, cleric* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | every humanoid and beast in a 20-foot radius sphere |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

each target makes a will save.

fail: choose one of the following effects:

- the target is immune to being charmed and frightened. if it is already charmed or frightened, those conditions are suppressed.

- the target is no longer hostile to creatures of your choice. this spell ends on the target if it takes damage or sees an ally take damage.

success: no effect.
