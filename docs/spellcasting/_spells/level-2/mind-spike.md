    #### Mind Spike

    | 2nd-Level Divination | *sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 120 feet |
    | target | one creature |
    | duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

    The target makes a will save.

    Fail: you deal 3d8 psychic damage, you always know the target's location while on the same plane of existence, and the target can't become hidden or invisible to you. (the target can still be hidden or invisible to other creatures.)

    Success: the target takes half as much damage.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 psychic damage per additional spell level.
