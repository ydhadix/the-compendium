    #### Spider Climb

    | 2nd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | reach |
    | target | one creature |
    | duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

    The target gains a spider climb speed equal to its speed.

    **At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
