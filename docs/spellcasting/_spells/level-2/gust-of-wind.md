    #### Gust of Wind

    | 2nd-Level Evocation | *druid, ranger, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | reach |
    | target | everything in a 10 × 60 foot line |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    The area blasts strong wind away from you and moves with you. when a creature in the area moves closer to you, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot.

    Bonus action: you change the rotation of the area around you.

    When a target enters the area (including when this spell is cast or the area moves) or ends its turn there, once per turn, it makes a fortitude save.

    Fail: the target is pushed 15 feet away from you.

    Success: no effect.
