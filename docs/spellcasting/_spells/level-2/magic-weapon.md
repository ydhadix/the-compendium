??? spell "Magic Weapon | 2nd-Level Transmutation"

    #### Magic Weapon

    | 2nd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, paladin, ranger, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | bonus action |
    | range | reach |
    | target | one weapon |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    The target is treated as a magic weapon and gains a +1 bonus to physical attack and damage rolls, unless it already has an equivalent or larger bonus.

    **At Higher Levels:** level 3-4: the bonus increases to +2.

    Level 5+: the bonus increases to +3.
