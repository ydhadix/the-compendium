### *Arcane Lock*

| 2nd-Level Abjuration | *artificer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m (consumes gold dust worth 25 gold) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one closed door, window, container, or other opening |
| duration | until dispelled |

the target can't be unlocked or opened by nonmagical means.

you can choose any creatures who ignore this effect.

you can also set a verbal password that unlocks the target for 1 minute.
