    #### Immovable Object

    | 2nd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m (consumes gold dust worth 25 gold) |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | reach |
    | target | one object that weighs 10 pounds or less |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    The target becomes fixed in place and can't move or be moved, even if it defies gravity. you can choose any creatures who ignore this effect. you can also set a verbal password that suppresses this effect for 1 minute.

    This spell ends if more than 8,000 pounds of weight is held by the target. weight less than that doesn't affect the target.

    A creature that can reach the target can take a utilize action to make an athletics check against your spell save dc. on a success, the creature can move the target up to 10 feet (ignoring any weight the target is holding).

    **At Higher Levels:** level 3: the weight limit is 10,000 pounds and the duration is 8 hours.

    Level 4-5: the weight limit is 12,500 pounds and the duration is 24 hours and the dc to move the target increases by 5.

    Level 6+: the weight limit is 20,000 pounds and the duration is until dispelled and the dc to move the target increases by 10.
