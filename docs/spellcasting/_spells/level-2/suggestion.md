### *Suggestion*

| 2nd-Level Enchantment | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | one creature who understands you |
| duration | 8 hours (concentration) |

you suggest a course of activity to the target. the target makes a will save.

fail: the target becomes charmed. while charmed, the target follows the suggestion to the best of its ability. this spell ends when the target completes the suggested activity or when you or an ally deal damage to it.

success: no effect.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
