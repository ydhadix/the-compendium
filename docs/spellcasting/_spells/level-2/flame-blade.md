    #### Flame Blade

    | 2nd-Level Evocation | *druid, sorcerer* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | bonus action |
    | range | - |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    You create a sword of solidified flames in your hand. the sword emits 10 feet of bright light and dim light 10 feet further. the sword vanishes after you drop or throw it.

    The sword is a simple melee weapon with finesse, light, and thrown (20/60 ft.). attacks using this sword can use your spellcasting ability for attack and damage rolls, and its damage die is 3d6 fire damage.

    Bonus action: you resummon the sword to your hand.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d6 fire damage per additional spell level.
