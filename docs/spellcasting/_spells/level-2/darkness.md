### *Darkness*

| 2nd-Level Evocation | *sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | a 15-foot radius sphere, or one object |
| duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

if the target is an object, the area is a 15-foot aura centered on the target, which can be covered or hidden.

the area is filled with darkness. darkvision can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't illuminate it.

when the area overlaps with bright or dim light emitted from a spell, if the other spell is lower level than this spell, it ends early.
