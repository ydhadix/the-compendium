### *Animal Messenger*

| 2nd-Level Enchantment | *bard, druid, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action, or ritual |
| range | 30 ft. |
| target | one cr 0 beast |
| duration | 24 hours |

you speak a short message aloud, then describe a recipient and a location (e.g., "a red-haired dwarf wearing a pointed hat in the city to the south"). the target makes a will save.

fail: the target attempts to deliver the message, mimicking your voice, to a recipient at a location that fits your description. the target travels up to 25 miles per day, or twice as fast if it can fly. if the beast doesn't reach its destination, the message is lost.

success: no effect.

**At Higher Levels:** add 48 hours per additional spell level.
