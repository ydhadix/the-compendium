### *Shadow Blade*

| 2nd-Level Illusion | *sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | bonus action |
| range | - |
| target | self |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

you create a sword of solidified shadows in your hand. while in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on attacks with the sword. the sword vanishes after you drop or throw it.

the sword is a simple melee weapon with finesse, light, and thrown (20/60 ft.). attacks using this sword can use your spellcasting ability for attack and damage rolls, and its damage die is 2d8 psychic damage.

bonus action: you resummon the sword to your hand.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 psychic damage per additional spell level.
