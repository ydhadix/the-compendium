### *Blindness/Deafness*

| 2nd-Level Transmutation | *bard, cleric, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 120 feet |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 minute |

choose blinded or deafened. the target makes a fortitude save.

fail: the target has the chosen condition. when the target's turn ends, it repeats the save.

success: this spell ends on the target.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
