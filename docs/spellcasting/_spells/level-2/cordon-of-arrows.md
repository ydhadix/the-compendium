    #### Cordon of Arrows

    | 2nd-Level Transmutation | *ranger* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action (r) |
    | range | reach |
    | target | everything in a 30-foot radius sphere |
    | duration | 8 hours |

    You plant up to four arrows in the ground in the center of the area.

    When a target enters the area (including when this spell is cast) or ends its turn there, once per turn, an arrow flies up to strike it. you can choose any creatures who do not trigger this effect. the target makes a reflex save.

    Fail: you deal 2d4 piercing damage.

    Success: no effect.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 2 arrows per additional spell level.
