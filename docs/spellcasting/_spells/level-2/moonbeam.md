### *Moonbeam*

| 2nd-Level Transmutation | *druid* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 120 feet |
| target | everything in a 5-foot radius, 40-foot high cylinder |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

the area is filled with dim light, and shapeshifters can't change form in the area.

magic action: you move the area up to 60 feet.

when a target enters the area (including when this spell is cast or the area moves) or ends its turn there, once per turn, it makes a fortitude save.

fail: you deal 2d10 radiant damage. a shapeshifted target returns to its true form.

success: you deal half as much damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d10 damage and 5 feet to the radius per additional spell level.
