    #### Rope Trick

    | 2nd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m (rope worth 1 gold) |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | reach |
    | target | - |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    One end of the material component hovers perpendicular to the ground, up to its length or until it reaches a ceiling. at the top, an invisible portal large enough for a medium creature opens to an extradimensional space which can be reached by climbing the rope.

    The space can hold up to eight medium or smaller creatures. attacks, spells, and other effects can't pass into or out of the space, but creatures inside it can see through the portal. anything inside the space drops out when the spell ends.
