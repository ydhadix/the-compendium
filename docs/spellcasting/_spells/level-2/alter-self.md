    #### Alter Self

    | 2nd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | - |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

    Choose one of the following transformations.

    Aquatic adaptation: you can breathe underwater and gain a swim speed equal to your speed.

    Change appearance: you make yourself look and sound different. you can't change your size category and must have the same basic arrangement of limbs. otherwise, the extent of the transformation is up to you.

    Natural weapons: you grow claws, fangs, horns, or hooves. unarmed strikes using the transformed appendage use your spellcasting ability and deal 1d6 bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage.
