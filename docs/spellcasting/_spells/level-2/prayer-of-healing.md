### *Prayer of Healing*

| 2nd-Level Abjuration | *cleric, paladin* |
|---|---|
| components | v |
| cast time | 10 minutes |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | 5 creatures |
| duration | - |

you heal the target by 2d8, and the target gains the benefit of a short rest.

a target can't be affected by this spell again until it finishes a long rest.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 healing per additional spell level.
