### *Spike Growth*

| 2nd-Level Transmutation | *druid, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 150 feet |
| target | everything in a 20-foot radius sphere |
| duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

the area is difficult terrain, and is camouflaged to look natural.

a creature that can see the area can take a search action to make a perception or survival check against your spell save dc. on a success, the area becomes visible to the creature.

whenever a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d4 piercing damage for every 5 feet it moves.
