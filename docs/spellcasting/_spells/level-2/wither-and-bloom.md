    #### Wither and Bloom

    | 2nd-Level Necromancy | *druid, warlock* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 60 feet |
    | target | everything in a 10-foot-radius sphere |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    One target of your choice can expend and roll up to 1 hit die. the target heals by the total of those dice + your spellcasting ability.

    Each target of your choice makes a fortitude save.

    Fail: you deal 2d6 necrotic damage.

    Success: you deal half as much damage.

    **At Higher Levels:** add up to 1 hit die, 1d6 necrotic damage, and 10 feet to the radius per additional spell level.
