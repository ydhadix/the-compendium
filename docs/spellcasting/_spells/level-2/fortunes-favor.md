### *Fortune's Favor*

| 2nd-Level Divination | *bard, sorcerer* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m (consumes a pearl worth 100 gold) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 hour |

after the target rolls a d20, it can roll an additional d20 and choose which d20 to use.

then, this spell ends.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
