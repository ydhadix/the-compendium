### *Summon Beast*

| 2nd-Level Conjuration | *druid, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m (a gilded acorn worth 200 gold, containing a feather, a tuft of fur, and a feather) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 90 feet |
| target | - |
| duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

you summon a bestial spirit companion. choose air, land, or water environment, and a kind of animal the companion looks like.

free action: you command your companion to move and take any action available to it.

this spell ends if the companion has 0 hit points.

**At Higher Levels:** the spell's level is used to determine your companion's armor class, hit points, and attack damage.

#### Traits

**flyby**: air: moving doesn't trigger opportunity attacks.

**pack tactics**: land or water: attacks have advantage while target is within ally's reach.

**water breathing**: water: can only breathe underwater.

**multiattack**: makes (spell level/2) beast strike attacks, rounded down.
