### *Cloud of Daggers*

| 2nd-Level Conjuration | *artificer, bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | everything in a 5-foot cube |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

when a target enters the area (including when this spell is cast or the area moves) or ends its turn there, you deal 4d4 slashing damage. a target only takes this damage once per turn.

magic action: you move the area up to 30 feet.

**At Higher Levels:** add 2d4 slashing damage per additional spell level.
