### *Crown of Madness*

| 2nd-Level Enchantment | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 120 feet |
| target | one humanoid |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

the target makes a will save and you take the following magic action (no additional action required).

fail: the target becomes charmed, and a spectral crown appears on the target's head. when the target's turn ends, it repeats the save.

success: this spell ends on the target.

magic action: you mentally choose a creature. on the target's next turn, it must move towards the creature and use its action to make a melee attack against them. (the target takes the rest of its turn normally after it attacks once, or if it cannot reach the creature.)
