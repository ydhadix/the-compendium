### *Aid*

| 2nd-Level Abjuration | *artificer, bard, cleric, druid, paladin, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | up to 3 creatures |
| duration | 8 hours |

each target's hit point maximum and current hit points increase by 5.

**At Higher Levels:** add 5 hit points per additional spell level.
