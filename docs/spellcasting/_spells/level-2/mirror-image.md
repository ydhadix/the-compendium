??? spell "Mirror Image | 2nd-Level Illusion"

    #### Mirror Image

    | 2nd-Level Illusion | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | - |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 1 minute |

    You create three illusory duplicates of yourself. the duplicates move with you, mimic your actions, and shift position to hide your true self.

    When you are hit with an attack, roll a d6 for each remaining duplicate. if any of the rolls are 3 or higher, one of the duplicates is hit instead, and is destroyed. the duplicates otherwise ignore all other damage and effects.
