### *Warding Bond*

| 2nd-Level Abjuration | *cleric, paladin* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m (two platinum rings worth 50 gold each) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 hour |

while both you and the target are each wearing one of the material components, and the target is within 60 feet of you, the target gains a +1 bonus to armor class and saving throws, and resistance to all damage.

when the target takes damage, you take the same amount of damage.

this spell ends if you are downed or if this spell is cast again on you or the target.
