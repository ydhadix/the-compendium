### *Locate Creature*

| 3rd-Level Divination | *bard, cleric, druid, paladin, ranger, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 1,000 feet |
| target | special |
| duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

choose a general type of creature, or a specific creature you've seen within 30 feet. you sense the direction to the nearest chosen target. if the target is moving, you know the direction of its movement. this spell is blocked by any amount of lead, and cannot locate a target that is transformed to a different form.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
