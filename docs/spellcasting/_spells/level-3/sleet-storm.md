### *Sleet Storm*

| 3rd-Level Conjuration | *druid, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 150 feet |
| target | everything in a 20-foot radius, 40-foot tall cylinder |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

the area is heavily obscured and exposed flames in the area are doused. the ground in the area is difficult terrain.

when a target enters the area or ends its turn there, it makes a reflex save.

fail: the target becomes prone and loses concentration.

success: no effect.
