    #### Water Walk

    | 3rd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, cleric, druid, ranger, sorcerer* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action, or ritual |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | up to ten creatures |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    Each target can move across any liquid surface as if it were solid ground (heat or cold from the surface can still deal damage) and can use the following action.

    Bonus action: the target passes the liquid's surface into the liquid, or vice-versa.
