    #### Feign Death

    | 3rd-Level Necromancy | *bard, cleric, druid, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action, or ritual |
    | range | reach |
    | target | one creature |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    The target appears dead to magical effects and physical inspection. the target is blinded, incapacitated, its speed is 0, and it gains immunity to poison and resistance to all damage except psychic.
