    #### Call Lightning

    | 3rd-Level Conjuration | *druid* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 120 feet |
    | target | everything in a 10-foot radius, 60-foot tall cylinder |
    | duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

    If you're outdoors in a storm when you cast this spell, the storm and the area underneath it becomes this spell's area instead. when you cast this spell, you take the following action (no additional action required).

    Magic action: everything in a 5-foot radius sphere makes a reflex save.

    Fail: you deal 3d10 lightning damage. if this spell's area is a storm, you deal an additional 1d10 lightning damage.

    Success: you deal half as much damage.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d10 damage per additional spell level.
