    #### Animate Dead

    | 3rd-Level Necromancy | *cleric, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | 1 minute |
    | range | 10 feet |
    | target | one humanoid corpse, or up to 4 undead |
    | duration | 24 hours |

    The target becomes an undead creature. if the target is just bones, it becomes a skeleton. otherwise, it becomes a zombie. the creature obeys your commands. if you don't issue a command, it takes the dodge action and moves to avoid danger.

    Bonus action: you issue a command to any creatures within 60 feet that you created with this spell.

    When this spell ends on a creature, it remains animated but stops obeying your commands. you can cast this spell to regain control of up to 4 creatures you created with this spell, instead of animating a new one.

    **At Higher Levels:** add two targets (animated or controlled) per additional spell level.
