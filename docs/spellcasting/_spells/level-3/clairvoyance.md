    #### Clairvoyance

    | 3rd-Level Divination | *bard, cleric, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m (a jeweled horn or glass eye worth 100 gold) |
    | cast time | 10 minutes |
    | range | 1 mile |
    | target | - |
    | duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

    Choose seeing or hearing. you create an intangible, invisible, invulnerable sensor. you can perceive using the chosen sense from the sensor's space.

    Bonus action: you switch between seeing and hearing.
