    #### Spirit Shroud

    | 3rd-Level Necromancy | *cleric, paladin, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | bonus action |
    | range | - |
    | target | everything in a 10-foot aura |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    Choose radiant, necrotic, or cold. your attacks deal an additional 1d8 damage of the chosen type to targets in the area.

    When a target of your choice starts its turn in the area, its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of its next turn.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 damage per additional spell level.
