    #### Plant Growth

    | 3rd-Level Transmutation | *bard, druid, ranger* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action, or 8 hours |
    | range | 150 feet |
    | target | v, s |
    | duration | - |

    This spell's effect changes depending on its cast time.

    Full action (overgrowth): choose a 100-foot radius sphere. you can exclude one or more areas of any size from the area. all normal plants in the area become thick and overgrown. each foot of movement in the area costs 3 additional feet.

    8 hours (enrichment): choose a half-mile radius sphere. all plants in the area yield twice the amount of food when harvested for the next year.
