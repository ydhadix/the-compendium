    #### Daylight

    | 3rd-Level Evocation | *cleric, druid, paladin, ranger, sorcerer* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 60 feet |
    | target | a 60-foot radius sphere, or one object |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    If the target is an object, the area is a 60-foot aura centered on the target, which can be covered or hidden. the area is filled with bright sunlight and emits dim light 60 feet further.

    When the area overlaps with darkness created by a spell, if the other spell is lower level than this spell, the other spell ends early.
