    #### Cacophonic Shield

    | 3rd-Level Evocation | *bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | - |
    | target | everything in a 10-foot aura |
    | duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

    You have resistance to thunder damage. ranged attacks have disadvantage if they pass through the area.

    When a target of your choice enters the area (including when this spell is cast or the area moves) or ends its turn there, it makes a fortitude save. a target only makes this save once each turn.

    Fail: you deal 3d6 thunder damage, and the target is deafened until the start of its next turn.

    Success: you deal half as much damage.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d6 damage per additional spell level.
