### *Conjure Constructs*

| 3rd-Level Conjuration | *artificer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | everything in a 5-foot radius sphere |
| duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

free action: you move the area up to 30 feet.

when a target of your choice enters the area (including when this spell is cast or the area moves) or ends its turn there, you can choose one of the following effects. a target can only be affected by this spell once per turn.

orderly ward: the target gains 1d6 + your spellcasting ability as temporary hit points.

clockwork force: the target makes a reflex save.

fail: you deal 3d6 force damage.

success: you deal half as much damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d6 damage and temporary hit points per additional spell level.
