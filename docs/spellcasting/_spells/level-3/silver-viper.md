    #### Silver Viper

    | 3rd-Level Conjuration | *druid, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | bonus action |
    | range | - |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    You gain 15 temporary hit points and a climb speed equal to your speed. this spell ends if you have no temporary hit points.

    Magic action: you make a ranged attack against a target within 50 feet.

    Hit: the target takes 1d6 poison damage and is poisoned until the end of your next turn. while poisoned, the target is also incapacitated.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 5 temporary hit points and 1d6 damage per additional spell level.
