    #### Speak with Dead

    | 3rd-Level Necromancy | *bard, cleric, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 10 feet |
    | target | one corpse with a mouth |
    | duration | 10 minutes |

    You can ask the target up to 5 questions. the target is animated enough to respond. it knows only what it knew in life and is not compelled to answer truthfully. the target's soul is not returned to its body, and it is incapable of learning new information, comprehending anything that happened since it died, and can't speculate about the future.
