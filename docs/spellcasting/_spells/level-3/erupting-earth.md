### *Erupting Earth*

| 3rd-Level Transmutation | *druid, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 120 feet |
| target | everything in a 20-foot cube |
| duration | - |

the area becomes difficult terrain indefinitely. each target makes a reflex save.

fail: you deal 3d12 bludgeoning damage.

success: you deal half as much damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d12 damage per additional spell level.
