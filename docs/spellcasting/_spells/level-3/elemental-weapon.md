### *Elemental Weapon*

| 3rd-Level Transmutation | *artificer, druid, paladin, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one weapon |
| duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

choose an elemental damage type. the weapon gains a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls, and deals an additional 1d4 damage of the chosen type.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d4 damage per additional spell level.

level 5-6: the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +2.

level 7+: the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +3.
