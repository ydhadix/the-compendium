### *Magnify Gravity*

| 1st-Level Transmutation | *artificer, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | creatures in a 10-foot radius sphere |
| duration | until the end of your next turn |

objects in the area that aren't worn or carried can't be moved or picked up. a creature can make an athletics check against your spell save dc to end this spell on the object (no additional action required).

each target makes a fortitude save.

fail: you deal 2d8 force damage. the target's speed is halved.

success: you deal half as much damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 force damage and 5 feet to the radius per additional spell level.
