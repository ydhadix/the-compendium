### *Caustic Brew*

| 1st-Level Evocation | *artificer* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | - |
| target | everything in a 5 × 30 foot line |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

each target makes a reflex save.

fail: you deal 2d4 acid damage. a target damaged by this spell is corroded (2d4). a creature that can reach the target can take a utilize action to end this spell on the target.

success: this spell ends on the target.

**At Higher Levels:** add 2d4 acid damage to the spell and corroded damage per additional spell level.
