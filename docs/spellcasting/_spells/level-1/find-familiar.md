    #### Find Familiar

    | 1st-Level Conjuration | *artificer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m (consumes burning incense worth 10 gold) |
    | cast time | 1 hour, or ritual |
    | range | 10 feet |
    | target | - |
    | duration | - |

    Choose a cr 0 beast and a creature type: celestial, fey, or fiend. your familiar is a companion spirit that takes the chosen form and is the chosen creature type instead of a beast. if you already have a familiar from this spell, it adopts the new form and creature type instead. your familiar uses the chosen beast's statistics.

    Free action: you can issue a command to your familiar.

    Magic action: you can temporarily dismiss the familiar to a pocket dimension or cause it to reappear within 30 feet of you.

    Share senses: while your familiar is within 120 feet of you, you can see and hear through the familiar's senses. while perceiving through your familiar, you are blinded and deafened to your own senses.

    Free action: you can switch between using your senses or your familiar's.

    Deliver spells: when you cast a reach spell, your familiar can use its reaction to deliver the spell through its reach instead.
