### *Wristpocket*

| 1st-Level Conjuration | *artificer, bard, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | s |
| cast time | full action, or ritual |
| range | reach |
| target | one object that weighs 5 pounds or less |
| duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

you dismiss the target to a pocket dimension.

magic action: you cause the target to appear in your hand, or return it to the pocket dimension.
