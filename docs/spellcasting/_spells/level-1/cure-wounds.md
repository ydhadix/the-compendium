### *Cure Wounds*

| 1st-Level Abjuration | *artificer, bard, cleric, druid, paladin, ranger* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one creature |
| duration | - |

you heal the target by 2d8 + your spellcasting ability.

**At Higher Levels:** add 2d8 healing per additional spell level.
