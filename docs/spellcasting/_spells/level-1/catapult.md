### *Catapult*

| 1st-Level Transmutation | *artificer, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | one thing that weighs 5 pounds or less and isn't worn or carried |
| duration | - |

the target flies in a straight line up to 90 feet in a direction of your choice. anything struck by the target makes a reflex save.

fail: you deal 3d8 bludgeoning damage to the target and whatever it struck. then, the target stops moving.

success: the target continues flying until it strikes something else, or it has moved 90 feet or more.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 bludgeoning damage and 30 feet range per additional spell level.
