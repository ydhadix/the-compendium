### *Longstrider*

| 1st-Level Transmutation | *artificer, bard, druid, ranger, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 hour |

the target's speed increases by 10 feet.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
