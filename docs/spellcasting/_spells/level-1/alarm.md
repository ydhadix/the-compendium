    #### Alarm

    | 1st-Level Abjuration | *artificer, ranger, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | 1 minute, or ritual |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | a 20-foot cube, one door, or one window |
    | duration | 8 hours |

    Choose an audible or mental alarm. whenever a creature touches the target or enters its area, the alarm goes off. you can choose any creatures who do not trigger this effect.

    Audible: the alarm emits the sound of a bell out to 60 feet for 10 seconds.

    Mental: if you are within 1 mile of the target, the alarm sends you a mental ping. the ping awakens you if you are asleep.
