    #### Animal Friendship

    | 1st-Level Enchantment | *bard, druid, ranger* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | one beast |
    | duration | 24 hours |

    The target makes a will save.

    Fail: the target becomes charmed. if you or an ally deal damage to the target, this spell ends on the target.

    Success: no effect.
