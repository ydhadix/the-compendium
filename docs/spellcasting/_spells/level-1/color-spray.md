### *Color Spray*

| 1st-Level Illusion | *artificer, bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | everything in a 15-foot cone |
| duration | until the end of your next turn |

each target makes a fortitude save.

fail: the target becomes blinded.

success: no effect.

**At Higher Levels:** add 15 feet of cone size per additional spell level.
