### *Comprehend Languages*

| 1st-Level Divination | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action, or ritual |
| range | - |
| target | self |
| duration | 1 hour |

you understand the literal meaning of any language you hear or see signed. you also understand any written language that you see, but you must be touching the surface on which the words are written.

this spell doesn't decode symbols or secret messages.
