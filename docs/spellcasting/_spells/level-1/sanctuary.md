    #### Sanctuary

    | 1st-Level Abjuration | *artificer, cleric, paladin* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | bonus action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | one creature |
    | duration | 1 minute |

    The target is protected from attacks and targeting spells. this spell ends if the target attacks, deals damage, or forces another creature to make a save.

    A creature that attacks the target with an attack or targets it with a damaging spell without an area makes a will save.

    Fail: the creature must choose a different target or lose the attack or spell.

    Success: no effect.
