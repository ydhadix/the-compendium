### *Silent Image*

| 1st-Level Illusion | *bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | - |
| duration | 10 minutes (concentration) |

you create the image of an object, creature, or some other visible phenomenon. the image must be no larger than a 15-foot cube and can't create sound, light, smell, or any other sensory effect.

a creature that can perceive the illusion can take a search action to make a perception check against your spell save dc. on a success, the illusion becomes faint to the creature.

magic action: you move the image to another location. as the image moves, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural.
