### *Divine Smite*

| 1st-Level Evocation | *paladin* |
|---|---|
| components | v |
| cast time | free action, when you hit with a physical melee attack |
| range | - |
| target | the target of the attack |
| duration | - |

you deal 2d8 radiant damage.

if the target is a fiend or undead, you deal an additional 1d8 radiant damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 radiant damage per additional spell level.
