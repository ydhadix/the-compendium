### *Guiding Bolt*

| 1st-Level Evocation | *cleric* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 120 feet |
| target | one thing |
| duration | until the end of your next turn |

you make a ranged attack against the target.

hit: you deal 4d6 radiant damage. the next attack made against the target has advantage, then this spell ends.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d6 radiant damage per additional spell level.
