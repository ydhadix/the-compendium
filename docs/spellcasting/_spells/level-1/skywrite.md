### *Skywrite*

| 1st-Level Transmutation | *artificer, bard, druid, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action, or ritual |
| range | line of sight |
| target | - |
| duration | 1 hour (concentration) |

you cause a brief sentence or phrase, or a simple image made from clouds to form in the sky.

this spell ends if the clouds are dispersed.
