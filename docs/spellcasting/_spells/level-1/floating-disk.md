    #### Floating Disk

    | 1st-Level Conjuration | *artificer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action, or ritual |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | - |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    You create a circular, horizontal plane of force that floats 3 feet above the ground. the disk is 3 feet in diameter and 1 inch thick.

    While you are within 20 feet of it, the disk is immobile. if you move more than 20 feet away from it, the disk follows you so that it remains within 20 feet of you. the disk can move across uneven terrain; up or down stairs, slopes, and the like; but it can't cross an elevation change of 10 feet or more.

    If the disk holds more than 500 pounds of weight or you are more than 100 feet away from the disk, this spell ends.
