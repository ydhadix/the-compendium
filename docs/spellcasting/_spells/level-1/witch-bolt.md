    #### Witch Bolt

    | 1st-Level Evocation | *sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 60 feet |
    | target | one thing |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    You make a ranged attack against the target.

    Hit: you deal 2d12 lightning damage. this spell ends if the target has total cover from you or is outside this spell's range.

    Miss: this spell ends.

    Bonus action: you deal 1d12 lightning damage.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d12 lightning damage and 15 feet to the range per additional spell level.
