    #### Ensnaring Strike

    | 1st-Level Conjuration | *ranger* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v |
    | cast time | bonus action, when you hit with a physical attack |
    | range | - |
    | target | the target of the attack |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    The target makes a fortitude save. a creature larger than you has advantage.

    Fail: the target becomes restrained. when the target's turn starts, you deal 1d6 piercing damage. a creature that can reach the target can take a utilize action to make an athletics check against your spell save dc. on a success, this spell ends on the target.

    Success: no effect.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 1d6 piercing damage per additional spell level.
