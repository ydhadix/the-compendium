    #### Sleep

    | 1st-Level Enchantment | *bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 60 feet |
    | target | every creature in a 5-foot radius sphere |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    Each target makes a will save.

    Fail: the target becomes incapacitated. when the target's turn ends, it repeats the save. if it fails a second time, the target becomes unconscious. this spell ends on the target if the target takes damage or if a creature that can reach it takes a utilize action to shake them awake.

    Success: this spell ends on the target.

    **At Higher Levels:** add 5 feet to the radius per additional spell level.
