### *Healing Word*

| 1st-Level Abjuration | *bard, cleric, druid* |
|---|---|
| components | v |
| cast time | bonus action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | one creature |
| duration | - |

you heal the target by 2d4 + your spellcasting ability.

**At Higher Levels:** add 2d4 healing per additional spell level.
