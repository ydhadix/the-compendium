### *Ice Knife*

| 1st-Level Conjuration | *druid, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | one thing, and everything in a 5-foot radius sphere around it |
| duration | - |

you make a ranged attack against the first target.

hit: you deal 1d10 piercing damage.

then, each target makes a reflex save.

fail: you deal 2d6 cold damage.

success: you deal half as much damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d6 cold damage and 5 feet to the radius per additional spell level.
