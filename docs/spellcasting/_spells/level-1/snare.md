    #### Snare

    | 1st-Level Abjuration | *artificer, druid, ranger, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | s, m (consumes rope) |
    | cast time | full action, or ritual |
    | range | reach |
    | target | special |
    | duration | 8 hours |

    The material component surrounds a 5-foot radius circle on the ground and becomes invisible. a creature that can see the area can take a search action to make a perception check against your spell save dc. on a success, the rope becomes visible to the creature.

    The first small, medium, or large creature that touches or enters the area makes a reflex save. you can choose any creatures who do not trigger this effect.

    Fail: the target becomes restrained and hoisted into the air, hanging upside down 3 feet above the ground. a creature that can reach the target can take a utilize action to make an athletics check against your spell save dc. on a success, this spell ends on the target.

    Success: the rope vanishes without effect and this spell ends.
