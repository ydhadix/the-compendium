### *Compelled Duel*

| 1st-Level Enchantment | *cleric, paladin* |
|---|---|
| components | v |
| cast time | bonus action |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

the target makes a will save.

fail: the target has disadvantage on attacks that don't target you, and it can't choose to move out of range. this spell ends if you attack or cast a spell on an enemy other than the target, if an ally damages the target, or if you end your turn while the target is out of range.

success: no effect.
