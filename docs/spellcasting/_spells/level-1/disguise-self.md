### *Disguise Self*

| 1st-Level Illusion | *artificer, bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | - |
| target | self |
| duration | 1 hour |

you make yourself and anything you are wearing or carrying look different. you can seem up to 1 foot shorter or taller and must have the same basic arrangement of limbs. otherwise, the extent of the illusion is up to you.

a creature that can perceive the illusion can take a search action to make a perception check against your spell save dc. on a success, the illusion becomes faint to the creature.
