### *Unseen Servant*

| 1st-Level Conjuration | *bard, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action, or ritual |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | - |
| duration | 1 hour |

you create an invisible, mindless, shapeless, medium force that performs simple tasks at your command. the servant has ac 10, 1 hit point, strength -5, and it can't attack.

this spell ends if the servant has 0 hit points or you command the servant to move outside of this spell's range.

bonus action: you mentally command the servant to move up to 15 feet and interact with an object. the servant performs the task to the best of its ability until completed, then awaits your next command.
