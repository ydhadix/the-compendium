### *Hideous Laughter*

| 1st-Level Enchantment | *bard, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

the target makes a will save.

fail: the target becomes incapacitated, prone, and can't stand up from prone. when the target's turn ends, it repeats the save. when the target takes damage, it repeats the save with advantage.

success: this spell ends on the target.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
