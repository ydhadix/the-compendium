### *Continual Flame*

| 1st-Level Evocation | *artificer, cleric, druid, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m (consumes ruby dust worth 50 gold) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one object |
| duration | until dispelled |

you create a heatless, harmless flame on the target.

the target emits 20 feet of bright light and dim light 20 feet further. the light can be covered or hidden, but not extinguished.
