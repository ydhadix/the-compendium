    #### Grease

    | 1st-Level Conjuration | *artificer, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 60 feet |
    | target | everything in a 10-foot square on the ground |
    | duration | 1 minute |

    The area is difficult terrain.

    When a target enters the area (including when this spell is cast) or ends its turn there, it makes a reflex save.

    Fail: the target becomes prone.

    Success: no effect.
