### *Purify Food & Drink*

| 1st-Level Transmutation | *artificer, cleric, druid, paladin* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action, or ritual |
| range | 10 feet |
| target | all food and drink in a 5-foot radius sphere |
| duration | - |

you remove nonmagical poison and rot from the target.
