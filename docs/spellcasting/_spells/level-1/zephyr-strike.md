??? spell "Zephyr Strike | 1st-Level Transmutation"

    #### Zephyr Strike

    | 1st-Level Transmutation | *ranger* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v |
    | cast time | bonus action |
    | range | - |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    Your speed increases by 10 feet and your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

    On your turn, you can shorten the spell's duration to the end of your turn. if you do, your speed increases by an additional 30 feet and the next physical attack you make has advantage and deals an extra 1d8 force damage.
