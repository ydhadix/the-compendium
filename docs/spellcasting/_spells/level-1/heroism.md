    #### Heroism

    | 1st-Level Enchantment | *bard, cleric, paladin* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | reach |
    | target | one creature |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    The target is immune to being frightened.

    When the target starts its turn, it gains temporary hit points equal to your spellcasting ability.

    **At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
