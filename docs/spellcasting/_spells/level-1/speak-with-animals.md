    #### Speak with Animals

    | 1st-Level Divination | *bard, druid, ranger, warlock* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action, or ritual |
    | range | - |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 10 minutes |

    You can comprehend and verbally communicate with beasts.

    Most beasts have little to say about topics that don't pertain to survival or companionship, but at minimum, a beast can give you information about nearby locations and monsters, including what it has perceived within the past day.
