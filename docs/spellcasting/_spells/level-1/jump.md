### *Jump*

| 1st-Level Transmutation | *artificer, druid, ranger, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | bonus action |
| range | reach |
| target | one creature |
| duration | 1 minute |

once on each of its turns, the target can use 10 feet of movement to jump up to 30 feet.

**At Higher Levels:** add one target per additional spell level.
