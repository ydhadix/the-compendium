### *Inflict Wounds*

| 1st-Level Necromancy | *cleric* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | reach |
| target | one thing |
| duration | - |

you make a melee attack against the target.

hit: you deal 3d10 necrotic damage.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d10 necrotic damage per additional spell level.
