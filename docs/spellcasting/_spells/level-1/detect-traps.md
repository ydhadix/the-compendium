    #### Detect Traps

    | 1st-Level Divination | *cleric, druid, ranger* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | self |
    | duration | 10 minutes |

    You sense the presence of any artificial traps. you learn the general nature of the trap, but not its location.

    This spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
