### *Chromatic Orb*

| 1st-Level Evocation | *sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m (a diamond worth 50 gold) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 90 feet |
| target | one thing |
| duration | - |

choose an elemental damage type and make a ranged attack against the target.

hit: you deal 3d8 damage of the chosen type. if you roll the same number on two of the damage dice, you can choose a new target within 30 feet of the previous target and repeat this attack. you can only repeat this attack once per level of this spell.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 damage per additional spell level.
