    #### Thaumaturgy

    | Transmutation Cantrip | *cleric* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | special |
    | duration | 1 minute |

    You choose one of the following effects. if you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three of its effects active at a time.

    Altered eyes: you change the appearance of your eyes.

    Booming voice: your voice booms up to three times as loud as normal. you have advantage on intimidation checks.

    Fire play: you light or snuff out a candle, torch, or small campfire.

    Invisible hand: you cause an unlocked door or window to fly open or slam shut.

    Phantom sound: a sound emits from a place in range, such as a rumble of thunder, the cry of a raven, or ominous whispers.

    Tremors: you cause harmless tremors in the ground.
