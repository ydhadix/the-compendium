    #### Elementalism

    | Transmutation Cantrip | *druid, sorcerer, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | special |
    | duration | 1 hour |

    You choose one of the following effects.

    Fire play: you light or snuff out a candle, torch, or small campfire.

    Control flames: choose a flame. you can double or halve the light cast by the flame, change its color, cause simple shapes to appear within the flames and animate as you like, or any combination of these effects.

    Beckon air: you create a breeze strong enough to ripple cloth, stir dust, rustle leaves, and close open doors and shutters in the area.

    Gust: you seize the air and move a medium or smaller creature or object up to 5 feet away from you. a creature can make a fortitude save to not be moved.

    Excavate: choose a 5-foot cube or smaller portion of dirt. you excavate it, move it along the ground, and deposit it up to 5 feet away.

    Mold earth: choose a 5-foot square or smaller portion of dirt or stone. you change it to difficult terrain or normal terrain.

    Beckon water: you create a spray of cool mist that lightly dampens everything in a 5-foot cube.

    Shape water: choose a 5-foot cube or smaller portion of water. you can change the water's color or opacity, cause it to form into simple shapes and animate it as you like, freeze or unfreeze the water, or any combination of these effects.
