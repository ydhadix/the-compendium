### *Sapping Sting*

| Necromancy Cantrip | *druid, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | one thing |
| duration | - |

the target makes a fortitude save.

fail: you deal 1d6 necrotic damage. a creature damaged by this spell becomes prone.

success: no effect.

**At Higher Levels:** add one damage die at levels 5, 11, and 17.
