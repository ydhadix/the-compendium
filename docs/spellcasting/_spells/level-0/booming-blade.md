### *Booming Blade*

| Evocation Cantrip | *artificer, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | s, m (a melee weapon) |
| cast time | full action |
| range | the material component's reach |
| target | one thing |
| duration | until the end of your next turn |

you make a melee attack against the target using the material component.

hit: you deal the material component's damage, and you can change the damage type to thunder. the next time the target uses movement or is moved, you deal 1d8 thunder damage and this spell ends.

**At Higher Levels:** add 1d8 thunder damage to the attack and effect damage at levels 5, 11, and 17.
