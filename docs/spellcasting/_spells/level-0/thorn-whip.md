    #### Thorn Whip

    | Transmutation Cantrip | *artificer, druid* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | reach + 25 feet |
    | target | one thing |
    | duration | - |

    You make a melee attack against the target.

    Hit: you deal 1d6 piercing damage. a large or smaller target is moved up to 10 feet closer to you.

    **At Higher Levels:** add one damage die at levels 5, 11, and 17.
