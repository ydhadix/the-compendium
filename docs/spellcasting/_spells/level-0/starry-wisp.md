### *Starry Wisp*

| Evocation Cantrip | *bard, cleric, druid* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 60 feet |
| target | one thing |
| duration | until the end of your next turn |

you make a ranged attack against the target.

hit: you deal 1d8 radiant damage. the target emits 10 feet of dim light and can't benefit from being invisible.

**At Higher Levels:** add one damage die at levels 5, 11, and 17.
