    #### Mind Sliver

    | Enchantment Cantrip | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 60 feet |
    | target | one thing |
    | duration | until the end of your next turn |

    The target makes a will save.

    Fail: you deal 1d6 psychic damage. the next time the target makes a save, you subtract 1d4 from the d20 roll and this spell ends.

    Success: no effect.

    **At Higher Levels:** add one damage die at levels 5, 11, and 17.
