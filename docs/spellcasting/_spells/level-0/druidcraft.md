### *Druidcraft*

| Transmutation Cantrip | *druid* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 30 feet |
| target | - |
| duration | - |

you choose one of the following effects.

fire play: you light or snuff out a candle, torch, or small campfire.

bloom: you make a flower blossom, a seed pod open, or a leaf bud bloom.

weather sensor: you create a harmless sensory effect that predicts the local weather for the next 24 hours.

nature effect: you create a harmless sensory effect, such as falling leaves, a gentle breeze, the sound of an animal, or a faint odor.
