### *Dancing Lights*

| Illusion Cantrip | *artificer, bard, sorcerer, wizard* |
|---|---|
| components | v, s, m |
| cast time | full action |
| range | 120 feet |
| target | - |
| duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

you create up to four hovering, torch-sized lights. each light emits 10 feet of dim light and vanishes if it is out of range or if it isn't within 20 feet of another light.

bonus action: you move the lights up to 60 feet. you can combine the lights into one medium, glowing, vaguely humanoid form or separate the lights.
