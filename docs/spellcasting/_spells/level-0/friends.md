    #### Friends

    | Enchantment Cantrip | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 10 feet |
    | target | one humanoid |
    | duration | 1 minute (concentration) |

    The target makes a will save. if you've damaged or cast this spell on the target in the last 24 hours, the target succeeds automatically.

    Fail: the target becomes charmed. this spell ends if the target takes damage, or if you attack, deal damage, or force another creature to make a save.

    Success: no effect.
