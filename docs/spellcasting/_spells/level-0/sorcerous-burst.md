    #### Sorcerous Burst

    | Evocation Cantrip | *sorcerer* |
    |---|---|
    | components | v, s |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 120 feet |
    | target | one thing |
    | duration | - |

    Choose an elemental damage type or psychic damage. you make a ranged attack against the target.

    Hit: you deal 1d8 damage of the chosen type. whenever you roll an 8 on a damage die for this spell, you can roll another d8 and add it to the damage. the maximum number of d8s you can add this way is equal to your spellcasting ability.

    **At Higher Levels:** add one damage die at levels 5, 11, and 17.
