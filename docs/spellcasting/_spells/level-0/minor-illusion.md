    #### Minor Illusion

    | Illusion Cantrip | *bard, sorcerer, warlock, wizard* |
    |---|---|
    | components | s, m |
    | cast time | full action |
    | range | 30 feet |
    | target | - |
    | duration | 1 minute |

    You create an illusion of a still image or sound. a creature that can perceive the illusion can take a search action to make a perception check against your spell save dc. on a success, the illusion becomes faint to the creature. this spell ends if you cast it again.

    Image: the image must be no larger than a 5-foot cube and can't create sound, light, smell, or any other sensory effect.

    Sound: the sound's volume can range from a whisper to a scream. it can be your voice, someone else's voice, a lion's roar, a beating of drums, or any other sound you choose. the sound continues unabated, or you can make discrete sounds at different times.
