# Quartermaster

The quartermaster directs the ship's crew and manages its active systems (such as weapons and repairs) using Crew Orders.

## Crew Orders

You can use your action to issue a number of crew orders up to your proficiency bonus. Each order affects a specific part of the ship, and you can issue the same order multiple times.

### Hull Orders

#### Brace for Impact!

Until your next turn, add 5 to your hull's Damage Threshold.

#### Damage Control!

The hull heals 6d6 HP.

### Pilot Orders

#### Trim the Sails!

Until your next turn, the pilot can use one pilot maneuver that requires a reaction without expending their reaction.

### Spelljammer Orders

#### Lay the Lines!

Until your next turn, the spelljammer can reroll one die rolled as part of a jamming protocol or concentration save.

### Weapon Orders

#### Open Fire!

You attack with the assigned weapon, targeting an enemy ship's hull or one of its components.

When you attack with a ship weapon, you can use any ability, provided you can explain how that ability helps you or your crew hit with the weapon. You add the chosen ability and the weapon's fixed attack bonus to the attack roll (You do not add anything to the damage roll).

#### Damage Control!

The weapon heals 6d6 HP.

### Component Orders

If a ship component has HP or an effect that can be activated in its description, you can issue the following orders.

#### Activate!

You use an effect listed in the component's description.

#### Damage Control!

The component heals 6d6 HP.
