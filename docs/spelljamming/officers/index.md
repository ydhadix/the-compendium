# Spelljammer Officers

Ships have three officer roles: Quartermaster, Pilot, and Spelljammer. Each officer controls different parts of the ship and has access to distinct officer actions.

Most of an adventurer's normal features aren't effective at the scale ships operate at. Nonetheless, an officer can use their officer action to instead take one regular action, bonus action, or cast a spell with a casting time of 1 minute or less. Officers still have their reaction, as normal.

Characters who don't have an officer role act as passengers or crew, and do not take turns during ship combat.
