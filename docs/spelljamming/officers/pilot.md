# Pilot

The pilot controls the ship's movement and position in combat, bringing the crew into range to unload their weapons while evading returning fire.

Pilots have three Pilot Actions they can take, as well as a set of Pilot Maneuvers they can use to enhance their pilot actions by expending Maneuver Dice.

A pilot begins each combat with twice their proficiency bonus in d6 maneuver dice.

## Pilot Actions

Some pilot actions require a piloting check, usually contested by an enemy ship's pilot. Piloting checks use your Precision proficiency and your choice of Strength or Dexterity.

Every ship has a size penalty, which is subtracted from your piloting checks, and determines how much damage your Ram action deals.

### Advance/Retreat

You maneuver closer to, or farther from, an enemy ship.

Make a piloting check against an enemy ship.

**success**: You move one range closer or farther.

**failure**: No effect.

### Hold Steady

You keep your ship's path as steady as possible.

Until your next turn, your ship has advantage on attacks, but attacks against your ship also have advantage.

### Ram

You collide with an enemy ship.

Make a piloting check against an enemy ship at Close range.

**success**: You roll d10s equal to twice your ship's size penalty and deal the result as bludgeoning damage to the enemy ship's hull, and half as much damage to your ship's hull.

**failure**: Until your next turn, attacks against your ship have advantage.

## Pilot Maneuvers

A pilot automatically knows each maneuver they qualify for, based on their class levels.

You can only use a maneuver when its condition is met, and you must expend one of your maneuver dice to do so.

Whenever a Pilot Maneuver refers to a ship's AC, or attacks made against a ship, this refers to the AC of the hull, as well as all the ship's weapons.

### Barrel Roll

*Level 1 Fighter, Monk, or Rogue*

You snap into a tight roll, kicking clear of an approaching enemy.

**before enemy advances or rams**: Add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you succeed, you can cause the enemy to overshoot and move one range farther from you.

### Parting Shot

*Level 1 Fighter, Paladin, Ranger, or Rogue*

You take advantage of a retreating turn for a clean hit.

**reaction, after you or enemy retreats**: Until your next turn, add a maneuver die to your ship's attack rolls.

### Punch It

*Level 1 Artificer or Barbarian*

You throttle the ship wide open to seize an opening advantage.

**before initiative**: Add a maneuver die to the initiative check. During the first round of combat, add the result of the maneuver die to your ship's AC and attack rolls.

### Ride the Current

*Level 1 Monk, Ranger, or Rogue*

You catch a rising current and let it launch you forward.

**before you advance or retreat**: Add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you succeed, you move an additional range.

### Rolling Scissors

*Level 1 Artificer, Barbarian, Fighter, or Paladin*

You twist into a tight spiral, dodging incoming fire.

**reaction, after enemy advances**: Until your next turn, subtract a maneuver die from damage your ship takes.

### Collision Course

*Level 5 Barbarian, Fighter, Paladin, or Rogue*

You exploit the enemy's charge, weaponizing their momentum.

**reaction, after enemy advances to close range**: You Ram the enemy and add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you fail, attacks against your ship don't have advantage.

### High Loop

*Level 5 Artificer, Monk, Ranger, or Rogue*

You pull the ship into a loop, swinging into a punishing descent.

**before you advance**: Add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you succeed, until your next turn, add the result of the maneuver die to your ship's damage rolls.

### Ironclad

*Level 5 Artificer, Barbarian, Fighter, or Paladin*

You lock down the ship, turning it into an immovable fortress.

**after you hold steady**: Until your next turn, add a maneuver die to your ship's damage rolls and subtract a maneuver die from damage your ship takes.

### Serpentine

*Level 5 Fighter, Monk, Ranger, or Rogue*

You veer unpredictably, cutting an erratic path around the enemy.

**before you advance or retreat**: Add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you succeed, until your next turn, add the result of the maneuver die to your ship's AC.

### Bombing Run

*Level 9 Artificer, Barbarian, Fighter, Paladin, or Rogue*

You dive in low, opening the window for a devastating volley.

**after you advance to close range**: Until your next turn, add a maneuver die to your ship's damage rolls and your ship's critical hit range increases by 2.

### Evasive Maneuvers

*Level 9 Fighter, Monk, Ranger, or Rogue*

You pull clear of an incoming strike with sharp, sudden turns.

**reaction, before enemy attacks**: Make a piloting check and add a maneuver die to the check. Use the result as your ship's AC against the triggering attack. Until your next turn, your ship has resistance to all damage.

### Locked On

*Level 9 Artificer, Monk, Ranger, or Rogue*

You bear down hard, holding the enemy in your sights.

**before you advance**: Add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you succeed, until your next turn, your ship's attacks have advantage.

### Ramming Speed

*Level 9 Barbarian, Fighter, or Paladin*

You push the engines to their limit and commit to a crushing blow.

**before you ram**: Add a maneuver die to your piloting check. If you succeed, your ship takes half damage from ramming. If you succeed by 5 or more, each enemy officer is Incapacitated until the end of their next turn.
