# Spelljammer

The spelljammer powers and augments the ship with their magic abilities.

While seated in the ship's spelljamming helm, the spelljammer can expend Spell Slots to use Jamming Protocols, special spell-like effects amplified for ship combat.

## Jamming Protocols

Jamming protocols behave like spells, except they do not require components. Jamming protocols use your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma for their spellcasting ability.

Whenever a jamming protocol refers to a ship's AC, or attacks made against a ship, this refers to the AC of the hull, as well as all the ship's weapons.

Each spelljamming helm comes with a set of inscribed protocols. A helm usually can't inscribe additional protocols, however you can use a jamming protocol that isn't inscribed in the helm, provided you can cast the protocol's base spell.

While concentrating on a jamming protocol, whenever the ship's hull takes damage, you make a Concentration save as though you took the damage.

Your concentration on a jamming protocol ends if you or the ship are Incapacitated, or you aren't seated in the helm.

### Eldritch Blaster

*Evocation Cantrip (Fire Bolt)*

You fire a bolt of condensed energy at an enemy.

**action**: You attack a target within Close or Long range.

**hit**: You deal 3d10 force damage. This damage increases by 2d10 at character levels 5, 11, and 17.

### Targeting System

*Divination Cantrip (True Strike)*

You lock-on to an enemy, making them easier to hit.

**action**: Until the end of your next turn, the next attack your ship makes against target Close-range ship or weapon has advantage.

### Warding Field

*Abjuration Cantrip (Blade Ward)*

You summon small, efficient barriers to protect the ship.

**action, concentration**: Until the end of your next turn, add your proficiency bonus to your ship's AC.

### Emergency Repairs

*1st-Level Abjuration (Mending)*

You restore a damaged part of the ship to working order.

**action**: Choose the ship's hull or one of its components.

- **hull**: The hull heals 4d8 HP.
- **component**: The component heals 2d8 HP. This effect can heal a component with 0 HP.

**upcast**: Add 4d8 healing or one target for each additional spell level.

### Air Envelope

*2nd-Level Abjuration (Air Bubble)*

You generate breathable air around your allies.

**action**: For 24 hours, up to six target Close-range, non-Leviathan creatures has a bubble of breathable air.

**upcast**: Add three targets for each additional spell level.

### Shields Up

*2nd-Level Abjuration (Shield)*

You quickly manifest a large, invisible barrier around your ship.

**reaction, before your ship is hit with an attack**: Until the end of your next turn, add 5 to your ship's AC, potentially causing the triggering attack to miss.

### Blackout

*3rd-Level Evocation (Darkness)*

You shroud your ship from view in a cloud of darkness.

**action, concentration**: For 1 hour, your ship's air envelope is filled with darkness. Darkvision can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't illuminate it.

### Deep Scan

*3rd-Level Divination (See Invisibility)*

You detect hidden threats in the local area.

**action, concentration**: For 1 hour, your ship sees a glowing outline around hidden, invisible, or ethereal ships and creatures within Close range.

### Distress Signal

*3rd-Level Evocation (Sending)*

You broadcast a desperate message, heedless of who receives it.

**action**: You speak a short message aloud.

Each creature that understands language within the plane, color bank, or local region of the astral sea you are in receives the message telepathically. Creatures that understand your message become aware of your current location.

This message repeats every 8 hours, or until you end it.

### Phase Drive

*3rd-Level Transmutation (Blink)*

You momentarily phase your ship into the ethereal plane.

**reaction, before your ship takes damage**: Your ship briefly becomes ethereal, avoiding the triggering damage.

### Cloaking Device

*4th-Level Illusion (Lesser Invisibility)*

You shift light around your ship, blending into the void.

**action, concentration**: For 1 hour, your ship is invisible.

This protocol ends if your ship attacks, deals damage, or uses another protocol.

### Aether Cannon

*5th-Level Evocation (Fireball)*

You launch concentrated arcane energy that sears space itself.

**action**: You attack a target within Close or Long range.

**hit**: You deal 12d10 force damage. Add 2d10 damage per additional spell level.

**miss**: You deal half as much damage.
