# Spelljamming Helms

## Artillerist's Helm

*Spelljammer Helm*

A helm designed to augment a ship's effective range.

**sniper**: your protocols that target at close range can also target at long range.

**protocols**: eldritch blaster, targeting system, warding field, plus two others

## Resonance Helm

*Spelljammer Helm*

A helm that harmonizes with its spelljammer.

**focused**: you can concentrate on an additional protocol (you make each concentration save separately).

**protocols**: eldritch blaster, targeting system, warding field, plus two others

## Righteous Helm

*Spelljammer Helm*

A helm that channels divine fury.

**divine smite**: when your ship hits with an attack, you can expend a spell slot to deal an additional 1d10 radiant damage, plus 1d10 radiant damage for each level of the spell slot expended.

**protocols**: eldritch blaster, targeting system, warding field, plus two others

## Standard Helm

*Spelljammer Helm*

A standard issue spelljamming helm.

**standard**: no additional traits.

**protocols**: eldritch blaster, targeting system, warding field, plus three others

## Warden's Helm

*Spelljammer Helm*

A helm suffused with abjuration magic.

**warding**: your warding field also increases your ship's damage threshold.

**protocols**: eldritch blaster, targeting system, warding field, plus two others
