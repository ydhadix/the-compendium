## Harpoon

*Quartermaster Weapon*

A hooked projectile that holds the enemy in place.

| ac | dt | hp | kp | range | atk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | — | 50 | 10 | close | +5 to hit, 2d10 piercing |

**grappling**: this weapon grapples its target and cannot be fired again until the target is freed. Attacks against a grappled ship have advantage. If the grappled ship retreats successfully, the ship doesn't move and the grapple ends.

## Living Hull

*Quartermaster Component*

Your hull is infused with life-giving, druidic magic.

**living**: when the quartermaster starts their turn, the hull heals 10 hp. Each day at dawn, the hull heals 1 kp.

## Reinforced Hull

*Quartermaster Component*

Thick armor offers superior defenses.

**reinforced**: the hull's hp maximum is doubled.
