# Pilot Components

## Afterburners

*Pilot Component*

Reserve thrusters that push your ship to its limits.

**action surge** (once per combat): after you advance or retreat, you immediately advance, retreat, or ram.

## Hunter's Sails

*Pilot Component*

Sharp sails that catch the wind best when prey is nearby.

**hunter**: when you advance, your pilot check has advantage.

## Mirage Sails

*Pilot Component*

Color-shifting sails that leave afterimages of your ship.

**mirage**: after you retreat, until the pilot's next turn, attacks against your ship have disadvantage.

## Naval Ram

*Pilot Component*

A thick, angled grate protects the ship's bow from collisions.

| ac | dt | hp | kp | range | atk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 10 | 100 | 20 | close | special |

**standard**: when you ram, your pilot check has advantage, and this ram takes damage instead of your hull.

## Wake Breakers

*Pilot Component*

Deflecting nodes around the hull disrupt nearby ships' movement.

**wake breaker**: other ships have disadvantage on pilot checks to advance into, or retreat out of, close range.
