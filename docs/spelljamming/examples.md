# Example Ships

A ship's stat block includes information about the ship's size, maneuverability, hull, and its capacity for components.

Some ships come with specific components — these components don't count against the ship's component limits.

## Hammerhead

A robust ship that prefers to ram its opponents.

| size | 250 × 25 feet |
|---|---|
| size penalty | 8 |
| hull material | wood |
| weight | heavy |
| ac | 15 |
| dt | 15 |
| hp | 350 |
| kp | 70 |
| components | pilot 2, quartermaster 3, spelljammer 1 |

**standard**: no additional traits.

### Hammer Ram

*Pilot Component*

A wide, rectangular ram protrudes from the ship's bow.

| ac | dt | hp | kp | range | atk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 10 | 100 | — | close | special |

**standard**: when you ram, your size penalty doesn't apply to the pilot check, and this ram takes damage instead of your hull.
